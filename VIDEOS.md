## How to save to your iPhone

1. Tap one of the videos above to play.
2. Tap the **share** icon (square with up-arrow) at the bottom of the player.
3. Choose **"Save Video"** — it goes to your Photos app.
4. From Photos, upload to TikTok / Instagram / YouTube / Facebook.
