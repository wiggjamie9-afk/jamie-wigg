Time Blindness Is Not An Excuse. It's My Reality.

In my brain, time is... weird. It's either infinite or it doesn't exist.

An hour feels like 10 minutes. 10 minutes feels like an hour.

I'm always late. To everything. Always.

I say "I'll be ready in 5 minutes" and actually believe it.

But 20 minutes pass and I haven't even started.

Now I'm 15 minutes late.

Again. For the 47th time this month.

People think I'm lazy or disrespectful.

But the truth is: my brain literally doesn't perceive time the way yours does.

This isn't a character flaw. This is how my ADHD brain is wired.

Time Blindness is real. Not lazy. Not disrespectful. Just a different brain.
