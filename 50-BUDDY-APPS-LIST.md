## 🎤 What Each Buddy Does

### Every App Includes:

**Chat**
- Type message → buddy responds with AI responses
- Free browser voice synthesis (no setup needed)
- Optional premium voice (ElevenLabs)
- Chat history saves locally

**Health Tracking**
- Heart rate measurement via camera (PPG)
- Mood tracking with daily prompts
- Manual health logging
- Health data export (premium)

**Personalization**
- Buddy-specific personality & voice
- Customizable settings
- Avatar generation (premium with Higgsfield)
- Talking head animation (premium)

**Offline**
- Works without internet
- All data local to phone
- No server uploads
- Privacy guaranteed
