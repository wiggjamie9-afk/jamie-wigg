## Launch Strategy: Wave-Based Rollout

Instead of dumping all 28 apps at once, we'll use a **wave strategy** to:
1. Build momentum with early launches
2. Gather reviews & ratings for social proof
3. Adjust based on market feedback
4. Maximize press coverage and organic reach

### Wave 1: Proof of Concept (Days 1-3)
**Goal:** Establish market presence and gather early data  
**Launch:** 4-5 apps, all categories represented

```
Day 1:
├── HEARTBEAT (Emotional AI) — Most innovative, highest upside
├── Expense Tracker (Financial) — Universal appeal
└── Simple Notes (Productivity) — High demand category

Day 2:
├── Meditation Guide (Wellness) — Trending category
└── Dreams (Sleep Health) — Growing market

Day 3:
├── English Pocket (Education) — Emerging markets
└── Quick Recipes (Lifestyle) — Casual users
```

**Wave 1 Strategy:**
- Soft launch (low budget spend)
- Monitor install velocity
- Gather early reviews
- Identify technical issues
- Adjust store listings based on CTR

### Wave 2: Category Expansion (Days 4-10)
**Goal:** Cover all categories with multiple entries  
**Launch:** 12-15 apps (2-3 per day)

## Crisis Management & Rollback Plan

### If Install Velocity Stalls
**Symptoms:** <100 installs/day after Day 5  
**Response:**
1. Review store listings for clarity issues
2. Run A/B tests on screenshots (swap #1 & #3)
3. Boost paid spending 50%
4. Activate influencer outreach
5. Check for critical app bugs (crash rate)

### If Crash Rate Exceeds 5%
**Symptoms:** App won't start or freezes on launch  
**Response:**
1. Immediately pause paid campaigns for that app
2. Debug on real Android 8/10/12 devices
3. Push hotfix update to Play Store
4. Post transparent update in store listing
5. Resume campaigns once fixed

### If Rating Drops Below 3.5 Stars
**Symptoms:** Negative reviews citing bugs or misleading descriptions  
**Response:**
1. Identify common complaint (crashes, missing feature, privacy concern)
2. Fix root cause
3. Push update with changelog mentioning fix
4. Reply to negative reviews professionally
5. Offer premium subscription as goodwill gesture

## Revenue Model & Pricing

- **Free Users:** 100% of installs
- **Premium Users:** 4% of free users (after 7 days)
- **ARPU:** $0.75/month (blended across geographies)

### Pricing by Region
| Region | Monthly | Annual | Annual (20% discount) |
|--------|---------|--------|--------|
| **Developed** (US, UK, AU) | $2.99 | $29.99 | $23.99 |
| **Emerging** (India, Brazil, Mexico) | $0.99 | $9.99 | $7.99 |
| **Least-Developed** | Free | Free | Free |
