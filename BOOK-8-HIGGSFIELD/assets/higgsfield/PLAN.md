## Story Breakdown

| Page | Text |
|------|------|
| 1 | Sunny's burrow was perfect. It was small and warm and completely hers. The entrance was hidden behind tall grass and a fallen log. |
| 2 | Inside, it opened up into a little chamber—just big enough for Sunny to curl up in, with soft dried grass lining the floor. |
| 3 | She had arranged it just right. Softer grass in one corner for sleeping. Smooth pebbles that caught the warmth of the day in another. |
| 4 | An opening to look out from, where she could watch the world but still be safe. As evening came, Sunny returned to her burrow. |
| 5 | She did this every night. It was her routine. Her ritual. Her home. She pushed aside the dry grass bedding, making it perfect. |
| 6 | She checked the entrance—safe, hidden, protected. She checked the roof—solid, secure, unbreakable. Everything was exactly as it should be. |
| 7 | Sunny settled into her burrow, and it was like the whole world disappeared. It was just her and this tiny, safe, warm space. |
| 8 | Nothing could hurt her here. Nothing could reach her here. The walls of the burrow held the warmth from the day. |
| 9 | Sunny could feel the earth all around her—protecting her, holding her, keeping her safe. Outside, the world was big and vast and full of unknowns. |
| 10 | But inside her burrow, everything was known. Everything was safe. Everything was perfect. Sunny could hear the sounds of evening outside—birds settling, insects quieting. |
| 11 | The forest preparing for night. But the sounds were muted, distant, not scary. She was in her safe place. Her home. Her shelter. |
| 12 | She arranged the grass around her, creating a nest. She pulled the softest dried leaves around herself like a blanket. |
| 13 | She curled up into a ball, small and protected and safe. Her burrow wrapped around her like arms. The earth held her like a parent. |
| 14 | The darkness was not scary—it was comfortable, familiar, known. This was where she belonged. This was where she was safest. This was home. |
| 15 | Sunny closed her eyes and felt the safety of her burrow wrap around her. Outside, the night came. Inside, Sunny slept, held by the earth. |
| 16 | Goodnight, burrow. Thank you for keeping me safe. Goodnight, home. Goodnight, Sunny. Sleep peacefully in your cosy shelter. |
