## Parallel vs Sequential: The Real Choice

**Sequential (One Brain at a Time)**:
- ✅ Safer (validate each before next)
- ✅ Simpler to manage
- ✅ Can pivot if one brain doesn't work
- ❌ Slower to revenue
- ❌ Takes 18-20 months

**Parallel (All at Once)**:
- ✅ Faster (12 months)
- ✅ Better network effects (all brains live together)
- ✅ Bigger launch impact (6 brains > 1 brain)
- ❌ Riskier (all 6 could fail)
- ❌ Requires team/contractors from start
- ❌ Costs more upfront

**Growth Path (Start with One, Expand)**:
- ✅ Safer (validate Founder's Brain first)
- ✅ Cheaper (revenue funds expansion)
- ✅ Smarter team building (hire as you scale)
- ✅ Learn from each brain before next
- ⚠️ Takes 18-24 months (middle ground)
- ✅ Revenue compounds throughout
