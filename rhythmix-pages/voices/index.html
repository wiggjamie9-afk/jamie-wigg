FAQ

Voice questions,
direct answers.

Is the demo really synthesizing the audio in my browser?

Yes. Every note you hear is generated live by your browser's Web Audio engine using vowel-formant synthesis. No audio files, no network calls, no backend.

Are these real artists?

No. The 12 voices are synthetic models trained on consenting session vocalists. Names are RHYTHMIX originals.

What languages can my cloned voice sing in?

Pro plans support 32 languages out of the box. The cloned model adapts your timbre to each language while preserving accent characteristics.

Can someone else clone my voice?

No. Cloning requires a live verified-recording handshake from your account. Voices are watermarked at output and revocable in one tap.

Can I sell tracks made with the stock voices?

Yes — full commercial rights on Creator and Pro. Free plan is for personal use only and includes a watermark.

What about deepfake risks?

Every clone requires liveness verification and the consent handshake. Output audio carries a forensic watermark and is logged per use, so unauthorized reuse is detectable.
