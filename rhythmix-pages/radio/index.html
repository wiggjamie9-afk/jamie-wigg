FAQ

Radio questions,
live answers.

Can I upload music made outside RHYTHMIX?

Yes — Creator and Pro plans accept external WAV/FLAC uploads. They go through a one-pass moderation and rights-check before going live. Tracks made on RHYTHMIX skip that queue.

How do royalties work?

Every spin is logged with country, channel, and listener tier. Payouts are calculated daily and sent to your linked wallet — bank, Stripe, or crypto. 70/30 artist/platform split.

Is RHYTHMIX Radio actually live, or pre-scheduled?

Both. Channels run a curated rotation that's the same worldwide at any given moment, with live takeovers for premieres and host segments. So Tokyo and Toronto really are hearing the same song at the same time.

Can I skip songs as a listener?

Free tier: 6 skips/hour. Creator/Pro: unlimited skips. You can also "save" tracks to your wallet — when you do, the original artist gets a bonus payout.

How do you stop spam uploads?

Each Free upload counts against a 3-track-per-month quota. Creator/Pro is unlimited but every track is fingerprint-checked, AI-detection-flagged, and human-reviewed before air.

Can my listeners donate or buy my music directly from Radio?

Yes — the now-playing card shows a tip jar and a buy-the-track button. 100% of tips go to the artist. Track purchases use the standard 70/30 split.
