FAQ

Sensible questions.
Direct answers.

Is this a label, or a layer on top of distribution?

Both work. Releases can fully replace your distributor end-to-end, or sit on top with two-way sync. Most artists start as a layer and migrate when they see the time savings.

What does the AI loop see?

Only what you connect: DAW exports, voice memos, calendar, store dashboards, fan messages. We never train shared models on your data. You can audit every input and revoke any source.

How accurate are the drafts?

Drafts come with full release context (genre, references, prior copy that worked). On a typical Pro deployment, ~78% of captions ship with no edits.

Where does my data live?

EU or US, your choice. Roster customers can pin per-region. SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR-compliant DPA.

How long is the migration?

Typically 1 day for solo artists, 1-2 weeks for a small team, 4-6 weeks for full rosters with custom workflows.

Do you take royalties?

No. Flat subscription. You keep 100% of streaming, sync, and merch revenue forever.
