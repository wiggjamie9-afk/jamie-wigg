THREE PILLARS

One platform.
Every discipline.

Composition, performance, and release — RHYTHMIX Pro brings every part of making and shipping a record into a single, version-controlled space.

01 — COMPOSE

Native multi-track engine

Beats, bass, harmony, vocals, and arrangement on one canvas. Every layer is editable, swappable, and reversible. Constraints sync across the whole track in real time.

02 — PERFORM

Voices, takes, & takes

Train voice models on your singers. Multi-take comping with automatic tuning and timing. Performances live alongside the arrangement, not in a different tool.

03 — RELEASE

Label-grade delivery

Mastered to platform spec, registered with the right rights orgs, and pushed to every store. Splits, sync clearances, and royalty flows — all in the same project.

COMPOSITION

From hook to arrangement, in one move.

Drop a melodic idea. The engine proposes counter-melody, drum design, and harmonic structure — locked to your chosen reference, tempo, and key. Edit any layer and the rest re-renders in seconds.

PERFORMANCE

Voice models that live next to the song.

Train on a singer's takes once. Use the model across the project for tracking, harmonies, ad-libs, and stems. Consent-locked, audited, watermarked at delivery.

Dual-stage mastering with platform-aware loudness and a transparent, defeat-able chain. A/B against any reference. Stems exported clean for sync and remix.

Splits, ISRCs, distribution, sync, and rights — handled inside the project. CI for releases: every change runs validation against store specs before delivery.

FAQ

Common
questions.

Want a deeper architecture doc, security review, or migration plan? We will send it over.

Does Pro import my existing project files?

Yes. Drag-and-drop sessions from common DAWs, plus stems, MIDI, and reference tracks. We preserve track structure, automation, and routing as we can.

How does the release pipeline actually work?

Stores, rights orgs, and distribution partners are pre-integrated. Deliver from the project — splits, ISRCs, and validation happen automatically.

Where do renders run?

Our managed cluster, with priority queues for Pro. Bespoke customers can attach a dedicated render cluster.

Do you take any of my royalties?

No. We charge a flat subscription. You keep 100% of streaming, sync, and sale revenue forever.

Is voice cloning consent-safe?

Yes. Models are consent-locked to identified singers, watermarked at delivery, and auditable per use.
