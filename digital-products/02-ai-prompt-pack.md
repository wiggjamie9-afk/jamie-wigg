## 1. Niche & validation (1–6)

**1. Niche pain finder**
> Act as a market researcher. I have these skills: [SKILLS]. List 10 underserved customer segments who would pay $500–$5000 for help with these skills. For each, give: who they are, the painful problem, and where they hang out online.

**2. Reddit pain mining**
> Search behavior: I want to find real pain points in `[NICHE]`. Generate 25 Reddit search queries that would surface posts where someone is asking for help and would likely pay for it.

**3. Competitor map**
> List 10 freelancers or small agencies serving `[NICHE]`. For each, infer their pricing, what they offer, and one gap in their offer. Output as a markdown table.

**4. Buyer interview script**
> I'm interviewing potential buyers in `[NICHE]`. Write a 12-question script that uncovers: their current pain, what they've tried, their budget, decision authority, and timeline. Avoid leading questions.

**5. Niche disqualifier**
> Critique this niche choice: `[NICHE + OFFER]`. List 5 reasons it might fail in 2026 and 3 reasons it might work. Be brutal.

**6. Niche pivot**
> My current niche is `[CURRENT]` and it's not working. Suggest 5 adjacent niches that use the same skills but reach buyers with more budget and less competition.

## 2. Offer design (7–13)

**7. Offer one-liner**
> Convert this messy offer description into a clean one-liner: *"I help [WHO] achieve [OUTCOME] in [TIME] without [PAIN]."* Description: `[YOUR DESCRIPTION]`.

**8. Outcome ladder**
> For my offer `[OFFER]`, list 5 outcomes from low-stakes to high-stakes that a buyer might want. Suggest a price for each.

**9. Productize a service**
> Turn this hourly service into a productized package with a fixed price and scope: `[SERVICE]`. Output: name, deliverables, price, timeline, what's NOT included.

**10. Tiered pricing**
> Build a 3-tier pricing structure (Basic / Standard / Premium) for `[OFFER]`. Include what's in/out of each tier and price anchors.

**11. Risk reversal**
> Write a guarantee for `[OFFER]` that removes buyer risk without exposing me to abuse. Include the conditions clearly.

**12. Bundle generator**
> I sell `[PRODUCT A]` for `[$X]` and `[PRODUCT B]` for `[$Y]`. Suggest a bundle price and a sales argument for why buying together is the right move.

**13. Anti-objection list**
> List the top 7 objections a buyer might have for `[OFFER]`. For each, write a one-paragraph rebuttal.

## 3. Sales copy (14–22)

**14. Landing page hero**
> Write a landing page hero for `[OFFER]`. Format: headline (max 10 words), subheadline (max 20 words), CTA button text (max 4 words). Tone: confident, specific, no jargon.

**15. Long-form sales page**
> Write a 1200-word sales page for `[OFFER]` priced at `[$X]`. Include: hero, problem, agitation, solution, proof (placeholders), how it works, FAQ, guarantee, CTA. Use second-person.

**16. Hook variations**
> Write 10 hooks for `[OFFER]` aimed at `[BUYER]`. Mix: question, contrarian, statistic, story, "imagine if."

**17. Cold DM**
> Write a 3-sentence cold DM from me to `[BUYER PROFILE]` about `[OFFER]`. No "hope you're well," no flattery, no link in first message.

**18. Cold email**
> Write a cold email under 90 words to `[BUYER PROFILE]` offering `[OFFER]`. Subject line should not look like marketing. End with a low-friction ask.

**19. Twitter thread**
> Write an 8-tweet thread teaching `[VALUABLE INSIGHT IN MY NICHE]`. Last tweet softly pitches `[OFFER]`. No emojis except for one in the hook.

**20. LinkedIn post**
> Write a LinkedIn post (~250 words) sharing a lesson I learned about `[TOPIC]`. Tone: matter-of-fact, no humble brag. End with a question.

**21. Case study draft**
> Turn this raw client win into a polished case study: `[RAW NOTES]`. Include: situation, action, result, quote (placeholder), what to do next.

**22. Headline tester**
> Critique these 5 headlines for `[OFFER]` and rank them by predicted CTR. Tell me why. Headlines: `[LIST]`.

## 4. Content & traffic (23–29)

**23. TikTok script (30s)**
> Write a 30-second TikTok script teaching `[INSIGHT]`. Hook in first 2 seconds, payoff at 25s, CTA at 28s.

**24. YouTube short**
> Write a YouTube Shorts script under 50 seconds about `[TOPIC]`. Visual cues every 5s. Subtle CTA to subscribe.

**25. Newsletter issue**
> Write a 600-word newsletter for `[NICHE]` with one big idea, two examples, and one actionable takeaway.

**26. Carousel post (Instagram/LinkedIn)**
> Outline a 7-slide carousel teaching `[INSIGHT]`. Slide 1 = hook, slide 7 = CTA. Each slide gets a 1-sentence headline + 2 bullets max.

**27. Blog SEO outline**
> Outline a 1500-word blog post targeting the keyword `[KEYWORD]`. Include H1, 5 H2s, FAQ section with 5 questions, and an internal link map.

**28. Hashtag research**
> Suggest 30 hashtags for TikTok/Instagram in `[NICHE]`, split into: 10 broad (>1M posts), 10 medium (100K–1M), 10 niche (<100K).

**29. Repurposing matrix**
> Take this long-form piece: `[CONTENT]`. Repurpose into: 1 Twitter thread, 1 LinkedIn post, 3 TikTok hooks, 1 newsletter intro, 1 quote graphic copy.

## 5. Sales calls & ops (30–35)

**30. Discovery call script**
> Build a 25-minute discovery call script for prospects considering `[OFFER]`. Sections: rapport (2 min), problem dive (10 min), goal clarification (5 min), fit check (5 min), next step (3 min).

**31. Proposal template**
> Write a 1-page proposal for `[CLIENT]` for `[PROJECT]` at `[$X]`. Include: scope, timeline, deliverables, payment terms, what's not included.

**32. Onboarding email**
> Write a welcome email for a new client of `[OFFER]`. Include: confirmation of payment, what happens next, how to contact me, expected timeline.

**33. Project kickoff form**
> Generate 10 questions to ask a new `[OFFER]` client during onboarding. Skip generic questions; focus on what unblocks delivery.

**34. SOP writer**
> Write a step-by-step SOP for `[REPETITIVE TASK]` so I (or a future hire) can do it in under 30 minutes without thinking.

**35. Deliverable QA checklist**
> Create a 12-point QA checklist I run before sending `[DELIVERABLE]` to a client.

## 6. Support & retention (36–40)

**36. Refund policy writer**
> Write a fair refund policy for `[OFFER]` priced at `[$X]`. Cover: window, conditions, what's non-refundable.

**37. Tough customer reply**
> Draft a reply to this unhappy customer message: `[MESSAGE]`. Tone: calm, ownership, propose a concrete fix.

**38. Upsell email**
> Write an upsell email to a customer 30 days after they bought `[PRODUCT A]`, offering `[PRODUCT B]`. Tie it to the result they got from A.

**39. Win-back email**
> Write a re-engagement email to a customer who hasn't bought from me in 6 months. Lead with curiosity, not desperation.

**40. NPS follow-up**
> Generate a 5-question follow-up survey for someone who rated my service 7+ on NPS. Goal: extract a usable testimonial.

## 7. Growth (41–45)

**41. Affiliate pitch**
> Write a 100-word pitch to ask `[CREATOR]` to be an affiliate for `[OFFER]`. Lead with what's in it for them.

**42. Partnership intro**
> Write an email proposing a content/affiliate partnership with `[COMPLEMENTARY BRAND]`. Include 3 specific collaboration ideas.

**43. Pricing experiment plan**
> Design a 4-week pricing experiment for `[OFFER]` testing `[PRICE A]` vs `[PRICE B]`. Define success metrics.

**44. Referral system**
> Design a customer referral system for `[OFFER]`. Include: incentive for referrer, incentive for new buyer, how to track, how to communicate it.

**45. Retainer pitch**
> Convert this one-off project into a monthly retainer pitch. Project: `[DESCRIPTION]`. Output: scope, monthly price, what triggers an upgrade.

## 8. Finance & boring stuff (46–50)

**46. Invoice template**
> Write a freelance invoice for `[CLIENT]`, project `[NAME]`, amount `[$X]`. Include: my info, their info, line items, payment terms (Net 14), late fee policy.

**47. Tax check-in**
> List the top 10 tax mistakes a side-hustler in `[COUNTRY]` makes in their first year, and how to avoid each. (Not legal advice — disclaimer).

**48. Financial model**
> Build a simple monthly financial model for my side hustle: `[CURRENT STATE]`. Include: revenue projections, costs, time investment, breakeven point.

**49. Quit-job math**
> Help me calculate at what monthly side-hustle revenue I could realistically quit my full-time job. My expenses are `[$X/month]`. Account for healthcare, taxes, runway.

**50. End-of-quarter review**
> Run an end-of-quarter business review with me. Ask me 8 questions covering: revenue, customer count, churn, time spent, biggest unblock, biggest mistake, next quarter goal.
