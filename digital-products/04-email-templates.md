## Acquisition (1–4)

### Email 1: Cold outreach (problem-aware prospect)

**Subject:** quick question about [SPECIFIC THING ON THEIR SITE/PROFILE]
**Alternates:** noticed you're hiring for X / a question about [their company] / [first name], 30 seconds

> Hey [FIRST NAME],
>
> Saw your [POST/SITE/JOB AD] mentioning [SPECIFIC THING]. I work with [SIMILAR COMPANIES] on [PROBLEM YOU SOLVE], and one thing usually trips them up: [SPECIFIC INSIGHT].
>
> Worth a 15-min call to compare notes? Happy to share what's worked for [SIMILAR EXAMPLE] either way.
>
> [YOUR NAME]

### Email 2: Cold outreach (referral angle)

**Subject:** [MUTUAL CONTACT] suggested I reach out
**Alternates:** intro from [MUTUAL] / re: [MUTUAL] / quick intro

> Hi [FIRST NAME],
>
> [MUTUAL CONTACT] mentioned you're working on [PROJECT/TOPIC] and thought I might be useful. I help [BUYER TYPE] with [OUTCOME] — recently helped [EXAMPLE] [SPECIFIC RESULT].
>
> Open to a short call this week or next? I'll come prepared with one specific idea for [THEIR SITUATION].
>
> [YOUR NAME]

### Email 3: Follow-up to non-response (after 5 days)

**Subject:** RE: [ORIGINAL SUBJECT]
**Alternates:** still relevant? / closing the loop / one more thing

> Hi [FIRST NAME],
>
> Bumping this — figured my last email might've gotten buried.
>
> If [PROBLEM] isn't a priority right now, no worries at all, I'll stop bothering you. If it is and the timing's just bad, just reply "later" and I'll circle back next quarter.
>
> [YOUR NAME]

### Email 4: Last touch (after 2 unresponded follow-ups)

**Subject:** closing your file
**Alternates:** wrapping up / final note from me / no harm no foul

> Hi [FIRST NAME],
>
> Going to assume the timing isn't right and stop emailing. If anything changes — or if you want to be introduced to someone in [THEIR FIELD] who's solved [PROBLEM] before — my inbox is open.
>
> [YOUR NAME]

## Sales (5–7)

### Email 5: Discovery call recap + proposal

**Subject:** [PROJECT NAME] — recap + next steps

> Hi [FIRST NAME],
>
> Quick recap of our call so we're aligned:
>
> **What you're solving for:** [PROBLEM IN THEIR WORDS]
> **What I proposed:** [OFFER, ONE SENTENCE]
> **Investment:** [$X], delivered [TIMELINE]
> **Outcome:** [SPECIFIC MEASURABLE RESULT]
>
> If that matches what we discussed, here's the payment link: [LINK]. I'll kick off within 24 hours of payment.
>
> Any concerns, just reply — happy to adjust.
>
> [YOUR NAME]

### Email 6: Objection handler (price)

**Subject:** RE: pricing

> Hi [FIRST NAME],
>
> Totally fair to think about it. Two things that might help:
>
> 1. [$X] is the all-in price — no surprises, no hourly creep. If [PROJECT] takes me 50 hours, that's on me.
> 2. If full scope is too much right now, I have a smaller version: [SMALLER OFFER] for [$Y]. Hits the most important part first — [SPECIFIC PIECE].
>
> Want to do that one and decide on the rest later?
>
> [YOUR NAME]

### Email 7: Urgency (legitimate, not fake)

**Subject:** booking [MONTH] — heads up

> Hi [FIRST NAME],
>
> Booking my [MONTH] capacity this week — I take on [N] projects per month. If [YOUR PROJECT] is still on, want me to reserve a slot? No payment yet, just a soft hold until [DATE].
>
> If now's not the right time, no worries — I'll loop back when you're ready.
>
> [YOUR NAME]

>

>
> [YOUR NAME]

>

>
> [YOUR NAME]

>
> [YOUR NAME]
