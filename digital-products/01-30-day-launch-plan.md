## Week 1 — Pick a niche and an offer

### Day 1 (Monday): Inventory your unfair advantages
- Spend 60 min listing: skills you've been paid for, problems coworkers ask you to solve, software you know unusually well, communities you're already in.
- Output: a list of 10+ items.

### Day 2: Map skills → buyer pain
- For each item from Day 1, write one sentence: "Who would pay me to ____ for them?"
- Cross out anything where the answer is vague.

### Day 3: Pick three candidate niches
- Choose three from Day 2 with the clearest buyer.
- For each, search Reddit/X for "I need help with ____" — note real complaints, screenshot them.

### Day 4: Validate buyer power
- Search "[niche] freelancer rates" and "[niche] tools they pay for."
- Cut any niche where buyers are broke (students, hobbyists with no income).
- Pick the winner.

### Day 5: Define your one offer
- Write: *"I help [specific buyer] [achieve specific outcome] in [timeframe] for [price]."*
- Make it concrete enough that a stranger could repeat it.

### Day 6 (weekend): Buy the domain. Set up a one-page site.
- Use the **One-Page Launch Site Template** (Product 5). 90 minutes max.

### Day 7 (weekend): Rest. Don't skip.

## Week 2 — Build the minimum viable offering

### Day 8: Write your offer page
- Use the AI Prompt Pack prompts: "landing page hero," "objection handler," "social proof scaffold."
- Goal: 4 sections — headline, what you do, how it works, CTA.

### Day 9: Define what "delivery" looks like
- One Notion/Google Doc template that captures every deliverable for one customer. (Use Product 3.)

### Day 10: Price it
- Look at 5 competitors. Price 20% under the highest, 30% above the lowest. Don't undercut on price 1.

### Day 11: Set up payment
- Stripe Payment Link, or Gumroad if it's a digital product. 30 minutes.

### Day 12: Write your "FAQ"
- 10 questions a skeptical buyer would ask. Answer them.

### Day 13: Build a 5-email follow-up sequence
- Use the Email Pack (Product 4) Templates 1–5.

### Day 14 (weekend): Soft launch to 10 friends/colleagues
- Goal isn't sales — it's feedback on confusion points.

---

## Week 3 — Get your first 5 customers

### Day 15: Pick one channel and commit
- Cold DMs on X/LinkedIn, Reddit answers, TikTok content, or in-person network. **Pick one.**

### Day 16: Write 30 cold outreach messages
- Personalized, ~3 sentences. Use Email Pack Template 1.

### Day 17: Send all 30. Track in a sheet.
- Expected reply rate: 5–15%.

### Day 18: Reply to every response within 4 hours.
- Don't pitch in the first reply. Get on a 15-minute call instead.

### Day 19: Run discovery calls.
- Listen 70%, talk 30%. Confirm the offer matches their pain.

### Day 20: Send proposals/payment links.
- Same day as the call. Speed kills hesitation.

### Day 21 (weekend): Send next 30 outreach messages.

---

## Week 4 — Systematize and scale to repeatable

### Day 22: Onboard your first 1–2 customers
- Use a single onboarding form (5 questions max).

### Day 23: Deliver the first piece of work
- Set a deadline 25% sooner than they expect.

### Day 24: Ask for testimonial mid-project
- "Anything I should keep doing?" — turn the answer into copy.

### Day 25: Build a referral ask into delivery
- "If you know anyone else with [problem], introducing me means $X off your next [thing]."

### Day 26: Write a public case study post
- Post on the channel from Day 15.

### Day 27: Raise your price 20% for the next customer
- Don't tell anyone. Just charge it.

### Day 28: Decide what you're killing
- Look at 4 weeks of data. Cut the activities that didn't produce a customer or a lead.

### Day 29: Plan the next 30 days
- One growth lever, one delivery improvement, one product expansion.

### Day 30: Take the night off.

## Failure modes to watch for

- **You spent week 1 picking a niche and never started week 2.** Niche perfection is procrastination. Pick the okay one and ship.
- **You wrote 30 outreach messages and sent 0.** Send before they're polished. Reply rate compensates for messy.
- **You took on a customer who's a bad fit "just to start."** They will eat your evenings. Refund and move on.
- **You undercharged the first sale.** Acceptable — but raise prices by customer 3.

## What to do if you fall behind

Skip ahead, never sideways. Missing day 14 is fine. Going back to redo day 4 on day 15 isn't. You're building momentum, not perfection.
