## Where to sell

- **Gumroad** — fastest, lowest friction, takes a cut. Good for cold-traffic offers.
- **Payhip** — cheaper fees than Gumroad, supports EU VAT.
- **Etsy** — high volume of digital-product buyers, but markup their algorithm wants.
- **Lemon Squeezy** — better for SaaS/subscriptions; fine for one-offs too.

Recommend: launch on Gumroad first, mirror to Payhip after week 1.

## What you (the seller) still need to do

This bundle is *content-ready*. Before selling you must:

1. Create accounts on Gumroad and/or Payhip.
2. Convert each `.md` to PDF (Pandoc, or paste into Google Docs → Download as PDF).
3. Convert the Notion template to actual Notion (import the markdown, structure into pages).
4. Take screenshots of each product for the listing.
5. Write a one-line value prop and 3-bullet description per product (drafts in each file).
6. Set up Stripe in your Gumroad account so payouts work.
7. Drive traffic — TikTok, Twitter/X, Reddit (`r/sidehustle`, `r/Entrepreneur`).

## Honest expectations

- **First sale:** anywhere from day 1 to month 1 depending on how much traffic you drive.
- **Realistic month-one revenue with effort:** $200–$2,000.
- **No traffic = no sales.** The hardest part isn't building the products; it's getting eyeballs.
- **Refunds happen.** Build a 7-day refund policy into your store settings.
