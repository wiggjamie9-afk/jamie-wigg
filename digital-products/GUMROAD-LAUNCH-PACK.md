## Cover images — easiest path

For each listing's cover image:

1. Open the PDF in iPhone Files → take a screenshot of page 1 (the title)
2. In Photos, **crop to a square** (1:1) — Gumroad covers look best square
3. In the Gumroad listing → tap **+ Add image** → upload that screenshot

It's not pretty but it's serviceable. Upgrade later — what matters is shipping today, not perfection.

If you want better covers later, I can write image-generation prompts you can run in the banana-claude skill (once you have a Gemini key set up).
