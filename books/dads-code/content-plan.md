# Dad's Code — Faceless Channel Content Pack & Monetization Plan

Channel concept: **Dad's Code** — short, honest reflections on fatherhood,
neurodivergence (dyslexia/ADHD), men's mental health, and staying present.
Faceless = your **voice + words over calm cinematic visuals**. No face needed.
Every clip points gently back to the book.

> Tone rules: real, not preachy. Share *experience*, not medical advice. On any
> mental-health clip, pin the support resources (bottom of this file). Hook in the
> first 2 seconds. 1 idea per reel. End with a soft line, not a hard sell.

Each reel below has: **Hook** (on-screen text, 0–2s) · **Voiceover** (you narrate,
~25–40s) · **Captions** (key on-screen words) · **Visual** (VEO3 prompt, 9:16) ·
**Caption/hashtags** · **CTA**.

- **Voice:** narrate in your own voice (you can clone it with the Voicebox tool in this repo so it's literally you). Authentic voice >> generic AI voice for memoir content.

# What sells alongside the ebook (monetization, easiest → highest ceiling)
1. **The ebook** — *Dad's Code* on Amazon KDP (~$4.99–9.99). You keep ~70%.
2. **Paperback** — same KDP upload, print-on-demand. Many memoir readers want a physical copy; higher price, real margin.
3. **Audiobook** — *Dad's Code* narrated in **your own voice** (clone via Voicebox / ElevenLabs). Memoir audiobooks sell well and your voice is the selling point. (ACX/Spotify/Apple.)
4. **Companion: "Dad's Code — 30-Day Presence Journal"** — a low-content workbook (prompts from your principles: presence, honesty, showing up). Cheap to make, high margin, sells *with* the book.
5. **Free first chapter = lead magnet** — give Chapter 1 free in exchange for an email. Build a list of dads/readers you actually own (not rented from an algorithm).
6. **Frequency / RHYTHMIX music** — your calm/healing tracks as a companion "read-along" or "sleep" album; tie it to the book's frequency theme.
7. **Affiliate links** — books/tools on ADHD, dyslexia, fatherhood, men's mental health in your video descriptions.
8. **Community / Patreon** — a space for dads navigating neurodivergence + mental health; monthly support.
9. **Speaking / school & club talks** — dyslexia, men's mental health, "showing up" — schools and sports clubs pay for this, and it sells books at the back of the room.
10. **Merch (later)** — "The Air Cuddle," "Be like McDonald's," principle cards — once the audience exists.

> Honest order of operations: **book live → reels driving an email list → audiobook + journal → community/speaking.** The reels build the audience; the products convert it. The book is the anchor everything points to.
