# Music Video Concept — "We Rise"

**Run time:** 3:20
**Genre:** Uplifting anthemic pop / cinematic indie
**Overall feeling:** Hopeful → swelling → euphoric → bittersweet warmth → triumphant unity. The viewer should leave with goosebumps and the urge to call someone they love.
**Visual signature:** Golden-hour warmth bleeding into electric neon night. Real people, real sweat, real hands. Practical lights over CGI. Hand-held when intimate, Steadicam when soaring, drone when the world opens up.

---

## Core Story

Three interconnected character arcs converge on the same rooftop at the song's final chorus. They never know each other through the verses — only the music, the city, and a thread of light connect them. By the bridge they drift toward one another. By the final chorus, the rooftop is full of strangers who have become a chosen family for one night.

### The Three Leads

1. **MAYA (early 20s)** — A nurse coming off a 14-hour shift. Tired eyes, scrub top under a denim jacket, hospital lanyard still around her neck. She represents the *weight people carry*. Black, natural curls pulled back, a small heart tattoo on her wrist.
2. **RAVI (late teens)** — A kid from a cramped flat with sketchbooks taped to every wall. He's a dancer who has never danced in front of anyone. Brown skin, oversized vintage tee, paint on his hands. Represents *hidden talent / private dreams*.
3. **ELENA (mid 30s)** — A widowed single mother carrying her 6-year-old daughter LILA on her hip. Soft eyes, a wedding ring she still wears on a chain. Represents *love that endured*.

### The Crowd (by the final chorus)
A grandfather with a cane. Two teenage girls in football kit (nod for you, mate). A drag performer mid-makeup. A construction worker still in hi-vis. A boy with a wheelchair-using friend pushing him to the front. An elderly couple slow-dancing. None of them speak — they just feel it together.

## Scene-by-Scene Storyboard

### 00:00 – 00:18 | INTRO (instrumental swell)
**Setting:** A city at dusk. London-ish but unspecified — wet pavement, terraced houses, distant tower blocks, a corner shop glowing amber.
**Camera:** Slow drone push down a long empty street. A single string of fairy lights flickers on across a balcony — then another, then another, like a chain reaction running through the city.
**Action:** Maya steps out of a hospital revolving door, exhales, looks up at the sky. Ravi stares at himself in his bedroom mirror, headphones on, still. Elena sits on her kitchen floor, Lila asleep on her chest, an old photograph in her hand.
**Feeling:** Quiet. Held breath. Something is about to begin.
**Colour:** Cool blues, deep indigo, one warm tungsten window in every frame.

---

### 00:18 – 00:48 | VERSE 1 — *"In the heart of the night, we find our way…"*
**Setting:** Three parallel cuts between Maya's bus ride home, Ravi's bedroom, Elena's kitchen.
**Camera:** Hand-held, intimate. Close on hands, eyes, breath fogging glass.
**Action:**
- Maya rests her head on the bus window. The streetlights pulse past her face like a heartbeat — synced to the kick drum.
- Ravi slides one tentative foot across the lino. Then a shoulder roll. He stops. Tries again.
- Elena strokes Lila's hair, then quietly hums along to the song playing from a tinny radio on the windowsill.
**Feeling:** Loneliness that is also a kind of tenderness. Each character is alone but the music is the same.
**Visual motif:** A single warm light source in each frame — bus interior, fairy lights, fridge bulb spilling onto the floor.

---

### 00:48 – 01:00 | BRIDGE — *"Let the lights guide our souls…"*
**Setting:** All three characters look up at the same moment. Outside each of their windows, the streetlights flicker on in a wave.
**Camera:** Match cut on the upward glance — three faces lit by warm light blooming.
**Action:** Ravi opens his bedroom window. Maya gets off the bus a stop early. Elena gently lays Lila down and pulls on a coat.
**Feeling:** Pull. Gravity. The city is calling them somewhere.

---

### 01:00 – 01:30 | CHORUS 1 — *"We rise like the sun, we rise like the tide…"*
**Setting:** The streets. Each character walking — not knowing where, just toward.
**Camera:** Smooth tracking shots, low angle, walking pace matching the beat. Wide lens. Sky takes up two-thirds of every frame.
**Action:**
- Maya passes a busker and drops her last coin in his case without breaking stride. He smiles.
- Ravi finally dances — alone — through an empty underpass. Spins. Throws his arms wide. Graffiti glows behind him under a flickering tube light.
- Elena walks past a couple arguing on a corner; she squeezes the older woman's hand as she passes. The woman bursts into tears, smiling.
- Wide drone shot: three tiny figures, three different streets, all walking the same direction.
**Feeling:** The world cracking open. First real lift of the song.
**Colour:** Magenta sunset bleeding into electric blue. Wet streets reflecting neon.

---

### 01:30 – 02:00 | VERSE 2 — *"With every whisper, together we stand…"*
**Setting:** A community garden tucked between two tower blocks. String lights overhead. An old upright piano someone dragged outside. Mismatched chairs.
**Camera:** Slower now. Steadicam circling. Faces lit by candles in jam jars.
**Action:** Strangers are arriving. Not a crowd yet — just five, then eight, then twelve. They are hesitant. They nod at each other. The grandfather sits at the piano and plays a single chord. A teenage girl laughs, surprised. The drag performer arrives still pinning her wig and someone helps her without asking.
**Feeling:** The shy magic of strangers becoming something. Like the first 20 minutes of a great party before anyone has spoken.
**Visual motif:** Hands. Hands offering chairs. Hands lighting candles. Hands resting on shoulders.

---

### 02:00 – 02:12 | BRIDGE 2 — *"Feel the rhythm, hear it loud…"*
**Setting:** Same garden. The crowd has grown to maybe thirty people.
**Camera:** Punchy cuts on the beat. Faces, feet, the swing of a coat.
**Action:** Ravi arrives at the gate. He freezes. A small girl (Lila — Elena got there first) takes his hand and pulls him in. He laughs in disbelief. Maya arrives last — she stops dead at the entrance, overwhelmed. The grandfather at the piano catches her eye and nods. She walks in.
**Feeling:** The threshold. The moment you step into a room and decide to belong.

---

### 02:12 – 02:50 | CHORUS 2 — *"We rise like the sun, we rise like the tide…"*
**Setting:** The garden becomes a rooftop — they climb together up a fire escape, candles in hand, until the whole rooftop of a tower block is alive with people.
**Camera:** This is the *big* sequence. Crane shot rising up alongside them. Then a 360° spin around the centre of the rooftop as the chorus hits its peak. Then handheld in the middle of the dancing — sweat, hair, laughter, blur.
**Action:**
- Ravi finally dances in front of people — and they cheer.
- Maya throws her hospital lanyard into the air, laughs, lets a stranger spin her.
- Elena lifts Lila onto her shoulders and points at the sky.
- The whole rooftop sings the chorus together — no microphones, just voices.
- The drone pulls back: the rooftop is a single point of warm light in a vast dark city. Then more rooftops light up. Then more. The whole skyline is glowing.
**Feeling:** Pure euphoria. The catharsis the whole song has been promising. Cry-while-smiling territory.
**Colour:** Gold, amber, deep violet sky, sparks from a sparkler someone lit.

---

### 02:50 – 03:10 | OUTRO — *"Hands in the air, we're shining bright, we rise together into the night."*
**Setting:** Same rooftop. The song softens.
**Camera:** Slow, lingering close-ups. Almost still.
**Action:**
- Maya and Elena meet eyes across the rooftop and just smile. They don't need to speak.
- Ravi sits on the ledge, breathless, grinning at his own hands like he can't believe what they did.
- Lila has fallen asleep again on Elena's shoulder, a sparkler dying in her small fist.
- The grandfather closes the piano lid gently.
**Feeling:** The warm exhale after joy. The quiet that means *I'll remember this forever.*

---

### 03:10 – 03:20 | FINAL FRAME
**Setting:** Drone pulls up and away. The rooftop is one of hundreds now lit across the city. The fairy lights from the opening shot are everywhere. The screen slowly fades — but not to black. To the warm amber of the very first window.
**Final shot:** A single hand-written card pinned to the community garden gate, in a child's handwriting: *"You are not alone."*
**Cut to black.** Beat of silence. Title card (optional): **WE RISE.**

## Production Notes

### Casting principles
- Cast *real* people, not models. Faces with stories. Wrinkles, freckles, gap teeth, real bodies of every size.
- Multi-generational, multi-ethnic, mixed ability — but never tokenistic. Everyone gets a moment that belongs to *them*, not to a quota.
- The three leads should feel like people you might actually sit next to on the bus.

### Wardrobe
- Lived-in, not styled. Jackets with pilling. Trainers with scuffs. One small pop of warm colour per character (Maya: mustard scarf. Ravi: red shoelaces. Elena: a marigold cardigan).

### Locations
1. **Hospital exterior** — any large municipal hospital with a revolving door at dusk.
2. **Bedroom (Ravi)** — small, art-covered, one window, blue curtain.
3. **Kitchen (Elena)** — yellow tiles, fridge with crayon drawings, single bare bulb.
4. **Underpass** — graffiti, flickering strip light, wet floor.
5. **Bus interior** — practical, real night bus, real reflections.
6. **Community garden** — string lights, mismatched chairs, upright piano, candles in jam jars.
7. **Rooftop** — tower block with a flat accessible roof. London/Manchester/Glasgow skyline ideal.
8. **Drone over the city** — golden hour into blue hour into night.

### Lighting philosophy
- Practical sources first: fairy lights, candles, streetlights, fridge bulbs, sparklers.
- Warm tungsten (3200K) for intimacy, cool 5600K only for the sky and pre-chorus tension.
- One warm light source visible in every frame — the "guiding light" motif of the lyric.

### Camera kit
- ARRI Alexa Mini or Sony FX6.
- 32mm and 50mm primes for intimate scenes; 24mm for the big rooftop wides.
- DJI Inspire 3 with a long lens for the city-wide drone reveals.
- Ronin gimbal for the crowd sequences.

### Edit rhythm
- Verses: long-ish takes, slow cuts (3–5 sec).
- Pre-chorus bridges: cuts shorten dramatically (1–2 sec).
- Choruses: a mix — held wides for grandeur, fast handheld cuts on the beat for energy.
- Outro: held shots, almost still, breathing.

### Sound design layered under the music
- Footsteps on wet pavement on the kick drum.
- A door creak, a window opening, a struck match — all carefully on the off-beats.
- The crowd's voices barely audible in the final chorus, *just* under the lead vocal.

### Budget tier suggestion
- **Indie / £8–15k:** One day on the rooftop, half day for each character vignette, real locations, mostly natural light, small crowd of ~30 friends. Achievable.
- **Mid / £40–80k:** Add the drone work, professional lighting on the rooftop, proper SFX/colour grade.
- **Premium / £150k+:** Full crew, multi-day shoot, name DOP, full crowd of 100+, original choreography for Ravi.
