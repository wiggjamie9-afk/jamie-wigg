## STYLE REQUIREMENTS (THE BIBLE)

**All illustrations:**
- Soft watercolor painted style
- Pastel color palette: soft purples, warm golds, gentle greens, creams, soft blues
- Sunny always appears the same: golden-brown fur, warm brown eyes, gentle smile
- Soft edges, breathing room, cozy feeling
- Dimensions: 1920×1080 (16:9 landscape)

**Text:**
- White or cream colored
- Positioned at bottom of page
- 2 lines per story page
- Clear and readable against background
- Child-friendly font

**The Book Works as:**
1. Mom reads the text aloud (what she says)
2. Child sees the illustration (what's happening in the story)
3. Text + illustration together tell the complete story visually
