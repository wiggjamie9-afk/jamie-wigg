# SURGE - Episode 1: "Discover"
## Professional Animated Series Script
### 13-Minute Pilot Episode

---

## ACT 1: THE WORLD OF ZIGGY (3 minutes)

### SCENE 1: MORNING IN CHAOS (0:00-0:45)

**INTERIOR - ZIGGY'S BEDROOM - MORNING**

*Alarm clock BLARES. Ziggy BOLTS upright in bed, eyes wide with energy.*

**ZIGGY** (V.O., energetic, optimistic)
Every single day is an adventure. And every single moment, my brain is BUZZING with a thousand different things.

*Quick cuts: Ziggy jumps out of bed, trips over backpack, catches self mid-fall, laughs.*

**ZIGGY** (V.O.)
School says I have ADHD. Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder. Sounds like a problem, right? Like something's wrong with me.

*Ziggy puts on bright orange hoodie with electric blue trim.*

**ZIGGY** (V.O.)
But what if... it's not a weakness? What if it's actually a SUPERPOWER?

---

### SCENE 2: WALKING TO SCHOOL (0:45-1:45)

**EXTERIOR - NEIGHBORHOOD STREETS - MORNING**

*Ziggy bounces down the sidewalk, noticing EVERYTHING: butterfly, interesting rock pattern, friend's house, cloud shape.*

*MIRA appears from side yard - calm, grounded, warm smile.*

**MIRA** (cheerful, supportive)
There you are! You're late again.

**ZIGGY** (excited)
Mira! Did you see the reflection in that puddle? It looked like a portal to another dimension!

**MIRA** (laughing, patient)
It's a puddle, Ziggy.

**ZIGGY** (grinning)
A MAGICAL puddle.

*They walk together. Mira's presence is noticeably calming around Ziggy.*

**MIRA**
Come on. Before we're both detention buddies.

**ZIGGY** (V.O.)
Mira's been my best friend since third grade. While my brain's everywhere, Mira's like... an anchor. Keeps me from floating away.

---

### SCENE 3: ENTERING SCHOOL (1:45-2:30)

**INTERIOR - SCHOOL HALLWAY - MORNING**

*Ziggy and Mira enter. Hallway BUSTLES with students.*

*Teacher nods at Ziggy - not unkindly, but tired.*

**TEACHER** (off-camera, distant)
Ziggy. Try to stay focused today.

**ZIGGY** (under breath)
Always the same thing.

*Suddenly, the LIGHTS FLICKER. A strange SHADOW passes across the hallway. Students pause. Something FEELS OFF.*

**MIRA** (noticing Ziggy's face)
You feel that?

**ZIGGY** (nodding, serious)
Yeah. Something's... different.

**ZIGGY** (V.O.)
I didn't know it yet. But my life was about to change forever.

---

## ACT 2: FIRST ENCOUNTER (4 minutes)

### SCENE 4: THE CAFETERIA (2:30-4:00)

**INTERIOR - SCHOOL CAFETERIA - LUNCH**

*Ziggy sits with Mira. Around them, other students including JORDAN (thoughtful, anxious) and KAI (younger sibling, joyful).*

**KAI** (bouncing in seat, young and bright)
Ziggy! Ziggy! Did you see the new video game trailer?

**ZIGGY** (engaging with Kai)
The one with—

*Suddenly, the CAFETERIA DISTORTS. Colors SHIFT. Sound becomes CHAOTIC and OVERWHELMING.*

*A DARK SHADOW materializes - ECHO. Not fully formed, but PRESENT. Chaotic energy radiating.*

**ECHO** (voice like static, distorted, mocking)
Ziggy. Finally awake.

*Only ZIGGY can fully perceive ECHO.*

**MIRA** (noticing Ziggy's reaction)
What's wrong?

**ZIGGY** (standing, confused, scared)
Do you... do you see that?

**JORDAN** (quietly, from nearby, observant)
See what?

*ECHO circles - a manifestation of chaos, distraction, sensory overload.*

**ECHO** (V.O., harsh)
I'm the part of you that can't focus. The part that forgets. The part that gets overwhelmed. I'm everything the world says is WRONG with you.

**ZIGGY** (defiant, despite fear)
You're lying.

*ECHO LAUGHS - a sound like shattering glass.*

**ZIGGY** (V.O.)
In that moment, I knew. This wasn't just my brain being "different." This was something REAL. Something I had to face.

---

### SCENE 5: AFTER SCHOOL - OFFICE (4:00-4:45)

**INTERIOR - SCHOOL COUNSELOR'S OFFICE - AFTERNOON**

*Ziggy sits across from MS. CHEN - the music teacher and neurodivergent mentor. She's wise, calm, understanding.*

**MS. CHEN** (warm, knowing)
You felt it again, didn't you? The chaos.

**ZIGGY** (surprised)
How did you—

**MS. CHEN** (gentle smile)
Because I felt it too. When I was your age.

*She stands, walks to window, looks out thoughtfully.*

**MS. CHEN**
They call it a disorder. A deficit. Something broken. But Ziggy... what if they're looking at it wrong?

**ZIGGY**
What do you mean?

**MS. CHEN** (turning back)
ADHD isn't a flaw. It's a different operating system. More bandwidth. More connections firing. More POWER.

*She sits back down, eye-to-eye with Ziggy.*

**MS. CHEN**
The question isn't: "How do I fix myself?" The question is: "How do I master this?"

**ZIGGY** (leaning forward, intrigued)
Master it? Like... a superpower?

**MS. CHEN** (knowing smile)
Exactly like that.

---

### SCENE 6: THE REALIZATION (4:45-5:30)

**MONTAGE - VARIOUS LOCATIONS**

*Quick cuts of Ziggy's memories, but seen differently:*

- *Being "distracted" = seeing patterns others miss*
- *Being "restless" = having unlimited energy*
- *Being "impulsive" = making bold creative choices*
- *Being "scattered" = making unexpected connections*

**ZIGGY** (V.O., dawning realization)
What if every "flaw" they said I had... was actually a feature?

**ZIGGY** (V.O.)
What if I'm not broken? What if I'm just... DIFFERENT? And different... could be incredible?

*ECHO appears again, but this time Ziggy doesn't flinch. Looks directly at it.*

**ZIGGY** (to ECHO, determined)
You're not my enemy. You're just... the part of me that's scared.

*ECHO recoils, momentarily weakened by Ziggy's acceptance.*

---

## ACT 3: ACCEPTANCE & POWER (4 minutes)

### SCENE 7: THE AWAKENING (5:30-6:45)

**EXTERIOR - SCHOOL ROOFTOP - LATE AFTERNOON**

*Ziggy stands on the rooftop. The city spreads below. Energy RADIATES from Ziggy - not angry, but ALIVE.*

*Mira, Jordan, Kai, and Ms. Chen appear - gathered around Ziggy protectively.*

**ZIGGY** (V.O., powerful)
This is who I am. Not despite my ADHD. Because of it. Every connection my brain makes. Every idea that comes at lightning speed. Every moment of hyperfocus where I lose myself in something I love.

*Ziggy's outfit GLOWS slightly - the orange hoodie and blue trim seem to shimmer with purpose.*

**ZIGGY** (V.O.)
I'm not trying to be normal. I'm going to be EXTRAORDINARY.

*ECHO appears one final time - but transformed. No longer a threatening shadow. A reflection of Ziggy's own power.*

**ECHO** (voice clearer, acknowledging)
Then let's see what you can do.

**MIRA** (placing hand on Ziggy's shoulder)
We're with you.

**JORDAN** (quiet but firm)
All of us.

**KAI** (jumping with joy)
This is SO cool!

**MS. CHEN** (nodding with pride)
Welcome, Ziggy. Welcome to your power.

---

### SCENE 8: FIRST TEST (6:45-7:30)

**EXTERIOR - SCHOOL GROUNDS**

*A moment of crisis: something goes wrong. A situation that would normally overwhelm Ziggy.*

*But now, with acceptance, Ziggy sees it differently. The "scattered" thinking becomes STRATEGIC thinking. The "hyperfocus" becomes LASER FOCUS.*

*Ziggy solves the problem in an unexpected way - creative, bold, brilliant.*

**CROWD** (amazed)
How did you do that?

**ZIGGY** (smiling, confident)
By thinking differently.

**ZIGGY** (V.O.)
And that's when everything changed. That's when I realized... my ADHD wasn't something to hide or fix. It was something to CLAIM. Something to USE.

---

### SCENE 9: CLIFFHANGER (7:30-8:15)

**INTERIOR - DARKENED LOCATION**

*ECHO transforms into something LARGER. More REAL. More DANGEROUS.*

*Not just Ziggy's internal chaos anymore. Something EXTERNAL. Something that will FIGHT BACK.*

**ECHO** (voice now powerful, threatening)
You think you've won? This was just the beginning, Ziggy.

*Shadow FILLS the screen.*

**VOICE** (mysterious, ancient)
There are others like me. Older. Stronger. And they're COMING.

**ZIGGY** (V.O., determined despite fear)
Bring it on. I'm ready.

---

### SCENE 10: EPILOGUE (8:15-8:45)

**EXTERIOR - ZIGGY'S HOUSE - EVENING**

*Ziggy sits on porch with Mira. Sun setting. Calm moment.*

**MIRA**
What happens now?

**ZIGGY** (thoughtful)
Now? Now we figure out how to save the world. One ADHD superpower at a time.

*They both laugh. Camera pulls back to show the neighborhood at dusk - peaceful, but full of potential.*

**ZIGGY** (V.O.)
My name is Ziggy. I have ADHD. And I'm about to change everything.

*Title card: **SURGE**
Subtitle: Episode 1 - "Discover"*

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Total Runtime:** 13 minutes  
**Tone:** Inspirational, action-adventure, authentic representation  
**Target Audience:** Ages 10-18, families  
**Key Themes:** Self-acceptance, neurodiversity as strength, finding your power

**Character Voice Notes:**
- **Ziggy:** Energetic, optimistic, younger voice (11-13 years old), slight enthusiasm in every word
- **Mira:** Calm, supportive, warm, grounded, slightly older-sounding
- **Echo:** Distorted, chaotic, mocking initially, then powerful and challenging
- **Ms. Chen:** Wise, kind, patient, adult mentor voice with authority
- **Jordan:** Quiet, thoughtful, observant, introverted but intelligent
- **Kai:** Young, bright, playful, infectious joy (8-10 years old)
