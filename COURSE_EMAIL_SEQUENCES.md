## Setup Instructions

**Choose a platform:**
1. **Mailchimp** (free up to 500 subscribers; best for email sequences)
2. **ConvertKit** (best for creators; built-in automation)
3. **Substack** (easiest to start; limited segmentation)
4. **Brevo** (formerly Sendinblue; cheap and powerful)

**For this course, I recommend ConvertKit:**
- Create "Landing Page Signup" form → goes to Sequence 1
- Create "YouTube Description Link" → goes to Sequence 1
- Create "Enrollment Form" (on course landing page) → goes to Sequence 2
- Create "Purchase Complete" → goes to Sequence 3

**Timing:**
- Email 1: Immediate
- Email 2: +1 day
- Email 3: +3 days
- Email 4: +8 days (enrollment opens)
- Email 5: +12 days
- Email 6: +18 days
- Email 7: +27 days
- Email 8: +30 days (pricing increases)
- Email 9: On purchase (separate trigger)
- Email 10-13: Based on days since purchase

**Metrics to track:**
- Open rate (aim for >40%)
- Click rate (aim for >15%)
- Conversion rate (track who enrolls)
- Unsubscribe rate (should be <0.5%)

**A/B test:**
- Subject lines (different hooks)
- Email length (short vs long)
- CTA placement (top vs bottom)

---

## Content Calendar for Email Frequency

After the launch sequences, maintain 2-3 emails/month:
- Weekly during video releases (Mon/Wed/Fri)
- Bonus module announcements
- Student wins (testimonials)
- Related content (YouTube shorts, blog posts, Twitter threads)
- Community highlights

This keeps your list warm without spamming.
