## Story Breakdown

| Page | Story Text |
|------|-----------|
| 1-2 | The breeze was Sunny's friend. It came and went, asking nothing, giving comfort. In the afternoon, the breeze was warm. |
| 3-4 | It moved through the grass and through Sunny's fur, carrying the warmth of the day. It felt like someone was petting her gently. Sunny closed her eyes and felt the warm breeze move across her face. |
| 5-6 | It was like being hugged by the whole world. The breeze moved the leaves, made them dance, made them play. But it never hurt them. |
| 7-8 | It never tore them away. It just moved them gently, playfully, peacefully. As evening came, the breeze changed. It became cooler. |
| 9-10 | It became softer. It began to carry the smell of the night—darkness and sleep and dreams. Sunny could feel the day being carried away by this gentle breeze. |
| 11-12 | Everything that had been warm and bright and active was being softly, gently tucked away into sleep. The breeze continued. Warm one moment. Cool the next. |
| 13-14 | Moving leaves. Moving grass. Moving Sunny's fur. Moving everything gently toward rest. There was no force in the breeze. No violence. No rush. |
| 15-16 | Just gentle, constant, patient movement. Sunny began to sway with the breeze too. Back and forth. Not resisting. Just moving with it. |
