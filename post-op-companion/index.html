Your Surgeon

⚠️ Call Surgeon Immediately If:

  • Heart rate >120 at rest or <50
  • Blood pressure >160/100 or <90/60
  • Severe abdominal pain
  • Persistent vomiting
  • Blood in vomit or stool
  • Fever >101°F (38.3°C)
  • Signs of infection (redness, swelling at incision)
  • Difficulty breathing
