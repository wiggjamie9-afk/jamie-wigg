Q: How long to launch?

Q: What's the cost?
A: ~$100 setup + $35–50/month fixed + 30–40% platform fees

A: Stripe (web), Google Play (Android), Gumroad (lifetime licenses)

A: Direct bank deposit daily (Stripe) or monthly (Google Play, Gumroad)
