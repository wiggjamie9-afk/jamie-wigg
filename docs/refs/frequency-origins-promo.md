# ⚠️ Reminder — what to avoid

- `#adhdcure` `#anxietycure` `#tinnituscure` `#depressionhealing` `#mentalillnesscure` — TOS risk + audience credibility
- Don't say "this cures ADHD" or "treats anxiety". Say "supports", "helps", "for".
- Don't claim 432 Hz heals — say "produced greater anxiety reduction than 440 Hz in a 2019 RCT"
- Don't add `#medicaladvice` or `#doctor` — you're not making medical claims
