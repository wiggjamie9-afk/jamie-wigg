# Human Body Frequencies: Comprehensive Emission Spectrum

A catalog of frequencies the human body emits, generates, or resonates with — useful for designing frequency-based wellness features, healing tone generators, and bioacoustic applications.

---

## 1. NEUROLOGICAL FREQUENCIES (Brain & Nervous System)

| Frequency | Range | Activity State | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Delta** | 0.5–4 Hz | Deep sleep, meditation | Lowest brain frequency; associated with healing, immune function |
| **Theta** | 4–8 Hz | Light sleep, hypnagogic state | Memory consolidation, creativity, relaxation |
| **Alpha** | 8–12 Hz | Relaxed wakefulness, calm focus | "Default mode"; light meditation |
| **Beta** | 12–30 Hz | Normal waking consciousness | Active thinking, conversation, problem-solving |
| **Gamma** | 30–40 Hz (up to 100+ Hz) | Peak focus, insight, learning | High-level cognitive processing |

**Subtype: Brainwave Entrainment**
- Binaural beats can entrain brain to target frequencies
- Example: 40 Hz gamma used in cognitive research, some hearing/tinnitus studies

## 2. CARDIOVASCULAR FREQUENCIES

| Frequency | Measurement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Heart Rate (Resting)** | 60–100 BPM = 1–1.67 Hz | Varies by age, fitness, stress |
| **Heart Rate (Exercise)** | 140–180+ BPM = 2.3–3 Hz | Peak exertion |
| **Heart Rate Variability (HRV)** | 0.1–0.5 Hz variation between beats | Indicator of nervous system health; lower HRV = higher stress |
| **Atrial Fibrillation** | 300–600 irregular signals/min | Pathological; chaotic, uncoordinated frequency |

**Subtype: Vagal Tone / Parasympathetic Activation**
- Slow rhythmic tones (60–80 BPM) can activate vagus nerve
- Associated with heart rate coherence and stress reduction

## DESIGN IMPLICATIONS FOR RHYTHMIX / FREQUENCY HEALING

### Evidence-Based Approach
1. **Heart rate entrainment** (60–80 BPM) + visual/audio feedback for HRV improvement
2. **Brainwave entrainment** (binaural beats at 40 Hz, 7–8 Hz, etc.) with proper research backing
3. **Breathing pacing** (slow, rhythmic guidance at 0.1 Hz = 6 breaths/min)
4. **Vagal toning** via slow, resonant tones matched to parasympathetic activation

### Exploratory / Research Direction
1. **Personalized frequency generation** based on user's measured heart rate, breathing, dominant brainwave state
2. **Frequency layering** (e.g., 528 Hz carrier with 40 Hz modulation for "healing + cognition")
3. **Circadian alignment** (morning activating frequencies; evening calming frequencies)
4. **Biofield visualization** (showing user's heart/brain coherence in real-time during music)

### Disclaimers / Ethics
- **Never** claim to heal physical damage (damaged heart valves, tumors, etc.) without medical evidence
- Frame as "supportive wellness," "stress reduction," "relaxation assistance"
- Include scientific transparency: distinguish proven vs. speculative
- Recommend users consult healthcare providers for serious conditions
