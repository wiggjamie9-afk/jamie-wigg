## What to put in the Gumroad "Content" tab (file delivery)

You don't need to attach files since the product is the live web page. But Gumroad requires *something* to be deliverable. Options:

**Option A (cleanest):** Upload a 1-page PDF welcome card that just says "Your lifetime access is live at rhythmixapp.com.au/frequency.html — bookmark it and add to home screen." Customers receive that PDF + the thank-you letter on purchase.

**Option B:** Set the product as a "Buyer redirect" — Gumroad pushes them to `rhythmixapp.com.au/frequency.html?source=gumroad` after the receipt. Configure under **Product → Settings → Confirmation → Custom URL**.

**Recommended: B.** Cleaner UX. The thank-you email does all the heavy lifting.
