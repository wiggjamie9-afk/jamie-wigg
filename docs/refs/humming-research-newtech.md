# Humming-as-Healing: Technology & Commercial Service Landscape (2018 – May 2026)

A research dossier on tech that's been delivered to real users in the humming/vagal-tone/sound-healing adjacency, focused on what's in market, what's working, what's vapor, and where the gap lives. Sister doc to `humming-research-origins.md`.

---

## 1. Vagus Nerve Stimulation Devices — Humming's Closest Commercial Cousins

These don't deliver humming. They deliver the *physiological end-state* humming claims to deliver (vagal activation, parasympathetic shift, HRV lift). Real evidence is concentrated in the prescription-grade implantable and cervical-electrical end of the spectrum; the consumer wearables sit on much thinner ice.

### Implantable & prescription (strongest evidence)

- **SetPoint Medical "SetPoint System" (RESET-RA trial)** — Implantable cervical VNS, FDA-approved **late July 2025** for moderately-to-severely active rheumatoid arthritis in patients with inadequate response to biologic/targeted-synthetic DMARDs. Pivotal RESET-RA trial: 242 patients, randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled, statistically significant improvement on ACR20 at 12 weeks. ([SetPoint press release](https://setpointmedical.com/setpoint-medical-receives-fda-approval-for-novel-neuroimmune-modulation-therapy-for-rheumatoid-arthritis/), [Healio coverage](https://www.healio.com/news/rheumatology/20250731/fda-approves-vagus-nerve-stimulator-for-rheumatoid-arthritis), [PMC summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12889326/)). Significance: first FDA approval for *neuroimmune modulation*, validates the vagal-anti-inflammatory pathway clinically. (Confirms the `frequency.html` reference.)

- **gammaCore Sapphire (electroCore)** — Non-invasive cervical VNS, prescription. FDA-cleared April 2017 (cluster headache acute), Jan 2018 (migraine acute), Nov 2018 (cluster preventive). Roughly **$598/month** subscription via prescription with copay assistance. ([gammaCore](https://www.gammacore.com/), [US Pharmacist](https://www.uspharmacist.com/article/gammacore-noninvasive-vagus-nerve-stimulator)). UX: medical-device feel, requires conductive gel; user retention in real-world data is modest.

### Consumer wearables — neck / ear / sternum

- **Truvaga 350 / Truvaga Plus (electroCore)** — Same underlying electroCore signal platform as gammaCore but sold direct-to-consumer at **$499** (Plus), wellness-only positioning, no FDA review and no disease claims. Named "Best Relaxation Gadget" in Esquire's 2025 Sleep Awards. ([Truvaga Plus](https://www.truvaga.com/product/truvaga-plus/), [electroCore press](https://investor.electrocore.com/news-releases/news-release-details/electrocores-truvagatm-plus-named-best-relaxation-gadget)). Real annual cost runs ~$600 with electrode-gel refills. Worth noting: the **regulatory frame** is a textbook example of bifurcating one platform into prescription (gammaCore) and consumer-wellness (Truvaga) — relevant precedent for a humming app/device.

- **Nurosym (Parasym)** — Transcutaneous auricular VNS, UK. Cited 50+ Parasym-related publications across HRV, cytokines, post-viral recovery; one RCT showed reduced inflammatory cytokines at 3 months vs sham, and ~18% HRV improvement / "up to 61% vagal activity" claims. ([Nurosym evidence page](https://nurosym.com/pages/scientific-evidence), [ME Association](https://meassociation.org.uk/medical-matters/items/nurosym/)). Marketed at ~£399–£699 in UK. Some independent reviewers warn the cited studies use earlier Parasym/CE-marked predecessors, not necessarily the current Nurosym SKU.

- **Pulsetto** — Lithuania, bilateral cervical taVNS, **~$250–$350**. Sold heavily via Yahoo Finance-style sponsored articles. ([Pulsetto](https://pulsetto.tech/), [Innerbody review](https://www.innerbody.com/pulsetto-reviews), [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/health-tech/pulsetto-review/)). **No peer-reviewed clinical trials on the Pulsetto device itself** — evidence is borrowed from generic taVNS literature. Marketing claims vs evidence: large gap.

- **Sensate (BioSelf Technology)** — The most relevant comparable. Bone-conduction infrasonic resonance pebble worn on the sternum; explicitly markets itself as the body's own humming. Founded 2019; >$15M revenue across 50+ countries; UK price £200/~$280. ([Sensate site](https://www.getsensate.com/), [Sensate science page](https://www.getsensate.com/pages/science-of-sensate), [Alts.co teardown](https://alts.co/sensate-review-does-it-pass-the-investment-stress-test/), [Cybernews review](https://cybernews.com/health-tech/sensate-review/)). **Evidence reality**: a company-sponsored 100-volunteer, 6-week, no-control study reported 86% showing HRV gains — described in [Medgadget](https://www.medgadget.com/2022/02/meet-sensate-a-device-that-calms-your-fight-or-flight-response-interview-exclusive.html) and a [ResearchGate Somacoustics paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/362805482_SensateR_Somacoustics_A_New_Wave_for_Stress_Management_Volume_I). No independent peer-reviewed RCT. The marketing is sophisticated; the science underneath is roughly equivalent to where the humming literature is today — which is itself the relevant lesson for the user.

- **Apollo Neuro** — Wrist/ankle vibration wearable, University of Pittsburgh roots. 7 completed clinical studies (1,700+ subjects), 14 underway, including a small University of Pittsburgh double-blind crossover RCT showing statistically significant HRV gains and ~25% cognitive-under-stress lift. ([Apollo research page](https://apolloneuro.com/pages/apollo-neuro-research), [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/groundbreaking-clinical-research-shows-the-apollo-wearable-improves-heart-rate-variability-or-hrv-301728587.html), [Rupa Health review](https://www.rupahealth.com/post/apollo-neuro-wearable-review-expert-insights-and-user-feedback)). Independent reviews call the body of evidence "mixed", with some small effects but most studies open-label, single-arm, or unpublished. Device is ~$349.

- **Neuvana Xen** — Auricular taVNS earbuds, $449 ([Neuvana](https://neuvanalife.com/products/xen)). Backed by university research (ASU, Maryland, Jefferson); one 2025 placebo-controlled study in elite shooters showed reductions in cognitive/somatic anxiety. UX critique: hundreds of waveform combinations with no preset programs — overwhelming for novices.

### Devices that died or pivoted

- **Cove (Feelmore Labs)** — Behind-ear mastoid-vibration headband. **Discontinued September 2024**; app was killed December 2022. ([Cove discontinued page](https://feelcove.com/discontinued), [Engadget launch piece](https://www.engadget.com/feelmore-cove-mental-health-gadget-130017256.html)). Marketed "41% less stress in 90% of users" — the only public data. Hardware was beautiful, evidence was thin, subscription model failed.

- **Hapbee** — Neuromagnetic "moods on demand" wearable. Did *not* shut down — instead executed a "strategic reset" through 2024, two private placements, and 198% Q4 2024 device-sales growth, distributed in Target. ([Hapbee investors](https://investors.hapbee.com/press-releases)). But the evidence base remains non-existent; this is a survival story, not a vindication.

- **SinuSonic (Healthy Humming, LLC)** — Acoustic-vibration + oscillating-expiratory-pressure nasal device, invented 2017, launched 2019. Has clinical-trial data ([Soler et al., 2020, *Int Forum Allergy Rhinol*](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/alr.22537)). The brand was **acquired/licensed by NeilMed** — site now reads "NeilMed SinuSonic" ([sinusonic.com](https://sinusonic.com/)). Meanwhile, a separate Healthy Humming psychological-wellbeing trial (NCT06268730) was **terminated in April 2025 because Healthy Humming LLC ceased operating** — so the original company unwound even as the product survived under new ownership. Useful cautionary tale: a humming-adjacent device can have a clinical paper and still go under.

## 2. Sound + Vibration Healing Wearables

Thin layer here. The category overlaps with VNS wearables (Sensate, Apollo) above.

- **inHarmony Vibroacoustic** — Meditation cushion, Sound Lounge 3 (~$4,321), Practitioner 2 mat, massage table (~$3,456). ([inHarmony collection](https://iaminharmony.com/collections/vibroacoustic-cushions-and-lounges)). 30-day guarantee, financing. No published clinical evidence I found, but it's prosumer studio kit, not a wearable in the everyday sense.

- **Hyperice Normatec Elite** — Listed in Time's 200 Best Inventions of 2024 ([Time](https://time.com/collections/best-inventions-2024/7094733/hyperice-normatec-elite/)). Compression, not vibration-as-healing. Adjacent at best.

- **Bose Sleepbuds II** — Discontinued, but successor **Ozlo Sleepbuds** (~$299) is being repurposed by tinnitus communities for nocturnal masking ([Ozlo on Bose Sleepbuds and tinnitus](https://ozlosleep.com/blogs/news/bose-sleepbuds-tinnitus)). Sound-masking, not active humming.

- **BioMat / amethyst infrared mats** — Marketed for "thermal therapy", makes no specific humming claims. Skipping — out of scope.

## 3. Sound-Healing & Breathwork Apps With Humming Features

The pattern is universal: **humming is a feature, never the centerpiece.** It lives 3–4 taps deep inside a breathwork library.

- **Othership** — 500+ guided sessions; bhramari/humming bee breath is included as one technique among many, used heavily in their sleep sequences ([Othership breathwork-for-anxiety blog](https://www.othership.us/resources/breathwork-for-anxiety), [Instagram reel "humming your way to better..."](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DFF9D33My_o/)). 7-day trial then subscription (~$13/mo). Hum is buried.

- **Breathwrk** — Uses "elongated exhale or humming" in sleep practices to activate parasympathetic ([Breathwrk](https://www.breathwrk.com/)). Humming is one micro-element of a few sleep flows.

- **Insight Timer** — Catalog includes many dozens of bhramari/humming-bee-breath sessions from independent teachers (Peta Thompson, Leah Sugerman, Alice Bagley-Harrison, Kevin Demot, Miloni Visaria, etc.) ([example](https://insighttimer.com/leahsugerman/video-guided-meditations/7-minute-relaxing-humming-bee-breath-bhramari-pranayama)). Free tier with paid teacher upsells. Search "humming" or "bhramari" surfaces 30+ tracks. No structured protocol — just a long list.

- **Calm / Headspace / Balance** — Searched their catalogs: humming/bhramari sessions exist but are not promoted, not in onboarding flows, and not part of any condition-specific track. Generic meditation apps treat it as a yoga reference.

- **Endel** — Generative AI ambient soundscapes (sleep/focus/relax) responding to time, weather, HR. Backed by a peer-reviewed Focus study in 2022. ([Endel science](https://endel.io/science), [Musicology Now critique](https://musicologynow.org/welcome-to-the-sound-wellness-revolution-endels-ai-generated-soundscapes-and-the-commodification-of-passive-listening/)). Passive listening, no humming.

- **Brain.fm** — Functional music for focus, with some clinical work behind it. Passive, no humming.

- **Oto** — Tinnitus-specific app combining CBT + ACT + mindfulness + curated sounds in a structured 27-day protocol. Real digital-therapeutic posture with a [feasibility study in PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12575424/) and an [active RCT at clinicaltrials.gov NCT06623123](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06623123). **Uses sound therapy and masking — does NOT prescribe active humming as a protocol step.** This is a notable gap given the nitric-oxide and vagal-tone case for humming as a tinnitus intervention.

- **Neurotone AI Tinnitus Pro** — Launched November 2025. Combines habituation + adaptation (TRT-derived) + notched-noise therapy + a "Personalized Therapeutic Tone" matched to the patient's own tinnitus pitch ([Hearing Review](https://hearingreview.com/hearing-loss/tinnitus/tinnitus-therapy/oto), [PRWeb](https://www.prweb.com/releases/neurotone-ai-launches-tinnitus-pro-the-next-revolution-in-tinnitus-treatment-302616530.html)). The pitch-matching idea is the closest the tinnitus-app space gets to "use the patient's own sound" — but again, no active humming.

- **ReSound Tinnitus Relief** — Companion app for ReSound hearing aids. Sound therapy, no humming protocol.

- **Standalone humming-centric apps** — Two exist that I could locate:
  - **Huma Meditation** (Nanoclicks LLC) — 4.22★ iOS over 310 ratings; ~9k total Android installs ([App Store CA](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/huma-meditation/id1628377565), [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/app/huma-meditation/tv.uscreen.huma)). Small, mixed reception. Hum-centric but the guide's voice is reportedly intrusive.
  - **Humming Bee** by Paul Salmon — Pranayama-focused, launched 2024/2025 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/humming-bee/id6753283504)). Solo-developer scale, not a category leader.

**Direct answer to the question "is there an app where humming is the centerpiece?":** Effectively no. Huma and Humming Bee are the only attempts and neither has scale, condition-specific protocols, AI feedback, or distinctive UX. The category is genuinely empty.

## 4. Recent Peer-Reviewed Trials on Humming (2023–2026)

There has been a small but real uptick. Quality is mixed — mostly small, single-center, often unblinded.

- **Trivedi et al., 2023** — "Humming (Simple Bhramari Pranayama) as a Stress Buster: A Holter-Based Study to Analyze HRV Parameters During Bhramari, Physical Activity, Emotional Stress, and Sleep." 23 participants; Holter HRV; humming reduced stress index more than other activities. ([PMC10182780](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10182780/)). Often cited in 2024–2025 popular coverage.

- **Bhramari + OM chanting for paediatric asthma RCT, 2023/2024** — 12-week intervention vs standard pharmacotherapy; better symptom control, QoL, reduced airway inflammation. ([Journal of Asthma, Vol 61 No 3](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/02770903.2023.2267113)).

- **OM Chanting at 528 Hz for hypertension RCT, 2025** — HRV, psychological wellbeing, sleep quality. ([PMC12700779](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12700779/)).

- **Slow-paced breathing vs humming breathing on HRV/affect pilot, 2025** — 16 healthy adults; both modalities significantly improved SDNN, total power, LF; humming and slow breathing produced similar relaxation lifts. ([ScienceDirect S0031938425001738](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0031938425001738)).

- **Negative / null result, 2024** — "Induction of nitric oxide via humming does not improve short-term cognitive performance or influence emotional processing." ([PMC10994277](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10994277/)). Important counterweight: the NO-from-humming mechanism doesn't automatically translate to acute cognitive/emotional benefit.

- **Pranayama meta-analysis for mental disorders, 2025** — Frontiers in Psychiatry systematic review/meta-analysis of RCTs ([Frontiers 1616996](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2025.1616996/full)).

- **Foundational mechanism papers (still the canon)** — Weitzberg & Lundberg, Karolinska, showing humming increases nasal NO 15–20× vs quiet exhalation ([ERS 2002](https://publications.ersnet.org/content/erj/22/2/323), [Eby ScienceDirect case report on 1hr/day hum to terminate chronic rhinosinusitis](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0306987705006328)).

- **Active research groups** — In India: AIIMS / S-VYASA / Patanjali Research Foundation around bhramari; in the West: Karolinska (Lundberg/Weitzberg) on nasal NO; UCSF Greater Good Science Center popularised humming for stress in 2024 ([Berkeley Greater Good podcast](https://greatergood.berkeley.edu/podcasts/item/happiness_break_a_humming_technique_to_calm_your_nerves_bhramari_pranayama_humming_bee_breath)). No US-based RCT machine has formed around it yet.

## 5. AI / ML-Enabled Humming Tech — The Actual "New Tech" Angle

Mostly empty in the *healing* application. The plumbing is mature; nobody has wired it to humming wellness.

- **Pitch-detection libraries** — Production-ready for browser/phone:
  - **CREPE** (MARL/NYU, ICASSP 2018) — deep CNN, most accurate, ~10MB model, run via TorchCrepe or TF.js ([CREPE repo](https://github.com/marl/crepe)).
  - **YIN** — autocorrelation, fast, ideal for monophonic vocal/humming input on-device ([Vocal Pitch app uses it](https://www.vocalpitch.app/)).
  - **Web Audio API + AudioWorklet** stack is a well-trodden path: `getUserMedia → AudioContext → AnalyserNode/AudioWorklet → FFT/YIN → F₀ → note` ([pitchdetector.com explainer](https://pitchdetector.com/real-time-browser-pitch-detection-explained/), [MusicalBoard blog](https://www.musicalboard.com/blog/2026-05-05-pitch-detection/)).
  - A humming-healing PWA can run entirely client-side on iPhone Safari without uploading audio.

- **Apple Vision Pro Mindfulness app** — Visuals/sounds follow user breathing rhythm; respiration-tracking feature reported as in-development ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/mindfulness-devae0eb1c57/visionos), [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2024/06/03/apple-vision-pro-mindfulness-respiration/)). No humming integration.

- **Meta Quest meditation apps** — Novobeing, Hoame, TRIPP, Mindway. All combine breathwork, soundscapes, immersive environments. None feature active humming as a prescribed protocol.

- **WHOOP** — Stress Monitor with breathwork developed with Andrew Huberman, including physiological-sigh and cyclic-hyperventilation protocols — but no humming. ([Fitt Insider](https://insider.fitt.co/whoop-launches-stress-tracking-breathwork-protocols/)).

- **Oxa / Moonbird / Lief / Mindbreath** — Hardware-coached breathing + HRV biofeedback. None ingest the user's hum. ([Oxa](https://www.oxalife.com/), [Moonbird](https://www.moonbird.life/), [Lief](https://lief.ai/), [Mindbreath](https://www.mindbreathapp.com/hrv-app)).

- **Endel / Brain.fm** — Generative ambient music. Output-only, no mic input.

**Direct answer to "is anyone doing AI-powered humming detection + feedback in a healing context?":** No. Soundverse and music-tech tools accept hum-input for music generation, but no healing/wellness product has shipped a mic-driven, on-device, hum-pitch-feedback loop tied to HRV, breath, or condition tracking. **This is the most defensible technical wedge in the entire space.**

## 6. Regulatory Landscape — FDA, TGA, Marketing-Claim Boundaries

- **FDA General Wellness 2026 guidance** ([Faegre Drinker analysis](https://www.faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2026/1/key-updates-in-fdas-2026-general-wellness-and-clinical-decision-support-software-guidance), [Kendall PC analysis](https://kendallpc.com/fdas-2026-guidance-on-general-wellness-devices-policy-for-low-risk-devices-key-compliance-and-regulatory-insights-for-digital-health-companies/)) — Broadens the exemption: non-invasive wearables/apps that estimate wellness metrics, make no disease claims, and pose minimal risk fall outside FDA device regulation. **An app that teaches humming for "stress relief, relaxation, better sleep, focus" is clearly within wellness.** Saying it "treats tinnitus", "treats sinusitis", "reduces depression", "treats anxiety disorders" pulls it across the line into Software-as-a-Medical-Device territory.

- **FDA warning letter precedent, July 2025** — Reminder that wellness claims only go so far; technical features, indications-for-use language, or even YouTube testimonials can establish a regulated "intended use" ([Mintz](https://www.mintz.com/insights-center/viewpoints/2791/2025-07-21-fda-warning-letter-reminds-industry-wellness-claims-only)). User-generated content moderation matters.

- **Devices actually FDA-cleared in this space:** gammaCore Sapphire (migraine/cluster headache, prescription), SetPoint System (RA, implantable, prescription), Inmedix CloudHRV (2025). Almost everything else (Truvaga, Pulsetto, Nurosym US, Sensate, Apollo Neuro, Cove, Hapbee, Neuvana Xen) is **wellness-only with no FDA clearance**.

- **TGA (Australia)** — Wellness apps and meditation/mindfulness apps are explicitly named as **excluded software categories** when they support general wellbeing rather than diagnose/treat/manage a specific condition ([TGA digital therapeutics overview](https://www.tga.gov.au/products/medical-devices/software-and-artificial-intelligence-ai/overview/types-software-based-medical-devices/digital-therapeutics), [TGA software-device classification](https://www.tga.gov.au/resources/guidance/understanding-if-changes-software-based-medical-device-regulation-affect-you)). For an Australian-built humming app, this is the cleanest possible launch path: stay general-wellness, market parasympathetic/calm/sleep/focus language, avoid named conditions, and globally the same posture clears FDA, MHRA, CE wellness lanes.

## 7. The Gap — What's Underbuilt

Rolling everything up: the humming-as-healing market is in roughly the same evidentiary place vagal-tone wearables were in 2018–2019. There is meaningful (if early) science, zero category leader, no AI-driven product, and the existing players treat humming as a footnote.

**Specific underbuilt opportunities, ranked by defensibility:**

1. **An AI-feedback humming PWA where humming IS the product.** Mic-driven on-device pitch detection (YIN in the Web Audio API + AudioWorklet — no upload needed, privacy-clean, iPhone-Safari-native, no app-store gate). Real-time visual feedback on F₀, sustain duration, exhale length, vibration "depth" (formant strength). Nothing in the market does this. This is the clearest moat.

2. **Condition-specific structured protocols.** Oto owns 27-day tinnitus structure with sound therapy; nobody owns the 28-day humming-for-tinnitus protocol — despite the nasal-NO / vagal-tone mechanistic case being legitimate enough that the user could legally market it under general-wellness "relaxation for ringing ears" language in AU/UK, and through provider-distribution channels in the US. Same play for chronic rhinosinusitis (Eby's case report literally proposes 1-hour-daily humming), anxiety, sleep.

3. **Hum + HRV pairing.** Pair the mic-based hum analysis with optical HRV from Apple Watch / Oura / WHOOP via HealthKit. Show the user their nervous-system shift *during and after* a hum session. This is what Apollo, Sensate, and Pulsetto all *claim* but none actually close the loop on with the user's own vocalization. Sensate is the natural acquisition target / hardware partner.

4. **Telehealth-anchored 8-week humming program.** Australian SaaS-style structured program, weekly check-ins, optional clinician review, sold B2B to physiotherapy / mental-health / tinnitus clinics. The B2C app is the wedge; the B2B clinic licensing is the revenue. This is the inHarmony commercial-studio model done in software.

5. **Hardware-software pairing — BYO Sensate, Apollo, or Truvaga.** Don't fight them. The user's app reads HRV, hears the hum, and dynamically tells the user when to engage the Sensate / Apollo on top of the hum. Becomes the *conductor* layer above the existing hardware market.

6. **Generative personalized hum backdrop.** Brain.fm / Endel-style adaptive ambient music tuned in real-time to *the user's own hum pitch*, so the soundscape harmonically supports their natural F₀. This is the "Endel meets karaoke" wedge — nobody has built it.

## 8. Top 5 "New Tech" Takeaways for the Build

1. **Make humming detection the centerpiece UI, not the content.** Every existing app treats humming as audio content played *at* the user. Flip it: the user hums *into* the app, the app reads pitch/duration/breath rhythm and reflects it back as a living visual. YIN in an AudioWorklet on iPhone Safari ships this today.

2. **Pair the hum with HRV via HealthKit.** Close the biofeedback loop the entire vagal-wearable category claims but never quite delivers in software. Apple Watch HRV + hum-session pre/post deltas. This is what makes the wellness claim feel earned.

3. **Stay wellness-positioned and stay Australian-launched.** TGA's explicit carve-out for wellness/meditation/mindfulness apps is the cleanest regulatory cradle on earth. Use it. Market "calm, focus, sleep, parasympathetic shift". Save condition claims for a future clinically-validated SKU.

4. **Structured protocols, not infinite libraries.** Oto's 27-day tinnitus arc, Apollo's daily vibe modes, the SetPoint once-daily framing — all win on *prescribed structure*. Insight Timer's bottomless catalog is the *anti-pattern*. Ship one signature 8-week humming program with weekly objectives.

5. **Treat the Cove/SinuSonic-LLC graveyard as the canonical warning.** Beautiful hardware and a published study still let a company go under. Software-first, low burn, evidence accumulated via lightweight user studies you can publish yourselves, hardware partnerships only after the app has retention. The entire VNS-consumer category's failure mode is shipping hardware before proving habit.

---

**Bottom line:** Humming-as-healing has no software incumbent, an unbuilt AI-feedback wedge, a permissive regulatory lane in Australia, and a mechanistic story (nasal nitric oxide, vagal tone, HRV) that's just credible enough to be sticky without being so over-claimed it triggers regulators. The closest commercial analogs (Sensate, Apollo, Truvaga, Pulsetto) collectively prove the appetite exists at $250–$500 ticket sizes — and crucially, none of them have done what's actually obvious: let the user produce the healing vibration *themselves*, with their own voice, and watch it work.
