# Business Plan — Reel Pipeline (working title)

_A solo, AI-leveraged short-form video business. Built on the one asset that's hard to copy:
a working AI video production pipeline. Australian-first. Honest version — real numbers, real
risks, no hype._

---

## 1. The business (one line)
**Done-for-you short-form property video for real estate agents** — recurring monthly packages,
produced through an AI/automation pipeline so one person can deliver at agency scale. Later:
productised into a self-serve tool (the durable, sellable asset).

## 2. Why this business (honest fit)
- **Your unfair asset:** you actually built a video pipeline (HyperFrames/Remotion + fal/Replicate
  + Kokoro voice). Most "AI video" people fumble production. You don't.
- **The customer needs video constantly:** every new listing = new video. Recurring by nature.
- **Most agents' video is bad or expensive.** Clear, obvious gap.
- **They pay** — commissions are large; marketing spend is normal.
- **Reachable solo:** local/regional. You can dominate one city before anyone notices. Far easier
  cold-start than global e-commerce UGC (which wants proven creators with portfolios).
- **AU context fits** (your `.com.au` domain; AU real estate is video-heavy: listings, auctions).

> **The one assumption you can override:** the *niche*. If you have real access/network in another
> market — gyms, med-spas, hospitality, e-commerce, trades — swap the customer; the machine and the
> plan are identical. Your existing access beats my guess. But if you have no edge anywhere, real
> estate is the highest-odds default. Pick one and commit.

## 3. The customer (specific)
Independent / small-team real estate agents and small agencies. They:
- List 2–10 properties/month.
- Need: listing reels, "just listed/just sold" social posts, agent personal-brand videos.
- Currently: pay $300–800 per video to a freelancer, or post bad phone clips, or nothing.
- Decision-maker = the agent. Short sales cycle. ROI is obvious ("more eyes on the listing").

## 4. The offer + pricing (AUD, real)
| Tier | What they get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Taster (foot in door) | 1 listing reel, 24–48h | $99–149 one-off |
| **Core retainer** ⭐ | 4 listing reels + 4 social cuts / month | **$990–1,490/mo** |
| Pro | 8–10 videos/mo + agent brand video | $1,990–2,490/mo |

Recurring is the whole game. **Target: 6–8 core retainers = ~$7–12k/mo.** That's a real solo
income, from one city.

## 5. How it's delivered (your actual stack)
Real estate video is mostly **listing photos + motion + captions + voice/music** — NOT full AI
footage. That's exactly what your tools do best:
1. Agent sends listing photos + details (simple form / email).
2. **HyperFrames / Remotion** → animated reel (Ken Burns, captions, price/feature overlays, branding).
3. **fal/Replicate (FLUX, Kling)** → optional AI b-roll / enhanced stills where useful.
4. **Kokoro** (free) or **MiniMax/Chatterbox** (premium) → voiceover.
5. **MusicGen / licensed track** → audio bed.
6. **ffmpeg** → export 9:16 + 1:1. Delivered same/next day.
This is a near-one-command pipeline once built. **Build it once, run it per listing.**

## 6. Unit economics (honest)
- **Cost per video:** ~$1–8 in API (fal/Replicate/voice) + your time (~15–30 min once the pipeline
  exists). Kokoro voice = free.
- **Margin:** 85%+. A $1,200/mo retainer costs you maybe $30–80 in API. The constraint is your
  *time and the pipeline's automation*, not cost.
- **Break-even:** essentially immediate — there's no inventory, no big capital.

## 7. Go-to-market (the hard part — be honest with yourself here)
This is where businesses live or die, not in the tech. The proven solo cold-start:
1. **Make 5–10 SPEC reels** — real, current listings from real local agents, unasked.
2. **Send them free:** "Made this for your 12 Smith St listing — no charge. Want the rest of your
   listings done like this?" Show, don't pitch.
3. **Convert** ~10–20% → your first 1–3 paying clients. **No ad spend needed.**
4. **1-page portfolio site** (you have site-build skills) with the spec reels.
5. **Scale by referral + niche domination:** agents talk; be "the video person" for one suburb/city.

**Outreach volume target:** 50–100 agents contacted in the first 30 days. That's the job. The
videos are easy; the outreach is the business.

## 8. 90-day plan (realistic)
- **Weeks 1–2:** Build the pipeline (`ugc/` one-command tool in Codespace). Produce 5 spec reels.
  Stand up a 1-page portfolio. *(I can build all of this with you.)*
- **Weeks 3–6:** Outreach to 50–100 agents with spec work. Land 1–3 paying clients. Deliver fast.
- **Weeks 7–12:** Collect testimonials + before/after case studies. Raise prices. Systematise
  intake (form → pipeline). Aim for **5–6 retainers (~$6–9k/mo)**.

## 9. The path to "business for life" (the durable asset)
1. **Now:** service (cashflow + you learn exactly what agents want).
2. **3–6 mo:** templatise everything; hire/automate the manual bits.
3. **6–12 mo:** **productise** → a self-serve tool: agent uploads photos + details → gets a reel.
   That's the SaaS — recurring, scalable, and *sellable*. The service is the on-ramp; the product
   is the asset you own for life.

## 10. Money (honest trajectory — NOT a guarantee)
- **Startup cost:** ~$0–150 (API credits + domain). No capital barrier.
- **Month 1:** ~$0 (building + outreach). Expect this. Don't quit here.
- **Month 2–3:** first **$1–3k**.
- **Month 6:** **$5–10k/mo** *if* outreach stays consistent.
- There is **no guarantee.** Revenue is a direct function of how many agents you contact. Stop
  outreaching, revenue stops.

## 11. Risks & honest failure modes
1. **#1 risk, by far: you don't do the outreach.** This is the same avoidance pattern (collecting
   tools instead of facing "no"). If this business fails, it fails *here* — not in the tech.
2. **Quality bar:** agents have standards. First reels must be genuinely good. (Doable with your
   stack.)
3. **Competition / in-house:** some agencies have their own. Target independents + small teams.
4. **Tool/API cost or policy changes:** keep 2 providers (fal + Replicate) so you're never stuck.
5. **Burnout doing it solo:** why the productise step (9) matters — don't stay a freelancer forever.

- **Do the outreach.** Every day. This is the business.
- **Talk to clients, deliver, follow up.**
