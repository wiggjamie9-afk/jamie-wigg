## Repurposing Strategy

1. **Create 1 hero video** (rhythmix-studio-promo-60s)
2. **Extract 4 short clips** (15s, 30s, 45s, 60s) from hero
3. **Adapt for each platform:**
   - TikTok: 15s vertical, trending audio
   - Instagram Reels: 15–60s vertical, captions on video
   - YouTube Shorts: 15–60s vertical, channel branding
   - LinkedIn: 15–30s landscape, text-heavy caption
   - Twitter/X: 30s, quote-tweet format
4. **A/B test hooks** (test 2–3 variants per platform per week)
5. **Measure & iterate** (CTR, save rate, share rate per platform)

## Tools & Workflow

1. **Video creation:** HyperFrames (rhythmix-studio-promo-60s)
2. **Audio/TTS:** Kokoro TTS or ElevenLabs
3. **Short-form clips:** FFmpeg or CapCut
4. **Scheduling:** Buffer / Later / Meta Business Suite
5. **Analytics:** Vercel Analytics (landing page), platform-native (TikTok/IG/YT)
6. **Copy brainstorm:** Use `/repurpose` skill to auto-generate variants
