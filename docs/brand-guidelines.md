## 3. Voice Framework

### Core voice rules

1. **Lead with the verb.** "Generate. Master. Distribute. Monetise." Not "RHYTHMIX is a platform that helps you generate…"
2. **Concrete over abstract.** "$2.4K average sync placement" beats "great earning potential."
3. **Punch with numbers.** 14 features. 4,800+ creators. 6-stem separation. Specifics build trust.
4. **Drop the marketing throat-clearing.** No "we believe", no "in today's world", no "innovative."
5. **Short sentences win.** Average ≤ 14 words. One idea per sentence. Period.

### Tone shifts by surface

| Surface | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hero / landing | Bold, declarative | "Your music career starts right now." |
| Feature copy | Functional + a kicker | "6-stem AI separation. Drag, drop, isolated stems in 12 seconds." |
| Pricing | Plain-English math | "$19/month. Or $149 once. Forever." |
| Email | Warm, peer-to-peer | "Hey — saw you joined yesterday. Want a 60-sec onboarding video?" |
| Social / ads | Stop-scroll, single claim | "World's first AI that masters AND distributes your track." |
| Error / empty states | Honest, helpful | "No tracks yet. Drop a WAV or MP3 to start." |
| Legal / fine print | Plain, non-evasive | "Annual plan auto-renews. Cancel anytime. Royalties are yours." |

### Words we use

`generate · master · distribute · monetise · release · drop · ship · own · royalty · stem · master chain · sync · DAW`

### Words we never use

`leverage · synergy · solution · empower · seamless · unlock · revolutionary · cutting-edge · game-changer · disrupt · ecosystem · journey`

## 4. Messaging Framework

### Three core messages (rotate these everywhere)

1. **Completeness** — "Every tool a musician will ever need. In one place."
2. **Independence** — "No labels. No gatekeepers. Your music. Your money."
3. **Speed** — "Idea to released track in under 30 minutes."

### Anti-positioning (what we are NOT)

- Not Suno. Suno is text-to-song novelty. We're a full music OS.
- Not DistroKid. DistroKid is just distribution. We're generation → distribution → monetisation.
- Not LANDR. LANDR is just mastering. We do mastering as one of 14 modules.
- Not a DAW. We feed your DAW (MIDI/stem/master export) and live alongside it.

### Proof points (cite often)

- 14 AI features in one platform (most of category)
- 4,800+ creators using it today
- 6-stem AI separation
- $2.4K average sync placement payout
- Smart-contract royalty auto-pay
- VR concert hosting + fan investment built in

## 5. Visual Identity

### Color system

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--color-bg` | `#0A0A14` | App background |
| `--color-surface` | `#13131F` | Cards, panels |
| `--color-border` | `#2A2A48` | Subtle dividers |
| `--color-text` | `#F5F5FA` | Primary text |
| `--color-soft` | `#A8A8C0` | Secondary text |
| `--color-muted` | `#6B6B85` | Tertiary text, captions |
| `--color-red` | `#FF1F5A` | Primary brand, CTAs |
| `--color-purple` | `#7C3AED` | Brand secondary, gradient pair |
| `--color-gold` | `#F5C000` | Lifetime / premium emphasis |
| `--color-green` | `#22C55E` | Success, positive states |

**Signature gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF1F5A, #7C3AED)` — used on primary CTAs, hero highlights, "popular" pricing badge, gradient-text spans.

### Typography

| Role | Font | Token | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headline / display | Space Grotesk | `--typography-font-heading` | 700–800 weight, tight letter-spacing (-2px on hero) |
| Body / UI | Inter | `--typography-font-body` | 400–600 weight, 1.5–1.75 line-height |
| Mono / labels / caps | JetBrains Mono | `--typography-font-mono` | 9–11px, +2px letter-spacing, all-caps for tags/badges |

Type scale (px): `9 · 10 · 11 · 12 · 13 · 14 · 15 · 17 · 20 · 26 · 32 · 44 · 52 · 76`

### Aesthetic rules

- **Dark mode is the default.** No light mode for v1.
- **Surfaces glow.** Cards have a 1px border in `--color-border` and a soft pink/purple bloom on hover.
- **Numbers are heroes.** Big, bold, gradient. They do the selling.
- **No emoji-as-icons in product UI.** Use SVG icons. Emoji are allowed in marketing copy where the energy fits.
- **One CTA per section.** Always gradient. Always "Start free" or "Get lifetime."

## 6. Logo

### Wordmark

`RHYTHMIX` — set in Space Grotesk 800, all caps, +1px letter-spacing.
The "X" can be replaced with the gradient mark when scale allows.

### Mark

Gradient circle (45 °angle, `#FF1F5A → #7C3AED`) with the letter `R` reversed out in white. Used for app icons, favicon, social avatar, watermark.

### Don'ts

- Never on light backgrounds without a dark plate behind it.
- Never re-color the gradient.
- Never stretch, italicise, or outline the wordmark.
- Never combine with another logo at equal weight.

## 7. Asset Naming Convention

```
{type}/{campaign-or-section}/{variant}-{size}.{ext}

assets/banners/launch/minimalist-1500x500.png
assets/social/launch-week/ig-story-01-1080x1920.png
assets/slides/rhythmix-pitch-251223.html
```

- **kebab-case only.** No spaces, no underscores, no caps.
- **Date prefix** `{YYMMDD}-` for time-sensitive campaigns.
- **Width × height in filename** for any rasterised export.

## 8. Approval Checklist

Before publishing any asset:

- [ ] Headline punches a verb in the first 3 words
- [ ] At least one number cited in the body
- [ ] Single CTA, gradient, action verb
- [ ] No banned words (see §3)
- [ ] Dark surface with proper contrast (≥ 4.5:1 for body, ≥ 3:1 for large)
- [ ] Logo on dark plate, gradient untouched
- [ ] File named per §7 convention
- [ ] Exported at exact platform dimensions
