# PhotoGIMP Promo — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Workshop-dark creative-tool aesthetic. Near-black slate canvas (warm undertone, not violet), punctuated by saturated burnt orange — a nod to GIMP's heritage palette pushed into a modern register. Electric cyan for accents and step numbers; off-white display text. Display sans is geometric and confident; monospace carries technical microcopy (file paths, shortcuts). Motion is sharp and decisive — fast `expo.out` entrances, no bounce, no elastic. The look should feel like an indie developer's workshop, not Adobe corporate marketing.

## Colors

- `#0d1014` — Canvas (near-black, neutral slate)
- `#ff6b1a` — Primary burnt orange (CTA, emphasis, wordmark)
- `#58e8ff` — Electric cyan (step numbers, accent counts)
- `#ffd166` — Hot gold (highlights, key terms)
- `#7c5cff` — Soft violet (secondary accent)
- `#f5f5f7` — Display text
- `#a8aab4` — Muted body
- `#1a1f27` — Card surface

## Typography

- Display: `"Space Grotesk", system-ui` — headlines, wordmark, big numbers
- Mono: `"JetBrains Mono", monospace` — taglines, labels, file paths, shortcuts

## Motion

- Fast in (0.4–0.7s), `expo.out` / `power3.out` for entrances
- Stagger 120–200ms across step cards
- Crossfade between scenes (0.5s)
- No bounce, no elastic — confident workshop register

## What NOT to Do

- No `#3b82f6`, `#333`, default Roboto/Arial — palette is fixed.
- No Photoshop sapphire blue — that's not us.
- No full-frame linear gradients on the canvas (banding); use radial or solid + localized glow.
- No scene-internal exit animations — the crossfade IS the exit.
- No bouncy or elastic eases — wrong tonal register.
