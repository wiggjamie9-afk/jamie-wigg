GIMP is powerful. But the layout? Years of muscle memory point the other way.

Meet PhotoGIMP — a free patch that makes GIMP look and feel like Photoshop.

Photoshop shortcuts. Photoshop tool order. Photoshop window layout. The same GIMP underneath — none of the friction.

Built for the Photoshop refugee, the open-source switcher, the curious creator who wants pro tools without the subscription.

Drop the patch into GIMP's config folder. Open GIMP. Done.

PhotoGIMP. Free. Open source. GPL v3.
