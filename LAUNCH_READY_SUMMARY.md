**Recommendation:** Start with 3-person team (you + 2 engineers). Hire if first month is successful.

### 2. Primary Market Focus

**Question:** Where do you target first?
- **Option A:** India/Africa (largest market, lowest willingness to pay, NGO partnerships)
- **Option B:** US/Western Europe (smallest market, highest willingness to pay, easier conversion)
- **Option C:** Both in parallel (languages, geo-pricing)

**Recommendation:** Start with India (500M+ target users, high app store adoption, your expertise). Expand to US/EU in Month 2.

### 3. B2B vs. B2C Revenue Focus

**Question:** Prioritize which revenue stream?
- **Option A:** Consumer subscriptions only (simpler, slower growth)
- **Option B:** Enterprise partnerships from day 1 (faster revenue, more work)
- **Option C:** Both (most ambitious)

**Recommendation:** Launch consumer B2C first (Week 1-3). Reach out to 50 partners in Week 4 (corporate wellness, universities, NGOs).

### 4. Marketing Spend

**Question:** How much to spend on user acquisition?

**Benchmarks:**
- Tier 1 (Emotional AI): $1-5 CAC (viral, high retention)
