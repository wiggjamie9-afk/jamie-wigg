## Sound Effects Library

### Mechanical / Ambient
- **Fluorescent hum**: 50–70Hz fundamental, 50dB baseline rising to 75dB
- **Fan click**: 100–150Hz mechanical click, repetitive, 60–70dB
- **Pencil tap**: 2–5kHz stick/tap, staccato, 50–70dB
- **Classroom murmur**: Multi-voice, 200–2000Hz, varying levels 40–75dB

### Psychological / Sensory
- **Heartbeat**: 60–90 BPM, drum-like, 60–80dB (varies with emotion)
- **Breathing**: Audible exhale/inhale, 40–60dB (anxiety = fast/shallow)
- **Synesthetic tones**: Electric Blue (3000–4000Hz shimmer), Burgundy (100–200Hz dark bass)
- **Time distortion**: Pitch-shifted loops, slight reverb/delay (underwater quality)

### Transformation / Empowerment
- **Electric charge**: Rising sine sweep (200–5000Hz), 50–60dB
- **Transformation chord**: 3-note harmonic (low, mid, high), 65dB
- **Focus laser**: Pure tone (8000Hz), brief, 60dB
- **Speed whoosh**: Doppler-style frequency sweep, 70dB
- **Empathy warmth**: Warm pad tone (200–500Hz), sustained, 60dB
- **SURGE hero sting**: 2-second orchestral swell, 80dB peak

### Music Underscore
- **Act 1**: Tense, building strings (minor key, 120 BPM)
- **Act 2**: Chaotic, layered synths (accelerating tempo)
- **Act 3**: Triumphant orchestral (major key, building to full orchestra)
- **Epilogue**: Hopeful, warm, community feeling (80 BPM, major key)
- **Teaser**: Cinematic, dramatic build (full orchestra + synth sting)

## Production Notes

### Mixing Guidelines
- **Act 1**: Center Ziggy's internal experience (close-mic heartbeat, breathing)
- **Act 2**: Overwhelm through layer stacking (all ambient + voice = chaotic mix)
- **Act 3**: Clean, clear, empowering (less reverb, more present)
- **Epilogue**: Warm, community-focused (open spaces, gentle reverb)

### EQ / Effects
- **Act 1**: Slight filter sweep upward (200Hz → 5000Hz) during anxiety escalation
- **Act 2**: Aggressive EQ cuts/boosts (sensory harshness)
- **Act 3**: Linear phase reverb + warm compression (clarity + warmth)
- **Epilogue**: Smooth, transparent mixing

### Loudness Targets
- **Integrated LUFS**: -16 LUFS (YouTube standard)
- **True Peak**: -2 dBTP (headroom for streaming)
- **Dynamic range**: 4–8 LU (allow dynamic peaks for impact moments)
