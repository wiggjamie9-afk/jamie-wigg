The Story

Ziggy's Monday is never-ending. The classroom feels like a pressure cooker of sensory overload—the fluorescent hum, the fan click, the pencil taps, the peer voices all mounting into a crescendo of anxiety. Everything is too much. Everything is too loud.

When shame crashes down in Act 2, Ziggy believes they're broken. But in the quiet moment of dissociation, something shifts. The Burgundy fog lifts. Electric Blue light emerges.

Ziggy discovers superpowers—not despite ADHD, but because of it. The hyperfocus becomes a superpower of tunnel vision. The rapid processing becomes superhuman speed. The emotional sensitivity becomes empathy that cuts to the heart.

In the epilogue, Ziggy uses those powers to help peers. And in that moment of connection, the journey from shame to community is complete.
