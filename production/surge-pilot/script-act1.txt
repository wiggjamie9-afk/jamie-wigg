## VOICE CASTING NOTES (Ziggy Chen — Interior Monologue)

**Age:** 10 years old
**Accent:** Midwest neutral (not regional, not affected)
**Energy:** Bright baseline, but anxious under pressure
**Key moments:**
- Line 1 ("Wait, what?") — confused, quick, not panicked yet
- Line 2 ("Hum... click...") — mounting anxiety, voice gets tighter, racing thoughts
- Silent moment (Shot 15) — shutdown, dissociation, no sound (just breathing effect)

**Direction:** Play Ziggy as intelligent and capable, but experiencing sensory and cognitive overwhelm. NOT a caricature of ADHD (hyperactive manic energy). Instead: a real 10-year-old whose nervous system is working overtime.

---

## AMBIENT SOUND DESIGN REFERENCES

**Fluorescent Hum:** Shimmer-like tone (60Hz electrical hum + shimmer, slightly warped/distorted to feel present and overwhelming)
**Ceiling Fan:** Rhythmic click-click-click (not mechanical, but present; familiar school sound)
**Pencil Tap:** Staccato, nervous energy; increases in tempo as anxiety rises
**Classroom Ambient:** Low-level murmuring of peers, paper shuffling, chair creaks (everyone else is calm, Ziggy is spiraling)
**Shame Moment Sound:** Cut abruptly to silence, then just Ziggy's breathing (isolated, alone)
