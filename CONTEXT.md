# RHYTHMIX

A workspace for producing short-form marketing videos ("promos") for RHYTHMIX, an AI music platform. Each promo is authored as a HyperFrames HTML composition with TTS narration and rendered to MP4 for distribution.

## Language

**Promo**:
A short-form RHYTHMIX marketing video. The unit of work in this repo. A Promo can have multiple Cuts.
_Implementation_: a HyperFrames composition folder (`rhythmix-<name>-<length>/`) containing `index.html`, `script.txt`, `narration.wav`, `hyperframes.json`, and a rendered `.mp4`.
_Avoid_: video, composition, clip — they conflate intent (marketing) with implementation (HyperFrames) or artifact (MP4).

**Cut**:
A specific length+aspect rendering of a Promo. E.g. a 60s landscape cut, a 30s TikTok cut, a 15s square cut. Each Cut is its own composition folder; cuts of the same Promo share script intent but not necessarily the script verbatim.
_Verb form_: "recut" — to produce a new Cut from an existing Promo.
_Avoid_: variant, version.

**Narration**:
The TTS-generated spoken audio for a Cut. Lives at `<cut>/narration.wav`. Generated by ElevenLabs (see `CREATIVE-AI-STACK.md`).
_Avoid_: voiceover, VO. (The root-level `voiceover-*.wav` files predate this convention and are scratch material — not part of any current Cut.)

**Hook**:
The opening attention-grabber of a Cut — typically a sequence of 1-3 short lines (often single words with emphasis) animated one after another in Scene 1. In the DOM these are `.hook` divs inside a `.hook-stack`. Example: "No&nbsp;_producer._ / No&nbsp;_studio._ / No&nbsp;_instrument._" in `rhythmix-anthem-60s`.
_Avoid_: intro, opener, teaser-line.

## Relationships

- A **Promo** has one or more **Cuts**.
- Each **Cut** has exactly one **Narration**.
- Each **Cut** opens with a **Hook**.
- A **Cut** is a HyperFrames composition; the production pipeline is governed by ADR-0001 (HyperFrames over Remotion).

## Example dialogue

> **New contributor:** "Where do I add the new TikTok version of the overview video?"
> **Maintainer:** "It's a 30s vertical **Cut** of the overview **Promo**. Recut from `rhythmix-overview-60s` into a new folder like `rhythmix-overview-tiktok-30s`. New `narration.wav`, new `index.html`, but the same overall script intent."
> **New contributor:** "Should I put it under `video/`?"
> **Maintainer:** "No — that's Remotion, and we don't use it for Promos. See ADR-0001."
