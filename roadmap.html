Why the staggered roadmap (not all-at-launch)

Building four pillars solo would have meant shipping nothing for 18 months. Shipping the music-video pillar first proves the orchestration, funds the next pillar, and gets early creators using the tool while the rest comes online. The founding-100 pricing covers the cost of building the back-end pillars while the front-end serves real users.

Sibling products (separate)

Three sibling products share RHYTHMIX infrastructure but are sold separately at AU$30 lifetime each: RESONATE (biometric music for AirPods Pro 3), DREAMS (generative bedtime ritual), HUM (humming practice). RHYTHMIX LIVE (the music-video subscription product) is at /live for those who want the music-video pipeline as standalone monthly access.
