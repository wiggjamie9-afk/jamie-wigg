# ECHO Character Design Specification

## Visual Overview

ECHO is a dark, energetic antagonist embodying sensory overwhelm and ADHD chaos. The character represents the negative manifestation of distraction, overstimulation, and cognitive chaos. The design conveys constant restless motion, sensory overload, and challenge through angular postures, sharp eyes, and electric color accents against a dark palette.

## Core Design Elements

### Body Structure
- **Posture:** Angular, dynamic, constantly suggesting motion
- **Silhouette:** Jagged, sharp edges creating visual discord
- **Proportions:** Slightly exaggerated angles for cartoon expressiveness
- **Limbs:** Long, thin, sharp-ended to suggest restlessness
- **Overall Energy:** Unstable, unfocused, chaotic

### Head & Face
- **Eyes:** Sharp, intense, narrow pupils expressing distraction and overstimulation
- **Expression:** Constantly shifting between chaos, mockery, overwhelm, and defeat
- **Hair/Head Shape:** Spiky, jagged, or sharp edges suggesting electric charge and chaos
- **Mouth:** Variable - from open/chaotic to smirking to withdrawn
- **Energy Aura:** Subtle electric glow or motion lines around head in active states

### Clothing & Accessories
- **Primary Colors:** Dark charcoal, midnight blue, deep purple
- **Accent Colors:** Electric cyan (#00d8ff), neon magenta (#ff1f5a)
- **Pattern Suggestions:** 
  - Chaotic striping or fractal patterns
  - Electric lightning-bolt motifs
  - Fragmented/scattered design elements
  - Movement lines suggesting motion
- **Style:** Modern, geometric, sharp-angled
- **Fit:** Tight or flowing unpredictably, suggesting instability

### Visual Motion Language
- **Motion Lines:** Dynamic, aggressive, pointing in multiple directions
- **Energy Effects:** Electric arcs, particle scattering, fragmentation effects
- **Aura:** Electric fields, distortion waves around character in action poses
- **Trails:** Trailing motion effects when in movement poses

### MIDDLE ROW: Four Emotional Expressions

#### 1. **Chaotic / Wild Expression**
- **Eyes:** Wide open, intense, pupils dilated
- **Mouth:** Open wide or in extreme expression
- **Energy Radiating:** Lines and particles bursting outward
- **Head Position:** Tilted back or forward aggressively
- **Aura:** Strong electric field effect surrounding head
- **Overall Feel:** Maximum sensory overload
- **Color Intensity:** Heavy use of electric cyan and magenta

#### 2. **Mocking / Taunting Smirk**
- **Eyes:** Narrow, confident, looking down or to the side
- **Mouth:** Distinct smirk or sneer
- **Posture:** Confident, dismissive
- **Head Tilt:** Arrogant angle
- **Energy Level:** Controlled, intense confidence
- **Expression:** Teasing, challenging, mocking
- **Color:** Cooler tones with magenta accents

#### 3. **Overwhelming / Intense State**
- **Eyes:** Distorted, unfocused, sensory overload visual
- **Features:** Slightly warped or melting appearance
- **Mouth:** Open in overwhelm
- **Aura:** Heavy electric field, distortion waves
- **Energy:** Chaotic, fractal patterns, multiple directions
- **Overall Feel:** Maximum sensory chaos visualization
- **Color:** Intense magenta and cyan with purple overlay

#### 4. **Defeated / Retreating Pose**
- **Eyes:** Reduced intensity, withdrawn
- **Posture:** Smaller, contracted, defensive
- **Expression:** Diminished, less confident
- **Energy:** Fading or imploding
- **Aura:** Minimal or contracting
- **Overall Feel:** Loss of power, retreat
- **Color:** Darker tones, less electric accent

---

### BOTTOM ROW: Four Action Poses

#### 1. **Creating Chaos Pose**
- **Position:** Arms spread wide open
- **Motion:** Dynamic, expansive gesture
- **Effect:** Fragments or particles scattering in all directions around character
- **Energy:** Outward explosion of chaos
- **Motion Lines:** Multiple directions, aggressive
- **Details:** Showing character actively creating sensory chaos
- **Color:** Heavy electric accents, particles in cyan and magenta

#### 2. **Swooping Attack Motion**
- **Position:** Diving or swooping forward dynamically
- **Angle:** 45° downward or forward motion
- **Motion Lines:** Trailing effect behind character
- **Speed Visualization:** Dynamic speed lines
- **Expression:** Aggressive, attacking
- **Details:** Full-body in motion, diving toward viewer or to side
- **Energy:** Intense forward momentum
- **Color:** Electric accents trailing behind

#### 3. **Scattering / Dispersing Movement**
- **Position:** Center with body fragmenting or dispersing
- **Effect:** Character or energy scattering outward
- **Particles:** Multiple small fragments moving away from center
- **Motion Lines:** Radiating outward in all directions
- **Expression:** Dispersing, spreading chaos
- **Details:** Dissolution effect, pieces moving independently
- **Color:** Electric cyan and magenta particles

#### 4. **Imploding / Contracting Pose**
- **Position:** Body contracting inward
- **Motion Lines:** Spiraling inward toward center
- **Effect:** Energy being pulled/compressed inward
- **Expression:** Intensity being concentrated
- **Details:** Opposite of explosion, everything converging
- **Energy Level:** Building intensity through compression
- **Color:** Deep purples and dark colors, electric accents compressed

- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (Landscape)
