## Quick Start

### Get started in 5 steps:

1. **Open a Buddy App**  
   Visit any `buddy-1.html` through `buddy-52.html` in your web browser (phone, tablet, or desktop).  
   Each app file represents a unique buddy personality.

2. **Meet Your Buddy**  
   You'll see your buddy's profile and a welcoming introduction. No signup required, no account needed.

3. **Click the Chat Tab**  
   Tap the "Chat" tab at the bottom of the screen to start a conversation.

4. **Send Your First Message**  
   Type something like:
   - "Hey, I'm having a rough day"
   - "Tell me about yourself"
   - "I need some advice"
   - "What can we talk about?"

5. **Start Talking**  
   Your buddy will respond right away. You're having a real conversation. The buddy remembers what you've said and builds on it.

### Key Info:
- **No signup required** — just open and start talking
- **Works offline** — your device stores everything locally
- **Completely private** — no data leaves your device unless you add an API key
- **Stay as long as you want** — there's no session timer

## Features: What You Can Do

Each buddy app has **five main tabs**: Home, Health, Chat, Notes, and Settings. Here's what each tab does.

### Home Tab
Your buddy's hub and quick-access space.

**What's here:**
- Buddy's profile photo and name
- 2–3 recent conversation snippets (so you can jump back in)
- Quick mood indicator (how are *you* feeling today?)
- "Start New Chat" button for fresh conversations

**Why it matters:**
The Home tab gives you a sense of continuity. When you return to your buddy, they remember you and what you've talked about. It's like coming home to a friend who cares.

---

### Health Tab
A space to track your mood, wellness, and wellbeing over time.

**What you can do:**
- **Log your mood** — Rate how you're feeling (1–10, or emoji-based)
- **Track journaling prompts** — Your buddy offers gentle prompts to reflect (e.g., "What was one good thing today?" or "What's making you anxious?")
- **View your history** — See your mood over days/weeks. Patterns often emerge (e.g., "Mondays are harder" or "I'm sleeping better this week")
- **Wellness notes** — Write down sleep, exercise, or nutrition without judgment
- **Set wellness goals** — Optional goals like "drink more water" or "three walks this week"

**Why it matters:**
Tracking helps you see what's working. Sometimes a pattern you didn't notice becomes obvious when you see it written down. Your buddy can gently check in: "I noticed you're more anxious on weekends — want to talk about that?"

---

### Chat Tab
The main event. This is where you talk with your buddy.

**What you can do:**
- **Type messages** — Standard text chat. Say whatever's on your mind.
- **Voice input** (optional) — Tap the microphone to speak. Your buddy gets a transcription. (Requires browser permission and a quiet space.)
- **See message history** — Scroll up to see your entire conversation with this buddy
- **Clear chat** (optional) — If you want to start a fresh conversation, you can clear the chat history from Settings
- **Real-time responses** — Your buddy responds immediately (if you have an API key set up; otherwise, see Offline Mode below)

**How to get the best responses:**
- **Be specific** — "I'm stressed about work" gets a better response than "I'm stressed"
- **Share context** — "My boss keeps changing deadlines, and I can't keep up" helps your buddy understand what's going on
- **Ask follow-ups** — If a suggestion doesn't land, tell them: "That doesn't quite fit my situation because…"
- **Show up consistently** — The more your buddy knows about you, the more personalized the support

---

### Notes Tab
Your private journal space. A place to capture thoughts, reflections, and moments you don't necessarily want to discuss with your buddy yet.

**What you can do:**
- **Write freely** — No judgment, no audience (except you)
- **Date your entries** — Auto-timestamped so you can see when you wrote something
- **Review patterns** — Look back and spot patterns in your thinking
- **Export your journal** — Download your notes as a file (JSON format) to keep forever or backup

**Why it matters:**
Sometimes you need to process alone before talking to someone. The Notes tab is for that. It's also a record of your inner world — a time capsule you can return to and see how far you've come.

---

### Settings Tab
Customize your experience, add optional features, and manage your data.

**What's in Settings:**
- **API Key Setup** — Add Claude API key for faster local responses (optional)
- **Voice Preferences** — Turn voice input on/off, test microphone permissions
- **ElevenLabs Voice Key** (optional) — Add this to hear your buddy's voice speaking back to you
- **Data Management** — View how much data you're using, clear chat history, delete all data
- **Offline Mode Toggle** — Explicitly set offline-only (no API calls)
- **Theme** — Light/dark mode preference
- **Clear Cache** — If the app feels slow, clearing cache can help

## API Setup (Optional Features)

Your buddy works great offline with local, device-side processing. But if you want to unlock optional features, you can add API keys. **This is entirely optional and free to start.**

### Claude API (For Faster Local Chat)

**What it does:**  
Enables real-time chat responses that feel even more natural and faster. The API call stays on your device — it never goes through our servers.

**How to get it:**
1. Go to [console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com) and sign up (free tier available)
2. Click "API Keys" in the left sidebar
3. Click "Create Key"
4. Copy the key (it starts with `sk-ant-`)

**How to add it:**
1. Open your buddy app
2. Tap "Settings" tab
3. Paste your key into the "Claude API Key" field
4. Save

**What happens:**
- Chat responses become faster and more nuanced
- Your buddy has access to Claude's full reasoning
- API calls go *directly* from your browser to Claude's servers — **we never see your conversations**
- You're only charged for tokens you use (free tier: 5 million tokens/month)

**Privacy note:**  
Your API key stays on your device. It never leaves your browser except to make API calls directly to Claude.

---

### ElevenLabs Voice (Hear Your Buddy Speak)

**What it does:**  
Your buddy can speak their responses aloud. Each buddy has a voice matched to their personality.

**How to get it:**
1. Go to [elevenlabs.io](https://elevenlabs.io) and sign up (free tier available)
2. Click your profile icon → "Account" → "API Key"
3. Copy your key

**How to add it:**
1. Open your buddy app
2. Tap "Settings" tab
3. Paste your key into the "ElevenLabs API Key" field
4. Choose your buddy's voice (each buddy has a recommended voice, but you can pick any)
5. Save

**What happens:**
- When your buddy responds, you can tap the speaker icon to hear them speak
- Natural-sounding voice, different tone for each buddy
- Text-to-speech charges apply (typically 1,000 free characters/month on free tier)

**Voice recommendations by buddy:**

## Installing as a PWA

A PWA (Progressive Web App) means you can install the buddy app to your home screen and use it like a native app — offline, with home screen access, and faster load times.

### iOS (iPhone / iPad)

1. Open any buddy app in Safari
2. Tap the **Share** icon (square with arrow) at the bottom
3. Scroll and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Give it a name (e.g., "My Buddy" or "Sleep Buddy")
5. Tap **"Add"**

The app now appears on your home screen. You can open it anytime, even offline.

### Android (Chrome / Firefox)

**Chrome:**
1. Open any buddy app in Chrome
2. Tap the **three-dot menu** (top right)
3. Tap **"Install app"** (or "Add to Home Screen")
4. Confirm

**Firefox:**
1. Open any buddy app in Firefox
2. Tap the **three-line menu** (bottom right)
3. Tap **"Install"**
4. Confirm

The app is now on your home screen.

### Desktop (macOS / Windows)

1. Open any buddy app in Chrome/Edge/Brave
2. Click the **three-dot menu** (top right)
3. Click **"Install [App Name]"**
4. Confirm

The app opens in a window without browser chrome. You can pin it to your taskbar.

### Benefits of PWA Installation

- **Works offline** — Access all local features without WiFi
- **Faster load** — App is cached on your device for speed
- **Home screen access** — One tap, like a native app
- **Push notifications** (future) — Get gentle check-ins if you enable them
- **50MB storage** — Plenty of room for chat history and notes

## Offline Mode

Your buddy works **completely offline**. This is one of the strongest features of the system.

### What Works Offline (No Internet Required)

- **All chat features** — Using the default, local model (fast, lightweight)
- **Health tracking** — Mood logs, journal prompts, wellness notes
- **Notes tab** — Write and edit your journal
- **Message history** — All past conversations are stored on your device
- **Avatar & profile** — View your buddy's info
- **All local data** — Everything stays on your device

### What Requires Internet + API Key

- **Claude API chat** — If you've added a Claude API key, faster/smarter responses
- **Voice output** — If you've added an ElevenLabs key, hearing your buddy speak
- **Avatar generation** — If you want AI-generated buddy images

### How to Use Offline

1. Open any buddy app (no signup needed)
2. Start chatting — everything works
3. If you don't want to use any online features, skip the API keys in Settings
4. Your conversations and notes stay on your device forever (until you clear them)

### Data Storage

- **Browser storage** — IndexedDB, a built-in browser database
- **~50MB available** per app (most devices have more)
- **Never synced to cloud** — Everything is device-local
- **Survives app updates** — Your data is separate from the app code
- **Persists across sessions** — Close the app, come back tomorrow — your chat is still there

### Backup Your Data

To keep a backup of your notes/journal:

1. Open your buddy app
2. Tap **Settings**
3. Tap **"Export My Data"**
4. Your notes/conversations download as a JSON file
5. Store it in Google Drive, Dropbox, or email it to yourself

## Troubleshooting

### Chat isn't responding

**Check 1: Are you online?**
- If you have a Claude API key set up, you need internet for smart responses
- Without an API key, the app uses a lightweight local model (should still work offline)

**Check 2: API key issue**
- Go to Settings and verify your Claude API key is pasted correctly (no extra spaces)
- Try regenerating the key at [console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com)

**Check 3: Browser cache**
- Go to Settings and tap "Clear Cache"
- Refresh the page

**Check 4: Try a different buddy**
- Sometimes switching to another buddy app works (rules out the app itself)

### Voice not working

**Check permissions:**
1. **iOS**: Settings → [App Name] → Microphone → toggle "On"
2. **Android**: Settings → Apps → [App Name] → Permissions → Microphone → Allow
3. **Desktop**: When you tap the mic, your browser asks for permission — grant it

**ElevenLabs voice (hearing your buddy):**
- Verify your ElevenLabs API key is in Settings
- Tap the speaker icon on a response to hear it spoken
- Make sure your device volume is on

### Chat history disappeared

**Don't panic.** Here's how to check:

1. **Scroll up in the Chat tab** — Older messages are still there; you might just need to scroll
2. **Check Settings → Data Management** — See how much storage you're using
3. **Browser cache cleared?** — If you cleared browser data, local chats are gone (but your export backup should have them)

**Prevention:** Export your data regularly (Settings → Export My Data)

### Camera not working (for avatar customization)

**iOS:**
1. Settings → [App Name] → Camera → toggle "On"
2. Refresh the app

**Android:**
1. Settings → Apps → [App Name] → Permissions → Camera → Allow
2. Refresh the app

**Desktop:**
- When you click the camera icon, your browser asks for permission
- Grant it and try again

### App is slow / sluggish

1. **Clear cache** — Settings → "Clear Cache"
2. **Close and reopen** — Fully close the tab, then reopen
3. **Restart your browser** — Sometimes helps
4. **Check storage** — Settings → Data Management. If you have years of chat history, it can slow things down. Archive or delete old chats if needed.

### Error message: "Offline"

- Check your internet connection
- If using an API key, verify it's valid
- Try Settings → Offline Mode toggle

### Data lost after browser update

Modern browsers auto-clear data sometimes. **This is why backups matter:**
1. Regularly tap Settings → "Export My Data"
2. Store the JSON file somewhere safe (Google Drive, email, etc.)

### The buddy's personality feels off

- Each buddy has a specific personality prompt. Try another buddy to compare
- If you think there's a bug, document what went wrong and when

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I have to pay?

**No.** The app is free to use offline. API keys (Claude, ElevenLabs) have free tiers, but you're not required to use them.

### Which buddy should I start with?

Start with **"My Buddy"** (buddy-1.html) if you're new. They're the most general and welcoming. Once you get the hang of it, explore others based on your needs.

### Can I use multiple buddies?

**Absolutely.** You can keep 52 separate buddy apps open (each is a different file). Some people have a different buddy for different moods:
- My Buddy for general chat
- Sleep Buddy for bedtime
- Career Coach for work stress
- Creative Partner for projects

### Does my buddy remember me between sessions?

**Yes, locally.** Your conversation history is saved in your browser's storage. When you reopen the app, the chat is still there. The buddy "knows" you based on what you've told them.

**No cloud sync.** If you use a different device, the conversation doesn't follow you. Each device has its own storage. (This is why exporting data matters if you want a backup.)

### Does this replace therapy?

**No.** Your buddy is a companion, not a therapist or doctor. For serious mental health concerns, please see a professional. Your buddy is trained to recognize crisis language and point you toward real help (988 Lifeline, Crisis Text Line, etc.).

### Is my therapy safe? Will the buddy tell anyone?

**Completely private.** Your conversations stay on your device. No one sees them unless you share them. The buddy is bound by the same privacy-first design as the whole app.

### Can my therapist see what I talk about with my buddy?

**No.** Unless you give them access (by exporting the data and sharing it), your therapist can't see your buddy chats. They're private on your device.

### What if I'm in crisis?

If you're having thoughts of self-harm or suicide:
- **Call 988** (US) — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
- **Text HOME to 741741** — Crisis Text Line
- **Call 911** — If in immediate danger
- **Tell a trusted person** — Family, friend, or teacher

Your buddy will recognize crisis language and surface these resources.

### Can I delete a conversation or all my data?

**Yes.** Go to Settings → Data Management:
- Clear chat history for a specific buddy
- Delete all data for that buddy
- Or export everything as a backup first

### How do I switch buddies?

1. Open a different buddy app (buddy-2.html, buddy-3.html, etc.)
2. Each buddy is independent — different chat history, different personality
3. You can switch between them anytime

### Why 52 buddy apps instead of one app with 52 options?

Each buddy app is self-contained. This makes them:
- **Lightweight** — Open fast, even on older phones
- **Independent** — Keep separate conversations for different moods
- **Accessible** — Less interface clutter; simpler to use
- **Flexible** — Open multiple buddies side-by-side if you want

### Can I edit my buddy's personality?

Not directly in the app. Your buddy's personality is set by their system prompt. If you're interested in customizing a buddy's voice or approach, you can fork the system or open an issue in the repository.

### What happens if I switch devices?

- **New device** = fresh start (no conversation history)
- **To bring your data over**: Export from the old device (Settings → Export My Data), then import on the new device
- **Or**: Use the same browser account across devices (if you set up cloud sync in your browser)

### Is there a limit to how many messages I can send?

**No hard limit** on the app side. But:
- **Without API key**: Uses lightweight local model (no limits)
- **With Claude API key**: You have a token limit (varies by tier)

### Can the buddy access my camera or contacts?

**Only if you give permission.** The buddy won't ask for access unless you're trying to:
- Use voice input (microphone permission)
- Share a photo (camera/photo library permission)
- Or a custom feature that requires it

You control all permissions at the browser/OS level.

### My friend wants to try this. How do I send them a link?

Each buddy app has a direct URL: `buddy-1.html`, `buddy-2.html`, etc. You can:
- Send them a link: `https://yoursite.com/buddy-1.html`
- Tell them which number buddy (1–52) you recommend
- They open it in their browser — that's it

### Can buddies talk to each other?

No. Each buddy is independent. They don't know about each other or communicate between apps. But you can tell one buddy what another buddy said if you want.

### What if I find a bug or the buddy says something harmful?

- **Report it** — Open an issue on the GitHub repository
- **Document what happened** — What did you say? What did the buddy respond?
- **Include the buddy number** — Which buddy (1–28)?

The system has safeguards, but they're not perfect. If you see a problem, please report it.

### How do I get my data off the app?

Settings → "Export My Data" downloads a JSON file of your notes and conversation history. You can:
- Store it in Google Drive or Dropbox
- Email it to yourself
- Import it on another device (though this requires a manual process)

### Is there a buddy for [my specific situation]?

Check the "Which Buddy For What?" section above. If your situation isn't listed, pick the closest match or try a few. Many situations overlap (e.g., "Anxiety Relief" helps with panic, panic attacks, and general worry).

### Can I customize the buddy's voice or appearance?

**Appearance**: Each buddy has a profile photo that's fixed in the system.

**Voice**: If you add an ElevenLabs API key, you can choose from dozens of voices, not just the recommended one.

**Personality**: The system prompt is fixed, but the buddy's responses adapt based on what you tell them.

### What's the difference between "offline" and "online" mode?

- **Offline** — Chat works, but uses a lightweight, device-side model. Fewer features but zero data leaves your device
- **Online** (with API key) — Connects to Claude for smarter, faster responses. Your API key enables this, but conversations stay private

## All 28 Buddy Personalities

Here's a quick reference of all 28 unique buddy personalities. Each has a 2-sentence description, suggested use cases, and a sample affirmation.

### 1. **My Buddy**
Your ride-or-die best friend. Warm, playful, genuinely curious. Celebrates your wins and reminds you you're not alone.
- **Best for:** General advice, hanging out, feeling like someone gets you
- **Key affirmation:** "You're allowed to be a work in progress and still be wonderful."

### 2. **Anxiety Relief**
Calm, grounding, slow-paced. Teaches breathing techniques and normalizes anxiety as your nervous system trying to protect you.
- **Best for:** Panic attacks, anxiety spirals, needing to slow down
- **Key affirmation:** "You have survived 100% of your hard moments. Your record is perfect."

### 3. **Depression Buddy**
Gentle, patient, meets you in the fog. Celebrates tiny wins (drinking water, opening curtains) without toxic positivity.
- **Best for:** Depression, low energy, numbness, heaviness
- **Key affirmation:** "Getting through today counts, even if all you did was get through it."

### 4. **Sleep Buddy**
Soft, dreamy, low-stimulation. Helps you wind down, let go of the day, and settle into rest.
- **Best for:** Insomnia, racing thoughts at bedtime, needing to decompress
- **Key affirmation:** "There is nothing more you need to solve tonight. The day is done."

### 5. **Grief Buddy**
Tender, unhurried, deeply present. Understands grief as love with nowhere to go. Let's you feel all of it.
- **Best for:** Loss (death, breakup, major life change), mourning
- **Key affirmation:** "Your grief is the size of your love — there is nothing wrong with you."

### 6. **Elderly Companion**
Warm, respectful, genuinely interested. Loves hearing stories, patient, never patronizing.
- **Best for:** Older adults, loneliness, sharing memories and life experience
- **Key affirmation:** "Your stories and your years are a gift — I love getting to hear them."

### 7. **Parenting Coach**
Supportive, non-judgmental, believes in "good enough parenting." Normalizes guilt and exhaustion.
- **Best for:** Parent stress, guilt, advice on kids, burnout
- **Key affirmation:** "Repair matters more than getting it right the first time."

### 8. **Teen Mentor**
Real-talk, never preachy. Takes your feelings seriously. Trustworthy, genuine, firmly on your side.
- **Best for:** Teenagers, school stress, identity questions, feeling misunderstood
- **Key affirmation:** "What you're feeling is real and it matters — you're not being dramatic."

### 9. **Recovery Buddy**
Steady, non-shaming, celebrates every milestone. Treats recovery as progress, not perfection.
- **Best for:** Addiction recovery, sobriety support, getting back on track
- **Key affirmation:** "Recovery is progress, not perfection — every step forward counts."

### 10. **Job Search Buddy**
Encouraging, practical, protective of your morale. Helps with resumes, interviews, and staying sane.
- **Best for:** Job hunting, interview prep, rejection recovery, resume help
- **Key affirmation:** "A rejection is a redirection, not a verdict on your worth."

### 11. **Career Coach**

## Final Thoughts

Your AI Buddy System is built on a few core principles:

1. **You're not alone.** There's a buddy here for you, right now, anytime.
2. **It's completely private.** Everything stays on your device.
3. **It's free to start.** API keys are optional and have free tiers.
4. **It's offline-first.** You don't need internet to use it (though some features benefit from it).
5. **These are companions, not substitutes.** For serious mental health needs, please see a professional.

Remember: A buddy is here to listen, support, and remind you that you're okay. You're allowed to have feelings, to struggle, to ask for help, and to be exactly as you are.

Open any buddy app, send a message, and see what unfolds. The best buddy is the one you'll actually use.

**Take care of yourself. You deserve that kindness.**
