# Character Design: SURGE

## Design Philosophy

Characters in SURGE are built from simple geometric shapes (circles, rectangles, cylinders) for modular animation across multiple teams. Asymmetry and intentional "imperfection" signal ADHD lived experience—restlessness, overstimulation, emotional intensity. No character is exaggerated or caricatured; all are grounded, real, with visible diversity across race, body type, and gender presentation.

**Key principle:** Simplicity + Motion = Emotional clarity. A character's movement, eye direction, and posture convey internal state without voiceover. Static expressions are supported by animation timing (fidgeting, jittery, hyperfocused).

## Ziggy Chen (Protagonist)

### Base Form (Reality)

#### Head
- **Shape:** Circle (10-year-old proportions, slightly smaller than adult head scale)
- **Hair:** Dark brown (#2B1B15), messy with 3–4 wispy strokes, asymmetrical. Hair stands in different directions (signal movement + chaos, never styled). When animated, hair floats/drifts slightly even at rest (perpetual motion).
- **Eyes:** Large circles for visibility; pupils are Electric Blue (#0052CC) circles. Pupils are **always moving**—not locked forward. They dart, scan, look away; this is core to Ziggy's character.
  - **Blink pattern:** Slower, less frequent than typical (hyperfocus/overrides blink drive when concentrating). Rapid/excessive when anxious.
  - **Expression signal:** Pupils indicate emotional state—wide = alert/overstimulated; squeezed/closed = shame/overwhelm; locked on target = hyperfocus
- **Mouth:** Simple line, curved or straight. Smile when content, neutral when observing, downturned when sad.
- **Skin:** Warm tan (#E8D4B8), East Asian representation matching Mom

#### Body
- **Shape:** Rectangle, tapered slightly at waist (athletic 10-year-old, lean)
- **Clothing:** Blue hoodie (#0052CC—primary color, locks Ziggy's identity), orange trim on cuffs and hood edge (#FF8C3A—connection + warmth)
- **Accents:** Asymmetrical socks (one pulled up to knee, one slouched to ankle)—intentional visual cue for "never still," resistant to adult regulation. One sock is Electric Blue, one is Warm Orange (split identity).
- **Sneakers:** Gray with orange laces, untied or loose (another constraint-resistance detail)
- **Build:** Athletic, thin, constant micro-movement. Posture shifts weight (never centered, always off-balance in reality form).
- **Modular design:** Hoodie can be removed/swapped for other shirts in variants (summer episodes, flashbacks, dream sequences)

#### Limbs
- **Arms & Legs:** Cylinders (consistent thickness, cartoon simplicity)
- **Hands:** 4-finger design (readable at small animation scales, cartoon convention)
- **Feet:** Simple ovals, gesture toward direction of movement/emotion

#### Details Signaling ADHD
- **Fidgeting hands:** Pencil spinning (held between fingers in class scenes), fingers tapping rhythm, picking at cuff strings
- **Restless posture:** Weight shifts side-to-side; knee bent even when standing; one arm across body (self-hug tension)
- **Eyes that scan:** Not staring/focused unless hyperfocused; pupils drift to movement, sounds, internal thought
- **Hair chaos:** Perpetually undone—it's Wednesday morning and Ziggy didn't brush it (parental pressure detail: Mom sighed, Ziggy didn't care, ran out door)
- **Clothing chaos:** Shirt tucked on one side, untucked on other; hoodie strings hanging asymmetrically

### Superhero Form (SURGE)

#### Head & Hair
- Same circle shape, but **hair stands on end** (3–4 strokes point upward like electrical static)
- Eyes maintain movement but gain **glow effect:** Electric Blue pupils surrounded by Neon Yellow aura (#FFFF00)
- Mouth opens (powerful, confident, not aggressive)
- Overall: Same proportions as base, but "electrified"

#### Body & Clothing
- **Core form:** Rectangle remains, but outline glows with Neon Yellow (#FFFF00) stroke, 3–5px soft glow
- **Transformation:** Hoodie dissolves into pure energy form—Electric Blue core with yellow outline, lightning bolt accents running down sleeves and torso
- **Color scheme:** Primarily Electric Blue (#0052CC) with Neon Yellow (#FFFF00) outline and accents. No new colors introduced (consistency with palette).
- **Aura:** Soft Neon Yellow glow surrounds entire figure (3–5px blur, 0.6 opacity). Glow intensifies during speed/hyperfocus moments.

#### Effects
- **Particles:** Electrical sparkles drift upward and around body (slow float, like bubbles—FLOATY motion archetype). 2–4px Electric Blue + Neon Yellow circles, staggered timing.
- **Speed lines:** When Ziggy moves, Electric Blue + Neon Yellow streaks trail behind (HYPERFOCUS motion). Lines fade rapidly (0.5–1 sec duration).
- **Size:** Same as base form (SURGE is not bigger/stronger, just transformed/lit-up; power is internal clarity, not physical growth)

### Expression Sheets (3 expressions)

#### Expression 1: Neutral/Observing (Baseline)
- **Eyes:** Forward, pupils scanning (not locked), blinking at normal rhythm
- **Mouth:** Slight smile (half-open, gentle curve) or neutral line
- **Posture:** Upright but restless—one leg bent, weight shifted to one hip, one hand fidgeting with something (pencil, shirt hem, desk corner)
- **Context:** In moments of potential, watching the soccer game, sitting in class before the shame spiral, moments of readiness or uncertainty
- **Shading:** None; neutral skin tone, no color tint
- **Companion gesture:** Hand near mouth (thinking), or arm across body (self-protective but open)

#### Expression 2: Shame / Overwhelm (Emotional Valley)
- **Eyes:** Downcast or squeezed partially closed; pupils look away or down
- **Mouth:** Downturned line (frown, or clenched/tight)
- **Posture:** Curved inward—shoulders hunched up, making body smaller, head forward/down. Feet close together (contracted, defensive).
- **Context:** Reading struggle moment (can't decode words), peer laughter (peer exclusion), moment when Mrs. Henderson's patience frays, internal failure narrative ("I'm stupid," "I don't belong")
- **Shading/Color shift:** Slight desaturation of skin tone (grayed-down #E8D4B8 → more muted), or optional light Deep Burgundy (#5D1E3B) tint at 20% opacity around eyes/mouth (emotional weight visual signal)
- **Companion gesture:** Hand over face, arm tight around body, or hands fidgeting frantically (losing control)

#### Expression 3: Hyperfocus / Joy (Emotional Peak)
- **Eyes:** Wide, bright, pupils locked on target (not scanning). Alert but calm (contrast to fidgeting baseline).
- **Mouth:** Open smile, genuine joy (smile reaches eyes)
- **Posture:** Forward-leaning, engaged, dynamic—weight on balls of feet (ready to move), one arm extended or reaching, body open
- **Context:** Soccer goal scored, solving a math problem after struggling, moment of Surge transformation (breakthrough), Mrs. Henderson saying "That's clever, Ziggy," connection with Jake
- **Shading/Color shift:** Slight saturation increase (skin tone brightens slightly). Optional Neon Yellow (#FFFF00) glow at 15% opacity around whole character (internal clarity/hyperfocus state).
- **Companion gesture:** Fist up, hands on hip (confident), or reaching toward a friend/goal

### Ziggy Construction Notes (for animators)

- **Line weight:** 2.5px main outlines (head, body, limbs), 1.5px details (fingers, facial features, clothing seams)
- **Symmetry principle:** Intentionally asymmetrical. Sock difference, hair messiness, weight shift are not animation errors—they are character choices. Lock this in design before animation starts.
- **Animation anchors:** Eyes and hair are primary motion points (pupils dart, hair floats). Hands are secondary (fidgeting, tapping). Body posture shifts (weight transfer) support emotional beats without dialogue.
- **Color consistency:** Blue hoodie is always #0052CC; orange accents always #FF8C3A. No color drift across episodes.
- **Modular construction:** Head, body, limbs, accessories (hoodie, socks, sneakers, hair variations) should be layer-separable in SVG/animation software for efficiency. Animators should be able to swap "happy Ziggy" head on any body pose without rebuilding.
- **Fidgeting library:** Build 4–6 loop-able fidget animations (pencil spin, finger tap, leg bounce, arm wrap) at 2–3 second duration, seamlessly chain-able.
- **Micro-motion guidelines:** Even in dialogue, Ziggy never fully stops. Hair drifts, fingers tap, weight shifts. This is character, not a bug.

## Mrs. Henderson (Supporting Cast)

### Base Form

#### Head
- **Shape:** Oval (adult proportions, slightly elongated compared to Ziggy's circle)
- **Hair:** Short, practical style, graying brown (#5D4E4B). No wispy movement—hair is controlled, structured. Represents the adult world's "together-ness" contrasting Ziggy's chaos.
- **Eyes:** Regular circles, pupils are calm (#333333 charcoal), not constantly moving like Ziggy. Pupils look directly at people/task (focus is stable). Eyes show kindness but also fatigue—slight lines at corners, slight droop (standing all day managing 25 kids).
- **Mouth:** Usually neutral line or soft smile (lips slightly curved up). Never aggressive or condescending.
- **Skin:** Light tan (#E0D0C8), white representation with warm undertones

#### Body
- **Shape:** Rectangle (adult proportions, slightly taller than Ziggy's body, curved slightly forward at shoulders from teaching posture)
- **Clothing:** Sage green cardigan (#2D8A3D—primary color locks her identity as grounding adult), over a neutral tan or cream blouse, sensible black or tan pants
- **Build:** Average adult, grounded posture (weight centered, balanced). Shoulders show slight tension (stress, but managed).
- **Modular details:** Cardigan can be unbuttoned/removed for variants (hot classroom, casual afternoon); blouse color can shift slightly (Tuesday vs. Thursday, no major personality shift)

#### Details Signaling Character
- **Slight stoop:** Teaching posture, sitting at desk, kneeling to help students. Not aggressive, but visibly tired.
- **Hands often holding:** Papers (grading), pen (writing notes), or gesturing (teaching mode)—hands are agents of her work
- **Facial kindness:** Eyes with genuine care, no disdain. This is foundational—Mrs. H. is never painted as a villain, even when she struggles with Ziggy.

### Expression Sheets (3 expressions)

#### 1. Professional/Routine (Baseline)
- **Eyes:** Forward, calm, engaged (watching class)
- **Mouth:** Neutral or soft smile
- **Posture:** Upright, centered, hands often gesturing or holding papers
- **Context:** Teaching mode, reading aloud, calling on students, routine classroom management
- **Gesture:** Hand extended toward board, hand holding paper, arm at side

#### 2. Concern / Confusion (Uncertainty)
- **Eyes:** Slight furrow between brows, eyes on Ziggy, pupils focused
- **Mouth:** Slightly worried (mouth slightly open, corners neutral or slightly down)
- **Posture:** Slight head tilt (curious, trying to understand), shoulders slightly forward
- **Context:** Ziggy is reading and stumbling; Mrs. H. notices the struggle and tries to diagnose ("Is this an attention thing?" or "Is Ziggy even trying?"). Moment before judgment hardens; moment of openness.
- **Gesture:** Hand on chin (thinking), head tilted, eyes narrowed slightly (focused attention)

#### 3. Recognition / Warmth (Understanding)
- **Eyes:** Bright, warm, pupils soft (kind gaze), slight crinkles at corners (genuine smile)
- **Mouth:** Open smile, warm (lips curved, eyes involved)
- **Posture:** Shoulders back, body open, slight forward lean (engaged, present)
- **Context:** The moment she truly sees Ziggy's intelligence—"Wait, you solved this differently... that's clever, Ziggy." Or moment when she recognizes the ADHD (not stupidity) and her frustration shifts to compassion.
- **Gesture:** Hand gesturing toward Ziggy in encouragement, hand on chest (genuine emotion), weight forward (present with Ziggy)

### Mrs. Henderson Construction Notes
- **Line weight:** 2.5px outlines, 1.5px details
- **Stability:** Unlike Ziggy, Mrs. H. is centered and grounded. Movement is purposeful (walks to desk, gestures to board) not fidgety.
- **Age signals:** Lines at eyes/mouth; graying hair; slightly slower movement (not diminished, but deliberate vs. Ziggy's constant motion)
- **Color consistency:** Sage green cardigan is always #2D8A3D. Blouse can vary slightly but stays warm neutrals.

## Jake (Peer / Best Friend)

### Base Form

#### Head
- **Shape:** Circle (10-year-old, similar to Ziggy's scale)
- **Hair:** Short, messy but in a "cute," straightforward way—not chaotic like Ziggy's. Suggest a natural blonde-brown or medium brown (#8B6F47, warm brown), representing ethnic/cultural diversity (recommendation: depict as a non-white character; ethnicity to be finalized in consultation with sensitivity panel).
- **Eyes:** Open, bright circles; pupils are steady (#333333), not darting. Straightforward gaze—Jake sees what's in front of him; not overwhelmed by sensory noise like Ziggy. Eyes are warm and uncomplicated.
- **Mouth:** Often smiling (uncomplicated joy is his baseline). Smile reaches eyes.
- **Skin tone:** Medium skin tone (#D4956E), representing ethnic diversity in the classroom

#### Body
- **Shape:** Rectangle (10-year-old, athletic build—coordinated, poised)
- **Clothing:** Warm orange shirt (#FF8C3A—mirrors Ziggy's orange accents, shows visual friendship connection). Blue shorts or blue jacket (mirrors Ziggy's blue hoodie; color symmetry). Sneakers (coordinated, laced properly—subtle contrast to Ziggy's loose laces).
- **Build:** Athletic, natural grace. Posture is stable and grounded (contrast to Ziggy's restlessness).

#### Details Signaling Character
- **Posture is stable:** Weight centered, balanced stance. Reflects his calm, grounded nature.
- **Hands often open:** Gesturing generously, waving, reaching out. Reflects his openness and lack of self-protective tension.
- **Movement is purposeful:** No fidgeting. When Jake moves, he goes somewhere with intention.

### Expression Sheets (3 expressions)

#### 1. Joy / Enthusiasm (Baseline)
- **Eyes:** Open, bright, forward-looking
- **Mouth:** Open smile, genuine
- **Posture:** Forward lean, open chest, one arm extended or reaching
- **Context:** Playing soccer, greeting friends, excitement about recess, offering help
- **Gesture:** High-five reach, thumbs up, arms open in invitation

#### 2. Concern / Confusion (Uncertainty)
- **Eyes:** Eyebrows slightly furrowed, eyes on Ziggy, pupils soft
- **Mouth:** Neutral or slightly worried (mouth slightly open)
- **Posture:** Head tilt, slight lean forward (wants to help but doesn't fully understand)
- **Context:** Ziggy is struggling and Jake wants to help but doesn't know how. Jake's straightforward brain meets Ziggy's complex internal state; moment of limitation and loyalty.
- **Gesture:** Hand on Ziggy's shoulder, hand reaching out, confused but caring

#### 3. Loyalty / Determination (Strength)
- **Eyes:** Confident, locked on Ziggy or the goal ahead
- **Mouth:** Confident smile or determined line
- **Posture:** Upright, grounded, one arm around Ziggy's shoulder or reaching toward him
- **Context:** Standing by Ziggy despite peer judgment, the moment he chooses friendship over social safety, SURGE moment when Jake is part of Ziggy's power
- **Gesture:** Arm around shoulder, fist pump, standing side-by-side (united)

### Jake Construction Notes
- **Line weight:** 2.5px outlines, 1.5px details
- **Grounded:** Like Mrs. H., Jake is centered and stable. His motion is coordinated, not jittery.
- **Color consistency:** Orange shirt (#FF8C3A) always this exact shade. Blue accents (#0052CC) lock his connection to Ziggy.
- **Contrast function:** Jake's stability makes Ziggy's restlessness visible and understandable to the audience. Animation timing: Jake moves decisively; Ziggy moves constantly. No dialogue needed to understand the difference.

## Sarah Chen (Mom)

### Base Form

#### Head
- **Shape:** Circle-oval (adult, East Asian features)
- **Hair:** Long or shoulder-length, dark brown (#2B1B15—matches Ziggy's hair color, shows genetic connection)
- **Eyes:** Warm circles with calm pupils (#333333), but pupils show fatigue—slight droop at outer corners, tired but kind. Eyes show emotional depth and complexity; she's managing a lot internally.
- **Mouth:** Soft smile (genuine care) or slight tightness around lips (balancing care + stress). Not resting angry face, but visible strain.
- **Skin:** Warm tan (#E8D4B8—matches Ziggy), East Asian representation

#### Body
- **Shape:** Rectangle (adult, petite build, energetic but with tension)
- **Clothing:** Lavender blouse (#D8B5E6—primary color, signals her emotional vulnerability + home safety), neutral black or dark gray jeans, maybe a minimal watch or simple necklace (busy parent detail—she checks the time a lot)
- **Build:** Petite, capable, energetic but visibly juggling. Posture is engaged and caring but sometimes tight-shouldered (stress stored in body).

#### Details Signaling Character
- **Perpetually busy hands:** Often holding phone, keys, coffee mug, or a stack of papers (Ziggy's permission slips, medical forms). Hands are agents of caregiving/management.
- **Hair slightly undone:** Not messy like Ziggy, but shows time pressure—maybe a bit disheveled at the edges, hair tie loose, strand falling loose (6 am, shower was quick, left house in controlled chaos)
- **Posture:** Engaged and forward-leaning when with Ziggy, but tight-shouldered in rest state (tension, stress storage)
- **Eyes show complexity:** Not simple joy or sadness, but layered emotion—love + worry + frustration + determination all visible

### Expression Sheets (3 expressions)

#### 1. Loving / Present (Warmth)
- **Eyes:** Warm, soft, genuinely engaged, crinkles at corners (genuine smile)
- **Mouth:** Open smile, warm
- **Posture:** Toward Ziggy, body open, hand reaching or touching (reassuring hand on shoulder)
- **Context:** Bedtime moment, cooking together, moment of understanding Ziggy's struggle, pride in Ziggy's achievement
- **Gesture:** Hug gesture, hand on chest (heart), gentle hand touch

#### 2. Overwhelmed / Frustrated (Strain)
- **Eyes:** Strained, pupils slightly squeezed, tired
- **Mouth:** Tight line or slight tension at corners (not angry, but at capacity)
- **Posture:** Shoulders up (tension), might look away (overwhelmed, not present), body slightly tense
- **Context:** Ziggy got another note from school, Mom forgot her lunch, work call running over, everything piling up at once, moment when parental capacity reaches limit
- **Gesture:** Hand to face (stress signal), shoulders tense, hand on forehead (overwhelmed)

#### 3. Recognition / Understanding (Breakthrough)
- **Eyes:** Bright, pupils focused, moment of clarity—tears possible (emotional release)
- **Mouth:** Peaceful smile, open (emotion breaking through)
- **Posture:** Relaxed shoulders, body open, hand on Ziggy (connected)
- **Context:** Moment when Mom realizes her own ADHD (symptom recognition in Ziggy mirrors her own experience); moment when she stops trying to "fix" Ziggy and starts seeing Ziggy as whole; internal validation ("I wasn't broken, I was just wired differently")
- **Gesture:** Hand on heart (internal recognition), embrace gesture, peaceful tears

### Sarah Chen Construction Notes
- **Line weight:** 2.5px outlines, 1.5px details
- **Emotional complexity:** Unlike Mrs. H. (grounded professional) or Jake (uncomplicated joy), Sarah carries visible emotional weight. Fatigue, love, frustration, and hope are all present simultaneously.
- **Color consistency:** Lavender blouse (#D8B5E6) is her primary color and signals her emotional/internal world (contrast to professional Mrs. H.'s Sage Green).
- **Movement:** Capable and energetic (not slow or tired), but with underlying tension. Animation shows a parent who's doing her best while carrying invisible weight.
- **Genetic connection:** Hair color matches Ziggy (#2B1B15). Skin tone matches Ziggy. Visible family resemblance (same East Asian features) grounds the story in biological/neurological inheritance (ADHD may run in families).

## Supporting Character: Sage (Imagined Figure / Fantasy Mentor)

### Note: Sage is an imagined/fantasy character that appears in Ziggy's internal world and dreams. Design is intentionally dreamlike and ethereal.

### Base Form (Imagined)

#### Head
- **Shape:** Oval (elder proportions, wise features)
- **Hair:** Long, silver-gray (#C0C0C0), flowing—not bound, free-moving. Represents generational wisdom and gentle authority.
- **Eyes:** Calm, knowing; pupils are sage green (#2D8A3D—color connects to Mrs. H.'s grounding presence but warmer/more introspective)
- **Mouth:** Knowing smile, peaceful
- **Skin:** Light tan (#E0D0C8), warm

#### Body
- **Shape:** Rectangle (tall, graceful adult, slight curve suggesting elder wisdom)
- **Clothing:** Flowing, soft fabrics—a cardigan or robe in Sage Green (#2D8A3D). No structured professional clothing; instead, comfort and timelessness.
- **Build:** Graceful, present, grounded

#### Expression (Single expression—Sage is consistent)
- **Eyes:** Warm, understanding, patient
- **Mouth:** Gentle smile
- **Posture:** Open, present, seated (Sage often appears in dream/imagined scenes sitting, listening)

### Sage Construction Notes
- **Dreamlike quality:** Sage has slightly softer outlines than reality characters (less sharp 2.5px stroke, more 2px + slight blur). This signals "imagined" vs. "real."
- **Limited expressions:** Sage has one primary expression (peaceful understanding). This consistency reinforces Sage as archetype, not realistic character.
- **Color:** Primary color is Sage Green (#2D8A3D), reinforcing the connection to grounding (both Mrs. H. and Sage are anchors, but in different ways).
- **Animation approach:** Sage moves slowly, deliberately, with fluidity. No restless fidgeting. Motion is calming.

## Representation & Inclusion Notes

### Racial/Ethnic Representation
- **Ziggy & Sarah:** East Asian (Chinese surname Chen, warm tan skin #E8D4B8, dark hair #2B1B15). Visible cultural representation in main protagonist role and family.
- **Jake:** Non-white representation (warm medium skin tone #D4956E, warm brown hair). Exact cultural identity to be finalized with sensitivity panel consultation.
- **Mrs. Henderson:** White representation (light tan skin #E0D0C8, graying brown hair #5D4E4B).
- **Sage:** Ethnically ambiguous elder (warm light tan, designed to be universally approachable; specific cultural heritage deferred to production/consultation).

### Body Diversity
- **Ziggy:** Athletic, lean (thin-frame ADHD representation, not caricatured)
- **Jake:** Athletic, coordinated (athletic peer, not "jock" stereotype; same age as Ziggy)
- **Mrs. Henderson:** Average adult build (not exaggerated; shows realistic teacher body)
- **Sarah:** Petite, energetic (smaller stature than average; not infantilized, but different body type visible)

### Gender Presentation
- **Ziggy:** Boy, clear masculine presentation (he/him); not questioning or non-binary in pilot (could be explored in later seasons)
- **Mrs. Henderson:** Woman, professional-casual (she/her); not sexualized or infantilized
- **Jake:** Boy, uncomplicated masculine presentation (he/him)
- **Sarah:** Woman, modern professional-casual (she/her); working parent, not defined solely by motherhood

### Neurodiversity / ADHD Representation
- **Ziggy:** Mixed-presentation ADHD (restlessness, reading difficulty, hyperfocus capability, emotional intensity, peer struggle)
- **Sarah:** Adult ADHD (implied/suggested; self-discovery in pilot/early episodes; validates intergenerational experience)
- **Mrs. Henderson:** Neurotypical teacher (no ADHD; represents system that can support or obstruct; not painted as villain, but learning)
- **Jake:** Neurotypical peer (uncomplicated, grounded; shows contrast without judgment)

### Accessibility
- **Visual clarity:** All characters readable at small animation scales (24px minimum) via bold outlines, distinct silhouettes, color differentiation
- **No color-only cues:** Characters differ by shape, hair, clothing, posture—not just color (supports colorblind viewers)
- **Emotional expression via posture + motion:** Doesn't rely on micro-facial features alone (supports lower-resolution playback, accessibility)

## Design Consistency & Replicability (for Outsourced Animation Teams)

### Locked Specifications
- **All hex codes:** Non-negotiable. Animators do NOT adjust saturation, brightness, or substitute similar colors.
- **Character proportions:** Scale locked via constructor notes (Ziggy head-to-body ratio 1:1.5, adult heads 1:2). Communicates via grid or pixel reference.
- **Line weights:** 2.5px main outlines, 1.5px details. Enforced in production files (layer naming conventions, style definitions).
- **Expression counts:** Minimum 3 expressions per character (neutral, emotional, positive). No improvisation.
- **Hair direction & asymmetry:** Ziggy's asymmetry is intentional, not error. Lock in design documentation and production files.

### Variable Specifications (Allowed Across Episodes)
- **Character poses:** Posture can vary scene-to-scene (sitting, standing, running, lying down)
- **Clothing swap:** Character outfits can change for story (e.g., Ziggy in sports uniform for soccer scene)
- **Background complexity:** Detail level can vary (full classroom vs. close-up dialogue), but character model consistency is fixed
- **Expression combinations:** Can blend expressions (75% shame + 25% determination), but base expressions remain consistent

### Production File Template Recommendations
For animators working in SVG / Illustrator / Procreate:
- **Organize by character:** Folder per character (Ziggy/, Mrs. H./, Jake/, Sarah/, Sage/)
- **Organize by component:** Head/, Body/, Limbs/, Accessories/, Effects/ (for modular reuse)
- **Color swatch file:** Include .ase (Illustrator) or Figma color library with all palette colors + character primaries
- **Expression library:** Separate file per character with 3+ expression variations, labeled clearly (ziggy-neutral.svg, ziggy-shame.svg, ziggy-joy.svg)
- **Animation notes:** Text file per character with fidget loops, movement guidelines, eye-dart patterns
