1. **Mom Sarah's dialogue in the car (Act 1, Line 241–246) reads slightly punitive.**

   - Revision: "I just see it. Like when I'm playing soccer—I see where everyone's gonna be before they move."

4. **Ensure voice direction for Ziggy avoids "fast and scattered" caricature in recording.**

   - Issue: If delivery sounds manic or stereotypically ADHD, it could feel patronizing.
   - Revision: Direct voice actor to play Ziggy as intelligent and present, with energy/speed arising from interest, not constant chaos. Fast thinking ≠ fast speech always.
   - Why: Prevents reduction to stereotype. Maintains authenticity.

5. **Consider small visual nod to Mom's ADHD in animation (optional enhancement).**
   - Current: Her hyperfocus is shown but not visually distinctive.
   - Issue: Missed opportunity to ground intergenerational theme visually.
   - Revision: In kitchen scene (Act 1), show Sarah's laptop surrounded by design sketches, open tabs, half-full coffee cups—visual language of hyperfocus state.
   - Why: Subtle signal to parents watching that might recognize themselves. No dialogue needed; visual metaphor.
