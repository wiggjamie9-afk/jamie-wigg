# Requirements: ADHD Superhero Animation

## Problem

ADHD representation in animation is nearly absent. Kids and teens with ADHD—and adults who grew up feeling "broken" in classrooms—see themselves portrayed as problems, not protagonists. No mainstream animated content validates their experience as both genuinely difficult AND as the foundation of unique strengths. Schools lack engaging, ADHD-affirmative educational content.

## Goal

Develop a full animated series pilot that authentically depicts ADHD struggles in a real classroom, then explodes into an empowering superhero fantasy. The pilot validates the core concept and establishes tone for 5–10 seasons. First deliverable: production-ready pilot script and character design system.

## Functional requirements

- **R1**: Pilot script (8–12 minutes) with three-act structure: classroom authenticity → moment of shame/overwhelm → superhero transformation sequence
- **R2**: Protagonist character profile (age, personality, specific ADHD manifestations, motivations, growth arc across series)
- **R3**: Supporting cast (teacher, peer, family member, fantasy mentor figure) with distinct voices and stakes
- **R4**: Series bible (5–10 episode arcs, overarching narrative, theme per season, how each episode explores a different ADHD strength)
- **R5**: Flat 2D animation style guide (color palette, character model sheets, motion language, reference boards)
- **R6**: Storyboard framework for pilot opening (classroom scenes with authentic sensory/attention details; transformation sequence)
- **R7**: Series positioning document (target audience, monetization angles, sensitivity/representation notes)

## Non-functional requirements

- **N1**: Sensitivity: Script reviewed by ADHD adults + educators before animation to ensure representation is affirming, not patronizing
- **N2**: Accessibility: Script includes closed-caption cues; character design includes diverse representation (race, body type, gender presentation)
- **N3**: YouTube optimization: Pacing designed to hold attention through mid-roll; hook in first 10 seconds; avg. 12–15 min per episode
- **N4**: Replicability: Style guide enables consistent production across 50+ episodes and multiple animation teams

## Out of scope

- Animation of the pilot episode (that comes after this spec is approved)
- Full production budget breakdown (scope for separate finance/greenlight doc)
- Live-action sequences or rotoscope (flat 2D only)
- Mobile/TikTok cuts (YouTube long-form only for pilot)
- Interactive elements or gamification (linear story focus)

## Open questions

- Target age range: 6–8, 8–12, or 12–16? (Affects complexity, humor, language; recommend 8–12 as entry point, scales both younger and older)
- Episode frequency: Weekly, bi-weekly, or batch? (Affects episodic vs. serialized structure)
- Character's specific ADHD manifestations: hyperactivity, inattention, emotional dysregulation, or mixed? (Recommend mixed + one primary to avoid stereotype)
