# Requirements: ADHD Superhero Animation

## Problem

ADHD representation in animation is nearly absent. Kids and teens with ADHD—and adults who grew up feeling "broken" in classrooms—see themselves portrayed as problems, not protagonists. No mainstream animated content validates their experience as both genuinely difficult AND as the foundation of unique strengths. Schools lack engaging, ADHD-affirmative educational content.

## Goal

Develop a full animated series pilot that authentically depicts ADHD struggles in a real classroom, then explodes into an empowering superhero fantasy. The pilot validates the core concept and establishes tone for 5–10 seasons. First deliverable: production-ready pilot script and character design system.

## Functional requirements

- **R1**: Pilot script (12–15 minutes, production screenplay format) with five-act structure: Sunday freedom → Monday dread → classroom sensory overload + reading struggle → shame moment → superhero transformation → snap back → micro resolution
- **R2**: Protagonist character profile (age 10, name, ADHD manifestations, motivation, series arc) including voice/casting direction (accent, speech pattern, energy), but not visual sketches
- **R3**: Single supporting cast (4 specific characters: teacher, peer, family member, fantasy mentor) with distinct voices, personal stakes, and non-villainous motivation
- **R4**: Series bible (season-level structure for 5–10 seasons, overarching narrative arc, one ADHD strength per season; episode-level outlines deferred to series production)
- **R5**: Flat 2D animation style guide (color palette, character model sheets, motion mood boards + reference films/GIFs; design-team-focused, not parametric animation specs)
- **R6**: Storyboard framework for pilot opening + transformation sequence (shot list with timing, visual notes, camera language; not full sketch frames)
- **R7**: Series positioning document (target audience age range + demo, monetization strategy, representation/sensitivity framework with sign-off process)
- **R8**: School/cultural context definition (US public K–8, specific location, classroom composition, staff types)
- **R9**: Approval authority and remediation process (lead ADHD consultant sign-off; 2-week revision loop for failed sensitivity review before greenlight)
- **R10**: Objective acceptance criteria for authenticity (sensitivity panel rates script on 1–5 scale for classroom truthfulness, voice distinctness, ADHD representation; ≥4/5 avg required)

## Non-functional requirements

- **N1**: Sensitivity: Script reviewed by ADHD adults + educators before animation to ensure representation is affirming, not patronizing
- **N2**: Accessibility: Script includes closed-caption cues; character design includes diverse representation (race, body type, gender presentation)
- **N3**: YouTube optimization: Target 8–10 min per episode (sweet spot for story structure + YouTube mid-roll monetization). Hard ceiling at 12 min max
- **N4**: Replicability: Style guide enables consistent production across 50+ episodes and multiple animation teams

## Out of scope

- Animation of the pilot episode (that comes after this spec is approved)
- Full production budget breakdown (scope for separate finance/greenlight doc)
- Live-action sequences or rotoscope (flat 2D only)
- Mobile/TikTok cuts (YouTube long-form only for pilot)
- Interactive elements or gamification (linear story focus)

## Open questions

- Episode upload frequency: Weekly, bi-weekly, or batch? (Affects episodic vs. serialized structure; recommend 1–2 week cadence for sustainable production)
- Series scope beyond pilot: 6-episode season 1 → greenlight for season 2, or pilot + full-series commitment? (Affects series bible depth and production roadmap)
