# Storyboard: Opening Sequence (Classroom Reading Struggle)

**Duration:** 4:30 (3:00–7:30 in pilot timeline)  
**Key frames:** 15 shots  
**Motion archetype:** Primarily JITTERY (overstimulation escalates); occasional micro-HYPERFOCUS (failed attempts); culminates in SHARP shame moment  
**Color palette:** Fluorescent cool (desaturated blues, grays, institutional cream); burgundy tint as shame intensifies  
**Sound design:** Layered sensory overload (hum + click + tap + ambient murmur, progressively louder)

---

## Shot 1: Classroom Establishing (Wide Interior)

**Duration:** 0:15  
**Timing code in pilot:** 3:00–3:15  
**Camera:** Wide shot, fixed angle (slight overhead perspective, 30° down angle)  
**Visual:** Interior classroom: two rows of eight desks arranged in neat lines. Teacher desk at front. Fluorescent ceiling lights (four panels visible, shimmer effect applied). Ceiling fan rotating in corner, click sound synchronized to each rotation. Window along one wall shows playground (bright, green, slightly out of focus to emphasize interior). Wall schedule in large letters: "ATTENDANCE / MATH / READING / RECESS / WRITING / LUNCH." Clock on wall reads 9:05.

**Motion archetype:** None (establishing, grounded, still)  
**Color grading:** Desaturated, cool blue-gray cast. Lights have slight shimmer artifact (horizontal lines, 60Hz flicker effect, subtle).  
**Audio cues:** 
- Fluorescent hum enters (50Hz tone, ~70dB, omnidirectional)
- Ceiling fan click (synchronous with rotation, ~65dB, every 2 rotations)
- Ambient classroom murmur (low, distant, ~55dB—kids settling)

**Visual effects:** Fluorescent shimmer (motion blur on lights, emphasizes their constant vibration); fan rotation slightly stuttered (not smooth, realistic bearing wear); slight sepia tone to desaturate the scene.

**Interior monologue:** None (establishing)  
**Purpose:** Sets sensory overload tone immediately. Audience experiences the classroom FROM Ziggy's POV—every sound is present, every visual detail sharp. Not chaotic yet, but DENSE.

## Animator Notes: Critical Blocking

### Motion Consistency Rules
1. **Jittery state:** Must have 80–100ms micro-oscillation on shoulders + fingers + leg bounce. Layer multiple jiiter loops at slightly different frequencies (80ms + 100ms = poly-rhythmic tension).
2. **Hyperfocus attempt:** ALL easing must be smooth (ease-in-out, 400–800ms timing). Remove all jitter. Lock pupils. Reduce blink rate.
3. **Shame moment:** Eyes downcast or squeezed, shoulders hunched, body curves inward. No jitter—shame is a FREEZE, not a vibration.
4. **Time distortion:** Clock moves in REAL TIME (no acceleration). This makes the wait feel eternal.

### Color Grading Notes
- **Opening shots 1–3:** Cool blue-gray, desaturated (institutional feel)
- **Shot 4 (sensory montage):** Saturation drops 20% as pressure builds
- **Shots 5–8:** Slow increase in burgundy tint (0% → 15% → 25%)
- **Shots 9–13:** Burgundy escalates (25% → 40%), vignette darkens
- **Shot 15 (shame peak):** Burgundy 40%, vignette dark, sickly institutional color cast

### Audio Sync Notes
- Hum + click layer continuously (no gaps). Hum is constant, click every 1.5 seconds (fan rotation).
- Mrs. Henderson's dialogue syncs to lip movements (100ms lead).
- Interior monologue appears 200ms after thought, lasts 1.5–2 seconds per line.
- Sound stinger (anxiety theme) begins at Shot 14, sustains through Shot 15.

### Key Moments for Animator Review
- **Shot 3:** Pencil spin must be visible + continuous (CRITICAL: this is Ziggy's first nervous habit, sets tone for escalation)
- **Shot 6:** Interior monologue VISIBILITY—text overlay must not obscure reading lips (text appears BELOW mouth, left-aligned)
- **Shot 11:** Eye dart speed must feel FAST (anxiety) vs. Shot 12 smooth tracking (hyperfocus). Clear contrast.
- **Shot 13:** Sock bounce amplitude must increase visibly (0.5px → 1.5px). This is bodily anxiety escalation.
- **Shot 15:** Jitter STOP must be abrupt (50ms snap, no easing). This is the CRITICAL TRANSITION from dysregulation to dissociation.
