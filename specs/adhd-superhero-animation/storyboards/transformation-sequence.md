# Storyboard: Transformation Sequence (Daydream + Surge + Snap Back)

**Series:** SURGE  
**Episode:** "The Longest Monday" (Pilot)  
**Sequence:** Act 3 — Superhero Fantasy & Transformation  
**Duration:** 4:00 (8:00–12:00 in pilot)  
**Key frames:** 16 shots mapped  
**Motion archetype:** Sharp (transition), Floaty (fantasy world), Hyperfocus (Surge in action), Sharp (snap back)  
**Color palette:** Electric Blue, Neon Yellow, Warm Orange, with gradual shift from cool → warm

**Animation notes:** Pixelation effect should feel organic (not digital artifact, but visual dissolution), color bleeding should feel warm + safe (not harsh), music transition gradual (no silence between classroom sound and daydream music)

## Shot 6: Surge's Power Montage (3 Quick Frames)
**Timecode:** 10:45–11:45 (duration 1:00, split into 3 × 0:20)  
**Camera angle:** Varied angles (wide, close-up, POV)  

### Frame A — Hyperfocus Moment (10:45–11:05, 0:20)
**Visual:** Surge's eyes lock on distant shape (city, person, problem — unclear, suggesting future use). Pupils dilate, focus-locked (Hyperfocus motion: smooth, tracking). Everything else blurs (depth of field isolates focus target).  
**Motion:** Eyes smooth tracking (ease-in-out, locked attention), body becomes statue-still (no fidgeting, pure focus), pupils dilate 15% (accommodation shift)  
**Sound design:** Sudden clarity tone (high-pitched sustained note), background ambient drops (silence around the focused moment)  
**Interior monologue:** "I can see it. I can see the whole picture."  
**Motion archetype:** Hyperfocus (smooth, controlled, precise)  
**Purpose:** Hyperfocus manifests as *clarity* and *pattern recognition*. Ziggy's racing mind, when channeled, sees solutions others miss.

### Frame B — Speed/Pattern-Reading Moment (11:05–11:25, 0:20)
**Visual:** Surge moves across the space with Electric Blue speed lines trailing (fast, fluid). Rapid movement, but not violent — graceful, kinetic. Electrical particles trail in wake (motion blur effect).  
**Motion:** Sharp start (50ms instant acceleration), then Hyperfocus smooth glide (400–600ms), electric particles trail (Floaty effect in wake)  
**Sound design:** Whoosh (air resistance sound), then rhythmic pulse (heartbeat-like, fast, 120bpm)  
**Interior monologue:** "I can move. I can think. I can *do*."  
**Motion archetype:** Sharp (start) → Hyperfocus smooth glide + Floaty particle wake  
**Purpose:** Speed is *power*. Ziggy's ADHD restlessness, when channeled, becomes efficient kinetic action. His body can *move* when his mind aligns with action.

### Frame C — Emotional Empathy Moment (11:25–11:45, 0:20)
**Visual:** Surge stands still. Around him, abstract shapes (other people? emotions?) glow softly. Surge's aura expands, envelops them (connection, empathy). Surge's body calm, centered. Warm orange light spreads.  
**Motion:** Floaty (aura spreads slowly, no aggression, 1.5-second ease-in), Surge body perfectly still (centered, meditative), surrounding shapes glow brighter (responding to Surge's presence)  
**Sound design:** Warm orchestral chord (gentle, resolved), ambient pad holds (safe, held space)  
**Interior monologue:** "I understand. I feel. That's not a weakness."  
**Motion archetype:** Floaty (gentle, weightless, safe, expanding)  
**Purpose:** Emotional intensity is *strength*. Ziggy's ADHD means he feels deeply — which manifests as empathy, intuition, understanding others' pain. His nervous system is wired for connection.

## Snap-Back Timing & Fantasy Duration

**Key narrative point:** The entire Surge fantasy (Shots 1–9) occurs in approximately **5 seconds of classroom time**. 

- **Classroom time:** Ziggy is called to read at 6:21 in the pilot. He returns from the daydream at 12:20. *Clock time elapsed: ~0 (instantaneous to observers)*
- **Subjective time (Ziggy's experience):** Fantasy sequences last 4:00, feeling like an eternity of empowerment
- **Narrative function:** This time dilation mirrors ADHD temporal perception — subjective time and objective time don't match

This maintains the realism: the daydream is *truly internal*, not a magical freeze of time. Mrs. Henderson experiences 5 seconds of Ziggy being silent. Ziggy experiences 4 minutes of transformation.

- [ ] **Floaty motion:** Cubic ease-in arcs, 1.5–3 second timings, particles drifting, no gravity, smooth curves
- [ ] **Hyperfocus moments:** Smooth tracking, pupils dilated/locked, no jitter, 400–800ms easing
- [ ] **Sharp transitions:** 50ms onset, instant without easing, synchronized to sound design
- [ ] **Color progression:** Cool blue (classroom) → Electric Blue + Neon Yellow + Warm Orange (fantasy) → back to cool (classroom)
- [ ] **Particle effects:** Accumulate during transformation (Shot 4), orbit during triumph (Shot 7), dissipate during return (Shot 9)
- [ ] **Interior monologue sync:** Text appears 200ms after thought, key emotional moments (Shots 6, 7, 12, 16)
- [ ] **Facial expressions:** Eyes dilate (Shots 4–5), Eyes lock (Shot 6a), Body calm (Shots 6c, 7), Eyes brighten (Shot 16)
- [ ] **Sound design:** Ethereal pad continues through Shots 2–7, abrupt bell interrupt (Shot 8), reverse warping (Shot 9), return to classroom ambient Shots 10–11
- [ ] **Time perception:** Fantasy lasts 4:00 subjectively but ~5 seconds objectively (classroom time doesn't advance visibly)
- [ ] **Glow effects:** Electric Blue outline (Shot 4), Neon Yellow eyes + aura (Shot 5), particles trail (Shots 6), all fade by Shot 10
- [ ] **Mrs. Henderson's presence:** Remains static during fantasy (invisible to Ziggy), reappears identical in Shot 11 (no time passage for her)

## Series Continuity Notes

Transformation sequence establishes:
- **Surge is internalized:** Not external magic, but Ziggy's own neurology made visible/empowering
- **Three core ADHD-as-powers:** Hyperfocus/pattern-recognition, speed/kinetic action, emotional intensity/empathy
- **Fantasy-to-reality flow:** The fantasy is *internal recognition*, not external rescue
- **Mrs. Henderson as ally:** She's beginning to *see* Ziggy's actual strengths, not just his struggles
- **Realism of classrooms:** The classroom doesn't change, but Ziggy's relationship to his ADHD does

This foundation supports all subsequent episodes' internal fantasy sequences and establishes that *understanding oneself* is the superpower, not external magical abilities.
