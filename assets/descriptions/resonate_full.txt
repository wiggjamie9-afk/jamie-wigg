RESONATE - Generative Music Synced to Your Heartbeat

Value Proposition
RESONATE creates a unique generative drone that listens to your heart and adapts in real-time. A closed-loop biofeedback instrument for deep meditation, healing, and nervous system regulation.

Key Features
• Heart Rate Sensing: Optical heart rate monitor integration (via device camera or Bluetooth)
• Generative Drone: AI-powered continuous tone that shifts with your heart rate
• Biofeedback Loop: Real-time HRV coherence feedback displayed visually
• Offline Audio: Sound synthesis happens entirely on your device
• Session Recording: Optional logging of heart rate during play (stored locally only)
• Haptic Sync: Vibration pulses sync to detected heart rate
• No Subscriptions: Free to install and use forever
• No Tracking: Complete privacy—no data leaves your phone

Who It's For
Meditation practitioners, holistic health professionals, biohackers, and anyone exploring heart-rate variability and nervous system optimisation.

Pricing
Free. No in-app purchases.

Privacy Statement
RESONATE requests camera permission only for optional heart rate sensing via optical detection. No video is recorded, transmitted, or stored. Optional HRV session data is stored locally in IndexedDB. We do not track user identity, location, or usage patterns. All audio synthesis and heart rate analysis happens offline on your device.

Accessibility
• High-contrast visual HRV display
• Audio cues for heart rate changes
• Haptic feedback for deaf/HoH users
• Adjustable sensitivity for optical heart rate
• Dark mode for low-light meditation
• Screen reader compatible navigation

Technical Details
• Web Audio API for generative synthesis (FM, subtractive)
• OpenCV.js for optical heart rate detection
• Bluetooth API for external heart rate monitors (chest straps, smartwatches)
• IndexedDB for local session logging
• Compatible with: iOS 14.5+ (camera required), Android 7+
• Storage: ~15 MB including optional ML model for HRV
• Estimated battery drain: 5–8% per 20-minute session (due to camera/processing)

Installation
1. Visit rhythmixapp.com.au/apps/resonate
2. Add to home screen
3. Grant camera permission (for heart rate sensing) or skip to use audio only
4. Sit still for 20 seconds while the app detects your baseline heart rate
5. Press play to begin generative session

Disclaimer
RESONATE is a wellness tool, not a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Do not use RESONATE if you have a pacemaker or heart arrhythmia without consulting your doctor first. Optical heart rate detection may be less accurate on darker skin tones; we are actively improving this. Always consult a healthcare provider for medical concerns.
