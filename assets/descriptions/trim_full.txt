TRIM - Subscription Cancellation and Renewal Tracker

Value Proposition
Stop paying for subscriptions you forgot about. TRIM tracks renewals, sends reminders, calculates yearly waste, and helps you cancel with direct company links. One-time $3.99.

Key Features
• Subscription Tracker: Log all recurring charges (streaming, apps, gym, insurance)
• Renewal Reminders: 7-day and 24-hour alerts before charges
• Yearly Waste Calculator: Total spent on subscriptions this year with category breakdown
• Cancel Direct Links: One-tap links to each company's cancellation portal
• Price History: Track if subscription prices increase over time
• Frequency Options: Monthly, quarterly, annual, or custom intervals
• Monthly Snapshot: Calendar view showing renewal dates
• Pause/Resume: Temporarily pause subscriptions without cancelling
• Local Export: CSV export of all subscriptions and spending
• Offline Ready: All data stored locally, no sync required

Who It's For
Budget-conscious consumers, people managing multiple subscriptions, digital minimalists, and anyone seeking to reduce recurring charges.

Pricing
One-time purchase $3.99. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads.

Privacy Statement
TRIM stores all subscription data locally in IndexedDB only. No data is transmitted to cloud servers or shared with third parties. Company cancellation links are public URLs; clicking them redirects to official company sites (not phishing). Your subscription list and spending data remain completely private.

Installation
1. Visit rhythmixapp.com.au/apps/trim/
2. Purchase one-time $3.99 licence
3. Add subscription (name, company, amount, renewal date, category)
4. Receive 7-day and 24-hour renewal reminders
5. Click "Cancel" link and follow company's cancellation steps
6. Mark subscription as cancelled in TRIM

Common Cancellation Challenges & Solutions
- Password needed: TRIM stores company login pages; click "Forgotten password"
- No direct cancel: TRIM flags subscriptions requiring email/phone cancellation
- Trial-to-paid conversion: TRIM alerts before trial end to prevent surprise charges
- Refund eligibility: TRIM tracks days remaining in billing cycle
