Reset - Recovery Tracking for Team Sport Athletes

Value Proposition
Daily recovery metrics for team sport athletes: HRV readiness score, sleep quality analysis, training load tracking, and recovery window prediction. Built for rugby, soccer, and contact sports.

Key Features
• HRV Readiness Score: Daily readiness rating based on morning heart rate variability
• Sleep Quality Logger: Duration, quality rating, and sleep debt calculation
• Training Load Tracker: Subjective session RPE and training impact
• Recovery Window: Predicted recovery time to peak readiness
• Trend Analysis: Weekly and monthly readiness trends
• Team Reporting: Share aggregated team readiness dashboard with coaching staff
• Offline First: All calculations on-device; optional cloud sync
• No Wearable Required: Works with manual logging or optional device sync
• Coach Insights: Export reports for injury prevention strategies

Who It's For
Team sport athletes (rugby, soccer, American football, hockey), coaches, and sports medicine professionals optimising recovery and training load balance.

Pricing
Free to install. Optional $2.99/month team premium for coach dashboards and 12-month trend analytics.

Privacy Statement
Reset stores all athlete data locally first. Team coaches can only access aggregated, anonymised readiness scores (not individual HRV data). Optional Bluetooth sync with wearables (Oura, Whoop, Apple Watch) happens device-to-device with optional cloud backup (encrypted). We do not sell, trade, or share athlete health data with third parties.

Accessibility
• Clear readiness colour coding (green/yellow/red)
• Haptic feedback for daily check-in prompts
• Screen reader compatible
• High-contrast display mode
• Large touch targets for quick logging

Technical Details
• Local IndexedDB for all athlete records
• HRV calculation: RMSSD (Root Mean Square of Successive Differences)
• Sleep debt algorithm: cumulative hours below 7-hour target
• Training load: Session RPE (0–10) × duration in minutes
• Compatible with: iOS 13+, Android 9+
• Optional sync with: Oura Ring API, Whoop, Apple HealthKit, Google Fit
• Storage: ~10 MB for 1-year 20-athlete team database

Installation
1. Visit rhythmixapp.com.au/recovery/
2. Add to home screen
3. Enter team name and athlete roster
4. Each athlete grants optional wearable permission (Oura, Whoop, Apple Health)
5. Log morning HRV and evening sleep rating daily
6. Coaches view team readiness dashboard
