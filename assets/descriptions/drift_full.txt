DRIFT - AI Sleep Debt Calculator and Bedtime Coach

Value Proposition
DRIFT calculates your cumulative sleep debt, predicts optimal bedtime, and coaches you toward 7–9 hours nightly with an adaptive wind-down routine. $5.99/month.

Key Features
• Sleep Debt Calculator: Tracks cumulative hours below your target (default 7h), alerts when critical
• Adaptive Bedtime AI: Recommends bedtime based on wake time and sleep need
• Sleep Score Algorithm: Rates sleep quality on latency, deep sleep, REM, interruptions
• Wind-Down Ambient Timer: Guided evening routine (21:00 start, customisable)
• Smart Notifications: Gentle reminders 60/30/10 min before optimal bedtime
• Sleep Journal: Log nightly sleep (duration, quality, mood, caffeine, exercise)
• Caffeine Tracker: Logs coffee/tea intake; calculates remaining caffeine at bedtime
• Sleep Debt Trend: Graph of cumulative sleep debt vs. target over 30 days
• Weekly Report: Email summary of sleep metrics and recommendations
• Offline Audio: Ambient sounds (rain, rain + thunder, night birds, ocean)

Who It's For
Shift workers, insomniacs, busy professionals, and anyone seeking to optimise sleep and recover from chronic sleep debt.

Sleep Debt Calculation
Sleep Debt = (Today's sleep - Target sleep) + Previous debt
Example: 5h sleep on 7h target = -2h + previous debt
Alert threshold: Cumulative debt > 10 hours triggers "recovery needed" status

Sleep Quality Scoring
- Latency: Time to fall asleep (ideal < 15 min)
- Deep Sleep: % of total sleep (ideal 10–15%)
- REM Sleep: % of total sleep (ideal 20–25%)
- Interruptions: Awakenings during night (ideal < 2)
Quality score: Weighted average of above factors (0–100)
