RHYTHMIX LIVE - AI Music Video Generator

Value Proposition
Create professional-grade AI music videos in seconds. Drop a track, select visual engines, and get a beat-synced video ready to export—all without leaving your browser.

Key Features
• One-Click Render: Select your track, pick a visual style, render to MP4 in seconds
• Beat Synchronisation: AI analyzes BPM and key to lock visuals to music
• Multiple Engines: FLUX 1.1 Pro, HunyuanVideo, and proprietary motion-sync models
• Portrait & Landscape: Render 9:16 (TikTok), 16:9 (YouTube), or 1:1 (Instagram)
• Real-Time Preview: See frame-by-frame before final render
• Batch Export: Queue multiple videos for overnight rendering
• Powered by Replicate: Access to cutting-edge generative models
• Your Token, Your Cost: Bring your own Replicate API token (free tier available)

Who It's For
Musicians, producers, content creators, music promoters, and social media managers seeking fast music video creation without subscription overhead.

Pricing
Free to use with your Replicate token. Costs depend on your Replicate account (free tier: 5 generations/hour; paid: pay-per-use, typically $0.02–$0.10 per video).

Privacy Statement
RHYTHMIX LIVE does not store your audio files, video outputs, or authentication tokens on our servers. Your Replicate token is used only for API calls; we do not log it. Rendered videos are stored in your browser cache and exported locally. No tracking, no telemetry beyond error logs (anonymised).
