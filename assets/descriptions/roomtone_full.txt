Roomtone - Real-Time Hearing Aid EQ Tuner

Value Proposition
Roomtone analyses your room's acoustic environment and suggests hearing aid EQ adjustments in real-time. Better hearing in any space—from quiet cafes to noisy conference rooms.

Key Features
• Real-Time EQ Analysis: Detect room acoustics in 3–5 seconds
• Hearing Aid Protocol: Frequency analysis matched to standard hearing aid bands
• EQ Recommendations: Suggested adjustments for your specific device (Oticon, Signia, Phonak, ReSound, Widex)
• No Calibration Needed: Works with any hearing aid; no special setup required
• Offline Processing: Audio analysis stays on your device
• Session History: Optional log of acoustic environments (stored locally)
• Accessibility First: Designed with deaf/HoH users and audiologists in mind
• No Account Required: Completely anonymous

Who It's For
Hearing aid users, audiologists, cochlear implant users, and people seeking to optimise their hearing experience across different environments.

Pricing
Free to install. No subscriptions. Optional $4.99 one-time purchase unlocks advanced analytics and cloud sync across devices.

Privacy Statement
Roomtone captures 10–30 seconds of ambient sound from your microphone to perform acoustic analysis. This audio is processed entirely on your device using local Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) algorithms. The raw audio is not stored, transmitted, or logged remotely. We do not collect microphone data, location, or user identity. Optional session data (EQ adjustments) is stored in local IndexedDB only.

Accessibility
• Designed for deaf and hard-of-hearing users first
• High-contrast interface with large touch targets
• Haptic feedback for notifications
• Text and visual indicators for audio levels
• Compatible with screen readers (iOS VoiceOver, Android TalkBack)
• Adjustable text size
• Captions for all in-app audio cues

Technical Details
• Web Audio API for FFT frequency analysis
• Real-time signal processing using ScriptProcessorNode
• Compatible with: iOS 14.5+ (camera/mic), Android 8+
• Hearing aid frequency bands: 125 Hz, 250 Hz, 500 Hz, 1 kHz, 2 kHz, 4 kHz, 8 kHz, 16 kHz
• Storage: ~8 MB
• Battery impact: ~2–3% per 10-minute analysis session

Installation
1. Visit rhythmixapp.com.au/apps/roomtone
2. Add to home screen
3. Grant microphone permission
4. Enter your hearing aid model (optional for generic recommendations)
5. Tap "Analyse Room" and hold phone at arm's length for 10 seconds
6. Review EQ suggestions and adjust your hearing aid settings

Disclaimer
Roomtone provides EQ recommendations only. It is not a substitute for professional audiological care. Always consult your audiologist before making significant EQ adjustments. Hearing aid compatibility depends on your device's API access; not all hearing aids allow remote adjustment. Roomtone works best in relatively quiet environments (below 70 dB) for accurate analysis.
