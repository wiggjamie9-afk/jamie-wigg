PLUMB - Plumbing Job Estimator and Invoice Tool

Value Proposition
Job estimator for plumbing and HVAC work, material cost tracker, and invoice generation. Offline calculations. Built for trade professionals. One-time purchase.

Key Features
• Job Estimator: Calculate labour + materials for common plumbing tasks
• Material Cost Tracker: Log part costs, markups, supplier pricing
• Labour Rate Wizard: Set hourly rate; calculate time-based charges
• Common Tasks Library: Pipe replacement, fixture install, leak repair, etc.
• Invoice Generator: Professional invoices with your business branding
• Call-Out Fee: Add flat call-out charge to jobs
• GST/VAT: Automatic tax calculation (AU/UK regional settings)
• Job History: Archive completed jobs for reference
• CSV Export: Ready for accountant or job tracking
• Offline Ready: Works without internet; calculate anywhere

Who It's For
Plumbers, HVAC technicians, trade contractors, and service professionals managing job quotes and invoicing.

Pricing
One-time purchase (pricing TBD). No subscriptions.

Privacy Statement
PLUMB stores job estimates and cost data locally in IndexedDB. No business data, client info, or revenue figures are transmitted to cloud servers or shared with third parties. Complete privacy for trade professionals.

Installation & Usage
1. Visit rhythmixapp.com.au/apps/untapped/plumb
2. Purchase one-time licence
3. Set hourly labour rate and call-out fee
4. Select job type (e.g., "Tap replacement", "Burst pipe repair")
5. Log material costs (parts sourced)
6. PLUMB calculates: Labour time + materials + GST
7. Generate invoice (PDF, printable, or email)
8. Mark job as completed; archive for future reference

Job Categories & Examples
- Fixture Installation: Taps, toilets, showers, sinks
- Pipe Work: Copper, PVC, replacement, extensions
- Repairs: Leaks, blockages, burst pipes, dripping taps
- Maintenance: Descaling, cleaning, servicing
- System Upgrades: Hot water, heating, ventilation

Estimating Features
- Labour: Hours estimate × hourly rate
- Materials: Itemised parts with cost + markup %
- Call-out: Fixed flat fee (e.g., $75 + labour)
- Travel: Optional distance-based travel charge
- Tax: AU GST 10% or UK VAT 20% (auto-calculated)

Invoice Elements
- Business name, logo, phone, email
- Client name and address
- Job date and invoice number
- Itemised labour and materials
- Subtotal, tax, total due
- Payment terms and method
- Work warranty (optional, customisable)
