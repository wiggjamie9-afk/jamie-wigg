MACRO - Photo-First Nutrition and Macro Tracker

Value Proposition
Photo-first calorie and macro tracker with animated progress rings, 50-food database, water counter, and weekly trend analysis. One-time $4.99.

Key Features
• Photo-Based Logging: Take a photo of your meal; auto-estimate calories and macros
• Animated Rings: Apple Activity-style rings (Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fat)
• Quick Entry: Search 50-food database (eggs, chicken, rice, vegetables, etc.)
• Macro Breakdown: Real-time pie chart of protein/carbs/fat distribution
• Water Counter: Log glasses or ml per day with hydration goals
• Weekly Trends: Graph of average calories, macros, and weight trend (optional)
• Nutritional Goals: Set daily targets (calories, protein, carbs, fat)
• Meal Photos: Visual meal history—see what you ate and when
• Export Data: CSV of daily logs for nutritionist or trainer review
• Offline First: All processing local; no cloud required

Who It's For
Fitness enthusiasts, bodybuilders, people managing nutrition for athletic performance, and anyone seeking to understand their macro composition.

Pricing
One-time purchase $4.99. No subscriptions. No in-app purchases.

Privacy Statement
MACRO stores all meal photos, calorie/macro data, and progress locally in IndexedDB. No personal health data is transmitted to servers. Meal photos remain on your device only. Optional weight tracking is stored locally. We do not track identity, share data with fitness apps or health platforms, or use data for advertising.

Technical Details
• Canvas-based animated rings (HTML5 Canvas)
• IndexedDB for meal/photo/weight data
• Calorie estimation using portion size recognition (ML model local)
• 50-food nutritional database (USDA reference, local JSON)
• Compatible with: iOS 12+, Android 8+
• Storage: ~100 MB for 1-year meal photos (300+ photos)
• Macro calculation: Gram precision for protein/carbs/fat

Macro Targets & Recommendations
- Moderate (avg. 2000 cal): 50% carbs, 25% protein, 25% fat
- High protein (athlete): 40% carbs, 35% protein, 25% fat
- Low carb / keto: 5–10% carbs, 20–25% protein, 65–75% fat
- Custom: Set your own targets based on coach or nutritionist guidance

Weekly Report Includes
- Average daily calories and macros
- Consistency % (days hitting targets)
- Most frequently logged foods
- Macro distribution trends
- Weight trend (if tracked)
