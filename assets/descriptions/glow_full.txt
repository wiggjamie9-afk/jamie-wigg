GLOW - Skincare Routine and Ingredient Scanner

Value Proposition
GLOW simplifies skincare with an AM/PM routine builder, ingredient scanner that flags irritants and conflicts, and a skin journal to track results. One-time $3.99.

Key Features
• Routine Builder: Drag-and-drop AM and PM routine steps (cleanser → toner → serum → moisturiser)
• Ingredient Scanner: Photo or manual entry of product ingredients; flags irritants and conflicts
• Irritant Database: 200+ known skin irritants and allergens (updated quarterly)
• Conflict Detector: Warns if incompatible actives (e.g., retinol + vitamin C same night)
• Skin Type Matcher: Recommends products for dry, oily, combination, sensitive skin
• Skin Journal: Daily photos + notes on texture, redness, breakouts, dryness
• Routine Timer: Step-by-step timer (wait 60s between layers, etc.)
• Product Tracker: Expiration dates and cost-per-use calculation
• Before/After Gallery: Side-by-side comparison over weeks/months
• Offline Ready: All data stored locally; no cloud required

Who It's For
Skincare enthusiasts, people with sensitive skin or acne concerns, and anyone seeking to simplify routines and understand product compatibility.

Ingredient Categories & Conflict Examples
- Actives: Retinol, vitamin C, niacinamide, salicylic acid, glycolic acid
- Hydrators: Hyaluronic acid, ceramides, glycerin, squalane
- Oils: Argan, jojoba, rosehip, marula, squalane
- Irritants for sensitive skin: Fragrance, alcohol, essential oils, salicylates
- Common conflicts:
  - Retinol + Vitamin C (can oxidize vit C)
  - Retinol + AHA/BHA (can irritate)
  - Niacinamide + Vitamin C (pH incompatible, use different times)
  - Exfoliants on same night (over-exfoliation risk)
