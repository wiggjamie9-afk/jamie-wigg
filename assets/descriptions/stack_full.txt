STACK - Personal Finance Net-Worth Calculator

Value Proposition
Track assets, liabilities, and net-worth growth over time. Offline calculations, privacy-first design, and detailed financial snapshots. One-time purchase.

Key Features
• Asset Tracking: Cash, savings, investments, real estate, vehicles, valuables
• Liability Tracking: Mortgages, loans, credit card debt, HECS (AU), student loans
• Net-Worth Calculation: Assets minus liabilities = net worth snapshot
• Growth Tracking: Monthly/quarterly/annual net-worth trend graph
• Category Breakdown: See which assets/liabilities drive your net worth
• What-If Scenarios: Simulate debt payoff or investment growth
• Milestone Tracking: Set net-worth goals (e.g., $100k, $500k, $1M)
• CSV Export: Ready for accountant or financial advisor review
• Offline Ready: All calculations on-device; no cloud required
• Currency Support: AUD, USD, GBP, EUR, CAD, and more

Installation & Usage
1. Visit rhythmixapp.com.au/apps/untapped/stack
2. Purchase one-time licence
3. Add assets (savings, investments, property, vehicles, valuables)
4. Add liabilities (mortgage, loans, credit card, HECS)
5. STACK calculates: Total assets - total liabilities = net worth
6. Take monthly snapshot to build net-worth history
7. View trend graph and track progress toward goals
8. Optional: Export data for accountant review

Asset Categories
- Cash: Savings accounts, emergency fund, cash on hand
- Investments: Stocks, ETFs, bonds, crypto, managed funds
- Superannuation: Retirement savings (AU)
- Real Estate: Primary residence, investment properties, land
- Vehicles: Cars, motorcycles, RVs, boats
- Valuables: Jewellery, art, collectibles, tools
- Other: Loans owed to you, insurance policies

Liability Categories
- Mortgages: Primary residence, investment property
- Personal Loans: Bank loans, family loans
- Credit Card Debt: Outstanding credit card balances
- Student Loans: HECS (AU), Stafford (USA), other education debt
- Car Loans: Vehicle finance
- Other: Medical debt, business loans, lines of credit

Net-Worth Calculation Example
Assets:
- Savings: $50,000
- Investments: $75,000
- House: $500,000
- Car: $25,000
Total Assets: $650,000

Liabilities:
- Mortgage: $300,000
- Car Loan: $10,000
Total Liabilities: $310,000

Net Worth: $650,000 - $310,000 = $340,000
