HUM - Daily Humming Practice with Coherence Breath

Value Proposition
HUM teaches the science of coherence breathing paired with healing frequencies. A 5-minute daily practice that synchronises breath, tone, and nervous system for measurable calm and focus.

Key Features
• Coherence Breath Protocol: 5-second inhale, 5-second exhale pattern backed by vagal tone research
• Tuned Frequencies: 432 Hz (calming), 528 Hz (healing love frequency), 7.83 Hz (Schumann resonance)
• Voice Guidance: Clear cues for breath timing and tone sustain
• Heart Rate Feedback: Optional device integration to track HRV response
• Offline Audio: All tones generated locally on your device
• Streak Counter: Visual motivation for daily practice
• No Ads: Completely ad-free experience
• No Login: Nameless, anonymous, private

Who It's For
Breath-work enthusiasts, meditation practitioners, and anyone seeking evidence-based nervous system regulation tools.

Pricing
Free to install. No subscriptions. No in-app purchases.

Privacy Statement
HUM operates 100% offline. No data is collected, transmitted, or stored remotely. Breath timing, heart rate data (if connected), and usage patterns remain entirely on your device in local storage. We do not track sessions, user identity, or location.
