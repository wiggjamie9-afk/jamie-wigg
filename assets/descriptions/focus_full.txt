FOCUS - Adaptive Pomodoro with Body Doubling and Audio

Value Proposition
FOCUS is the Pomodoro timer for people with ADHD and attention challenges. Adaptive intervals, body doubling voice, binaural 40 Hz gamma waves, and real-time focus analytics. $4.99/month.

Key Features
• Adaptive Pomodoro: Learns your optimal work/break ratio (default 25/5, can adjust 15–60 min)
• Body Doubling Voice: Ambient voice presence (coworker simulation) to reduce procrastination
• Binaural 40 Hz Gamma: Scientifically-tuned frequency to enhance focus and cognitive load
• Brown Noise: Constant ambient sound to mask distracting background noise
• Focus Analytics: Completed sessions, average focus time, daily trends
• Quick Start: No setup—tap play and start immediately
• Distraction Logging: Optional note-taking for recurring distractions (e.g., "checked phone 3 times")
• Session History: 30-day calendar heatmap of productive sessions
• Dark Mode: Minimal, distraction-free interface
• Offline Ready: All audio generated locally; works offline

Who It's For
Students, knowledge workers, ADHD/neurodivergent individuals, freelancers, and anyone seeking to maintain focus and track productivity.

Pricing
$4.99/month subscription. Free 7-day trial. Cancel anytime.

Privacy Statement
FOCUS stores focus session history locally in IndexedDB only. No personal data, location, or identity information is collected. Optional "distraction notes" are stored locally; they are never transmitted to servers. We do not track you between sessions or correlate your focus data with other apps. Zero analytics beyond error logging.
