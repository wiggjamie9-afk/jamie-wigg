DREAMS - Sleep Ritual with Breath Pacing and Soundscape

Value Proposition
DREAMS guides you through a complete nightly ritual designed for better sleep and morning recall. Combine ancient breathing techniques with AI-generated soundscapes to create the perfect conditions for restful sleep.

Key Features
• 4-7-8 Breath Pacer: Proven relaxation breathing technique with visual guidance
• Generative Soundscape: AI-created ambient sound unique to each session
• Dream Recall Journal: Morning prompt to capture and track dreams
• Haptic Feedback: Silent vibration cues for breath synchronisation
• Offline First: All processing happens on your device—no data leaves your phone
• No Subscriptions: One-time install, no recurring payments

Who It's For
People seeking better sleep quality, dream workers, and wellness practitioners who value privacy and evidence-based techniques.

Pricing
Free to install. No in-app purchases. No premium tier.

Privacy Statement
DREAMS operates completely offline. No internet connection required. No personal data, breathing data, or sleep metrics are collected, transmitted, or stored anywhere. Your dream journal stays in your local browser storage only. We do not track usage, location, or any biometric data.

Accessibility
DREAMS supports:
• Screen reader compatible (iOS VoiceOver, Android TalkBack)
• High contrast mode
• Adjustable text sizes
• Haptic feedback toggle for users with hearing difficulties

Technical Details
• Developed with Web Audio API for audio generation
• Uses IndexedDB for local-only storage
• Compatible with: iOS Safari 14+, Android Chrome 88+, Samsung Internet 12+
• Requires microphone permission for optional voice-guided sessions (can be skipped)
• Estimated storage: 5–10 MB

Installation
1. Visit rhythmixapp.com.au/apps/dreams on your mobile browser
2. Tap "Install" and add to home screen
3. Open DREAMS and allow microphone permission (optional)
4. Start your first breath session immediately
