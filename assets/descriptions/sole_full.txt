SOLE - Footwear Fit and Comfort Tracker

Value Proposition
Shoe fit journal, comfort scoring system, and wear-out predictor. Helps runners and footwear enthusiasts optimise their shoe investment and prevent injuries. One-time purchase.

Key Features
• Shoe Inventory: Track all shoes with model, brand, size, purchase date, cost
• Fit Journal: Daily wear comfort scoring (fit, blister risk, arch support, cushioning)
• Odometer: Distance tracking per shoe (km/miles accumulated)
• Wear-Out Predictor: Estimates lifespan based on mileage and wear pattern
• Rotation Planner: Suggests which shoe to wear today (alternating shoes extends life)
• Injury Log: Track if shoe caused pain, blisters, or injury
• Replacement Alert: Notifies when shoes near end-of-life (typically 500–800 km)
• Photo Journal: Take photos of shoe wear (heel, forefoot, overall tread)
• Cost Analysis: Cost per km worn (ROI on shoe investment)
• Offline First: All data stored locally

Who It's For
Runners, hikers, athletes, footwear enthusiasts, and people seeking to maximise shoe lifespan and prevent injury through proper rotation.

Pricing
One-time purchase (pricing TBD). No subscriptions.

Privacy Statement
SOLE stores shoe data and wear logs locally in IndexedDB. No personal running data, health metrics, or location tracking is transmitted. Complete privacy for footwear tracking.

Installation & Usage
1. Visit rhythmixapp.com.au/apps/untapped/sole
2. Purchase one-time licence
3. Add shoe (brand, model, size, purchase date, cost)
4. Take photo of new shoe (wear reference)
5. Log daily wear (distance, comfort score)
6. SOLE tracks: Cumulative km, predicted lifespan, cost per km
7. Rotate shoes to extend lifespan
8. Replace when SOLE suggests end-of-life

Shoe Tracking Features
- Inventory: Model, brand, size, fit, purchase date, cost
- Distance: Track km/miles per shoe (manual or optional GPS)
- Condition: Photo progression (new → worn out)
- Cost per km: Total cost ÷ cumulative distance
- Replacement cost: Average cost per shoe

Comfort Scoring (1–5 scale)
- Fit: Too tight, snug, perfect, loose, way too loose
- Blister risk: None, mild, moderate, high
- Arch support: Insufficient, adequate, excellent
- Cushioning: Hard, moderate, plush
- Overall comfort: Poor, fair, good, great, excellent

Wear-Out Prediction
- Base lifespan: 500–800 km typical for running shoes
- Wear pattern: Heel-strike heavy = faster wear
- Activity type: Road running, trail, casual wear
- Mileage accumulation: Suggests time-based replacement too
- Alert threshold: 80% lifespan triggers "consider replacement" notice

Rotation Planner
- Suggests alternating shoes daily (extends life by 30–50%)
- Tracks days since last worn
- Alerts if shoe rested too long (cushioning rebounds need use)

Disclaimer
SOLE is a footwear wear tracking tool, not a medical device or injury prevention system. Shoe selection should be based on gait analysis, foot type, and running style (consult a running specialist or podiatrist). While rotating shoes extends lifespan, proper fit and replacement are more important for injury prevention than total mileage. Always consult a healthcare provider for running injuries.
