Frequency Guide
- 4 Hz (Theta): Deep relaxation, sleep, meditation
- 10 Hz (Alpha): Calm focus, light meditation, creativity
- 40 Hz (Gamma): Sharp focus, alertness, learning
- Isochronic Tones (alternative): Pulse-based carrier-free frequency

Binaural Beat Science
Binaural beats work by playing slightly different frequencies in each ear (e.g., 100 Hz left, 110 Hz right, creating a perceived 10 Hz beat). Research suggests potential benefits for relaxation, focus, and sleep, though evidence is still emerging. Results vary by individual; some people are not susceptible.
