HERD - Livestock Herd Management System

Value Proposition
Herd roster with health records, breeding calendar, genealogy tracking, and performance history. Built for smallholder and pastoral livestock operations. One-time purchase.

Key Features
• Herd Roster: Individual animals with ID, breed, gender, date of birth, parents
• Health Records: Vaccination, illness, treatment history with dates and costs
• Breeding Calendar: Track heat cycles, mating dates, calving/lambing dates
• Genealogy: Visual pedigree tree (parents, grandparents, siblings)
• Performance Metrics: Weight, milk yield, growth rate, meat grade
• Medication Log: Treatment history with dosage and withdrawal periods
• Feed Tracking: Feed type and consumption per animal or group
• Cost Analysis: Lifetime cost-per-animal and cost per kg gain
• Report Export: CSV or PDF herd summary for vet or nutritionist
• Offline First: No internet required; works in remote areas

Who It's For
Smallholder farmers, pastoral operators, cattle ranches, dairy farms, sheep and goat herds, breeding programs, and agricultural extensions.

Pricing
One-time purchase (pricing TBD). No subscriptions.

Privacy Statement
HERD stores all herd data locally in IndexedDB. No data is transmitted to cloud servers or shared with feed companies, vets, or agricultural cooperatives unless you explicitly export. Complete farmer privacy and data ownership.

Installation & Usage
1. Visit rhythmixapp.com.au/apps/untapped/herd
2. Purchase one-time licence
3. Add animals (ID, breed, gender, date of birth)
4. Link parents to build genealogy
5. Log health events (vaccination, illness, treatment)
6. Track breeding cycles and set calving/lambing alerts
7. Record weight and performance metrics
8. Export herd report for vet or breeding decisions

Animal Management
- Individual ID: Custom naming (e.g., "Bessie-2024-01", "Ram-07")
- Breed: Dairy, meat, dual-purpose categories
- Performance: Weight, milk yield, meat grade, growth rate
- Health: Vaccination calendar, illness history, treatment log
- Breeding: Heat cycle tracking, mating dates, progeny list

Breeding Features
- Heat cycle timer: 17–24 day cycle notification
- Pregnancy counter: Days to expected calving/lambing
- Progeny tracking: All offspring linked to parents
- Genealogy: 3-generation pedigree view
- Inbreeding alert: Warns if related animals mated (configurable)

Reports & Analysis
- Herd summary: Total animals, age distribution, breed breakdown
- Performance: Average weight gain, milk yield per head
- Cost analysis: Total spend, cost per animal, cost per kg gain
- Health: Vaccination status, disease incidence, mortality rate

Disclaimer
HERD is a herd management tool, not veterinary advice. For animal health concerns, consult a veterinarian. Breeding recommendations are data-driven but not breed-specific expert advice. Inbreeding coefficients are simplified estimates. Always keep physical records as backup and consult local agricultural extension services for best practices.
