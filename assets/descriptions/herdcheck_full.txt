HerdCheck - Dairy Farm Screening and Animal Health

Value Proposition
Phone-camera screening tool for smallholder dairy and livestock farmers. Detect lameness, mastitis, and imminent calving using visual assessment—no veterinarian required for first screening.

Key Features
• Lameness Detection: Sprecher 5-point locomotion scale assessment with photo guidance
• Mastitis Screening: Visual signs (udder swelling, discharge) flagged from photo
• Calving Predictor: Gestation day counter + behavioural sign checklist for imminent calving
• Photo Journal: Timestamped animal photos for herd history tracking
• Offline Capable: Works without internet in remote areas
• Multi-Language: Kiswahili, Amharic, Bengali, French (built-in, no translation API)
• Herd Roster: Animal ID, breed, gender, age, vaccination records
• Report Export: PDF export for veterinary consultation
• No Login: Anonymous tracking, no data harvesting
• Free Forever: Completely free to use

Who It's For
Smallholder farmers in Africa and South Asia (500M+ global population), veterinary extension workers, and NGO animal health programmes.

Pricing
Completely free. No in-app purchases. No premium tier.

Privacy Statement
HerdCheck operates 100% offline. No internet connection required for core functionality. Photos are stored locally only; no images are transmitted to servers. Herd records (animal IDs, health notes) are stored locally in IndexedDB. No tracking, no analytics, no data collection for commercial purposes. Photos may be optionally exported as PDF reports for veterinary consultation, but all files remain under your control.

Installation
1. Visit rhythmixapp.com.au/livestock/
2. Add to home screen
3. Create herd roster (animal ID, gender, breed, birth date)
4. For each animal: take lameness assessment photo, mastitis screening photo
5. Log behavioural observations (calving signs, vaccination dates)
6. Export herd report as PDF to share with vet or extension officer

Lameness Scoring (Sprecher Scale)
- 1: Normal gait (no lameness)
- 2: Mild lameness (slightly stiff, weight-bearing lameness)
- 3: Moderate lameness (head bob, uneven gait, reluctant to move)
- 4: Severe lameness (three-legged gait, significant pain)
- 5: Non-weight-bearing lameness (unable to bear weight, recumbent)

Disclaimer
HerdCheck is a screening tool, not a replacement for veterinary diagnosis. Lameness, mastitis, and calving assessment are visual aids only. Always consult a licensed veterinarian for medical treatment decisions. Scores should be used to prioritize veterinary consultation, not to delay care. Calving predictor is based on gestation averages; individual variation is normal and expected.
