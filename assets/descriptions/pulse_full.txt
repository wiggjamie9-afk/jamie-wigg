PULSE - Habit Tracker with GitHub-Style Heatmaps

Value Proposition
PULSE is your daily habit companion with GitHub-style contribution heatmaps, smart nudges, particle celebrations, and deep analytics. $3.99/month subscription.

Key Features
• Habit Dashboard: Track up to 30 daily habits (custom names, colours, icons)
• GitHub-Style Heatmap: Visual calendar showing your 365-day consistency at a glance
• Habit Streaks: Consecutive days counter with personal best tracking
• Smart Nudges: Customisable reminders at specific times (or adaptive timing)
• Particle Celebration: Animated confetti and particle effects on completion
• Habit Categories: Fitness, mindfulness, health, productivity, learning, creativity
• Weekly Report: Email summary of completion rate and trend analysis
• Habit Insights: Most consistent habits, longest streaks, completion rate by time of day
• No Judgment: Miss a day? Reset counter or continue streak with explanation
• Offline First: All progress stored locally, optional cloud sync

Who It's For
People building daily habits, fitness enthusiasts, students, meditators, and anyone seeking accountability and visual motivation.

Pricing
$3.99/month subscription. Free 7-day trial.

Privacy Statement
PULSE stores habit completion data locally in IndexedDB. No personal identity data is collected beyond an email address (for weekly reports, optional). Habit data is not shared with third parties, health apps, or advertisers. Optional cloud sync uses end-to-end encryption. Zero tracking across apps or websites.

Accessibility
• High-contrast heatmap with colour-blind modes (deuteranopia, protanopia, tritanopia)
• Large habit buttons optimised for touch
• Haptic feedback on completion
• Audio cue for habit reminders
• Screen reader compatible heatmap (reads as table)
• Customisable notification volume and frequency
• Dark mode only (reduced eye strain)

Technical Details
• Canvas-based GitHub-style heatmap rendering
• IndexedDB for habit log and streak calculation
• Notification API for smart reminders (iOS Web App, Android)
• Email API for weekly reports (optional, encrypted)
• Compatible with: iOS 13+, Android 9+
• Storage: ~3 MB for 1-year habit data (30 habits)
• Syncing: WebSyncable or manual JSON export/import

Installation
1. Visit rhythmixapp.com.au/apps/pulse/
2. Add to home screen
3. Create account with email
4. Add your first habit (name, category, reminder time)
5. Complete habit check-in (tap the habit or set reminder)
6. View heatmap and weekly progress email

Habit Categories & Examples
- Fitness: Run, yoga, strength training, walk 10k steps
- Mindfulness: Meditate, journal, breathwork, pray
- Health: Drink 8 glasses water, take vitamins, stretch
- Productivity: Deep work session, learn something new, side project
- Learning: Read, language practice, course module, podcast
- Creativity: Write, draw, music practice, photograph

Particle Celebration Modes
- Confetti: Bursts on screen at completion
- Particle burst: Stars and geometry shapes
- Milestones: Special animation at 7-day, 30-day, 100-day streaks
- Silent mode: Haptic-only feedback (no visual/audio)
