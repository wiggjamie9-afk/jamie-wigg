## Production notes (all cuts)

- **Audio ducking** — narration always above bed music by 6 dB minimum; the soft pre-launch tone breaks if music fights the VO.
- **Burned-in captions** — every cut must have legible on-screen text (sound-off is the default viewing mode on TikTok/Reels/Shorts).
- **Safe zones** — keep all text inside the central 80% vertical band; TikTok UI eats the bottom 15% (caption, CTA, username) and top 10% (back arrow, "For You" tab).
- **Logo lockup timing** — the RHYTHMIX logo should hold on screen for at least 1.5s minimum on every cut for brand recall.
- **No price in cutdowns** — the $149 lifetime price is in the 60s master but stripped from 15s/30s cuts to keep the soft-launch curiosity tone. Save it for retargeting.
