**Character:** ZIGGY - Energetic ADHD Superhero Protagonist

**Format:** Character design sheet (16:9 landscape, 11+ variations)

- **Energetic** - Barely-contained motion in every variation

## Design Sheet Layout

### 16:9 Landscape Grid

```
┌─────────────┬──────────────┐
│  3 Heads    │  4 Expressions│
├──────────────────────────────┤
│     Full-Body Main Pose       │
├─────────────┬──────────────┤
│  2 Poses    │  2 Poses     │
│  (Run/Jump) │  (Think/Cel) │
└─────────────┴──────────────┘
```

### Component Breakdown

**Head Variations (3)**
- Front: Symmetrical, welcoming
- 3/4 Left: Dimensional, confident
- 3/4 Right: Mirror angle, balanced

**Expressions (4)**
- Happy/Excited: Joyful, energetic
- Focused/Determined: Concentrated, purposeful
- Nervous/Anxious: Vulnerable, relatable
- Powerful/Confident: Bold, heroic

**Main Pose (1)**
- Center: Barely-contained energetic motion, superhero stance

**Action Poses (4)**
- Running: Forward momentum, speed
- Jumping: Celebratory, arms raised
- Thinking: Contemplative, hand-to-chin
- Celebrating: Victory, inspirational

## Design Intent

ZIGGY is designed to embody ADHD as a superpower - not a limitation. Every design choice communicates:
- Boundless energy and enthusiasm
- Brilliant problem-solving capability
- Relatable vulnerability and strength
- Inspirational optimism and determination
- Authentic, youthful personality

The character should feel:
- **Authentic** to neurodivergent experience
- **Inspirational** without being preachy
- **Energetic** in every variation
- **Professional** in execution
- **Production-ready** for animation
