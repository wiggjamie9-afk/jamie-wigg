SONNY'S COZY QUOKKA BEDTIME TALES — PODCAST
===========================================

WHAT'S IN HERE
--------------
episodes/   35 MP3 episodes (one per book), ~2 min each, ~76 min total.
            Each has proper tags (title, series, track #, author) and the
            book's cover embedded as episode artwork.
art/        Square episode-art images (1500x1500), one per episode.
cover.jpg   Square show cover (3000x3000) for the podcast itself.
feed.xml    A ready-made podcast RSS feed (see "Option B" below).
episodes.json  The episode list with titles + durations (reference).

Narration is the same warm read-aloud voice already used in your books.
Total runtime: ~76 minutes across 35 episodes. Ages 1-5.


HOW TO PUBLISH — TWO WAYS
-------------------------

OPTION A — EASIEST (free, recommended): Spotify for Creators
  1. Go to podcasters.spotify.com and sign in (free).
  2. Create a new show: name it "Sonny's Cozy Quokka Bedtime Tales",
     paste the description below, upload cover.jpg as the show art.
  3. For each episode, click "New episode", upload the MP3 from episodes/
     (they're numbered 01-35 in story order), and publish.
  4. Spotify auto-generates your RSS feed and distributes to Spotify.
     You do NOT need feed.xml for this route.
  - To also appear on Apple Podcasts: submit that Spotify feed URL at
    podcastsconnect.apple.com (free listing).

OPTION B — SELF-HOSTED (if you want to own the feed)
  1. Upload the whole podcast/ folder to any web host / static bucket
     (e.g. GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, S3).
  2. Open feed.xml and replace every
        https://REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-HOST.example.com/podcast
     with the real public URL of the uploaded folder.
  3. Submit feed.xml's URL to Apple Podcasts, Spotify, etc.


SHOW DESCRIPTION (copy-paste)
-----------------------------
Gentle, soothing bedtime stories starring Sunny the little quokka and her
friends from the Australian bush. Soft, rhythmic read-aloud tales to help
babies, toddlers and preschoolers wind down and drift off to sleep. A new
cosy animal friend every episode. Ages 1-5.

Category: Kids & Family > Stories for Kids
Language: English (Australia)
