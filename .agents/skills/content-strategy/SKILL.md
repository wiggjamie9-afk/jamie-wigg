# Content Strategy

Plan what content to create, when to create it, and how to distribute it across channels.

## When to use

- Planning your YouTube channel
- Creating content calendar
- Deciding between blog vs video vs email
- Building launch campaigns
- Managing seasonal content
- Growing audiences systematically

## Content Strategy Framework

### Phase 1: Foundation (Month 1)

**Goal:** Establish credibility and build audience base

**Content types:**
- 5-10 "how-to" tutorials (searchable, permanent)
- 2-3 product walkthroughs (show what RHYTHMIX does)
- 1 brand story video (who you are, why RHYTHMIX)

**Distribution:**
- YouTube: 2 videos/week
- Blog: 1 deep-dive guide/week
- Email: Weekly newsletter (tips + link to latest video)

**Metrics:**
- YouTube: 500-1000 subscribers
- Blog: 100-200 monthly visitors
- Email: 100-200 subscribers

### Phase 2: Growth (Month 2-3)

**Goal:** Establish consistent presence, build authority

**Content types:**
- 20+ tutorials (library grows, search traffic increases)
- 10 "watch me create" videos (entertainment value)
- 5 comparisons vs competitors (capture search traffic)
- Case studies: creator results (social proof)

**Distribution:**
- YouTube: 2-3 videos/week
- Blog: 2 posts/week (tutorials + case studies)
- Email: 2x/week (tutorials + case studies)
- Twitter/Instagram: Daily clips from videos

**Metrics:**
- YouTube: 5-10K subscribers
- Blog: 1-5K monthly visitors
- Email: 500-1000 subscribers
- Social following: 500-1000

### Phase 3: Scale (Month 4-6)

**Goal:** Dominate niche, build community

**Content types:**
- 40+ tutorials (become "go-to" resource)
- Podcast or interview series (build network)
- Community-driven content (user-generated)
- Partnerships with other creators

**Distribution:**
- YouTube: 2-3 videos/week (consistent)
- Blog: 2-3 posts/week (SEO compound growth)
- Email: 2-3x/week (personalized)
- Podcast: 2x/month (new channel)
- Twitter/Instagram: Curated content + engagement
- TikTok: 3-5 shorts/week (capture young audience)

**Metrics:**
- YouTube: 20-50K subscribers
- Blog: 5-20K monthly visitors
- Email: 2-5K subscribers
- Podcast: 100-500 downloads/episode
- Social: 5-20K followers combined

## Content Pillar Strategy

Organize content around 4-5 pillars:

```
Pillar 1: TUTORIALS (How-to, searchable)
├─ "How to generate beats in RHYTHMIX"
├─ "Music production for beginners"
└─ "How to use [specific feature]"

Pillar 2: CASE STUDIES (Social proof, conversions)
├─ "How creator X went from 0 to 100K"
├─ "Behind the scenes: album release"
└─ "Creator interview: production process"

Pillar 3: COMPARISONS (Traffic capture from competitors)
├─ "RHYTHMIX vs Splice"
├─ "AI vs Human: beat generation comparison"
└─ "Music tools ranked: 2024 guide"

Pillar 4: TRENDS (Timely, algorithm-favored)
├─ "New AI music models ranked"
├─ "Trending beats: how to make them"
└─ "Creator reactions to AI music"

Pillar 5: COMMUNITY (Engagement, retention)
├─ "Your music: monthly showcase"
├─ "Creator spotlights"
└─ "Community challenges"
```

## Channel Strategy

### YouTube (Main growth driver)

**Focus:** Tutorials (70%) + Entertainment (20%) + Community (10%)

**Posting schedule:** 2-3 videos/week (consistent day/time)

**Playlist organization:**
- Beginners: First 5 videos
- Features: Deep dive per feature
- Case studies: Creator stories
- Trending: Latest on music AI

### Blog (SEO long-term)

**Focus:** Long-form (1000-2000 words), evergreen

**Posting:** 1-2 posts/week

**Content types:**
- How-to guides (searchable)
- Roundups (best beats, producers, etc.)
- Industry analysis (trend reports)
- How RHYTHMIX solves problems

**SEO strategy:**
- Target long-tail keywords
- Link to YouTube videos
- Internal linking between posts

### Email (Highest engagement)

**Focus:** Value first, pitch second

**Frequency:** 1-2x/week

**Flow:**
1. Weekly tip (tutorial, insight)
2. Weekly story (case study, interview)
3. Monthly special (exclusive content, offer)

**Segmentation:**
- Free tier users: "Upgrade tips"
- Pro users: "Pro-only features"
- Churned users: "Come back" campaign

### Social Media (Discovery)

**Twitter:** Industry news + tips + engagement
- Frequency: 1-2 tweets/day
- Focus: Retweets, threads, discussions

**Instagram:** Beautiful visuals + behind-the-scenes
- Frequency: 3-5 posts/week
- Focus: Reels (beat creation), stories (daily life)

**TikTok:** Quick tips + trends + entertainment
- Frequency: 5-7 shorts/week
- Focus: "Satisfying" beat generation, before/after

**LinkedIn:** Thought leadership + business insights
- Frequency: 2-3 posts/week
- Focus: "Why creators should use AI", industry insights

## Content Performance Metrics

### YouTube

Track per video:
- Views (accumulation)
- Click-through rate (title + thumbnail quality)
- Average view duration (content quality)
- Subscription rate (conversion)

Target:
- Average duration >60% of video length
- CTR >4%
- Subscription rate >5%

### Blog

Track per post:
- Pageviews
- Time on page (engagement)
- Bounce rate
- Conversion (email signup)

Target:
- Pageviews: 500+ (first month)
- Time on page: 2+ minutes
- Conversion: 5-10%

### Email

Track per campaign:
- Open rate (subject line quality)
- Click rate (relevance)
- Unsubscribe rate (frequency/relevance)

Target:
- Open rate: 25-35%
- Click rate: 5-10%
- Unsubscribe: <0.5%

### Overall

Track monthly:
- New subscribers (all channels)
- Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares)
- Traffic to main site
- Conversion to paid users

## Growth Acceleration Tactics

### 1. Collaboration
Partner with other creators (cross-promotion = both grow faster)

```
Reach out to:
- Music YouTubers (2-10K subscribers)
- Music production blogs
- Music podcasters

Propose: Guest appearance, interview, feature
Result: Tap into their audience
```

### 2. Trend Riding
Post content about trending topics within 24-48 hours

```
If "AI music" is trending:
- YouTube video within 24h
- Blog post within 48h
- Email blast within 24h
→ Capture search traffic surge
```

### 3. Repurposing
Turn 1 piece of content into 10

```
Original: 15-minute YouTube video
  ↓
Becomes:
- Blog post (1000 words)
- Email sequence (5 emails)
- Twitter thread (15 tweets)
- TikTok series (5 shorts)
- Podcast segment (5 min)
- Pinterest pins (5 pins)
- Instagram reels (3 reels)
```

### 4. Community Building
Make viewers feel part of something

```
Tactics:
- Reply to every comment (first 100)
- Feature viewer content
- Monthly challenges
- Discord community
- Monthly newsletter exclusively for subscribers
```
