FAQ

Sensible questions,
direct answers.

Is this a CRM, or a layer on top of my CRM?

Both work. Helix can replace your CRM end-to-end, or sit on top of Salesforce / HubSpot with two-way sync. Most teams start as a layer and migrate when they see the data quality difference.

What data does the AI loop see?

Only what you connect: email, calendar, calls, support, billing, product. We never train shared models on your data. You can audit every input and revoke any source any time.

How accurate are the drafts?

Drafts are prepared with full account context (last 90 days of touchpoints, deal stage, persona). On a typical Growth deployment, ~78% of drafts ship with no edits.

Where does my data live?

EU or US, your choice. Enterprise customers can pin per-region. SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR-compliant DPA.

How long is the migration?

Typically 1 day for SMB, 1-2 weeks for mid-market, 4-6 weeks for enterprise with custom objects. Migration is included on Growth and Enterprise.
