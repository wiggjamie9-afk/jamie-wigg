FAQ

Quick questions,
quick answers.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Type what you want in plain English. Forge handles the code. If you do know how to code, you can drop into the source and edit anything.

What can I build?

Daily journals, mini games, calculators, dashboards, landing pages, internal tools, social card generators — anything that fits in a browser.

Can I keep my apps private?

Free apps are public so the community can remix them. Pro and Team plans support private apps with their own URLs.

Can I export the code?

Team plans can export the full source as a zip. Open it in any editor and host it wherever you want.

Is there a mobile app?

Forge runs fully in the browser, including on iOS and Android. A native app is on the roadmap.
