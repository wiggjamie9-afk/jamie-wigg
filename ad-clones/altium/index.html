FAQ

Common questions.

Want a deeper architecture doc, security review, or migration plan? We will send it over.

Does Altrum import existing CAD and schematic files?

Yes. We import KiCad, Altium, OrCAD, and Fusion / SolidWorks projects with full history. Most teams migrate a project in under a day with our import tool.

How does the supply network actually work?

Every component and contract manufacturer in the network is pre-qualified and price-refreshed daily. Quote a BOM and you can route to PO directly inside Altrum.

Where do simulations run?

Our managed cluster, with dedicated EMI/thermal/timing solvers. Enterprise customers can attach an on-prem cluster.

Is it just for big teams?

No. Solo engineers get the Studio plan free, with the same component database and simulation queue as the Team plan, just at lower priority.

Do you support ITAR / regulated work?

Yes — on Enterprise. Region-locked storage, ITAR-controlled access, and audited workflows.
