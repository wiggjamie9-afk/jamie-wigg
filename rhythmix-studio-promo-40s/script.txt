You've got a track. You want a music video. But you don't have a studio, a director, or eight thousand dollars.

This is RHYTHMIX Studio. You give it your song and a theme — "neon-soaked midnight city," "underwater cathedral," whatever you want. It analyzes the beat, splits the track into intro, verse, chorus, bridge, outro, and routes each section to the right AI model. Kling for cinematic shots. Hunyuan for motion. Luma Ray for atmosphere. MiniMax for energy.

Cuts snap to your BPM. ffmpeg stitches it together. Eight to twenty-five dollars per video. Sixty seconds, end to end.
