# ZIGGY Character Design Sheet Specification

## Overview
Professional 2D cartoon character design sheet for ZIGGY - an energetic, optimistic middle-school protagonist with ADHD superpowers. World-class illustration quality matching professional animation standards.

## Output Specifications
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape (1920×1080 or 2560×1440)
- **Style:** Professional 2D cel-shading-friendly cartoon
- **Background:** Clean white studio, professional lighting
- **Quality:** High-resolution, crisp line clarity, animation-ready

## Character Core Design

### Physical Appearance
- **Age:** Middle-school (11–13)
- **Skin Tone:** Warm peachy complexion
- **Hair:** Short dark (nearly black) spiky hair with visible shine and movement lines
  - Spikes show energy and constant motion
  - Highlights indicate dimension and light catch
- **Eyes:** Large, bright, expressive with animated shine/sparkles
  - Pupils responsive to emotion
  - Eyebrows convey character expression
  - Iris color: warm brown or deep amber
  
### Primary Clothing: Orange Hoodie
- **Color:** VIBRANT ORANGE (#FF8C00)
- **Style:** Well-fitted youth hoodie/sweatshirt
- **Details:**
  - Hood with drawstrings
  - Front kangaroo pocket
  - Thumb holes optional (for motion emphasis)
  - Comfortable, athletic fit (not oversized)

### Secondary Accents: Electric Blue Trim
- **Color:** ELECTRIC BLUE (#00D8FF)
- **Placement:**
  - Cuff ribbing
  - Zipper track/slider
  - Pocket borders
  - Hood lining (visible when hood is up)
  - Optional: Small stripe on sleeves or side seams

### Lower Body
- **Pants:** Dark gray or charcoal, casual/athletic cut
- **Shoes:** Comfortable sneakers
  - Base color: white, gray, or black
  - Accents: ELECTRIC BLUE heel stripe, laces, or side panel
  - Style: Age-appropriate, modern athletic shoe

### Overall Silhouette
- Posture conveys barely-contained movement
- Dynamic stance even when standing still
- Proportions: realistic for middle-school age (not stylized/chibi)
- Expression: hopeful, optimistic, energetic

## Visual Style Guidelines

### Line Work
- **Weight:** Consistent 1–2px bold outlines
- **Style:** Clean, crisp, cel-animation-friendly
- **Curves:** Smooth, intentional strokes (no wobbly lines)
- **Purpose:** Define shapes and add graphic clarity

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Orange #FF8C00 (vibrant, warm, energetic)
- **Secondary:** Electric Blue #00D8FF (tech, optimism, contrast)
- **Tertiary:** Warm peachy skin, dark brown/black hair
- **Neutrals:** White background, dark pupils, subtle shadows
- **Saturation:** High contrast, no washed-out or desaturated areas
- **Avoid:** Default blue (#3b82f6), pastel tones, muddy colors

### Shading & Dimension
- **Technique:** Cel-shading (flat color blocks with 1–2 highlight/shadow layers)
- **Shadows:** Subtle, clean edges, dark brown or dark orange accents
- **Highlights:** White or light blue, placed for form definition
- **Hair:** Shine lines indicate movement and dimension
- **Depth:** Minimal but effective (no photorealistic gradients)

### Personality in Design
- **Energy:** Movement lines, dynamic posture, expressive features
- **Optimism:** Bright expression, open body language
- **Neurodivergent superpower:** Conveyed through:
  - Dynamic movement in hair and posture
  - Engaged, attentive eyes
  - Confident stance
  - Hopeful expression

### Animation Readiness
- **Symmetry:** Head/body proportions allow for rotation
- **Joints:** Clear articulation points (shoulders, elbows, hips, knees)
- **Rigging-friendly:** Clean silhouette, no overlapping/ambiguous sections
- **Expression layers:** Eyes, brows, mouth are separable/adjustable

## Technical Specifications

### Resolution & Format
- **Canvas Size:** 1920×1080px (16:9) minimum
- **File Type:** PNG with alpha transparency
- **DPI:** 300 DPI for print quality
- **Color Space:** sRGB (web standard)
- **Compression:** Lossless (no artifacts)

### Quality Standards
- **Line Clarity:** No anti-aliasing artifacts or soft edges
- **Consistency:** Character proportions identical across all views
- **Cleanliness:** No stray pixels, rough edges, or accidental strokes
- **Professionalism:** Matches Disney Channel / Cartoon Network production quality

### Rendering Style
- **Reference:** Professional commercial animation
- **Influences:** Adventure Time character design, Craig of the Creek, Amphibia
- **Avoid:** Generic AI aesthetic, heavy blurring, soft-focus rendering, stylized chibi proportions
- **Goal:** Show-ready character sheet that could be used in actual production
