Boilerplate

Short form (one line):

RHYTHMIX is a Perth-built AI music platform that gives indie musicians a one-payment alternative to the subscription-stacked production-and-release pipeline.

Standard (50 words):

Founded by Jamie Wigg in 2025 and shipping in 2026, RHYTHMIX is an Australian-built AI music platform with a four-pillar vision — Generate, Master, Distribute, Earn. The umbrella product, the beat-synced AI music-video pipeline, ships day-one at AU$149 lifetime. The remaining pillars roll out across 2026 at no additional cost to founding members.

Long form (120 words):

RHYTHMIX is a one-payment alternative to the subscription-stacked indie music release pipeline. Built solo by Jamie Wigg in Perth, Western Australia between 2025 and 2026, the platform launches with a beat-synced AI music-video tool that orchestrates four frontier video models (Kling 2.6, Hunyuan, Luma, MiniMax) into a single render. Three additional pillars — automated mastering, multi-store distribution, and royalty + merch routing — roll out across 2026 and are included in the AU$149 lifetime price for founding members. Three sibling products — RESONATE (biometric music for AirPods Pro 3), FREQUENCY DREAMS (generative bedtime ritual), and HUM (vagus-nerve humming practice) — share infrastructure and ship at AU$30 lifetime each.
