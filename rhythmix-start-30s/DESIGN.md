# RHYTHMIX — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Dark, energetic, neon AI-music aesthetic. Deep near-black canvas with violet undertones, punctuated by saturated magenta, cyan, and electric green. Modern grotesque sans for display, mono for taglines and numerals. Motion is sharp and confident — fast eases in, decisive holds, no bounce. Numbers and stats are the heroes; everything else supports them.

## Colors

- `#08050d` — Canvas (near-black with violet bias)
- `#ff1f5a` — Primary magenta (CTA, emphasis)
- `#7c3aed` — Secondary purple
- `#00d8ff` — Electric cyan
- `#00e887` — Signal green
- `#f5c000` — Hot gold (highlights, money)
- `#ff6fc8` — Soft pink (accent counts)
- `#ffffff` — Display text
- `#a0a0b0` — Muted body
- `#1a1325` — Card surface

## Typography

- Display: `"Space Grotesk", system-ui` — headlines, wordmark, big numbers
- Mono: `"JetBrains Mono", monospace` — taglines, labels, microcopy

## Motion

- Fast in (0.4–0.7s), `power3.out` / `expo.out` for entrances
- Stagger 60–120ms across grid items
- Crossfade between scenes (0.5s)
- No bounce, no elastic — keep it confident

## What NOT to Do

- No `#3b82f6`, `#333`, or default Roboto/Arial — palette is fixed.
- No full-frame linear gradients (banding); use radial or solid + localized glow.
- No scene-internal exit animations — the crossfade IS the exit.
- No emoji-heavy decoration; one icon glyph per feature card max.
- No bouncy or elastic eases — wrong tonal register.
