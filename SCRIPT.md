## Delivery notes

- **Tone**: warm but confident — pitching, not selling. Pause on the brand name and on "Coming soon".
- **Emphasis**: bold the four pillar verbs (Generate · Master · Distribute · Earn) and the three step verbs (Describe · Refine · Release) — they land on screen at the same beat.
- **Pauses**: half-second pause after each rhetorical question in the hook. Half-second pause before "RHYTHMIX" at the end.
- **Don't add**: specific user counts, dollar amounts, named testimonials, launch dates, pricing, or a URL. Those aren't in the video and shouldn't be in the voice track until they're real.
- **Recording tip**: read the whole thing through once at natural pace and check your total — should land between 58s and 62s. Adjust pacing rather than cutting words.
