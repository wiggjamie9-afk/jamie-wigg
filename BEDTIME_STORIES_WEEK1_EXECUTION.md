### Step 3: Record First 2 Stories

**Story 1: "The Sleepy Cloud"** (6 min)
- Audience: Ages 2-4
- Theme: Whimsical, simple, very calm

*Read this script in a warm, slow, sleepy voice (think Mr. Rogers):*

```
[NARRATION BEGINS - speak slowly, softly]

Once upon a time, high up in the sky, there was a little cloud named Cumulus.

Cumulus was a fluffy white cloud, and today, Cumulus was very, very tired.

The sun was setting. The sky was turning pink and purple and blue. 

And Cumulus thought to itself... "I need a nap. A nice, long nap."

So Cumulus floated slowly, slowly, slowly down toward the mountains.

[PAUSE 3 seconds]

Cumulus saw a little girl down below. She was lying in her bed, looking up at the stars.

Cumulus whispered, "Hello, little one. I am going to sleep now. Will you sleep with me?"

And the little girl smiled. She closed her eyes. And she began to drift, drift, drift away to the most wonderful dreams.

Cumulus floated gently past the moon.

The moon was smiling.

"Goodnight, Cumulus," said the moon.

"Goodnight," whispered Cumulus.

And as the night grew quiet and peaceful, Cumulus became smaller and smaller and softer and softer.

Until finally... Cumulus was fast asleep.

And all the children below were fast asleep too.

[PAUSE 2 seconds]

The end.

Sweet dreams.

[END]
```

**Recording tips:**
- Speak at 60% of normal volume
- Pause between sentences (2-3 seconds is fine)
- Smile while speaking (people hear it)
- If you mess up, just start the paragraph again — you'll edit it out
- One take is fine; you're going for warm, not perfect

### Step 4: Record Instructions

**What you need:**
- Quiet room (bedroom at night is perfect)
- Phone or USB microphone (phone is fine)
- Script (copy/paste the text above)
- No background music (just your voice)

**OBS Setup (FREE, if you want to record on computer):**
1. Download OBS Studio
2. Add audio input (your mic)
3. Hit Record
4. Read script slowly and warmly
5. When done, click Stop
6. File → Export → Audio → WAV format

**OR on phone (easiest):**
1. Open Voice Memos (iPhone) or Google Recorder (Android)
2. Hit Record
3. Read script
4. Save

**OR on computer (simplest):**
1. Open Windows 11 Voice Recorder (search "voice recorder")
2. Hit Record
3. Read script
4. Save

### Step 5: Create Simple Video

Your recorded audio + a simple visual = your video.

**Option A: Still image + narration (easiest)**
1. Take a photo or find a free image (Unsplash, Pixabay) of:
   - Story 1: A sleepy cloud in a sunset sky
   - Story 2: A bunny in a meadow
2. Open DaVinci Resolve (FREE)
3. New project → 1920×1080, 30fps
4. Drag image into timeline
5. Drag your narration audio into the audio track
6. Trim audio to length (script should be ~6-7 min)
7. Export → MP4, 1080p, H.264

**Option B: Animated background (slightly fancier)**
1. Use Canva (free tier, search "bedtime story background")
2. Pick a calming background (moon, stars, clouds)
3. Add text overlay: "Bedtime Stories for Kids"
4. Download as MP4 (it'll be 10 seconds)
5. Open DaVinci Resolve
6. Drag MP4 video into timeline
7. Extend it to 6+ minutes (right-click → Speed → Hold Last Frame)
8. Add narration audio
9. Export MP4

**Option C: Actually simplest - just narration over black screen**
1. Open DaVinci Resolve
2. New project → 1920×1080, 30fps
3. Create black background (Color Generator → black)
4. Drag to timeline for 6 minutes
5. Add your narration audio
6. Export MP4

*Black screen + warm voice is totally fine. Kids will close their eyes anyway.*
