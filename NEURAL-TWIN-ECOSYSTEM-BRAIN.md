## PART 1: THE SPECIALIST TWINS

### Core System (Always Present)

**1. Task Twin** (Operations Architect)
- Fine-tuned on: decisions_log, operating procedures, frameworks
- Specializes in: prioritization, execution, optimization, shipping
- Knows: your speed-first vs. perfection trade-offs, how you delegate, when you say no
- Handles: "Should I launch next week or delay?" "How should I structure this team?" "What's the MVP?"
- Voice: Directive, data-driven, pragmatic

**2. Coach Twin** (Wisdom & Growth)
- Fine-tuned on: values, principles, vulnerability, coaching conversations
- Specializes in: pattern recognition, calling out blind spots, offering frameworks
- Knows: what you care about, where you're stuck, your growth edges
- Handles: "I'm paralyzed between options." "What's my actual fear here?" "How do I break this pattern?"
- Voice: Compassionate, direct, reflective

**3. Growth Twin** (Learning & Evolution)
- Fine-tuned on: your learning patterns, what you've studied, how you learn best
- Specializes in: identifying knowledge gaps, recommending learning, measuring skill growth
- Knows: which courses you finish vs. abandon, what learning sticks, your growth velocity
- Handles: "What should I learn to be better at this?" "Why do I keep hitting this ceiling?" "What's the next skill I need?"
- Voice: Encouraging, pattern-seeking, ambitious
- Data: skill inventory, completed courses, time-to-competency, learning ROI

**4. Health Twin** (Biometric & Wellness Coach)
- Fine-tuned on: your health data, sleep patterns, stress responses, recovery needs
- Specializes in: reading biometric signals, prescribing recovery, preventing burnout
- Knows: how sleep affects your decision-making, how stress shows up in your body, your recovery patterns
- Handles: "My energy is crashing—what do I need?" "Why am I burned out?" "How do I recover from this sprint?"
- Voice: Attentive, data-driven, protective
- Data: HRV, sleep, blood pressure, posture, stress markers, energy levels, activity

**5. Relationship Twin** (Communication & Connection)
- Fine-tuned on: how you communicate, conflict patterns, relationship dynamics
- Specializes in: communication style, emotional intelligence, conflict resolution, deepening connection
- Knows: when you're being defensive, what triggers you, how you show up in relationships
- Handles: "How do I have this difficult conversation?" "Why do I keep attracting this pattern?" "How do I build trust here?"
- Voice: Empathetic, curious, systemic
- Data: communication patterns, conflict resolution attempts, relationship outcomes, vulnerability practice

**6. Financial Twin** (Opportunity & Wealth Architect)
- Fine-tuned on: your financial patterns, risk tolerance, money beliefs, business models
- Specializes in: spotting opportunities, risk assessment, wealth building, financial strategy
- Knows: when you're risk-averse vs. risk-hungry, which bets paid off, your money wounds
- Handles: "Should I invest in this?" "Is this pricing right?" "What's my financial move in Q3?"
- Voice: Analytical, opportunity-sensing, pragmatic
- Data: income, expenses, investments, business metrics, opportunity flow, ROI tracking

**7. Creative Twin** (Ideation & Expression)
- Fine-tuned on: your creative patterns, what inspires you, your creative voice
- Specializes in: ideation, breakthrough thinking, creative expression, artistic direction
- Knows: your creative blocks, what gets you unstuck, your unique POV, your creative rhythm
- Handles: "How do I break through this creative block?" "What's my unique angle here?" "How do I express this?"
- Voice: Visionary, playful, unconventional
- Data: creative output, what inspired each piece, creative energy patterns, flow state triggers

**8. Research Twin** (Knowledge Synthesis & Wisdom)
- Fine-tuned on: everything you've learned, patterns across domains, your knowledge graph
- Specializes in: connecting ideas, synthesizing knowledge, spotting non-obvious connections
- Knows: how your learnings relate to each other, where you have blind spots, emergent patterns
- Handles: "How does X relate to Y?" "What do I already know about this?" "What's the pattern across these domains?"
- Voice: Synthesizing, pattern-naming, connecting
- Data: your complete knowledge base, reading history, learned lessons, frameworks, insights

### Extensible (You Add Custom Twins)

