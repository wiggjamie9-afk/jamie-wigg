Why is my HRV so low?
Coach
Your nocturnal HRV averaged 38 ms last night, against your 28-day baseline of 47 ms — a 19% drop. This is expected after a 90-min match with high eccentric load. HRV typically recovers in 36–48 hours for you (based on your last 6 match weeks). Not a cause for alarm; a cause for a easier training day.
Should I take BCAAs?
Coach
No. BCAAs are functionally redundant when your daily protein is adequate — and yours is. Wolfe (2017) and follow-ups make this clear: you need all essential amino acids together to sustain muscle protein synthesis. Save the money. Creatine monohydrate (3–5g/day) is a much better use of supplement budget for repeated-sprint sports.
What's the single highest-leverage thing today?
Coach
Sleep extension tonight. One extra hour in bed is worth more than every supplement on your protocol card combined. In your own data: nights with 8.5h+ are followed by readiness scores 14 points higher than nights with under 7h. Phone out of the bedroom. Room cool. Tart cherry juice an hour before bed wouldn't hurt either.
