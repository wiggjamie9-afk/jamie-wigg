**Design Standard:** Match "Sunny and the Lorikeet Rainbow" + "Sunny and the Little Quoll" covers exactly

## CHARACTER SPECIFICATION

**Sunny the Quokka** appears in EVERY page (except teaser where she's implied to discover something)

- **Fur:** Golden-brown, soft watercolor texture
- **Eyes:** Warm brown, gentle, expressive (not cartoonish)
- **Expression:** Gentle smile, peaceful, calm
- **Body:** Small, round, cuddly (like a teddy bear, not realistic)
- **Consistency:** IDENTICAL SUNNY in every page - same size, proportions, eyes, smile

Reference: Use the Sunny from the two provided cover images as your exact template for all pages.

---

## COLOR PALETTE (THE BIBLE)

**Taken directly from your reference covers:**
- Deep navy night skies (#1a3a5c range)
- Soft cream and white (#f5f1e8)
- Warm golden tones (#d4a574, #e8b88d)
- Soft greens (#7a9b6f, #6d8b5f)
- Peachy/coral accents (#e8a68f)
- Pale moon whites (#f0eee5)
- Rich but soft - NOT bright or neon

- Soft edges, not hard lines
- Transparent layering visible
- Painted, hand-drawn quality
- Breathing room and white space
- Dreamy, peaceful aesthetic

## STYLE GUIDE REQUIREMENTS

✓ **Watercolor technique** - visible brushstrokes, layered transparent colors  
✓ **Soft edges** - not hard lines or vectors  
✓ **Peaceful, dreamy quality** - perfect for bedtime  
✓ **Consistent Sunny** - same appearance, size, style in every page  
✓ **Rich but soft colors** - match the provided reference covers  
✓ **Professional quality** - comparable to published children's book illustrations  
✓ **Australian native plants/animals** - where relevant  
✓ **Nighttime/twilight mood** - calm and sleep-inducing  
✓ **Text integration** - cream/white text fits naturally at bottom

## TEXT SPECIFICATIONS

**Font:** Soft, rounded serif or gentle sans-serif (similar to reference covers)  
**Color:** Cream or off-white (#f5f1e8 or similar)  
**Position:** Bottom of page, centered, two lines  
**Size:** Large enough to read comfortably (8-10pt minimum at final size)  
**Spacing:** Adequate breathing room from page edges  
**Shadow/Outline:** Subtle drop shadow or thin outline for readability over backgrounds
