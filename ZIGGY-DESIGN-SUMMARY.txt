================================================================================

Name: ZIGGY
Age: Middle-school (11-13 years old)
Genre: Professional 2D Cartoon Animation
Personality: Energetic, brilliant, hopeful, determined, resilient

COLORS:
- Primary: Vibrant Orange #FF8C00 (Hoodie)
- Secondary: Electric Blue #00D8FF (Accents)
- Skin: Warm Peachy #FFB8A6
- Hair: Dark Charcoal #2a2a2a
- Eyes: Large and bright with animated shine
- Background: White (studio quality)

KEY VISUAL ELEMENTS:
- Short dark spiky hair with shine and movement
- Big bright expressive eyes with animated highlights
- Vibrant orange hoodie (main design focus, slightly oversized)
- Electric blue trim on cuffs, hood, zipper, pockets
- Dynamic posture showing barely-contained energetic motion

DESIGN SHEET LAYOUT (16:9 LANDSCAPE):

KEY INFORMATION FOR ANIMATORS
================================================================================

For anyone using this design sheet in animation production:

CONSTRUCTION NOTES:
- Character proportions: approximately 4.5 head heights tall
- Joint locations are clearly indicated
- Limb segment lengths are consistent across all variations
- Rigging should accommodate full range of motion in all joints
- Head can rotate to all visible angles per reference sheet

EXPRESSION NOTES:
- Eyes support blinking animation
- Eyebrows move for emotion expression
- Mouth accommodates speech animation
- Face proportions allow for natural deformation

MOVEMENT NOTES:
- Orange hoodie fabric suggests natural movement constraints
- Posture allows for walking/running cycle
- Spine articulation supports jumping and action
- Clothing and hair respond to motion naturally

COLOR ACCURACY:
- Primary Orange: #FF8C00 (maintain exact hex throughout)
- Secondary Blue: #00D8FF (maintain exact hex throughout)
- All other colors per reference sheet
- Keep colors vibrant and saturated
- Avoid muting or desaturating colors

DESIGNER NOTES & PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

DESIGN INTENT:
ZIGGY embodies ADHD as a superpower, not a limitation. The character design
communicates boundless energy, brilliant problem-solving, and authentic
vulnerability. Every design choice - from the vibrant orange hoodie to the
barely-contained motion in every pose - reinforces this core message.

CHARACTER VOICE:
The visual design alone should communicate:
- Authentic neurodivergent experience
- Inspirational without being preachy
- Energetic in every variation
- Professional in execution
- Production-ready for animation

VISUAL STORYTELLING:

- Happy/Excited: "I'm ready to take on the world!"

- Jumping: "Pure joy and celebration"
- Thinking: "Intelligence and problem-solving"

COLOR PSYCHOLOGY:
