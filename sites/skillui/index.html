The part nobody else solves

It gets past the cookie wall — so you don't have to.

Consent banners and GDPR overlays sit on top of the page and poison every screenshot, scrape and colour sample. SkillUI detects the banner, dismisses it the way a real visitor would, and only then reads the design underneath.

  • Detects CMPs automatically — OneTrust, Cookiebot, Osano, Quantcast, TCF and custom banners.
  • Clean colour sampling — no grey overlay tint bleeding into your palette.
  • Zero manual steps — no "please close the popup and try again."
