FAQ

Good questions, honest answers

Is my data really private? +
Yes. Your conversations, photos, journals and health data are stored on your device — not on our servers. We never sell or share it, and you can wipe everything in one tap. Privacy isn't a setting; it's the architecture.
Is this a replacement for therapy? +
No — and we'll never claim to be. The Buddy System is a companion that complements professional care, not a substitute for a licensed therapist or doctor. For diagnosis or treatment, please see a professional. In a crisis, your buddy will guide you straight to real help.
How does the AI actually work? +
Each buddy is powered by Claude AI for warm, context-aware conversation, ElevenLabs for natural voice, and a generative model for its avatar. Personalities are tuned per buddy so a grief companion feels nothing like a startup mentor.
Where is my information stored? +
On your phone, in encrypted local storage. Nothing about your inner life leaves the device unless you explicitly choose to back it up. No accounts of your conversations sit in a cloud database somewhere.
What happens in an emergency? +
Every buddy has one-tap access to police, ambulance, a crisis helpline, and your own personal contacts. Buddies also watch for signs of distress and gently steer you toward immediate, real-world support.
Can I use more than one buddy at once? +
Absolutely. Many people keep a small circle — a sleep buddy at night, a fitness buddy in the morning, a grief buddy when they need it. Plus gives you 5, and Unlimited unlocks all 28.
