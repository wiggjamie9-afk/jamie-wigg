Tips for Best Results
Use headphones. Binaural beats only work when each ear receives a different frequency. Speakers merge the tones and the effect is lost.
Keep the screen on. The app uses the Wake Lock API to prevent your screen from dimming during a session. If your phone overrides it (battery saver mode), disable that temporarily.
Morning is optimal. Geomagnetic flux is typically lowest in the first two hours after sunrise — this is when HRV coherence is easiest to achieve.

For HRV camera mode: rest your fingertip gently on the rear camera lens in a well-lit room. Keep it still for the first 30 seconds while the baseline calibrates.
