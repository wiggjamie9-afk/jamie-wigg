How a certification works

Learn by building, verify by doing.

  • Interactive lessons & workshopsBite-sized, self-paced, run right in the browser.
  • Labs & reviewsApply concepts, then reinforce them before moving on.
  • QuizzesConfirm you've truly grasped the material.
  • Five required projectsShip real, working projects to qualify for the exam.
  • Pass the exam → claim your certYours forever — linkable and employer-verifiable.
