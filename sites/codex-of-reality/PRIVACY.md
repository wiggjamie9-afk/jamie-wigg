# Codex of Reality — Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 2026-05-18
**Last updated:** 2026-05-18

This policy describes what the Codex of Reality collects, why, and how it's stored. Read it before you tap "Claim Founding Membership."

## The short version

- The Codex runs in your browser. Most of it never sends anything to a server.
- Heart-rate data from your camera is processed on-device. It is **not** uploaded.
- Bluetooth heart-rate sensor data (Polar, etc.) is processed on-device. It is **not** uploaded.
- Streak counts and session history live in your phone's local storage. They stay there unless you sign in.
- If you create an account, we store your email, your purchase record, and your aggregated session metrics (date, duration, average coherence score — never the raw heart waveform).
- We do not sell data. We do not run third-party ad trackers. We never will.

## What we collect

### Locally on your device (does not leave your phone unless you sign in)
- Camera video frames during a Coherence Engine session — processed in real time, never stored, never uploaded.
- Bluetooth heart-rate sensor packets (Polar H10, Verity Sense, etc.) — processed in real time, never stored, never uploaded.
- Streak count, last-session date, session history (slug + duration + average coherence + timestamp).
- Personal resonance frequency, if you've run Resonance Discovery.
- Cached environmental data (last NOAA Kp reading, last Schumann reading).

### On our servers (only after you create an account)
- Your email address — for sign-in and account recovery.
- A record of your Founding Membership purchase (Stripe payment intent ID, amount, currency, date).
- Optional: an opted-in sync of the aggregated session metrics above so your streak follows you between devices.

### From third parties
- **Stripe** — when you purchase, Stripe receives your payment information directly. We never see your full card number. See [Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).
- **NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center** — we fetch the planetary K-index from their public endpoint. No data goes to NOAA.
- **HeartMath Global Coherence Monitoring System** *(planned)* — we fetch the current Schumann resonance reading via a server-side proxy. No data goes to HeartMath.

## What we never collect

- Raw heart-rate waveforms or PPG video.
- Brainwave data (when Muse / EEG support ships, raw EEG stays on your device).
- Location, contacts, photos, microphone audio (apart from playback you initiate).
- Browser fingerprinting beacons. No third-party advertising cookies. No social-media tracking pixels.

## Permissions explained

- **Camera** — only requested when you start a Coherence Engine session. Released the moment the session ends.
- **Bluetooth** — only requested when you tap "Pair" in Settings. The browser shows the device picker; we never see other devices on your network.
- **Notifications** *(future)* — only requested if you opt into morning / evening practice reminders.

## How long we keep things

- Local data lives on your device until you tap **Reset progress** in Settings or uninstall the app.
- Account data is kept while your Founding Membership is active. If you request deletion, all account data is removed within 30 days. Stripe payment records are retained as required by financial regulation.

## Your rights

You can:
- Export your session history at any time from Settings (planned).
- Delete your local data instantly from Settings.
- Request account deletion by emailing `privacy@codexofreality.com`. We respond within 7 days.
- Object to processing. Withdraw consent. Lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

## Children

The Codex is not designed for users under 16. Do not create an account for a child.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be announced via email to active members and a banner on the home screen. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
