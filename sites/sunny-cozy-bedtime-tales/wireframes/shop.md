# Wireframe: Shop Page — Sunny's Cozy Bedtime Tales

**URL**: `/shop`  
**Purpose**: Showcase merchandise and drive Printful/Etsy sales.  
**Brand Colors**: Navy `#192D55`, Cream `#F5F0EB`, Gold `#D4A574`  
**Target Audience**: Parents, caregivers, gift buyers

---

**Layout**: Full-width hero banner with text overlay

**Dimensions**:
- Desktop: 100% width, 400–500px height
- Mobile: 100% width, 300–350px height

**Content Blocks**:
- **Headline**: "Sunny's Cozy Collection"  
  Font: Large serif or warm sans-serif, Navy `#192D55`
- **Tagline**: "Quality merchandise for bedtime lovers — t-shirts, pajamas, pillows, and more."  
  Font: Medium, Cream `#F5F0EB` on darker overlay, 1–2 lines
- **Background**: Soft illustrated scene (storybook aesthetic, warm watercolor tones—children sleeping peacefully, stars, moon)
- **CTA Button**: "Shop Now" (primary, Navy background with Cream text, slight shadow)

**Interactive Elements**:
- Button links to S2 (Product Grid) via smooth scroll or page anchor
- Optional: Subtle animation on hero image (parallax scroll or gentle fade-in on load)

**Copy Suggestions**:
> "Bring Sunny's world home. Cozy tees, dreamy pillows, and bedtime essentials designed for calm evenings."

## Copy & Messaging Guidelines

**Hero**: Emphasize **quality**, **cozy**, and **trust** — parents are the buyers.

**Product Descriptions**: Short, benefit-driven. Avoid jargon. Example:
- ❌ "100% cotton unisex apparel with heat transfer printing"
- ✓ "Soft cotton tee featuring Sunny and friends"

**Reviews**: Authentic parent voices. Focus on **child happiness**, **quality**, **shipping speed**.

**Design Philosophy**: Bridge the gap between YouTube content and physical products. Emphasize **consistency of brand**, **ethical production**, **safety**.

---

## Accessibility Notes

- All images have descriptive alt text (product name, color, price)
- Color contrast: Navy on Cream (WCAG AA compliant), Gold on Navy works for text at 14px+
- Focus states: Buttons and links have visible outlines (Navy or Gold border)
- Mobile: Touch targets ≥ 48px × 48px for buttons
- Screen readers: Product grid marked as `<section>` with heading; each card is `<article>` or `<div role="region">`
- Carousel (if used): Keyboard-navigable with arrow keys + Enter to select
