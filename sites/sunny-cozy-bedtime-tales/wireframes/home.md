## Overall Notes

### Spacing & Typography
- **Headings**: Serif font (warm, classic feel) — Navy for most sections, Gold for heroes
- **Body text**: Sans-serif, Navy on cream/white backgrounds, Cream on navy backgrounds
- **Padding**: 40–60px vertical between sections, 20–24px horizontal margins on mobile
- **Line height**: 1.6 for readable body text

### Color Palette Application
- **Navy (#192D55)**: Headlines, body text, buttons (secondary), borders
- **Cream (#F5F0EB)**: Backgrounds, footer text, button text (primary)
- **Gold (#D4A574)**: Accents, icons, CTA buttons (primary), highlights

### Responsive Behavior
- **Desktop**: 1200px+ (full layouts as described)
- **Tablet**: 768–1199px (2-column grids become 1–2 columns, adjusted spacing)
- **Mobile**: <768px (1-column stacked layout, larger touch targets, simplified navigation)

### Accessibility
- All buttons and links have clear focus states (gold border or highlight)
- Color contrast meets WCAG AA standards (Navy + Cream, Navy + Gold)
- Form inputs have clear labels and visible focus states
- Accordion headers marked as interactive (cursor pointer, hover state)
- Alt text placeholders for all illustrations and product images
