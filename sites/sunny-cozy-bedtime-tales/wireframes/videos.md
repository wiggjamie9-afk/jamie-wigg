## Design Notes

### Color Usage
- **Navy #192D55**: All headlines, body text, step numbers
- **Cream #F5F0EB**: Page background, card backgrounds, input fields
- **Gold #D4A574**: CTA buttons, accent borders, hover states, step badges

### Typography
- **Headings (H1/H2)**: 36–48px, Navy, bold, center-aligned in hero sections
- **Body text**: 16px, Navy, left-aligned
- **Button text**: 16–18px, bold
- **Card titles**: 20px, Navy, bold
- **Card descriptions**: 14px, Navy, italic

### Spacing
- Section padding: 60px top/bottom
- Card grid: 24px horizontal gap, 32px vertical gap
- Button padding: 12px 24px (horizontal/vertical)

### Interactive States
- **Button hover**: Gold text on Cream background, slight shadow
- **Card hover**: Subtle lift (shadow), 1.02 scale, smooth 0.3s transition
- **Link hover**: Navy text with Gold underline

### Accessibility
- All buttons have clear focus states
- Form inputs have associated labels
- Images have alt text (episode title and description)
- Color contrast meets WCAG AA standards (Navy/Cream, Gold/Navy)

### Image Specs
- **Video thumbnails**: 16:9 aspect ratio, 280×158px (card), 560×315px (large)
- **Hero background**: 1920×600px (desktop), 1024×400px (tablet), 640×400px (mobile)
- **Section backgrounds**: Illustrated soft colors (cream, light blue, light gold tints)
