# Sitemap: Sunny's Cozy Bedtime Tales

## Audience
Parents and caregivers seeking calming, professional bedtime content for children ages 2-8.

## Primary CTA
Watch videos on YouTube (drive channel growth and watch time).

## Pages

### / (Home)
- **Purpose**: Convert visitors to YouTube channel subscribers and introduce the brand.
- **Sections**:
  - **S1 Hero** — "Sunny's Cozy Bedtime Tales" headline, tagline (calming bedtime stories), CTA button to YouTube channel, soft illustrated background
  - **S2 Social Proof** — Parent testimonials/reviews (2-3 quotes), trust badges, subscriber/view counts
  - **S3 Featured Episodes** — 3-4 video cards linking to YouTube (e.g., "Sunny Watches the Stars," "Sunny and the Flying Fox")
  - **S4 Why Sunny** — 3-4 feature cards highlighting benefits (professional narration, calming visuals, 149 episodes, no ads/distractions)
  - **S5 Merchandise Gallery** — 5-6 product cards (t-shirt, pajamas, pillow, mug, hat, tote) with "Shop Now" links to Etsy/Printful
  - **S6 Story Series Overview** — Brief description of all 149 episodes, CTA to browse full collection
  - **S7 Newsletter Signup** — Email capture form ("Get weekly story recommendations")
  - **S8 FAQ** — 5-6 common questions (age range, episode length, availability, commercial-free, etc.)
  - **S9 Footer CTA** — Final YouTube subscription push + links

### /videos
- **Purpose**: Showcase all 17 remade episodes and drive subscriptions.
- **Sections**:
  - **S1 Hero** — "All Episodes" headline, brief intro to the series
  - **S2 Episode Grid** — 17 video cards, each linking to YouTube (title, duration, brief description, thumbnail)
  - **S3 How to Watch** — 2-3 steps to subscribe, enable notifications, watch regularly
  - **S4 Coming Soon** — Teaser for upcoming episodes, newsletter signup
  - **S5 Footer** — Back to home, contact link

### /shop
- **Purpose**: Showcase merchandise and drive Printful/Etsy sales.
- **Sections**:
  - **S1 Hero** — "Sunny's Cozy Collection" headline, tagline (quality merchandise for bedtime lovers)
  - **S2 Product Grid** — 8-10 products (t-shirts, pajamas, pillows, mugs, hats, totes, books, etc.) with images, prices, "Shop on Etsy" / "Shop on Printful" links
  - **S3 Design Philosophy** — Brief explanation of the brand aesthetic (warm, illustrated, professional quality)
  - **S4 Reviews/Social Proof** — Customer quotes, 5-star ratings
  - **S5 Footer** — Return to home, YouTube link

### /about
- **Purpose**: Build trust and connection with the creator story.
- **Sections**:
  - **S1 Hero** — "About Sunny's Cozy Bedtime Tales" headline
  - **S2 Creator Story** — 1-2 paragraphs on Jamie Wigg's vision, why bedtime stories matter
  - **S3 The Mission** — "Dream Big, Little One" tagline + 2-3 core values (professional, calming, 149 stories, etc.)
  - **S4 Credentials/Numbers** — 149 episodes, 17 professional videos, merchandise, download count, YouTube subscribers
  - **S5 Contact/Social** — Email contact form, YouTube link, Instagram/TikTok if applicable
  - **S6 Footer** — Home, Shop, Videos links

## Internal Navigation
- **Header**: Logo/brand (links to home) | Videos | Shop | About | YouTube icon (links to channel)
- **Footer**: Quick links (Home, Videos, Shop, About, Contact) | Social links (YouTube, Instagram) | Newsletter signup | Copyright
- **In-page anchors**: Home page S3 (Featured Episodes) accessible from header nav

## Out of Scope
- Blog/news section (can add later)
- Community forum or user accounts
- Direct merchandise fulfillment (using Printful/Etsy instead)
- Detailed episode descriptions (linked from YouTube)
