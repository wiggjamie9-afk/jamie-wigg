Brainwave Analyzer

Import raw brainwave samples, compute an FFT, split the spectrum into Delta · Theta · Alpha · Beta · Gamma bands, graph the result, and export to CSV. Everything runs in your browser — no upload, no server. Supports OpenVibe and MindwaveReader512 CSV exports, or generic single-/multi-column sample files.

5 · Cross-frequency coupling (phase–amplitude)

Does the phase of a low-frequency rhythm modulate the amplitude of a high-frequency rhythm? Computed via bandpass + Hilbert analytic signal, with a surrogate (shuffled-amplitude) significance test.
