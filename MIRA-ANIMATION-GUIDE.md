# MIRA Animation Guide

## Character Animation Reference

Complete animation guidance for bringing MIRA to life while maintaining character consistency and emotional authenticity.

---

## Principles of Movement

### Core Movement Philosophy

MIRA's movement should embody:
- **Groundedness:** Stable, balanced movements
- **Presence:** Every action is intentional and present
- **Warmth:** Movements are open and welcoming
- **Calmness:** No sudden jerks, smooth natural motion
- **Support:** Movements communicate care and listening

### Movement Quality
- **Pace:** Generally slower than Ziggy, more deliberate
- **Weight:** Movements feel grounded with clear weight distribution
- **Ease:** Flowing, natural movements without tension
- **Responsiveness:** Immediate but not reactive; thoughtful
- **Space:** Uses personal space well, respects boundaries

## Head & Facial Animation

### Eye Animation
- **Blinks:** Regular, natural blinks (approximately every 4-6 seconds)
- **Gaze Direction:** Follows conversation partner naturally
- **Pupil Movement:** Smooth, tracked movement toward focus point
- **Eye Contact:** Direct in supportive moments, brief and respectful

#### Expression-Specific Eye Movements

**Warm Welcoming:**
- Eyes widen slightly in genuine openness
- Eyebrows raised naturally
- Crinkle at corners suggests genuine smile
- Gaze directed toward conversation partner

**Listening/Understanding:**
- Eyes slightly narrowed in focus
- Eyebrows may furrow slightly in concentration
- Direct, steady gaze
- Head tilts slightly toward speaker

**Playful Laughing:**
- Eyes crinkle in joy
- Eyebrows raised
- Pupils may dilate slightly in delight
- Gaze still warm and connected

**Concerned/Supportive:**
- Eyes softened with compassion
- Slightly furrowed brow showing care
- Eyebrows slightly raised
- Gaze gentle and reassuring

### Mouth Animation
- **Smile:** Warm, genuine, using full mouth
- **Lip Sync:** Clear mouth shapes for dialogue
- **Resting Face:** Soft, naturally content expression
- **Breathing:** Subtle chest and shoulder movement suggesting natural breathing

### Head Tilting
- **Listening Pose:** Gentle tilt toward conversation partner (10-15°)
- **Understanding:** Small nod to show comprehension
- **Concern:** Slight head tilt combined with concerned expression
- **Warmth:** Natural head position with open posture

## Body Animation

### Arm Movement

#### Walking Sequence
- **Opposite Leg/Arm:** Natural cross-body walking motion
- **Swing:** Natural arm swing from shoulder, not forced
- **Relaxed:** Elbows slightly bent, wrists relaxed
- **Length:** Arc approximately 30-45° front to back

#### Listening/Sitting
- **Arm Resting:** One or both arms resting naturally
- **Hand Placement:** Hands on lap or at sides
- **Openness:** Arms generally not crossed
- **Readiness:** Hands available to gesture or offer support

#### Offering Support
- **Extension:** Smooth arm extension forward/outward
- **Palm:** Open palm facing partner (vulnerability and openness)
- **Shoulder:** Slight forward lean accompanying gesture
- **Timing:** Matches moment of emotional support

#### Celebration
- **Raising:** Smooth upward arm movement
- **Height:** Arms raised to shoulder height or higher
- **Position:** Both arms raised, open and free
- **Energy:** Lighter, faster movement than normal

### Torso & Spine Movement
- **Breathing:** Subtle rise/fall with natural breathing
- **Leaning:** Gentle forward lean when engaged in conversation
- **Postural Shift:** Smooth transitions between poses
- **Stability:** Lower body remains grounded even during arm movement

### Hand Animation
- **Gesturing:** Natural, purposeful hand gestures during conversation
- **Resting:** Relaxed hand position, not tense or clenched
- **Offering:** Open palm when extending support
- **Detail:** Fingers should be visible and well-defined

### Leg & Feet Movement
- **Standing:** Feet shoulder-width apart, weight evenly distributed
- **Sitting:** Legs relaxed, natural crossing or positioning
- **Walking:** Smooth stride with natural knee bend
- **Balance:** Visible weight shift during movement

## Expression Transitions

### Smile to Listening (Happy to Thoughtful)
**Timing:** 4-6 frames at 24fps (0.17-0.25 seconds)
1. Smile naturally fades
2. Eyes slightly widen in focus
3. Eyebrows adjust to listening position
4. Head tilts toward speaker
5. Hold in attentive listening expression

### Listening to Concerned (Attentive to Caring)
**Timing:** 6-8 frames at 24fps (0.25-0.33 seconds)
1. Eyes soften
2. Eyebrows furrow slightly
3. Mouth relaxes to neutral
4. Head may tilt slightly
5. Overall expression shows care and concern

### Concerned to Supportive Gesture (Expression to Action)
**Timing:** 8-12 frames at 24fps (0.33-0.5 seconds)
1. Expression holds concerned/supportive
2. Arm begins to extend smoothly
3. Shoulder rotates forward slightly
4. Hand opens during extension
5. Hold extended position with warm expression

### Listening to Playful/Laughing (Serious to Joy)
**Timing:** 8-10 frames at 24fps (0.33-0.42 seconds)
1. Eyes suddenly brighten
2. Eyebrows raise
3. Mouth opens in genuine smile/laugh
4. Eyes crinkle at corners
5. Entire face shows joy
6. (Optional: Small head shake or nod of amusement)

## Scene-Specific Animation

### Walking Beside Ziggy
1. **Synchronized Stride:** Match pace to Ziggy
2. **Side Position:** Maintain arm's reach distance
3. **Attentiveness:** Head position suggests engagement
4. **Connection:** Natural arm gestures toward Ziggy
5. **Responsiveness:** React naturally to Ziggy's pace changes

**Key Frames (Walking Cycle):**
- Frame 0: Contact left foot
- Frame 12: Passing position
- Frame 24: Contact right foot
- Frame 36: Passing position (return to Frame 0)
- *Cycle time: 1.5 seconds at 24fps*

### Sitting While Listening
1. **Settling:** Smooth descent to sitting position (2-3 seconds)
2. **Positioning:** Seat with open body language
3. **Focus:** Eyes tracking Ziggy
4. **Presence:** Subtle breathing and natural head position
5. **Responsiveness:** Nodding at key story points
6. **Reaction:** Expression changes reflect story content

**Key Positions:**
- Initial standing position
- Transition to sitting (2-3 frames)
- Settled sitting (holds variation frames)
- Subtle breathing loop (10-15 frames)

### Offering Support
1. **Reach:** Smooth arm extension (6-8 frames)
2. **Palm:** Open hand, fingers slightly spread
3. **Eye Contact:** Direct, warm gaze
4. **Body:** Slight forward lean

6. **Follow-through:** React to Ziggy's response

### Celebration Movement
1. **Build:** Energy gradually increases
2. **Raise:** Smooth arm raising (8-10 frames)
3. **Peak:** Hold raised position with joy
4. **Pulse:** Subtle up/down pulse of arms
5. **Decay:** Gradual return to natural position

## Movement Range & Limits

### Safe Movement Radius
- **Forward/Back:** 30-50% of Ziggy's range (grounded)
- **Side to Side:** Normal walking distance
- **Vertical:** Head height varies 10-15% max
- **Rotation:** Torso rotates max 45° left/right

### Gesture Limits
- **Arm Extension:** Max reach with arm extended
- **Head Tilt:** Max 20° in any direction
- **Body Lean:** Max 15° forward
- **Overall:** Keep character readable and recognizable

### Speed Guidelines
- **Walking:** 0.3-0.5 seconds per step (slower than Ziggy)
- **Arm Gestures:** 0.25-0.5 seconds for most movements
- **Expression Changes:** 0.15-0.33 seconds
- **Head Movements:** 0.25-0.5 seconds

## Technical Animation Specifications

### Frame Rate
- **Standard:** 24 fps (film/animation standard)
- **Smooth Motion:** 60 fps for interactive media
- **VR/Special:** 90 fps or higher

### Animation Timing

**Expression Changes:**
- Quick reactions: 4-6 frames (0.17-0.25 sec)
- Normal changes: 8-12 frames (0.33-0.5 sec)
- Slow transitions: 12-24 frames (0.5-1 sec)

**Movement Actions:**
- Quick gestures: 6-12 frames (0.25-0.5 sec)
- Standard actions: 12-24 frames (0.5-1 sec)
- Slow movements: 24-48 frames (1-2 sec)

### Interpolation Methods
- **Ease In/Out:** Standard for natural movement
- **Linear:** Rare, for specific effects
- **Custom Curves:** Match character personality

### Lip Sync Timing
- **Lead:** Anticipate mouth shapes slightly (1-2 frames before audio)
- **Follow:** Natural lip-sync to dialogue
- **Lag:** Eyes/body follow slightly after mouth (natural)

## Common Animation Combinations

### Greeting Sequence (3-5 seconds)
1. Wave or hand gesture (8 frames)
2. Warm smile expression (4 frames)
3. Eye contact (maintain)
4. Slight head nod (6 frames)
5. Relaxed standing pose (hold)

### Support Offering (4-6 seconds)
1. Listening expression (hold 2 sec)
2. Arm extends smoothly (8 frames)
3. Open palm offered (hold 2-4 sec)
4. Warm, caring expression maintained
5. Eyes track partner's response

### Celebration (3-5 seconds)
1. Build energy (8 frames)
2. Arms raise (10 frames)
3. Joyful expression (hold 2 sec)
4. Small pulse movements (12-frame cycle)
5. Settle down (12 frames back to neutral)

### Walking with Friend (2-4 seconds per step)
1. Natural stride (1.5 sec per step)
2. Occasional glance at friend
3. Subtle head movement with walking rhythm
4. Natural arm swing (opposite to legs)
5. Responsive adjustment to friend's pace

## Emotion Amplitude Levels

### Subtle (Background) Expressions
- Lip corners raise 25%
- Eyes widen 10%
- Head tilt 5°
- Use for ambient/background moments

### Normal (Foreground) Expressions
- Lip corners raise 50%
- Eyes widen 20%
- Head tilt 10°
- Standard use for scenes

### Strong (Dramatic) Expressions
- Lip corners raise 75%
- Eyes widen 30%
- Head tilt 15°
- Use for key emotional moments

### Extreme (Peak Moments)
- Full expression range (100%)
- Maximum eye opening
- Head tilt to 20°
- Use for climactic moments only

## Anti-Patterns (What to Avoid)

### Movement Errors
- ✗ Jerky, robotic movement (should be smooth)
- ✗ Unnatural arm/leg swings
- ✗ Twitching or fidgeting (she's calm)
- ✗ Exaggerated gestures (she's grounded, not comedic)
- ✗ Asymmetrical balance (should be stable)

### Expression Errors
- ✗ Cold or distant expressions (should be warm)
- ✗ Unclear mouth shapes (hard to understand)
- ✗ Staring without blinking (eerie/unnatural)
- ✗ Extreme expressions (should be authentic)
- ✗ Frowning/angry expressions (not her character)

### Timing Errors
- ✗ Too fast movement (loses calmness)
- ✗ Too slow animation (boring, lifeless)
- ✗ Uneven motion (should feel natural)
- ✗ Dialogue out of sync (confusing)
- ✗ No breathing/idle movement (stiff)

## Animation Software Notes

### For Frame-by-Frame Animation
- Use character sheets as reference
- Maintain consistent stroke weight
- Use onion skinning for smooth motion
- Keep layers organized (head, body, arms, etc.)

### For Rigged Animation (Puppet-Pin)
- Create joint placement for natural movement
- Set up IK chains for limbs
- Create expression blend shapes
- Test movement range and limits

### For 3D Animation
- Reference 2D character for color and style
- Use flat, cel-shaded rendering
- Maintain bold outline appearance
- Keep proportions consistent with 2D

### For Motion Capture Cleanup
- Use reference to ensure character consistency
- Add personality to captured movements
- Adjust timing for character feel
- Enhance with hand-drawn details

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Movement Feels Stiff
**Solution:** Add more in-between frames, ease in/out curves, subtle breathing movement

### Expression Feels Cold
**Solution:** Increase eye opening, add eyebrow raise, ensure mouth shape is warm

### Dialogue Hard to Understand
**Solution:** Increase mouth shape clarity, ensure good lip-sync timing, review phoneme accuracy

### Character Looks Imbalanced
**Solution:** Check feet/weight position, ensure shoulders level, adjust stance

### Movement Too Slow
**Solution:** Reduce frame count for movement, tighten timing curves, maintain energy

### Expression Transfers Aren't Working
**Solution:** Check expression timing overlap, ensure smooth transitions, test frame-by-frame
