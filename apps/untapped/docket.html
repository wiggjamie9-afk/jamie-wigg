IMPORTANT — read this

DOCKET is decision support.
Not legal advice.

We help you understand your court paper, track deadlines, and assemble a first draft. We are not a law firm and we do not represent you. Where possible, get a lawyer or call legal aid.

The honest version

DOCKET pulls from official court rules and templates for your state, but every case turns on its own facts. A computer-drafted response is a starting point — not a substitute for a lawyer who has read your file.

If your case involves a possible loss of housing, custody, immigration status, or a criminal consequence — please try to get human legal help before you file anything.

DOCKET does not give legal advice (which would be the unauthorized practice of law). What we do: organize information you give us, calculate dates, and produce template documents you can edit and use.

Free legal help near you

These are real, free or low-cost legal-aid organizations. Most take eviction, debt, and family cases without a fee:

  • Legal Services Corporation — find legal aid by ZIP
  • LawHelp.org — state-by-state self-help portals
  • ABA Free Legal Answers (civil only)

  • Florida Family Law Self-Help
  • ABA Lawyer Referral Directory

Your data

Everything you upload or type stays in your browser's local storage on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. If you clear your browser data, your case file disappears — so use Export binder (top-right ↓ button) to save a copy.

About DOCKET

Built for the ~80% of civil defendants in U.S. courts who appear without a lawyer. The goal is dignified, calm decision support — not a chatbot, not a billable lawyer, just a clear path to your next correct step.

Open source, MIT-licensed, designed to work offline as a PWA. Version 1.0 · 2026.

WELCOME

DOCKET is a calm place for a hard week.

If you got served with a court paper and don't have a lawyer, you're not alone — and you're not late yet. DOCKET helps you read what was filed, track every deadline, and put together a first draft of your response.

This is decision support, not legal advice. We don't represent you. Where you can get a lawyer or legal aid, please do — there's a list in the Help tab.
