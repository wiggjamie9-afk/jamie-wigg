Regulatory

TYMPAN is an accessory app for hearing-aid wearers. It is not a medical device on its own, and it does not replace clinical fitting or care. Always consult your hearing professional for diagnosis and prescription. In the US, TYMPAN operates as a Class I accessory under the FDA's policy for hearing-aid software, including with OTC aids (Lexie, Eargo, Sony, Jabra Enhance).
