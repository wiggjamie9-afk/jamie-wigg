THE MATH

Resellers spend 8 hours doing what RACK does in 40 minutes.

Itemize. Photograph. Brand-research. Price-research. Title-write. Cross-list. Track. Repeat. Multiply by 50 items.

Manual reseller workflow ~8h / 50 items

  1. Photograph each item on a clean background (60 min)
  2. Look up brand + measurements one by one (45 min)
  3. Check sold comps across 4 channels per item (90 min)
  4. Write titles + descriptions that don't get flagged (60 min)
  5. Manually list on Poshmark (75 min)
  6. Re-list same items on Depop, eBay, Mercari, Vinted (180 min)
  7. Delist sold items across other channels to avoid double-sale
  8. Track inventory across 5 disconnected platforms · forever

RACK workflow ~40 min / 50 items

  1. Dump pile on floor · phone photo of the lot (3 min)
  2. RACK auto-segments + ID's every brand (instant)
  3. Condition grade A/B/C with reason per item (instant)
  4. Pulls live comp data from all 5 channels (instant)
  5. Routes each item to the channel paying most for it
  6. One-click cross-posts everything at once
  7. Auto-delists sold items everywhere else
  8. One inventory. One dashboard. One earnings forecast.
