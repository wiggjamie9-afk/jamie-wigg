Step 3 of 3 · Microphone

Let STACK listen for pollution sources

Your microphone is the best free sensor you've already got. STACK listens for the sound signature of frying, gas burners, HVAC hum, vacuum, traffic. Audio never leaves this device. Nothing is recorded or uploaded — we only extract frequency bands.

You can change this any time in Settings. Without the mic, STACK still tracks any sensor readings you enter and flags risk patterns.
