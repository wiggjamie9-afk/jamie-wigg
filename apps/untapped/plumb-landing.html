What PLUMB catches

Eight defect types. On-device.

Trained on 40,000 walkthroughs from working trades. No cloud upload required. The classifier runs on the phone — fast enough to overlay markers in real time.

D01 📐

Off-plumb walls

LiDAR measures deviation to 0.5°. Flags joints, top plates, door jambs that won't pass framing inspection.

D02 🔌

Missing cover plates

Outlets, switches, junction boxes. Code violation, fast fix — but the most-missed punch item on every job.

D03 🎨

Paint scuffs & misses

Touch-up flags with location pinned to wall ID. Distinguishes scuff from intentional accent.

D04 📏

Trim & baseboard gaps

Anything over 1/8" — flagged as code-borderline. Caulk or shim recommendations included.

D05 ⚡

Drywall cracks & pops

Hairline, vertical, settling — categorized so you know whether it's cosmetic or structural concern.

D06 💧

Moisture & staining

Ceiling stains, behind-wall hints from thermal pattern. Auto-flags to investigate above before paint.

D07 🪵

Floor scratches & gouges

Distinguishes from natural grain. Refinish recommendations per material — hardwood, LVP, tile.

D08 🌬️

Window & door seal gaps

Visible perimeter gaps. Air-leak flags. Suggests caulk vs. weatherstrip vs. re-shim.
