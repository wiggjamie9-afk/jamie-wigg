FAQ

Real questions.

Is this actually ELD-compliant?

Yes. AXLE is registered on the FMCSA approved-ELD list. Roadside inspections accept the standard ELD output report directly from the app. If an inspector ever fails the inspection due to AXLE, we pay the fine. (We've never had to.)

Does my phone really do all this without overheating?

The on-device model is sub-30MB and runs at 10fps. Tested on devices as old as iPhone 11 and a $200 Samsung A24. We do recommend a dash mount with airflow on long summer hauls.

Are you going to send my data to my insurance company?

Only if you opt in. AXLE is driver-controlled — you choose whether to share telemetry with an insurer in exchange for the discount. Default is private; nothing leaves your phone.

What about my fleet's dispatcher? Can they see this?

If you're an owner-operator, there is no dispatcher view. If you're on a small-fleet plan, the fleet admin sees aggregate safety scores — never raw cam footage. Drivers can audit every share.

Does it work without cell signal?

Yes. AXLE is offline-first. ELD logs, alerts, and safety scores all run locally. When you hit signal again, things sync up.
