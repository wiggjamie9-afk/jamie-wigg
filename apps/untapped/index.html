The shape of the opportunity

Each app lives in a niche where enterprise software already proves the value at $50k+/seat — OR where rich-country consumers have an option but 500M+ people elsewhere don't. That's where the white space is — not inventing demand, but routing existing demand through cheaper modern stacks (on-device ML, small multimodal models, voice agents).

All ten prototypes are single-file HTML. Open them in any browser. No build, no deps. The landing pages are unrelated visual identities — each tuned to its niche. The READMEs are short business cases meant to be skimmed.
