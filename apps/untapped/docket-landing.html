This is not legal advice. It is self-help.

What that means: DOCKET is a self-help tool that fills out court forms and explains procedural rules — the same things you'd learn at a court self-help center, just faster. Every template is reviewed by a licensed attorney in the relevant state. We are not your lawyer. We do not represent you in court. We do not give advice about your specific case strategy.

If you can talk to a lawyer or legal aid attorney about your case — please do. We list free legal aid for every county we operate in, and we will hand off your file to them with one tap. DOCKET partners with court self-help centers and legal aid organizations because they should be doing this work; we just want to make their work scale.
