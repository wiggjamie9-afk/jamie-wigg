Five detections, on-device

What HERD catches before you can.

No tags. No collars. No installed cameras. The model runs on a $80 Android handset — fully offline. Each detection is hand-labelled by veterinary teams in our partner districts.

01

Lameness

Gait-asymmetry scoring 1–5 from a 4-second video clip. Catches limps before the cow refuses to milk.

02

Mastitis

Quarter asymmetry + heat detection. Flags potential clinical cases ~24h before the milker spots them.

03

Early calving

Posture + isolation behaviour pattern. Predicts stage-2 labour onset within ±2 hours.

04

Body condition

BCS 1–5 from rib and hook-bone profile. Catches drift before lactation collapses or breeding fails.

05

Rumination

Counts cud-chews per minute from jaw motion. Drop in rumination is the earliest sickness signal we have.
