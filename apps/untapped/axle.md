# AXLE

**Fleet-grade safety. Single-truck price.**

## Pitch
Phone-as-dashcam. Fatigue detection (eye closure, head pose, micro-sleep), lane-keeping warnings, ergonomic posture coaching, ELD-compliant logbook. Samsara/Motive sell to fleets at $40/truck/month — they ignore the 350k US owner-operators and don't even try the 15M+ trucks across Latin America, India, and Southeast Asia. AXLE: $19/mo, BYO phone, insurance-discount-ready.

## TAM
- **US owner-operators:** 350,000.
- **US company drivers (small fleets <20 trucks):** ~1.7M — currently underserved by enterprise tools.
- **Mexico:** ~1.2M registered commercial trucks.
- **Brazil:** ~2.5M.
- **India:** ~10M+ commercial vehicles.
- **Indonesia + SEA:** ~5M+.
- **Africa:** ~3M+.
- **Total addressable:** ~25M trucks where fleet-grade telematics is unaffordable.
- **Penetration of fleet telematics outside North America:** <5%.

## Who buys
- **US owner-operators** (1-truck businesses — biggest single segment, $19/mo is impulse-priced).
- **Hot-shot haulers** (Class 3-5 trucks doing freight-on-demand).
- **Regional small-fleet operators** (3-15 trucks — too small for Samsara).
- **International drivers** in emerging markets where any telematics is unaffordable.
- **Insurance partners** (Progressive Commercial, Geico Commercial, NEXT) — usage-based pricing for solo truckers.

## Why now
- **On-device fatigue detection:** smartphone CPUs run face/pose models that needed dedicated DSPs three years ago.
- **Insurance UBI (usage-based insurance)** is the fastest-growing segment in commercial auto. Insurers actively seek telematics they can underwrite on.
- **Driver shortage** = retention matters → safer drivers stay employed longer.
- **FMCSA ELD mandate** + global equivalents (EU tachograph, India AIS-140) make compliance non-negotiable; today's solo drivers cobble together $400 standalone ELDs.

## Tech
- **On-device driver-monitoring:** face landmark + eye-aspect-ratio model. ~30MB. Runs at 10fps on a $200 Android.
- **Lane detection:** rear-facing phone-cam OR BYO consumer dashcam via Bluetooth/RTSP.
- **HOS (hours-of-service) logbook:** FMCSA-spec ELD output. Compliance audit + roadside-inspection PDF.
- **Ergonomic posture:** front cam tracks driver posture; gentle audio prompts ("lumbar slumped, adjust seat").
- **Multi-language:** EN, ES, PT, HI, ID, AR — primary trucker languages.
- **Offline-first:** signal is unreliable on rural/cross-country routes; AXLE caches and syncs.

## Regulatory landscape
- **US FMCSA:** ELD certification required. ~$30k certification process; AXLE certifies day 1.
- **EU:** Tachograph regulations don't directly apply to AXLE (it's supplemental safety, not legal time-keeping). Optional EU expansion later.
- **India AIS-140:** mandatory GPS + panic-button compliance for commercial vehicles. AXLE meets spec.

## 90-day GTM
- **Days 1-30:** Partner discussion with ONE insurer (Progressive Commercial — largest US commercial auto, active UBI program). Build the telematics-data-share API to their underwriting.
- **Days 31-60:** Launch with trucker influencers on TikTok and YouTube — there's a thriving owner-operator content community ("Trucking with Dean," "Long Haul Trucker"). High organic conversion.
- **Days 61-90:** Mid-America Trucking Show (Louisville, KY — March) or GATS (Dallas — August). Trucker trade shows are where the buying decisions happen.

## Moat
- **Insurance partnerships** — multi-year contracts that exclude competitors per truck-mile-covered.
- **Multilingual + emerging-market** focus — Samsara won't follow into a $9/mo PMPM market.
- **Driver-trusted brand** — truckers are deeply suspicious of "fleet" tools that surveil. AXLE markets itself as "for the driver, not the dispatcher."
- **ELD certification + AIS-140 + tachograph compatibility** — each takes months. Stacking jurisdictions is defensible.

## Tone notes
Blue-collar, blunt, money-forward. Truckers reject Silicon Valley aesthetics on sight. Marketing copy: "fleet-grade safety, single-truck price" is the hook. Avoid SaaS jargon — say "your truck," not "your fleet endpoint." Pricing pages quote in dollars and cents, not "starting from."
