🔍 Buddy Receipt Verifier

Load an exported ledger. This page recomputes every hash and signature in your browser. We can't fake a pass — the math either checks out or it doesn't.
What this checks, honestly:
1. hash — recomputes SHA-256 of each receipt body; proves contents weren't edited.
2. sig — verifies the ECDSA P-256 signature against the public key in the file; proves it was signed by the key that built the ledger.
3. chain — checks each receipt links to the previous one's hash; proves nothing was deleted or reordered.

What it does NOT claim: it can't prove the response text came from Anthropic (that needs Anthropic to sign their output). It proves the log is honest and unaltered.
