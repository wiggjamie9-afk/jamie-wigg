Three structured checks

Made for the 30-cow farmer with no formal training.

A vet with years of experience can spot these signs. HerdCheck helps you make the same observations, score them the same way every time, and know when it's time to call for help. Each check ends in a tier and a plain-language action list.

Lameness

Sprecher 5-point locomotion scale

Record a short clip of the animal walking, then score its gait on the published 1–5 locomotion scale — back arch, stride length and weight-bearing. The score is the signal; nothing overrides it.

Score 4–5 — clear lameness, inspect hooves today
Score 2–3 — early signs, re-score in 48 hrs
Score 1 — normal gait

Mastitis

Visible signs + milk + photo check

Tick the visible signs — swelling, asymmetry, redness, heat, pain, yield drop — and pick the milk appearance (normal, watery, clotted, bloody). A photo of the udder adds a light corroborating signal, never the deciding one.

Clotted or bloody milk, or heavy signs — withhold milk, call vet
Some signs present — strip the quarter, re-check in 12 hrs
No signs today — maintain teat hygiene

Calving predictor

Gestation day + pre-calving signs

From the breeding date and the species' gestation length, HerdCheck works out how many days are left, then weighs the pre-calving signs you observe — bagging up, vulva swelling, mucus, restlessness, water bag. A visible water bag forces "active labour" on its own.

Within 24 hrs or water bag — move to a clean calving area now
Within 1–14 days — prepare the pen, check twice daily
Still weeks out — routine monitoring
Built around your animals

Four species, the right gestation length for each.

The calving predictor uses species-specific gestation defaults so the days-left estimate is honest for whatever you keep. It's only ever as accurate as the breeding date you enter.

Three taps in the field

Point the camera, answer a few prompts, get an action.

No reading manuals. No internet round-trips. Everything happens on the phone, in seconds, even in a shed with no signal.

1

Point the camera

Film a few seconds of the animal walking, or snap the udder. The capture stays on your device — nothing is uploaded.

2

Answer a few prompts

Tap through the structured checklist — large buttons, plain words, your language. Add the breeding date once and HerdCheck tracks the rest.

3

Get a red / amber / green action

A clear risk tier with the reasons behind it and concrete next steps written for a non-vet — then the worst-off animals rise to the top of your herd list.

It's decision support, not a diagnosis.

The photo analysis is a coarse colour-and-symmetry heuristic that can only nudge a result — it can never flip a clear result on its own, and it is not a computer-vision diagnosis. HerdCheck helps you observe, score and track consistently. It does not replace a vet or extension worker. Any red mastitis result means withholding that animal's milk from human consumption until a vet has reviewed it.

Field-ready by design

Offline-first. No account. Your data stays on your phone.

HerdCheck was built for low-coverage areas and farmers who shouldn't have to trust a server with their herd. By default, nothing leaves the device.

Works fully offline

A service worker and on-device database mean every check runs with no signal. Nothing syncs unless you choose to export.

No login, no telemetry

No account to create, no password to lose, no tracking. Your animals, observations and photos live in your phone's storage alone.

You own the export

Only you decide if data leaves the device — export to CSV for a co-op, JSON for a full backup, or share an alerts summary by SMS.

Install it like an app

Add HerdCheck to your home screen for a full-screen experience on Android and iOS — big tap targets and sunlight-readable colours.
