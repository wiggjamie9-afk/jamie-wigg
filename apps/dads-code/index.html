Learn the harm — what today's stress does to you

Chronic stress keeps cortisol high, suppresses your vagal tone, and dysregulates the nervous system that's meant to calm you down. Slow breathing and humming push the other way — in minutes.

Humming raises nasal nitric oxide roughly 15× and, on a Holter monitor, produced the lowest stress index of any state measured — including sleep.
