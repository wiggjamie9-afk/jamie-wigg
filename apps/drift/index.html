Set a consistent wake time
Your circadian rhythm anchors to your wake time, not your bedtime. Even on weekends, try to wake within ±30 minutes of your target.
📵
Screen-free wind-down
Blue light suppresses melatonin for up to 3 hours. Put your phone face-down 60 minutes before your target bedtime.
🌡️
Cool room = deeper sleep
Core body temperature needs to drop 1–2°C to initiate sleep. Keep your bedroom between 16–19°C for optimal slow-wave sleep.
☕
Caffeine half-life is 5–7 hours
An afternoon coffee at 3pm still has half its caffeine in your system at 9pm. Aim for no caffeine after 1pm if you're a slow metaboliser.
