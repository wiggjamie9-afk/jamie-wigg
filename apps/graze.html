About GRAZE

A planning aid for rotational / managed grazing. Computes a starting estimate of grazing days per paddock from stocking rate and available forage, builds a forward move schedule, and tracks rest days so you never return to a paddock too soon. Always confirm against what you see in the field. Pairs with HerdCheck for animal health.
