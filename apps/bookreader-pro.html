🤝 Buddy Mode

🎵 Voice-First Reading for Visually Impaired

Welcome to Your Reading Buddy! 👋

"Hi there! I'm your reading buddy. I'm here to help you enjoy books, articles, and documents by reading them aloud in a clear, friendly voice. Everything I read, I'll also explain in simple words. You can control the speed, ask questions, and I'll help you understand everything. Let's start by scanning a book or article!"

How Your Buddy Can Help:

📖 Read Books Aloud
Scan or upload a book page. Your buddy reads it to you with high-quality voice.
🔍 Explain Words
When you hear a hard word, ask "What does [word] mean?" and your buddy explains it.
❓ Answer Questions
Ask anything about what you're reading. Your buddy explains in simple language.
📚 Describe Pictures
Your buddy describes any images so you know what's happening in the story.
⏸️ Pause & Control
Control speed, repeat sections, go back—whatever pace works for you.
💬 Just Chat
Not in the mood for reading? Chat with your buddy about anything!

💡 Pro Tips for Best Experience:
  • • Use headphones for the clearest sound
  • • Start with slower speed if you're new—you can speed up anytime
  • • Don't hesitate to ask your buddy to repeat or explain
  • • Your buddy learns what topics help you most
  • • No rush—reading should be enjoyable, not stressful
