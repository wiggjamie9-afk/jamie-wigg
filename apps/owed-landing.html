Questions

Honest answers

Is this legit? Is the money real?

Yes — class-action settlements are real and court-approved, and unclaimed funds run into the billions. Owed points you to the official administrator for each one so you can verify and file directly. We're an information and organisation tool, not the people paying out.

Do you see my personal data?

No. Your profile, claims and saved details live only on your device (in your browser/app storage). We don't have a server that stores them, we don't read your email, and we never ask for your bank login or SSN.

Will you file claims for me?

Owed helps you prepare and organise your claim and links you to the official site to submit it. You stay in control and file on the court-approved administrator's page. Only ever file for settlements you genuinely qualify for — false claims are fraud.

Is this legal advice?

No. Owed is not a law firm and doesn't give legal advice. For anything specific to your situation, talk to a qualified attorney.
