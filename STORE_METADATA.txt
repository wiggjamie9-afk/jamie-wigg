## NOTES

1. **Pricing psychology:** $2.99/month is optimal for emerging markets (India, Africa, Southeast Asia). Users see it as premium but affordable.

2. **Keywords:** Research in Google Play Console and App Store Connect search bars. Use apps that rank for your keywords.

3. **Icons:** 512×512 PNG (Google Play), 1024×1024 PNG (App Store). Rounded corners optional.

4. **Screenshots:** Keep them simple. Show app UI + benefit text. Avoid complex graphics.

5. **Descriptions:** Optimize for keywords naturally. Don't keyword-stuff.

6. **Updates:** Plan monthly updates to stay visible on app store fronts (even if just tweaking screenshots/description).
