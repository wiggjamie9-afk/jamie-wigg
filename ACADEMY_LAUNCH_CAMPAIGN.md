## Success Criteria

Launch is successful if by end of week 2:
- ✅ 20+ students enrolled
- ✅ $10K+ revenue
- ✅ 0 refunds requested
- ✅ Discord active (10+ messages/day)
- ✅ First module feedback positive
- ✅ No major bugs
- ✅ Automation working (emails, discord bot)

If any of these miss:
- **Low enrollments**: Boost Twitter/Reddit ad spend, cold email warm list
- **Refund requests**: Improve module clarity, add extra support, revise FAQ
- **Inactive Discord**: Do more daily posts, run engagement challenges, share wins
- **Technical issues**: Fix bugs, test all flows end-to-end
