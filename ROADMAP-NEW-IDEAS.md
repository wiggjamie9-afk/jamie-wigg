# Roadmap — New Product Ideas (with honest notes)

Captured as you raised them. Honest assessment on each so we build the *real* version,
not a fantasy version.

## #11 — Smart Spend (cost-of-living deals agent)
**Your idea:** an agent that finds the best buys for each person and surfaces real-time
online sales tailored to them — fighting the cost-of-living squeeze.

**Honest take:** Genuinely useful and timely. People would love this. BUT the hard part
isn't the "brain" — it's the **data**. Real-time prices/deals come from retailer APIs,
affiliate feeds (Amazon Associates, Skimlinks, etc.), or scraping (which many sites block
and some forbid in their terms). So the realistic build is:
- **v1 (buildable now):** a personal "deal helper" — you paste/enter what you want to buy,
  it gives smart buying guidance: when to buy, what to compare, price-drop tactics, dupes,
  red flags ("fake discount" detection). No live feed needed. Honest and useful.
- **v2:** plug into 1–2 legit affiliate/price APIs for a few categories. Revenue via
  affiliate commissions (transparent to the user).
- **What it is NOT:** a magic "scans the whole internet live" engine on day one. That needs
  real data deals/partnerships and money.

**Revenue:** affiliate commissions + optional premium. Big market.

## #12 — Market Pulse (business trend reader) — ⚠️ read this carefully
**Your idea:** a brain that reads trends and trading like Wall Street, for business.

**Honest take — important:** I will not build, and you should be wary of anyone who sells,
a system that claims to "trade like Wall Street" or predict markets for profit. The honest
facts: markets are largely unpredictable, the large majority of active traders underperform
a simple index fund, and automated trading advice is **regulated** (financial-advice laws).
A "quantum brain" does not change this. Promising people market returns is how people lose
money — that's the opposite of the mission.

**What we CAN build honestly and valuably:**
- A **market & business *education* brain** — one lesson a day on how markets, money,
  cycles, and business trends actually work (demystifying, not predicting).
- A **research/summary assistant** — reads public news/filings and *summarises* what's
  happening and why, clearly labelled as information, **not advice**.
- A **small-business trend reader** — spots what's rising in a niche (search interest,
  social signals) to help someone decide what to make/sell. Useful, lower-risk.

**Rule:** information and education = yes. "We'll predict the market and make you money" = no.
