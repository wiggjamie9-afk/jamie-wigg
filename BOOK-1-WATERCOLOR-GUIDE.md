## PAGE 1: COVER PAGE

### WATERCOLOR ILLUSTRATION DETAILS:
Soft watercolor illustration of Sunny the quokka sitting peacefully under a starry night sky. Golden-brown quokka with warm, gentle eyes and a soft smile. Surrounded by soft pastel blues (twilight sky), warm golds (moonlight), and soft purples. Tall eucalyptus trees frame the sides. A full moon glowing cream-white overhead. Stars scattered across deep blue-purple sky. Flowers and moss at Sunny's feet in soft greens and golds. Overall mood: calm, dreamy, bedtime-ready. Watercolor style with soft edges, no harsh lines. Pastel palette throughout.

### COVER TEXT:
Sunny's Cozy Quokka
Bedtime Tales

By Jamie Wigg

## PAGE 2: PAGE 2: GOLDEN AFTERNOON

### WATERCOLOR ILLUSTRATION DETAILS:
Soft watercolor: Sunny the quokka sitting on a mossy rock in golden afternoon light. Sky is transitioning from warm gold to soft peachy-orange. Gentle rays of sunlight filtering through eucalyptus leaves in pale yellows and warm golds. Ground covered in soft moss in muted greens. Sunny in foreground, looking peaceful, warm brown fur catching the golden light. Background shows distant gum trees in soft green and blue hues. Overall feeling: warm, safe, drowsy.

### TEXT (2 LINES):
As the warm golden afternoon faded gently away, little Sunny the quokka sat on his favourite mossy rock and looked up at the sky.

## PAGE 3: PAGE 3: SUNSET COLORS

### WATERCOLOR ILLUSTRATION DETAILS:
Watercolor sunset scene: Sky ablaze with soft pink (like flower petals), warm peachy-orange, and beginning of deep soft purple. Sunny still on rock, eyes wide with wonder looking upward. Silhouettes of gum trees becoming darker against the colored sky. Soft lavender shadows on the ground. Pastel pink clouds. Stars just beginning to appear. Mood: magical transition, bedtime approaching.

### TEXT (2 LINES):
The sky was turning the most beautiful colours Sunny had ever seen. Soft pink, like the inside of a flower. Warm orange, like a ripe peach.

## PAGE 4: PAGE 4: TWILIGHT QUIET

### WATERCOLOR ILLUSTRATION DETAILS:
Watercolor twilight scene: Deep blue-purple sky. Sunny sitting very still on rock, surrounded by darker shadows. Birds tucking into tree nests (visible as silhouettes). Soft glow of remaining light in warm cream and pale gold on horizons. Stars becoming visible. Crickets represented as small glowing dots. Pastel shadows in soft purples and blues. Ground in dark greens and browns. Overall mood: quiet, peaceful, transition to night.

### TEXT (2 LINES):

## PAGE 5: PAGE 5: FIRST STAR

Soft glow around the star in pale gold and cream. Trees in dark silhouette below. Ground in deep greens and purples. Mood: magical moment, wonder, the beginning of night's beauty. Soft watercolor wash technique.
