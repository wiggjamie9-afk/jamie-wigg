## 5. Posting Time Strategy — Australia/Perth (AWST, UTC+8)

The big problem: Perth is 12 hours offset from US Eastern. Posting at 9am AWST hits 6pm PT the previous day, which is too late for US morning engagement.

**The fix: post twice per day on launch week, once for each hemisphere.**

| Tweet | AWST | EST | PT | London | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Big launch tweet | **Fri 09:00 AWST** | Thu 21:00 EST | Thu 18:00 PT | Fri 02:00 BST | Hits US evening scroll + Asia/Oceania morning |
| Launch thread | **Fri 09:30 AWST** | Thu 21:30 EST | Thu 18:30 PT | — | Same wave, lets the headline breathe 30 min first |
| US morning re-up (RT or fresh QT of the launch tweet) | **Fri 22:00 AWST** | Fri 10:00 EST | Fri 07:00 PT | Fri 15:00 BST | Catches the US workday open |
| Drip tweets (T-3 to T+7) | **09:00 AWST** | 21:00 EST prev day | 18:00 PT prev day | 02:00 BST | One per day, US-evening primary window |
| Reply triage | Run replies between **08:00–22:00 AWST** | 20:00–10:00 EST | 17:00–07:00 PT | — | Catches both hemispheres during waking hours |

**Rule of thumb for solo-founder reach from Perth:**
- Launch headlines + threads → post at **09:00 AWST** (US evening, the highest-engagement window globally).
- Reflection / founder-voice tweets → post at **22:00 AWST** (US morning, lower competition, higher reply depth).
- Never post 12:00–17:00 AWST — that's the global engagement dead zone (US asleep, Europe lunch, Asia commuting).

**Reply window:** Stay reachable for the 14 hours that overlap US waking hours (20:00–10:00 AWST). Mute the timeline outside that.
