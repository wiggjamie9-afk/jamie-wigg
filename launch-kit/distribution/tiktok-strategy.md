## 1. Channel positioning

RHYTHMIX TikTok is not viral-music TikTok. We are not chasing dance trends, sped-up snippets, or 8-second hook compilations. The audience we want is the **working creator** — bedroom producers, first-time songwriters, solo artists who already pay LANDR, DistroKid, Suno, or Udio every month and are quietly tired of stitching five subscriptions together to release one track.

The channel sells one idea: *Suno writes the song. Udio writes the song. LANDR masters the song. Then what?* RHYTHMIX runs the whole journey — generate, master, distribute, earn — for AU$149 once, lifetime.

We win attention by **building publicly** (founder shipping from an iPhone in Perth), commenting on **AI music news** before the music-tech podcasts catch up, **showcasing real customers** releasing real tracks, and teaching **"how to release without a label"** as evergreen value. Polish loses here. Authenticity, specificity, and an obvious point of view win.

## 2. Posting cadence

**5 posts/week**, Tuesday through Saturday. Skip Sunday/Monday — engagement dips and creator audiences are recharging.

- **Tue 7am AWST** (Mon 4pm PT / 7pm ET) — catches US evening scroll, the largest single creator pool.
- **Wed 9am AWST** (Tue 6pm PT) — same window, second push.
- **Thu 7pm AWST** (Thu 7am UK / 12pm CET) — UK/EU morning commute.
- **Fri 8pm AWST** (Fri 5am PT) — global Friday energy, best day for shareable hot-takes.
- **Sat 11am AWST** (Fri 8pm PT) — US Friday-night scroll, peak weekend watch time.

Batch-shoot Sunday afternoon, schedule via TikTok Studio. Reserve one slot/week for **reactive content** (drop a scheduled post if AI music news breaks). Reply-to-comment videos count as bonus posts and don't replace the 5.

## 4. Per-video structure (universal)

Every video follows this beat sheet. If a concept can't be told in this shape, it isn't a TikTok — push it to long-form.

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0-1s | Visual + on-screen text. No logo, no intro, no "Hey guys." Pattern-interrupt only. |
| **Setup** | 1-4s | Establish stakes. "POV: …" or "Here's the thing about …" Audience should know what they're about to learn or feel by 4s. |
| **Payoff** | 5-50s | Deliver. Demo, story, hot-take, reveal. Cuts every 2-3 seconds. Captions burned in for sound-off. |
| **CTA** | 50-55s | Single, specific action. "Link in bio" + URL ticker. Never two CTAs. Never "follow, like, comment, share." |

Hard limits: total length 15-55s. No videos over 60s unless story-driven and tested. First frame must contain on-screen text — TikTok preview thumbnails are 1080×1920 frame-zero by default.

## 5. Sound + caption strategy

**Sounds.** Use trending sounds for POV/skit (P1-P5) and reaction (R1-R2) videos — they're the algorithm's preferred carrier. Check TikTok Creative Center weekly for sounds with 10k-100k uses (sweet spot — proven but not saturated). Demos (D1-D5) and founder voice (F1-F3) carry their own VO; layer a subtle lo-fi bed under VO (-18dB) so the video isn't dead silent in the For You scroll. Original sounds on founder videos build a sound library competitors can't copy.

**Captions.** Burn in TikTok-native auto-captions, then hand-correct. Sound-off viewers are 50%+ of the feed. Caption pacing matches speech, never longer than 4 words/line. Use the in-app TikTok caption font (white, drop-shadow) — looks native, not "edited."

**Hashtag stacks (3 variants per post):**
- **Discovery (broad reach):** #musicproducer #bedroomproducer #aimusic #suno #udio #landr #distrokid #musicianlife
- **Niche (qualified audience):** #soloartist #firsttimemusician #musictech2026 #releaseyourmusic #musicroyalties #aimusicians
- **Brand + AU:** #rhythmix #rhythmixapp #aussiemusicians #perthmusic #buildinginpublic #foundervlog

Rotate stacks per post. Never repeat the same 5-tag combo two posts in a row — TikTok flags it as low-effort.
