---

## 1. Positioning vs TikTok

Instagram's music-creator audience skews 25-44, English-speaking, and is materially closer to the industry than TikTok's 16-24 entertainment-first base. They follow producers, A&R reps, sync agents, and indie-label founders. They save Reels they intend to act on rather than re-share for clout. That changes the content mix: less dance-format trend-chasing, more "behind the build" craft, more product-in-context demos, more credibility-laden comparisons. The hook still has to land in 2-3 seconds, but the payoff can be a 25-second teardown rather than a 9-second punchline. Caption length matters here — Instagram users *read* captions before deciding to save, where TikTok users scroll past them.

## 2. Posting cadence + format mix

Weekly: **4 Reels + 2 carousels + 1 grid post**. Reels drive 70%+ of reach and Explore-page discovery; the carousels deepen the saved-by-pros pile (frameworks, comparison tables, screenshots-with-callouts); the single grid post per week is the "credibility anchor" — a polished still from a 60s clip, the four-pillar diagram, or a customer quote card. Cross-post 2 of 4 Reels to TikTok the same week but lead with IG-native edits (no TikTok watermark, IG-safe music). Post Reels Tue/Wed/Thu/Sat 7-9pm AEST. Carousels Mon/Fri morning. Grid post Sunday — the "weekly recap" slot.

## 3. The 12 Reel concepts

Each concept lists: hook, beats, payoff, length, CTA. All vertical, 1080×1920, 30fps.

### Behind the build (3)

**B1. "I built this from my iPhone."**
- Hook (0-2s): Close-up of an iPhone screen showing the RHYTHMIX dashboard with a track rendering. No text.
- Beats (2-15s): Quick cuts — Higgsfield prompt → AI video frame → ElevenLabs voice → HyperFrames render → final MP4 on the phone. Show actual file names, no fake screen recordings.
- Payoff (15-25s): "No desktop. No Pro Tools. No studio." Text overlay: *Made entirely on iPhone 15 Pro.*
- CTA: "Pipeline link in bio."
- Length: 25s.

**B2. "Here's the AI music video pipeline."**
- Hook (0-3s): A finished 30s RHYTHMIX music video plays at 2x speed in the corner.
- Beats: Caption-by-caption walkthrough — Suno/Udio → master → Higgsfield image-to-video → Kling 2.6 beat-sync → distribute. Four screen-recordings stitched.
- Payoff (20-30s): Side-by-side: same track, with vs without the visual layer.
- CTA: "Built into RHYTHMIX. AU$149 lifetime."
- Length: 30s.

**B3. "Why I made it AU$149 lifetime."**
- Hook (0-2s): Hand crossing out a "$29/mo" sticker and replacing it with "$149 once."
- Beats: Voiceover — "Suno is $30/mo. Udio is $30/mo. LANDR is $99/yr. DistroKid is $23/yr. Together that's $700+ in year one." Numbers stack on screen.
- Payoff (18-25s): "RHYTHMIX is one platform. One payment. Built by one person. So it can be one price."
- CTA: "Lifetime tier closes when I hit 500 users."
- Length: 25s.

## 4. Hook templates (5 reusable openers)

1. **The receipt/screenshot reveal** — open with a real artifact (bank notification, Spotify dashboard, cancelled subscription email). Blur amounts, keep the brand visible.
2. **The split-second timer** — "X seconds. Watch." A countdown overlay forces watch-time.
3. **The unfinished sentence** — voiceover starts mid-thought ("…and that's when I realised the label was the bottleneck"). Curiosity gap, no preamble.
4. **The price strike-through** — visible cross-out of a competitor's monthly fee replaced by "$149 once." Works because it telegraphs the entire video.
5. **The contrarian one-liner** — text-only frame: "You don't need a producer." Hold for 1.5s. Then cut to the proof.

## 5. Caption + hashtag strategy

**Caption shape (every Reel):**
- Line 1: A standalone provocation or hook restatement, ≤8 words. Often a question.
- Line 2-3: One concrete claim with a specific number.
- Line 4: A save/share prompt — "Save this if you've been quoted $5k for a single track."
- Line 5: CTA — "rhythmixapp.com.au · AU$149 lifetime."
- Line 6: 25-30 hashtags, grouped (see below).

**Hashtag stack — 28 tags, three tiers:**

*Broad (10):* `#musicproducer` `#musicproduction` `#aimusic` `#indiemusic` `#bedroomproducer` `#songwriter` `#musicstudio` `#musicianlife` `#newmusic` `#producers`

*Niche (12):* `#aimusicproduction` `#sunomusic` `#udio` `#landralternative` `#distrokid` `#musicdistribution` `#musicroyalties` `#syncready` `#indielabel` `#musictech` `#solomusician` `#firsttimeartist`

*Branded + campaign (6):* `#rhythmix` `#rhythmixapp` `#fourpillars` `#generatemastered` `#149lifetime` `#bedroomtocharts`

**Cadence:**
- Reels: 28 tags, mix all three tiers, rotate per post.
- Carousels: 20 tags, lean broad-to-niche.
- Grid: 15 tags, lean branded + niche.

**Save/share copy that proven to land on IG:**
- "Save this for the next time someone says AI music can't be monetised."
- "Share with the producer in your group chat who's still paying for LANDR."
- "Tag the bedroom artist who needs to see this."

**Posting time (Sydney-based audience, AEST):**
- Reels: Tue/Wed/Thu/Sat 7-9pm AEST (8am-9am US East, peak global overlap).
- Carousels: Mon/Fri 7am AEST.
- Grid: Sun 10am AEST.
