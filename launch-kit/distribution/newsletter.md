## The big news

**RHYTHMIX turns your MP3 into a cinematic AI music video.** You drop the track in. It listens for the beat, the build, the drop. It picks shots across four frontier video models — Kling for cinematic scenes, Hunyuan for motion and realism, Luma for dream sequences, MiniMax for character shots — cuts on transients, color-locks the whole thing to your track's mood, and renders the finished cut. About 45 minutes from upload to download. No editing. No prompts. No timeline. The track itself is the brief.

Alongside the main app, four companion tools ship the same week — they share the FREQUENCY DNA but specialise:

- **RESONATE** — closed-loop biometric × generative music × spatial audio. Music that adapts to your nervous system in real time.
- **DREAMS** — generative bedtime ritual with dream-recall journalling the next morning.
- **LIVE** — beat-synced AI music video co-pilot, faster + lighter than the main RHYTHMIX engine. For producers who release weekly.
- **HUM** — daily humming practice. Two minutes, designed for the morning, tracks tone over time.

Each landing page is up at rhythmixapp.com.au. Pick the one that fits you. Or get the bundle and stop thinking about it.

## Why now, why this

The economics of music video changed twice in the last 18 months. First, four genuinely cinematic AI video models came online — each producing real output for cents per second of footage. Second, indie musicians figured out that a strong visual cuts attention-cost-per-stream by 4–6x on TikTok and Reels.

So a $5,000 music video became, mathematically, a $20 inference job.

But the tools that actually shipped to musicians did the thing tools always do: they slapped a $10–15/month subscription on top. Suno Pro $10/mo. Runway $15/mo. LANDR somewhere between. Each one covers one slice — Suno writes the song, Udio writes the song, LANDR masters the song. **Then what?** Five years of that and you've spent what a real studio used to cost. With the tracks still locked behind an active card on file.

RHYTHMIX is what I wanted to exist. One payment. Four pillars under one roof. Generate, master, distribute, earn. None of the named competitors do all four. That's the bet.
