## What RHYTHMIX is

You drop in your MP3. RHYTHMIX listens for the beat, the build, the drop. It picks shots from all four frontier models — Kling, Hunyuan, Luma, MiniMax — runs them through a transient-cutter, color-locks the whole thing to your track's mood, and renders the finished music video.

**No editing. No prompts. No timeline. The track itself is the brief.**

About 45 minutes from upload to download. As many videos as your credits allow. Lifetime updates on the product itself — every model upgrade, every new feature, every new shot library, automatically unlocked on your account.

## What you get the moment you purchase

**The Studio** — drop-zone for MP3, automatic beat / build / drop detection, shot-library selector, mood lock, render queue.

**Four frontier engines under one cut:**

· **Kling** — cinematic, photoreal scenes, the closest a generation model gets to a Steadicam tracking shot
· **Hunyuan** — motion-rich, realistic action, the model that handles dancers and crowds
· **Luma Dream Machine** — surreal, dreamlike sequences, the model for ambient and lo-fi
· **MiniMax** — character shots, lip-synced talking heads, performance close-ups

**The Beat-Sync Cutter** — analyses the waveform for transients, locks shot durations to your track's BPM, holds long shots on builds, cuts hard on drops. The same editing instinct a music-video director uses, automated.

**Mood Lock (Color Grading)** — analyses the track's spectral character and locks the colour grade across all shots to match. Lo-fi gets warm grain. Trap gets deep blacks. Ambient gets desaturated wash. House gets neon. Folk gets film stock. Your video looks like one piece, not a montage of disparate model outputs.

**Shot Libraries** — 400+ pre-tested shot prompts across 16 genres (electronic, hip-hop, indie rock, ambient, folk, jazz, classical, lo-fi, trap, house, drum-and-bass, country, pop, R&B, metal, experimental). Pick a library, RHYTHMIX picks the shots. Or override individual shots if you want creative control.

**Render Queue** — queue 5 videos in parallel. Push your album in one afternoon.

**Lifetime updates** — every new model that comes online (Kling 3, Hunyuan v3, Sora when it opens, whatever's next) gets added to RHYTHMIX and is available to lifetime members at no additional cost. The product price never goes up for buyers locked in today.

**Generation credits bundled with purchase** — enough credits to render approximately 12–15 typical 3-minute music videos. Top up at cost when you exceed them (we don't make money on inference, only on the product). Bring-your-own API key supported for power users.

**The Brief Page Generator** — auto-generates a press-kit-style brief page for every video you render, with the lyrics, the shot list, the model breakdown, and a direct embed link. Drop it on your release page or send it to playlist editors.
