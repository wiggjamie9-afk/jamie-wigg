## Prepped FAQ replies

### Q1 — "Is this another meditation app? How is it different from Calm/Headspace/Endel?"

```
Fair ask — the bedtime category is crowded. Three real differences:

1. Generative, not library. The soundscape is composed fresh from your spoken intention each night. Calm and Headspace ship libraries; Endel has a generative soundscape but no breath, no landscape, no recall.

2. Four layers under one ritual. Soundscape + 4-7-8 breath voiceover (tempo-matched) + 3D Marble dream landscape (drifts, never cuts) + morning dream-recall page. Nobody else stacks all four.

3. Lifetime, AU$30. Not AU$110/year forever. A Calm annual sub covers 3.5 years of DREAMS. Founding-member pricing this week.

Also: on-device, browser-based (no app store), nothing uploaded — the audio and the journal live on your phone, full stop.
```

### Q2 — "AU$30 lifetime sounds too cheap. What's the catch? Is this sustainable?"

```
No catch — and yes, sustainable. Three things:

1. Founding tier opens this week at the floor. Pricing rises after launch; founding members never get touched by increases.

2. The engine is already built. FREQUENCY (the awake-state version) has been shipping for months. DREAMS reuses that engine — the marginal cost of adding nightly users is close to zero because everything runs on-device.

3. No server costs to amortise. No audio uploaded, no AI inference in the cloud, no per-user infrastructure bill. That's why AU$30 once works where AU$110/year is what Calm needs.

The 12-month content roadmap (new breath patterns, new soundscape modes, new lineage cards) is included. No second tier, no upsell.
```

### Q3 — "How does the on-device dream-recall agent work? Is my data private?"

```
Short answer: nothing leaves the device. Ever.

Longer answer: the recall agent runs on Apple Foundation Models — Apple's on-device LLM stack shipped with iOS 18+. When you capture a dream by voice or text in the morning, the agent helps you find the thread (recurring symbols, emotional tone, fragments worth pulling on) without imposing an interpretation. It is a journalling aid, not a diagnostic tool.

The soundscape generation, the breath voiceover, the Marble landscape rendering (via the open-source Spark iOS renderer), and the recall agent all run locally after the first load. No audio uploaded. No journal entries synced to a server. No login. The "scroll" — your archive of past pages and landscape thumbnails — lives on your phone, exportable and deletable.

Not a medical device; consult a clinician for sleep disorders.
```
