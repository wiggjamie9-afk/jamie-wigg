# Compose your night.

For two and a half thousand years, contemplative traditions have treated the threshold of sleep as a practice. The Upanishadic hum. The Christian office of compline. The Sufi *khalwa*. The Hatha Yoga Pradīpikā's Bhramari. All of them spend the same care on bedtime that we spend, today, scrolling.

FREQUENCY DREAMS is the modern equivalent — built on the engine you already trust from FREQUENCY, made for the night, learned by tonight.

You speak an intention. A feeling. A question. A fragment of last night's dream.

The app composes **four interlocking layers**, performed in sequence, on your phone:

· A **solfeggio-tuned soundscape**, generated by the RHYTHMIX FREQUENCY engine conditioned on your intention.
· A **4-7-8 breathwork voiceover** in your chosen voice — Andrew Weil's pattern, tempo-matched to the soundscape.
· A **Marble-rendered 3D dream landscape** — Gaussian splat scenes from World Labs, displayed via the open-source Spark iOS renderer. The camera drifts. The camera does not cut.
· A **morning recall page** — on-device Apple Foundation Models agent helps you find the thread, never imposes an interpretation.

Nothing leaves the device.
