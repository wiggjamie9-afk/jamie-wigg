## Lead

Perth-based RHYTHMIX today launched **FREQUENCY DREAMS**, a nightly ritual app that composes a bespoke 25–40 minute soundscape, a 4-7-8 breath voiceover, a 3D dream landscape, and a morning dream-recall page for every user — on-device, no subscription, AU$30 once. The launch directly challenges the AU$80–$110 annual pricing of Calm, Headspace and Endel, positioning lifetime ownership as a viable category alternative to the sleep-tech subscription stack.

## Body

DREAMS is built around a four-layer nightly ritual. A user speaks an intention — a feeling, a question, a fragment of last night's dream. The RHYTHMIX FREQUENCY engine generates a solfeggio-tuned soundscape conditioned on that intention. A breath voiceover, following Dr Andrew Weil's 4-7-8 pattern, is tempo-matched to the audio and shifts to 5.5/5.5 cardiac coherence on waking. A 3D dream landscape, rendered by World Labs' Marble model and displayed via the open-source Spark iOS Gaussian-splat renderer, drifts on a slow pan — the camera does not cut.

In the morning, an on-device agent built on Apple Foundation Models helps the user recall and capture the dream. Nothing leaves the device. There is no login, no app store, and no audio upload. DREAMS runs as a browser-native progressive web app added to the iPhone home screen.

The pricing model is the explicit market argument. A Calm or Headspace annual subscription would fund three and a half years of DREAMS; Endel's would fund nearly three. RHYTHMIX has stated the founding-member price will rise, but founding-member accounts will not be re-billed. Twelve months of additional breath patterns, soundscape modes and lineage cards are included.

DREAMS is the first product to combine generative intention-conditioned audio, paced breath voiceover, on-device 3D dreamscapes and a foundation-model dream-recall agent under a single nightly ritual. It is sold as a wellness practice, not a medical device, and carries the standard contraindication notes for breath practice.
