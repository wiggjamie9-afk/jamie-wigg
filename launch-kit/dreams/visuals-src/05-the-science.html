FREQUENCY DREAMS
A practice with a lineage

From Upanishad to the breath lab.

~500 BCE Bedtime as practice Across the contemplative lineages — Upanishad, Christian compline, Sufi khalwa — the threshold of sleep is treated as the day's last and most attentive practice.
1981 · WEIL 4-7-8 breath Andrew Weil's 4-7-8 pattern — inhale four, hold seven, exhale eight — is widely associated with parasympathetic settling. (Wellness, not medical: a settling pattern, not a cure for insomnia.)
1995 · LEHRER 5.5/5.5 coherence Cardiac coherence at ≈0.1 Hz (5.5s in / 5.5s out) sits at the resonant frequency of the baroreflex. Used here for waking, not falling.
2026 · MARBLE A dreamscape on your phone World Labs' Marble renders Gaussian-splat 3D worlds from text. Spark displays them on iPhone. The first time a generated landscape can drift with you, not at you.
