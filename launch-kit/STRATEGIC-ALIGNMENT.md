# Strategic alignment — vision vs day-1 ship

> Surfaced by the FAQ-agent audit (2026-05-21). The brand promos sell a 4-pillar platform; the actual gumroad listing ships only the music-video layer. This doc resolves the gap.

## The gap

| Source | What it says RHYTHMIX is |
|---|---|
| `rhythmix-anthem-60s/narration.txt` | "Four moves change everything. Generate. Master. Distribute. Earn." |
| `rhythmix-launch-60s/narration.txt` | "Built for the whole journey. … RHYTHMIX runs the career." |
| `launch-kit/seo/rhythmix-vs-suno.md` | "RHYTHMIX wins for anyone whose goal is to release music (distribution, royalties, merch bundled into one AU$149 payment)." |
| `launch-kit/distribution/twitter-campaign.md` | "Generate. Master. Distribute. Earn. One platform. AU$149. Once." |
| `launch-kit/rhythmix/gumroad-listing.md` | **"Drop in your MP3. Get back a cinematic AI music video. Four frontier video models under one beat-synced cut. $149 once."** |

The first four sell the **vision**. The fifth sells **what actually ships on day 1.** A buyer paying AU$149 expecting the four-pillar career platform will be disappointed when they get a music-video tool.

## The resolve (recommended position)

**Frame the AU$149 lifetime price as founding-member entry to the full roadmap, not as payment for day-1 scope.**

> "AU$149 once. The cinematic music-video pipeline ships today. Master, Distribute, and Earn ship across 2026. Buy once, get the whole roadmap as it lands. No subscription, no upgrade fees, no exclusions."

This:
- Keeps the 4-pillar narrative intact (it's the truth of what's being built)
- Doesn't lie about day-1 scope
- Justifies AU$149 (lifetime access to the whole roadmap is real value)
- Removes the "are you Suno+LANDR+DistroKid in one?" trap — answer is "yes, eventually; today we're the music-video tool"

## What this means for the four product apps

The 4 separate RHYTHMIX-line apps — **RESONATE, FREQUENCY DREAMS, RHYTHMIX LIVE, HUM** — are unaffected. Each is its own standalone product at its own price (AU$30 lifetime for RESONATE/DREAMS/HUM, subscription for LIVE). Their press releases, gumroad listings, and promo narrations all describe what THAT product actually does and ship. No edits needed.

The gap is **only** at the RHYTHMIX-umbrella brand level — where the umbrella vision sells more than the umbrella product currently ships.
