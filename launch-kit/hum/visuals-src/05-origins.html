HUM
2,500 years of practice

From Bhramari to the vagus nerve.

~500 BCE Bhramari Pranayama Yogic bee breath, codified in the Hatha Yoga Pradipika — a humming exhalation woven into early nāda-yoga.
1921 The vagus nerve Otto Loewi shows the vagus calms the heart, founding modern parasympathetic science.
2002 Sinus resonance Weitzberg & Lundberg measure a ~15× nitric oxide rise in the sinuses during humming.
