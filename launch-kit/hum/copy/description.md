## 5. Long description (~1,100 words)

### HUM — a pocket practice for the oldest nervous-system intervention on record

HUM is a quiet web app for one thing: helping you hum, daily, the way humans have been humming for two and a half thousand years. It is not a meditation library. It is not a soundscape. It is not an AI companion. It is a voice-guided, breath-paced, lineage-aware practice tool for a single technique — the soft humming exhale — and the small structured arc of doing it four times a day until your nervous system notices.

### Who it's for

People who already know they should be doing *something* for their nervous system but bounce off the bottomless catalogues of the big meditation apps. People with tinnitus who have tried sound masking and want to try producing the sound themselves. Sleep-anxious people who want a bedtime ritual that takes nine minutes, not forty. Bhramari practitioners who'd like their daily count tracked without a streak-shame app yelling at them. Anyone who has read about the vagus nerve and wants to do the thing the wearables claim to do — without the wearable.

### What's inside

**Today.** A single ninety-second start button, a four-pip daily-dose row (Rise · Noon · 4 pm · Sleep), and a small wall of receipts — the four peer-reviewed findings the practice is anchored on, with sources you can verify.

**Practice.** Three target tones — *Chest Hum* at 130 Hz (the Eby nitric-oxide optimum), *Open Om* at 174 Hz (Solfeggio-aligned), *Bhramari* at 220 Hz (the classic bee pitch). A breath orb expanding and contracting on a ten-second cycle. A microphone listener that watches your pitch on-device and tells you when you're holding the tone. No audio leaves your phone.

**Programs.** Four structured arcs with start dates and end dates, not infinite libraries.
- *The Quiet* — 28 days for tinnitus, built on the two Bhramari RCTs reporting effect sizes of 0.86 and 0.92 on the Tinnitus Handicap Inventory.
- *The Clearing* — 4 days of strong nasal humming on the Eby (2006) sinus protocol.
- *The Descent* — nine-minute nightly sequence engineered around the Trivedi (2023) Holter finding that humming produced the lowest autonomic stress index of any state measured, lower than sleep.
- *The Tone* — fourteen days of five-minute daily humming at 0.1 Hz exhalation pacing, designed for HRV gains the vagal-wearable category sells but you can produce with your own voice.

**Lineage.** A scroll from the Mandukya Upanishad (c. 500 BCE) through Sufi dhikr, Hildegard of Bingen, the Hatha Yoga Pradīpikā, the Gyütö Tantric College, Weitzberg & Lundberg at Karolinska in 2002, the Kalyani fMRI on OM in 2011, the Trivedi Holter study in 2023, the SetPoint FDA approval in 2025 — and you, today. Twenty-five centuries of practice lined up against the modern research that vindicated it.

**You.** Counts, minutes in vibration, streak, longest streak, first-session date. Stored in your browser only. Nothing in the cloud. Nothing tracked. A single export button.

### The science behind humming

Three pillars hold the practice up.

**Weitzberg & Lundberg, Karolinska Institute, 2002.** Ten healthy subjects, chemiluminescence measurement of nasal nitric oxide during quiet exhalation versus humming. NO rose fifteen-fold during humming. The two-compartment nose-sinus model showed oscillating airflow dramatically increases gas exchange between sinus and nasal cavity. Replicated by Maniscalco in 2003.

**Trivedi et al., Cureus, 2023.** Twenty-three participants on clinical-grade Holter monitors across humming, physical activity, emotional stress, and sleep. Humming produced the lowest autonomic stress index of any state — lower than sleep itself.

**Kalyani et al., NIMHANS Bangalore, 2011.** Twelve subjects in fMRI during OM chanting versus a "ssss" control. OM produced significant deactivation of the right amygdala — the same pattern observed during clinical vagus nerve stimulation for depression.

Three findings, three peer-reviewed sources, three mechanisms — nasal nitric oxide, autonomic stress reduction, and amygdala deactivation — converging on the same simple act.

### What HUM is not

HUM is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. It is a wellness practice associated with parasympathetic shift, autonomic calm, and the same vagal pathway being studied in clinical trials. If you have severe acute sinusitis, vocal cord nodules, or recent ear surgery, consult your doctor first. If symptoms persist, see a clinician.

HUM does not use AI in the practice loop. The microphone listens on-device using a deterministic pitch detector. There is no language model, no soundscape generator, no chatbot. The practice is older than computers and works fine without them.

HUM does not track you. Sessions are stored in your browser's local storage. No accounts, no server, no analytics on practice data. The reservation email is the only thing that ever leaves your device, and it goes to one person — the maker.

### Pricing

**AU$30, one payment, lifetime access.** No subscription. No renewal. No usage tier. Available at `rhythmixapp.com.au/hum.html`. The web app loads on iPhone Safari, Android Chrome, and desktop browsers. There is no download. You add it to your home screen if you want it there.

### Closing

Five thousand years of practice. One hundred years of science. One pocket. One throat. Ninety seconds.
