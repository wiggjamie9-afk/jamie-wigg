## RHYTHMIX LIVE launches first beat-locked AI music-video pipeline for indie artists

*Subscription tool turns a Suno or Udio track into three platform-ready videos in a single render — cuts synced to the waveform, not the vibe.*

RHYTHMIX today released RHYTHMIX LIVE, a web-based music-video tool that ingests an MP3 or WAV and outputs three beat-synchronised videos — a 60-second vertical, 15-second square, and four-minute landscape — in one render pass. Every cut, transition, and camera move is locked to the audio waveform. The product is available now at rhythmixapp.com.au/live with a permanent free tier, Pro at USD $19 per month, and Studio at USD $49 per month.

RHYTHMIX LIVE orchestrates four generative video models behind a single render. **Kling 2.6**, released in December 2025, is the first widely-available model that conditions video output on the audio waveform itself, producing cuts that land on downbeats rather than on editor intuition. **Hunyuan Video** handles long-form shot continuity. **Luma** drives camera motion and dynamic range. **MiniMax** generates character and performer shots. A mood-board layer gives users register-level control — neon kinetic, soft analog, glass-and-mirror, monochrome, hand-drawn — without writing prompts.

The three deliverables are rendered in one pass and exit as platform-correct files: 9:16 for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts; 1:1 for Instagram and Facebook feeds; 16:9 for YouTube. No re-editing, no cropping, no second render.

The launch positions RHYTHMIX LIVE distinctly from general-purpose video generators. OpenAI's Sora and Runway Gen-4 produce cinematic clips, but neither reads the waveform of an attached audio track — cuts are timed by prompt, not by the kick. RHYTHMIX LIVE is built specifically for music releases, not film or advertising.

The Pro tier (USD $19/month) ships unlimited renders, no watermark, and a one-click Fourthwall integration for releasing tied merchandise — vinyl, hoodie, lyric print — with the same artwork as the release. The Studio tier (USD $49/month) adds persistent voice cloning across an album catalogue via the Suno API and a priority render queue. The free tier ships one watermarked render per month with no card required.

> "Suno gave a million people the ability to make a song they couldn't have made yesterday. The question every one of them hits next is *now what?* You can't just drop the MP3 — you need a video, you need it in three formats, and the cuts need to actually land on the beat. Until December 2025, no model could read the waveform. Kling 2.6 changed that. RHYTHMIX LIVE is what you build the day that ships."
>
> — **Jamie Wigg**, founder, RHYTHMIX

RHYTHMIX LIVE is the second product in the RHYTHMIX line, following the AU$149 lifetime RHYTHMIX platform launched earlier in 2026. LIVE is a standalone music-video subscription, distinct from the umbrella RHYTHMIX platform — not part of its four-pillar roadmap.
