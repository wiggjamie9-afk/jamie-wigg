## 8. Long description (~1,200 words) — full Gumroad / landing-section pitch

### RHYTHMIX LIVE — beat-synced AI music videos for everyone shipping on Suno

You made the song. Then what?

You opened Suno. You wrote a prompt. Two minutes later you had a track that did not exist yesterday. You loved it. You felt the rush. And then you stood up from the laptop with one open question that nobody on YouTube has a clean answer to.

**Now what?**

You can't just drop an MP3. Spotify uploads need cover art and an artist-name story. TikTok needs a vertical video. Reels needs the same vertical video except 15 seconds. IG feed wants a square. YouTube wants something at least four minutes long. The clip you finally make in CapCut isn't synced to the beats — it's synced to a vibe. The thumbnail is whatever Canva had stock that day. The merch link, if you got that far, sells one Bella+Canvas tee.

RHYTHMIX LIVE is built for that exact moment.

### What it does

You drop a track. Suno, Udio, an old session bounce-down — any MP3, any WAV. You pick a visual mood-board. We produce three deliverables in a single render:

- A **60-second vertical** for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Stories.
- A **15-second square** for Instagram feed and Facebook.
- A **4-minute landscape** for YouTube.

And here is the part nobody else has yet: **every cut, every transition, every camera move locks to your actual waveform.** Not "feels musical." Not "matches the vibe." Locked. Beat by beat.

This is possible because of **Kling 2.6** — the first widely-available video model with audio-conditioned cuts, which shipped in December 2025. Other tools paste b-roll behind your audio. Kling 2.6 reads the audio and outputs video that is *of* the audio. The cuts land on the downbeats because the model knows where the downbeats are.

We orchestrate Kling 2.6 against your specific track, with mood-board control over the visual register, and we render the three formats in one pass. That last bit matters. One render → three platform-ready files. Not "render and crop." Not "re-edit for vertical." One pass, three platform-correct cuts.

### Then drop the merch

This is the other half of the "now what" problem. You've published your single. You have one good ten-second window of attention from people who actually felt the track. RHYTHMIX LIVE integrates directly with **Fourthwall** so you can drop a vinyl, a hoodie, or a lyric print tied to that release, with one click, after you publish the video. The art borrows directly from your mood-board. The product page is live before the algorithm has decided what to do with you.

Vinyl. Hoodie. Lyric prints in A2 matte. The standard release-merch kit, with the click count down from "set up a Shopify, set up Printful, learn what a mock-up generator is, design a hoodie graphic" to one.

### The stack (for the technical reader)

- **Suno API** — your audio. Persistent voice clone across an album in Studio tier, so a 12-track release sounds like one artist instead of twelve.
- **Kling 2.6** — the beat-aware video model. Released December 2025. The only model right now that conditions video output on audio waveform in a meaningful way.
- **HyperFrames** — the composition layer that handles caption-syncing, multi-aspect rendering, and turning three render outputs into three platform-correct files.
- **Fourthwall** — for the merch side. You connect your account, we drop your release-art products.

We didn't invent any of these. We orchestrated them. The new piece RHYTHMIX LIVE owns is the beat-sync orchestrator — the part that reads your audio, decides where the cuts should land, and conducts Kling 2.6 to match. Everything else is plumbing.

### Pricing

**Free** — 1 video / month, with a small watermark. Test the output, see the quality, decide whether to upgrade. No credit card to start.

**Pro** — $19 / month. Unlimited videos. No watermark. Fourthwall integration unlocked.

**Studio** — $49 / month. Persistent voice cloning across your catalogue (so a multi-track release has one voice, not twelve). Priority render queue (your video goes to the head of the line). Everything from Pro.

No annual lock-in. Cancel any time. We're priced to be the no-brainer recurring cost in an indie artist's release stack, sitting beside Distrokid ($23/year), Patreon (variable), and Fourthwall (free).

### Who it isn't for

If you are making meme TikToks with AI music, this is not the tool. Get CapCut. Use the AI music as background. Move on.

If you are a major-label producer with a director and a $40,000 video budget, this is also not the tool. We're not competing with Hype Studios. We're not making your debut single look like an Apple ad.

RHYTHMIX LIVE is for the indie artist who is **serious about releasing** — someone shipping multiple singles a year, building a catalogue, treating each release like an event but without the budget or the team to act like a label.

### Who it is for

- **The Suno artist** with a catalogue of 12 tracks and zero music videos.
- **The bedroom producer** releasing monthly on Bandcamp who wants a real promo cycle per drop.
- **The composer-for-hire** building a TikTok presence to fund the project work.
- **The brand artist** running their own LLC / sole-trader release model where every saved hour is a real hour.
- **Anyone on Suno paying for a paid plan** — you're already in the funnel for the next tool that takes your output and finishes the release.

### What's locked in already

- The pipeline. Built on RHYTHMIX, which already owns the music-rendering side.
- The visual identity. RHYTHMIX-neon. The same palette and tone the brand has been shipping since the teaser.
- The three formats. We render every one of them. Always. No "Pro feature" gate on the basic deliverables.
- Fourthwall partnership. Tied at the API level — your release art *is* your merch art.

### What's still moving

- The mood-board library is growing weekly.
- Voice cloning quality at Studio tier (Suno's persistent-voice feature is itself early — expect the cross-track consistency to keep improving in 2026).
- Long-form support. Currently 4 minutes is the YouTube ceiling. We'll push past that as Kling 2.6 evolves.
