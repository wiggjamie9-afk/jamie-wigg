## Sequence notes

- **Cadence:** T-7, T-3, T-0, T+2, T+5. Three emails in launch week (max).
- **Exit:** Upgrade to Pro or Studio → suppress from launch nudges, re-route to onboarding sequence.
- **Suppression:** Hard-bounce, unsubscribe, or 3 consecutive unopened → drop from sequence.
- **Segmentation:** If subscriber clicked Email 2 (pipeline) but didn't convert by Email 3, prioritise the case-study angle in Email 4. If they clicked pricing in Email 3 but didn't buy, Email 5 leads with the founding-price lock.
- **Personalisation tokens:** `{{first_name}}`, `{{last_track_link}}` (if integrated with Suno/Udio), `{{platform_preference}}` (TikTok/IG/YouTube — drives the hero format named in body copy).
