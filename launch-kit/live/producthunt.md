## Three prepped FAQ replies

### Q1: "How is this different from CapCut / Canva / Sora?"

```
Three honest distinctions:

CapCut needs you to cut to the beat manually. RHYTHMIX LIVE does it via Kling 2.6, which reads your audio waveform — every cut, transition, and camera move locks to the actual beats, not a vibe.

Canva resizes. We render three native formats in one pass — 60s vertical, 15s square, 4min landscape. No cropping, no re-edit.

Sora is great at Hollywood-grade cinematography but doesn't know your song. RHYTHMIX LIVE is the first to treat the waveform as the script.

We're a creator tool, not a Sora replacement. Honest about that.
```

### Q2: "What's the difference between $19 Pro and $49 Studio?"

```
Pro ($19/mo) is the default tier for everyone shipping releases. Unlimited videos, no watermark, full Fourthwall merch integration. If you're a Suno artist dropping monthly, Pro is the right pick.

Studio ($49/mo) adds two things for serious release operators:

1. Persistent voice cloning across a catalogue — your 12-track album sounds like one artist, not twelve. Powered by the Suno API.
2. Priority render queue — your job jumps the line during peak times.

If you're running a multi-track build or an LLC release model, Studio pays itself back in saved hours. Otherwise Pro is genuinely enough.

Free tier (1 video/mo with small watermark) is always there to test the output before you commit.
```

### Q3: "Do I keep the rights to my videos and audio?"

```
Yes. Full ownership.

RHYTHMIX LIVE is a creator tool, not a music-rights manager. We don't distribute your tracks. We don't claim your masters. We don't take a margin on your merch (Fourthwall handles their own fulfillment margin separately).

You retain 100% of:
· The audio you upload

· The merch revenue (minus Fourthwall's standard production cost)

The only data we hold is your account, your render queue, and your mood-board picks — so you can re-render or iterate. Delete your account, all of that goes.

14-day no-questions refund on subscriptions. After that, write to wiggjamie9@gmail.com and we'll work it out.
```
