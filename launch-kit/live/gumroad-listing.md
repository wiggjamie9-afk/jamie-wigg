## Why beat-locked actually matters

Until December 2025, every AI music-video tool did the same thing: generate visuals, paste them behind your audio, hope the cuts feel musical. They mostly didn't. The cuts landed on someone's intuition about the vibe — not on the actual beats.

In December 2025, **Kling 2.6** shipped. It's the first widely-available video model that reads the **audio waveform itself**. Cuts land on downbeats because the model sees the downbeats. Transitions hit the kick because the model knows where the kick is. Camera moves slow into the bridge because the model reads the dynamics.

RHYTHMIX LIVE orchestrates Kling 2.6 against your specific track, with mood-board control over the visual register, and renders the three platform-correct formats in one pass. Not "render and crop." Not "re-edit for vertical." One pass, three correct files.

RHYTHMIX LIVE is not a substitute for an actual music video director if your budget supports one. It's the tool for everyone who doesn't.
