# RESONATE — 7-Email Post-Purchase / Onboarding Sequence

Audience: RESONATE buyers (just paid AU$30 lifetime).
Goal: activation → retention → advocacy. Not selling.
Arc: welcome → second mode → understanding → reflection → advocacy → preview → belonging.
Exit condition: unsubscribe, or refund.
Cadence: 7 sends across 30 days. Plain text, mobile-readable. One CTA per email.

## Email 3 — T+3 days · How to read your coherence score

**Role:** Education
**Subject A:** How to read your coherence score
**Subject B:** What the number actually means
**Preheader:** HRV in plain English, no PhD required.

Hi —

You've probably noticed the number on the session summary screen — your **coherence score**.

Here's what it is, in one paragraph.

Your heart doesn't beat like a metronome. The gap between beats varies, and that variability is controlled by your vagus nerve. When you breathe slowly and evenly — around six breaths a minute — your heart rate rises on the inhale and falls on the exhale in a smooth wave. That wave is **coherence**. The closer your heart-rate variability tracks your breath, the higher the score.

A score above 60 means the loop closed. The music opened because your body opened first.

You don't need to chase the number. The score is a mirror, not a target. Some days you'll hit 80 in two minutes. Other days you'll sit at 40 for the whole session and that's also fine — your nervous system is doing what it can with what you brought it.

Watch the trend over a week, not any single session.

— Jamie

→ Run a session and check your score

## Email 4 — T+7 days · Your week

**Role:** Retention / reflection
**Subject A:** Your first week with RESONATE
**Subject B:** Seven days. Here's what your nervous system did.
**Preheader:** A small mirror.

Hi —

Seven days in. Quick reflection.

Open the app and tap **Insights**. You'll see:

— Total minutes in coherence this week
— Your most-used mode (Focus, Calm, or Rest)
— Your average coherence score, and how it trended day-to-day
— The time of day your nervous system settled fastest

That last one is the interesting one. Most people discover they have a window — usually mid-morning or just after dinner — where the loop closes in under ninety seconds. Once you know yours, you can stop forcing sessions at the wrong time.

If your week was quiet, that's also useful information. The app waits. It doesn't email you guilt. There's no streak to lose and no badge to earn. Come back when you're ready.

If your week was busy with sessions, thank you. You're the reason this thing exists.

— Jamie

## Sequence map

```
T+0    →  Email 1  Welcome + 3-step quick-start   (open app CTA)
T+1d   →  Email 2  Try Calm                       (open Calm CTA)
T+3d   →  Email 3  Read your coherence score      (run a session CTA)
T+7d   →  Email 4  Your week                      (check Insights CTA)
T+14d  →  Email 5  Quiet ask (advocacy)           (share one-liner CTA)
T+21d  →  Email 6  What's coming (soft cross-sell) (reply CTA)
T+30d  →  Email 7  Founding 100 Discord           (invite CTA, gated)
```

**Exit conditions:** Refund → remove and add to refund-followup flow. Unsubscribe → remove. Three consecutive unopened → suppress remainder (do not re-engage; respect the inbox).

**Segmentation:** Email 7 sends only to buyers in the first 100 (tag: `founding-100`). Buyers 101+ receive a modified Email 7 with a "regular member" thank-you and no Discord invite.

**Send timing:** 7:30am AEST for Emails 1–6 (consistent with pre-purchase sequence). Email 1 sends immediately on purchase confirmation regardless of time of day (transactional). Email 7 at 8:00am AEST Saturday for weekend Discord onboarding.

**A/B test priority:** Email 2 subject line. It's the highest-leverage retention email — the difference between a buyer who tries one mode and quits, vs a buyer who builds the habit. Test "Day Two — try Calm" against "Same app, different nervous system" 50/50, measure 7-day session count as the success metric (not opens).

**Personalization tokens:** `{{first_name}}` optional in greeting; sequence reads fine without it. `{{purchase_date}}` for Email 1 receipt block. `{{session_count_week_1}}` and `{{top_mode}}` for Email 4 if your ESP can pull from app data; otherwise Email 4 stays generic and directs to in-app Insights.
