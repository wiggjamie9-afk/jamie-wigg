## Australian indie studio releases first closed-loop biometric music app for AirPods Pro 3

*RESONATE generates music in real time from the listener's heart rate and breath, with all inference running on-device. AU$30 lifetime, no subscription.*

RHYTHMIX FREQUENCY today released **RESONATE**, an iPhone application that generates music in real time and steers it from the listener's own biometrics. Heart rate is read from the AirPods Pro 3 in-ear optical sensor; breath rate is streamed from Apple Watch; the resulting score adapts within two seconds. The app is sold as a one-time AU$30 lifetime purchase at rhythmixapp.com.au/resonate and ships with three modes — Focus, Calm, and Rest.

RESONATE is positioned as the first consumer iPhone app to combine real-time generative music, a closed loop on the user's biometrics, head-tracked spatial audio, a contemplative aesthetic, and fully on-device privacy in a single product. Comparable adaptive-audio apps including Endel, Brain.fm, and Calm pair at most two of those elements; none combine all five. The score is generated continuously on Lyria RealTime, with Magenta RT available as a self-hosted fallback, and is rendered into three-dimensional space using Apple PHASE with ARKit head-tracking.

The product's mechanism draws on a century of autonomic-nervous-system research. Otto Loewi's 1921 Nobel-winning work on the vagus nerve established the parasympathetic pathway between breath and heart. Paul Lehrer and colleagues identified the autonomic resonance frequency at approximately 0.1 Hz in 1995 — a respiratory rate of about five and a half seconds in, five and a half seconds out, at which heart rate, breath, and vagal tone enter coherence. A 2025 Lancet meta-analysis on music therapy reported large effect sizes against anxiety. RESONATE's Calm mode targets the 0.1 Hz coherence point directly: as the user's breath approaches resonance the score opens, and as heart-rate variability drops the score narrows to guide them back.

All inference runs on Apple Foundation Models under iOS 26. There is no account, no cloud session log, and no analytics on practice data; the session history is stored locally on the device and is exportable or deletable at any time. The privacy posture is a direct response to the consolidation of the adaptive-audio category around subscription-and-cloud business models, and to the post-mortem on cloud-dependent wellness wearables of the past three years.

"Calm wants ninety dollars a year to teach you to breathe. Endel wants seventy. RESONATE is thirty dollars, once, forever," said Jamie Wigg, founder of RHYTHMIX FREQUENCY. "Three findings from the last hundred years of coherence research, four pieces of hardware and software that all happened to ship in 2026, and one app that puts them in a pair of AirPods. That was the brief."

RESONATE is a wellness practice instrument and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition.

## About RHYTHMIX FREQUENCY

RHYTHMIX FREQUENCY is an independent Perth-based studio building contemplative audio products at the intersection of generative music, biometrics, and on-device AI.
