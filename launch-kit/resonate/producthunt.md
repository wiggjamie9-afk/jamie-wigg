## 3 Prepped FAQ-Style Replies

### Reply 1 — "Doesn't this already exist? Endel, Brain.fm, Apollo, Calm…"

```
Fair challenge — I lived in this space before I built it.

None of them close the loop on biometrics. Endel adapts to weather, time-of-day, and heart rate from your Watch — but it's rule-based, runs cloud-side, and crossfades between presets. Brain.fm is preset playback with personalisation, no biometric input. Apollo is haptics on an open-loop schedule. Calm is authored content. Muse uses EEG but maps to pink noise, not generative music.

The five-column test I held myself to: (1) real-time generative music, not presets, (2) closed loop on YOUR biometrics, (3) head-tracked spatial, (4) contemplative aesthetic, (5) on-device.

No competitor has more than two. RESONATE is the first with all five. If you find one that has three, tell me — I'll send you a refund and a thank-you note.
```

### Reply 2 — "Why lifetime and not subscription? How is this sustainable?"

```
Honest answer: because subscription wellness is a trap and I refuse to build one.

The unit economics work because (a) inference runs on-device — I have zero per-user compute cost, (b) there's no cloud session log, no analytics pipeline, no infra to scale, (c) AU$30 × volume is enough runway for the monthly content drops I've committed to for the next 12 months.

Founding tier opens this week at AU$30. The price goes up. Founding members are grandfathered forever — future increases never touch your account.

I'd rather have 10,000 people who own RESONATE than 1,000 who rent it and resent the renewal email. Calm's churn is brutal because the product creates negative emotion every billing cycle. I'm trying to build the opposite.

If I'm wrong about the economics, you still own the app.
```

### Reply 3 — "Is the science actually real, or is this wellness theatre?"

```
The science is real. The product is a practice instrument, not a medical device — I'm clear about that distinction.

The three load-bearing findings: (1) Otto Loewi, 1921 Nobel — vagus nerve modulates heart via acetylcholine. Foundational, undisputed. (2) Lehrer et al., 1995 — resonance frequency of the autonomic system sits around 0.1 Hz (5.5s in, 5.5s out) where heart-rate variability peaks. Replicated dozens of times. (3) Lancet 2025 meta-analysis on music therapy — large effect sizes against anxiety across 26 RCTs.

What RESONATE does NOT claim: that it treats anxiety, ADHD, depression, or insomnia. It supports a daily practice associated with parasympathetic activation. That's it.

The biometric readings are practice indicators, not clinical measurements. AirPods Pro 3's optical sensor is good enough to steer music. It is not good enough to diagnose anything — and I will never market it as such.
```
