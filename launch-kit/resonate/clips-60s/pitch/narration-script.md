## Delivery notes

- **Line 1**: This is the line that has to land. "Music that breathes with you" — read it like a thesis, not a tagline. Steady pace. "From the makers of FREQUENCY" is the legitimising tail — softer, almost a footnote.
- **Line 2**: Technical, calm. Both clauses are equally weighted — *AirPods, your heart · Watch, your breath*. The parallel structure is the point. Don't rush the second clause.
- **Line 3**: This is the moat sentence. "Sub two-second response" reads as one phrase — don't break for the hyphen. "Nothing leaves your phone" lands with the same weight as a full sentence — small pause before, full stop after.
- **Line 4**: The single most contemplative line. "Approach" needs space; pause 0.3s after "coherence" before the em-dash; "the music opens" is exhaled, not announced. This is where the practice lives.
- **Line 5**: Three words, three beats. *Focus.* *Calm.* (half-beat) *Rest.* The italic word in the visual is "Rest" — give it a tiny bit more time than the other two. Honour the FREQUENCY-positioning trinity.
- **Line 6**: Steady, slightly warmer. "RESONATE" at the start is the brand mark — give it weight. Read the URL clearly — "rhythmixapp dot com dot au slash resonate". Final word lands; do not trail off.
