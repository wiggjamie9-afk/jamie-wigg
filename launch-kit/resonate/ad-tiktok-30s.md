# RESONATE — 30s Paid Ad (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical · 30 seconds · sound-on optimised, on-screen text for sound-off
**Platforms:** TikTok Spark Ads, Instagram Reels (Meta Ads), YouTube Shorts
**Audience:** AirPods Pro 3 owners, biohackers, meditation-app refugees (Calm/Endel/Headspace churned), focus-music listeners 25–45

---

## Hook variants (A/B/C — test all three, run winner)

- **Hook A (chosen — strongest scroll-stop):** *"What if your music could feel your heartbeat?"*
- **Hook B (alt):** *"AirPods Pro 3 are a medical device. Almost nobody is using them as one."*
- **Hook C (alt):** *"Calm wants $90/year. This is $30. Once. Forever."*

> **Pick:** Hook A leads because it reframes a familiar object (AirPods) with a strange, curiosity-loop question that doesn't telegraph "ad." Hook B is the credibility play for biohacker audiences. Hook C is the price-anchor play for switchers — best for retargeting warm traffic.

---

## Suggested audio

**Mood:** sub-bass heartbeat pulse layered under a single sustained gold-coloured pad. Slow 0.1 Hz breath cycle (5s in / 5s out) — synced to the on-screen breath orb expansion. No vocals. No drop. Build of warmth at 22s ("the music opens" beat) by adding a high-shimmer harmonic layer. Cuts to near-silence (heartbeat only) at 26s for the CTA card. Royalty-free or licensed ambient/generative cue — no recognisable track.

## Production notes

- **Colour grade:** lock to RESONATE brand — deep navy `#0A0F1F` field, gold `#D4AF37` accents, cream `#F4E4BC` text. Avoid violet (that's HUM, sister brand).
- **Typography on overlays:** Cormorant Garamond italic 500 for the wordmark + bloom-gradient fill. JetBrains Mono ALL CAPS letter-spacing 0.22em for all eyebrow / data text.
- **Breath orb:** must animate at 0.1 Hz (5s expand, 5s contract) wherever it appears — this is the brand's signature motion. Frame 8 and frame 17 specifically must sync to the orb cycle.
- **Voice:** same narrator as the 60s pitch + science clips (see `clips-60s/pitch/narration.mp3`) — keep continuity across the campaign.
- **Captions:** burn-in mandatory (≥60% of feed viewing is sound-off on Reels). Use cream `#F4E4BC` on a 40% navy backplate for readability.
- **Frame 4–5 (competitor logos):** mock as plain-text price cards, not actual logos, to stay clear of trademark complaints on Meta Ads.
- **Hook test plan:** run A/B/C as three separate ad sets, $30/day each for 72h, kill the two lowest 3-second hold rates and scale the winner.
