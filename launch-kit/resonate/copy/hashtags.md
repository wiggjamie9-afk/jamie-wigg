## 13. Negative keywords (don't accidentally rank for these)

Things RESONATE is **not**, that you should not let SEO drift toward:

- "vagus nerve stimulator" — that's a medical device. RESONATE is not.
- "tinnitus cure" — RESONATE is not a tinnitus app; HUM is closer.
- "ADHD cure", "anxiety cure", "depression cure" — wellness, not medical.
- "binaural beat generator" — too commodity; we are not selling tones.
- "white noise app" — distance us from that category in the same breath we mention it.
- "AI girlfriend", "AI companion" — RESONATE is not parasocial.
- "Vision Pro" — addressing market is AirPods + Watch, not Vision Pro.
