## 8. Long description (~1,200 words) — for Gumroad / landing-page hero block

### RESONATE — the first song that listens back

RESONATE is the flagship app of the RHYTHMIX FREQUENCY family. It is a closed-loop biometric music app: a real-time generative score steered, in sub-two-second loops, by the same nervous system the music is being played to. It is the first consumer app to combine five things that, until 2026, had only ever been combined in pairs.

### What it actually does

You put in your AirPods. You open RESONATE. It reads your heart rate from the AirPods Pro 3's in-ear optical sensor and your HRV from your Apple Watch's `HKWorkoutSession`. From the first beat, the music is generated — not chosen from a library, not crossfaded between presets — using Lyria RealTime as the audio model and your existing FREQUENCY catalogue as the sonic seed.

That music is rendered head-tracked in three-dimensional space through the AirPods Pro 3's spatial audio pipeline. Turn your head; the score follows. The spatial bloom is not a gimmick — it is the difference between music being something you wear in your ears and something that lives in the room around you.

And then the loop closes. As your breath slows toward 0.1 Hz — the cardiac coherence rate, five and a half seconds in and five and a half out, the rhythm where heart, lungs, and the vagus nerve enter resonance — the music opens. More harmonic. More spatial. More resolved. As your HRV drops, the score narrows and slows to walk you back. The control-to-effect latency is under two seconds, so you feel the loop close.

There are three modes — **Focus**, **Calm**, **Rest** — the same trinity FREQUENCY already established. Focus targets 40 Hz gamma. Calm targets 0.1 Hz cardiac coherence. Rest targets 2 Hz delta. Each is a different prompt schedule, a different bed, a different opening curve.

Nothing leaves your phone. All inference runs on-device via Apple Foundation Models (iOS 26). There is no account, no cloud session log, no analytics on practice data. Privacy is not a marketing claim — it is an architecture choice.

### The moat — five columns no competitor combines

Every adaptive-audio incumbent has at most two of these:

1. **Real-time generative music** (not a rules engine, not crossfades, not a static preset library).
2. **A closed loop on your biometrics** (not weather, not time of day, not an open-loop schedule).
3. **Head-tracked spatial audio** (not stereo, not "3D-ish").
4. **A contemplative aesthetic** (not the functional-utility register most adaptive-audio apps default to).
5. **On-device privacy** (not cloud-streaming, not "we promise not to look").

Endel has the closest position with two columns; Brain.fm has one; Calm and Headspace have one; Apollo Neuro has one in a different modality; Muse has one; Sensate has one. RESONATE has all five. That is the moat.

### Lineage

This is not a research project. It is a working app built on a hundred years of measurement:

- **1921 · Otto Loewi.** The vagus nerve calms the heart through acetylcholine. The foundation of parasympathetic science. Nobel Prize in 1936.
- **1995 · Lehrer & colleagues.** The resonance frequency of the autonomic system is identified at around 0.1 Hz. Slow breathing at that pace synchronises heart rate variability, blood pressure waves, and respiratory rhythm.
- **2025 · The Lancet meta-analysis.** Music therapy produces large effect sizes against anxiety. Adaptive, body-responsive audio crosses from research curiosity into evidence-graded territory.
- **2026 · Four technologies, same year.** Lyria RealTime (Google) — the first generative music model with a real-time control input. AirPods Pro 3 — in-ear optical heart-rate sensor + dynamic head tracking. Apple Foundation Models — on-device LLM via Swift API. ASAF + Audio Ray Tracing — generative ambisonic spatial output. All four shipped between June 2025 and May 2026. No-one has put them in one app yet.

That last paragraph is the entire reason this app exists in 2026 and didn't in 2024.

### What it's good for

Designed to support:
- attention, in the Focus mode
- relaxation and parasympathetic shift, in the Calm mode
- pre-sleep wind-down, in the Rest mode
- the same vagal pathway being studied in clinical trials around vagus nerve stimulation
- a daily ritual that doesn't require a wearable you don't already own

### What it isn't

RESONATE is a **wellness practice**. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease — including ADHD, anxiety disorders, depression, or insomnia. If you have a cardiac condition, are experiencing a mental health crisis, or are pregnant, consult a qualified healthcare provider before adopting any new breath or audio practice. Audio practices should not be done while driving or operating machinery.

The biometric readings are practice indicators, not clinical measurements.

### What you need

- An iPhone with iOS 26 or later
- AirPods Pro 3 (the optical heart-rate sensor is the differentiator — older AirPods will work for spatial audio output, but the closed loop needs the new sensor)
- An Apple Watch (Series 6 or later recommended) for HRV streaming
- Headspace, optional. Headroom, mandatory.

### Pricing

**AU$30. One payment. Lifetime.** No subscription. No renewal. No usage tier. Available at `rhythmixapp.com.au/resonate`. Future price increases never touch your account.
