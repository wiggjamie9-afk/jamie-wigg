## Tips on iPhone

- **In ElevenLabs Studio**, you can regenerate a single line by tapping it. Don't re-roll the whole clip if just one phrase drifts.
- **Filename matters** — must be exactly `narration.mp3`, lowercase, no spaces. Working Copy lets you rename on save.
- **If `Alice` isn't pinned in My Voices**, search the Voice Library: tap +, search "Alice", preview, add. ~15 seconds.
- **Working Copy**: tap the clip folder → Add File → drop in the MP3 → commit → push.
- **Don't worry about credits** — 8 clips × ~600 chars = ~4,800 chars total. About 5% of a Starter monthly allocation.

---

Take your time. Quality > speed. If a clip sounds wrong, regenerate. The cost is negligible.
