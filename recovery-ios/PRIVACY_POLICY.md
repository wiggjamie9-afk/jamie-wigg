# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 26, 2024  
**Effective Date:** June 26, 2024

## Introduction

Welcome to Reset ("App," "we," "us," "our," or "Company"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App. By accessing and using Reset, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all the terms of this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account Registration Information:**
- Full name
- Email address
- Date of birth
- Profile photo (optional)
- Sport/team affiliation
- Recovery preferences

**Health and Fitness Data:**
- Recovery sessions and logs
- Workout history
- Injury reports and recovery notes
- Biometric data (if you choose to sync with health apps)
- Recovery metrics and scores

**Communication Information:**
- Messages sent through in-app messaging
- Support inquiries and feedback
- Survey responses

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Device Information:**
- Device type, operating system, and version
- Device identifiers (IDFA for iOS)
- Mobile network information
- Crash logs and error reports

**Usage Analytics:**
- Pages or features accessed
- Time spent in the App
- User interactions and feature engagement
- App session duration and frequency

**Location Information:**
- Approximate location (if permission granted)
- Location-based service usage

**Health Data Integration:**
- Data from Apple Health (with your explicit permission only)
- Heart rate, sleep, and activity data (if synced)

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- **Service Delivery:** Providing, maintaining, and improving the App and its features
- **Personalization:** Customizing your experience and recovery recommendations
- **Communication:** Sending updates, support messages, and feature announcements
- **Analytics:** Understanding usage patterns to improve the App
- **Safety and Security:** Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, and security incidents
- **Legal Compliance:** Meeting regulatory and legal requirements
- **Medical Research:** Aggregated and anonymized research (with opt-in consent)
- **Marketing:** Sending promotional materials (only with your consent)

## 3. How We Share Your Information

### 3.1 We Do NOT Share:

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

### 3.2 We May Share With:

**Service Providers:**
- Analytics services (Firebase, Amplitude)
- Cloud hosting providers (AWS, Cloudflare)
- Error tracking services (Sentry)
- Email and notification services

**Legal Requirements:**
- Law enforcement, courts, or government agencies if legally required
- Protection of our legal rights and safety of users

**Business Transfers:**
- If we are acquired or merged, your information may be transferred as part of the transaction

**With Your Consent:**
- Coaching staff or medical professionals (if you grant permission)
- Social sharing (share recovery achievements with friends)

## 4. Data Security

### 4.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

- **Encryption:** Data in transit uses TLS 1.3; sensitive data at rest is encrypted
- **Authentication:** Secure password hashing and optional biometric authentication
- **Access Control:** Role-based access to user data
- **Regular Audits:** Security assessments and penetration testing
- **Secure Coding:** OWASP compliance and secure development practices

### 4.2 Data Retention

- **Account Data:** Retained as long as your account is active, plus 90 days after deletion
- **Analytics Data:** Retained for 12 months
- **Backups:** Kept for up to 30 days for disaster recovery
- **Legal Hold:** Retained if required by law

## 5. Your Privacy Rights

### 5.1 Access and Portability

You have the right to:
- Request a copy of your personal data
- Export your data in a portable format
- Receive data correction or deletion requests

### 5.2 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected
- Know whether personal information is sold or disclosed
- Delete personal information collected
- Opt-out of the sale of personal information
- Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

### 5.3 European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Rectify inaccurate data
- Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
- Restrict processing
- Data portability
- Object to processing
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

### 5.4 How to Exercise Your Rights

Contact us at privacy@rhythmixapp.com.au with:
- Your full name and email
- Description of your request
- Proof of identity (for sensitive requests)

We will respond within 30 days (or legally required timeframe).

## 6. Apple Health Integration

If you enable Apple Health integration:

- We request explicit permission before accessing Health data
- We only access data categories you explicitly approve
- Data is encrypted during transmission and storage
- You can revoke access at any time in Settings > Health
- We do not share Apple Health data with third parties without your consent
- Apple's privacy policy also applies: https://www.apple.com/privacy/

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete such information.

For users aged 13-17 (minors):
- We provide additional privacy protections
- Guardians may request data access or deletion
- Marketing communications are restricted
