## 4. CapCut technical recipe

- **Project:** 1080×1920, 30 fps.
- **Audio bed:** soft ambient + low binaural pad. Suggested Replicate prompt for a custom bed: `"ambient pad, 60 BPM, soft piano, manifestation, warm, no drums, 60 seconds"` via MusicGen on Replicate.
- **Captions:** CapCut > Text > **Auto Captions**. Style: white with a soft gold drop-shadow (#E8C97A). Font: Inter or SF Pro Display, weight 800. Position: 50% from top (above the TikTok UI bar).
- **Hook text preset:** Bounce In > 0.3s, scale 110% → 100%.
- **Number stamps (3 · 6 · 9):** Text Animation > Typewriter In, 0.2s. Font: bold serif (e.g. Playfair Display) in gold (#E8C97A) on indigo background.
- **Transitions:** stick to **Crossfade** (0.2s) and **Film Burn** for the Tesla → modern jump. Avoid wipes — they cheapen manifestation content.
- **Color grade:** LUT > **Warm Film** + lift the shadows. Push midtones slightly toward gold (+8 yellow, +4 red). This matches the Codex cover.

## 5. Recording the voiceover

### Option A — Record your own voice (most authentic)
- iPhone Voice Memos with the mic ~15 cm from your mouth, 45° off-axis.
- Record each cut once at natural pace. The 60s cut should land between 58–62s — adjust pacing, don't trim words.
- Clean it up in CapCut > **Audio > Reduce Noise + Enhance Voice** (built-in AI).

### Option B — Clone your voice in ElevenLabs ($5/mo Starter)

## 8. What's NOT in this kit (and why)

Following the RHYTHMIX `SCRIPT.md` rule: don't promise things that aren't real yet. So these are left out:

- No specific user counts or testimonials (use them only when you have receipts).
- No dollar amounts or pricing in the VO (let the landing page handle that).
- No spiritual outcome guarantees ("you WILL manifest") — keep the language permissive ("people are manifesting").
- No launch dates inside the audio — easier to re-cut for evergreen if the audio is dateless.
