How It Works

A loop. Two seconds wide.

Heart rate and breath rhythm stream from your AirPods Pro 3 and Apple Watch. A prompt is composed every two seconds. Lyria RealTime returns a 48 kHz stereo segment of generative music. Apple's spatial audio engine wraps it around your head. Your nervous system responds. The next sample carries that response forward. Six steps. The whole circuit closes in under four seconds.

The Three Modes

Focus. Calm. Rest.

Three modes. Same loop, different prompt schedule. Each one steers the generative layer toward a target state — and watches your nervous system to know whether it's getting there.

I
For the working hour

Focus

Beta and gamma-leaning. Sparse, percussive, locked. Music tightens as your attention does.

Target: sustained 14 Hz attention band, low-variability breath, gentle HRV depression. Signature: RHYTHMIX dry pads with isochronic-shaped 40 Hz pulse buried in the sub-bass.

II
For the overwhelmed hour

Calm

Alpha and theta-leaning. The 0.1 Hz cardiac-coherence breath drives the bassline. As you settle, the harmonic field opens.

Target: 5.5s in / 5.5s out — the resonant frequency of the baroreflex. Signature: Verdi-tuned drone bed at 432 Hz, Lyria-generated melody layer on top.

III
For the closing hour

Rest

Delta-leaning. The music descends with you. As your HR drops below your resting baseline, the spatial field collapses inward and dims.

Target: 2 Hz delta entrainment, sub-50 bpm cruising heart rate. Signature: sub-bass at 174 Hz Foundation, with whisper-low brown noise spatialised at the back of the head.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your phone.

The intent classifier is Apple Foundation Models — a 3-billion parameter LLM that runs on your A-series chip at thirty tokens a second. No API key. No exfiltration. No analytics ping. Your session log lives in a CoreData store inside the app sandbox. The only thing that crosses your network is the Lyria audio stream itself — a stateless generative request, no identifiers attached, no replay possible.

It's a quiet kind of moat. Endel can't add this without rewriting their backend. Calm and Headspace can't add it without losing the engagement-tracking pipeline that drives their growth metrics. We built it the privacy way because the privacy way is also the cheaper way — Apple's framework is free, on-device, and doesn't need a backend to scale.

Is the science actually real, or is this wellness theatre?

The science is real. The product is a practice instrument, not a medical device — I'm clear about that distinction.

Three load-bearing findings: (1) Otto Loewi, 1921 Nobel — vagus nerve modulates heart via acetylcholine. Foundational, undisputed. (2) Lehrer et al., 1995 — resonance frequency of the autonomic system sits around 0.1 Hz (5.5s in, 5.5s out) where heart-rate variability peaks. Replicated dozens of times. (3) Lancet 2025 meta-analysis on music therapy — large effect sizes against anxiety across 26 RCTs.

What RESONATE does NOT claim: that it treats anxiety, ADHD, depression, or insomnia. It supports a daily practice associated with parasympathetic activation. That's it. The biometric readings are practice indicators, not clinical measurements.
