## Verification Rules

1. **Only count published, publicly accessible sources** — exclude paywalled reports unless available through institutional access
2. **Require specific data points** — not general claims (e.g., "% of families watch together" is good; "many families watch together" is not)
3. **Publication date must be 2023 or later** — 3-year recency window
4. **Cross-reference claims across sources** — flag contradictions
5. **Document all URLs and DOIs** — enable reader verification
6. **Note access restrictions** — if a source is paywalled, note "Institutional access required" in citation
