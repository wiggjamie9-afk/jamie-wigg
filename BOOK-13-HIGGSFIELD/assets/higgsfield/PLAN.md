| Page | Text | Notes |

| 1 | Sunny discovered the meadow of wildflowers by accident. She was walking through the bush when suddenly the trees opened up. | Meadow discovery |
| 2 | And there—a whole field of flowers, wild and free and beautiful. They weren't arranged in neat rows like the garden flowers. | Wild vs. garden |
| 3 | These flowers grew where they wanted, how they wanted. They were untamed. They were real. Some were tall. Some were short. Some were bright. Some were pale. | Wild freedom |
| 4 | They grew together in a jumble of colour and form and life. Sunny walked through the meadow slowly. The flowers brushed against her fur. | Walking through |
| 5 | They were soft and alive and gentle. She noticed how each flower was unique. Some had five petals. Some had more. Some were simple circles. | Flower uniqueness |
| 6 | Some were complicated spirals. But they were all beautiful. They were all worthy of being looked at. They were all part of the meadow's beauty. | All beautiful |
| 7 | Sunny lay down in the middle of the flowers and looked up at the sky. The flowers surrounded her. The petals swayed above her. The colours were all around her. | Lying in flowers |
| 8 | This was peace. This was beauty. This was everything Sunny needed. She didn't need the flowers to be perfect. | Perfect imperfection |
| 9 | She didn't need them to look a certain way. She just needed them to be here, being themselves, being beautiful in their own way. | Being themselves |
| 10 | As the afternoon turned to evening, the light on the flowers changed. The colours became deeper, more mysterious, more magical. | Changing light |
| 11 | The flowers seemed to glow from inside. They seemed to be releasing their beauty, their perfume, their essence into the evening air. | Glowing essence |
| 12 | Sunny breathed in the scent of a thousand flowers. She looked at the colours of a thousand petals. She felt surrounded by natural, wild, untamed beauty. | Sensory immersion |
| 13 | And she felt at peace. She felt small in the way that was comforting. She felt like part of something bigger and more beautiful than herself. | Part of something bigger |
| 14 | As the light faded, the flowers seemed to settle. They began to close, preparing for night. But even closing, they were beautiful. | Flowers closing |
| 15 | Sunny closed her eyes and fell asleep surrounded by the soft petals of wildflowers. At peace in this wild and beautiful place. | Falling asleep |
| 16 | Goodnight, wildflowers. Thank you for being beautiful. Sleep surrounded by natural beauty. | Final closure |
