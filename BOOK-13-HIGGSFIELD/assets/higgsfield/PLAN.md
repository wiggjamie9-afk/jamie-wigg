## Story Breakdown

| Page | Text |
|------|------|
| 1 | Sunny discovered the meadow of wildflowers by accident. She was walking through the bush when suddenly the trees opened up, and there—a whole field of flowers, wild and free and beautiful. |
| 2 | They weren't arranged in neat rows like the garden flowers. These flowers grew where they wanted, how they wanted. They were untamed. They were real. |
| 3 | Some were tall. Some were short. Some were bright. Some were pale. They grew together in a jumble of colour and form and life. Sunny walked through the meadow slowly. |
| 4 | The flowers brushed against her fur. They were soft and alive and gentle. She noticed how each flower was unique. Some had five petals. Some had more. Some were simple circles. |
| 5 | Some were complicated spirals. But they were all beautiful. They were all worthy of being looked at. They were all part of the meadow's beauty. |
| 6 | Sunny lay down in the middle of the flowers and looked up at the sky. The flowers surrounded her. The petals swayed above her. The colours were all around her. This was peace. This was beauty. |
| 7 | This was everything Sunny needed. She didn't need the flowers to be perfect. She didn't need them to look a certain way. She just needed them to be here, being themselves, being beautiful in their own way. |
| 8 | As the afternoon turned to evening, the light on the flowers changed. The colours became deeper, more mysterious, more magical. |
| 9 | The flowers seemed to glow from inside. They seemed to be releasing their beauty, their perfume, their essence into the evening air. Sunny breathed in the scent of a thousand flowers. |
| 10 | She looked at the colours of a thousand petals. She felt surrounded by natural, wild, untamed beauty. And she felt at peace. |
| 11 | She felt small in the way that was comforting. She felt like part of something bigger and more beautiful than herself. As the light faded, the flowers seemed to settle. |
| 12 | They began to close, preparing for night. But even closing, they were beautiful. Sunny closed her eyes and felt surrounded by the soft petals. |
| 13 | The meadow had become a sanctuary. A place where wildness and beauty existed in perfect harmony. Where nothing needed to be changed or improved. |
| 14 | The flowers continued to release their evening scent. The air grew cool. Everything was preparing for night, preparing for sleep. |
| 15 | Sunny felt held by the wildflowers, supported by their natural beauty. She was part of this wild place. She belonged here. |
| 16 | Goodnight, wildflowers. Thank you for being beautiful. Goodnight, meadow. Goodnight, Sunny. Sleep surrounded by natural beauty. |
