## Key Information Quick Reference

### Character Identity
- **Name**: MS. CHEN
- **Role**: Music teacher, neurodivergent mentor
- **Age**: Adult (40s-50s)
- **Ethnicity**: East Asian
- **Function**: Shows healthy, grounded adulthood

### Color Codes
| Element | Hex | Name |
|---------|-----|------|
| Skin | #8B6F47 | Warm Medium-Brown |
| Cardigan | #1B5E5E | Deep Teal |
| Shirt | #F5F1E8 | Cream |
| Jewelry | #DAA520 | Gold |
| Hair | #2c1810 | Dark Brown |
| Background | #FFFFFF | White |

### Design Components
- **Central Figure**: 3/4 view, graceful posture
- **Head Variations**: 3 different angles
- **Mentor Poses**: 4 poses (teaching, listening, playing, supporting)
- **Expressions**: 4 variations (serene, encouraging, musical, nurturing)
- **Layout**: 16:9 aspect ratio (1920×1080 recommended)

### Generation Requirements
- **Model**: FLUX 1.1 Pro (black-forest-labs/flux-1.1-pro)
- **Dimensions**: 1920×1080 (16:9)
- **Format**: PNG
- **Prompt**: In `ms-chen-character-sheet.md`
