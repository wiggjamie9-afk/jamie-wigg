- **Length**: Long, flowing down back and over shoulders
- **Color**: Dark brown (#2c1810)

## Animation Reference Notes

(For future animated interpretations)

### Posture Foundation
MS. CHEN's graceful posture translates beautifully into animation because:
- Slight forward lean suggests natural movement direction
- Balanced stance allows for dynamic poses
- Graceful bearing suggests smooth, flowing animation
- Hair movement is natural basis for animation flow

### Expression Animation
- Eyes change subtly between expressions
- Smile changes gradually (not snap transitions)
- Head tilt supports listening/engagement poses
- Hair responds naturally to head movement and rhythm

### Movement Quality
- Slow, purposeful (not twitchy)
- Graceful, flowing (not rigid)
- Responsive to others (not self-focused)
- Musical rhythm (embodies beat/tempo)

## Design Consistency Checklist

When creating variations or adaptations, verify:

- [ ] Colors match specification (check with color picker)
- [ ] Skin tone is warm medium-brown (#8B6F47)
- [ ] Cardigan is deep teal (#1B5E5E)
- [ ] Gold accents are present (#DAA520)
- [ ] Eyes show wisdom and kindness (not cold, not overly sweet)
- [ ] Posture is graceful (not rigid, not slouched)
- [ ] Hair flows gracefully (suggests movement, not static)
- [ ] Expressions vary (not always smiling)
- [ ] Character feels like a mentor (trustworthy, present, wise)
- [ ] Cultural representation is authentic (not stereotyped)

## Summary

MS. CHEN's visual identity is built on:

1. **Grounding Colors**: Deep teal + warm brown + gold
2. **Graceful Posture**: Balanced, present, engaged
3. **Expressive Eyes**: Wise, kind, patient, understanding
4. **Mentor Poses**: Teaching, listening, creating, supporting
5. **Varied Expressions**: Serene, encouraging, musical, nurturing

Together they create a character who is:
- Professional yet warm
- Wise yet accessible
- Grounded yet creative
- Strong yet compassionate
- A mentor worth following

**This is what healthy adulthood looks like.**
