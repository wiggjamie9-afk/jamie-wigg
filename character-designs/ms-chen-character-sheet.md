## CHARACTER OVERVIEW

MS. CHEN is an adult mentor figure—a music teacher showing what healthy, grounded adulthood looks like for neurodivergent individuals. She embodies wisdom, grace, musical sensitivity, and accessible mentorship. Her design conveys deep listening, patience, and the ability to guide others through both creative and personal growth.

Each showing:
- Different angles of hair styling
- Consistent wise, kind expression
- Slight variations in emotional depth

**Four Mentor Poses (stacked vertically)**

   - One hand gently placed on someone's shoulder (or gesture position)

   - Expression: Nurturing, supportive, compassionate

### Color Treatment
- Warm, accepting color palette throughout
- Generous use of gold accents showing value and wisdom
- Deep teal providing calming professional aesthetic
- High contrast with white background for studio quality

### Lighting & Shading
- Soft, warm studio lighting
- Subtle shadows showing form without harsh lines
- Warm highlights in hair and skin showing dimensionality
- Even, professional lighting across all variations

### Emotional Tone
- Wisdom without coldness
- Accessible without condescension
- Professional without rigidity
- Warm without excessive sweetness
