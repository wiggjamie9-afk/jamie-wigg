# RHYTHMIX Freebeat Ad — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Cinematic AI music video. Astronaut musician in deep space, cosmic nebulas, neon purple and electric pink palette, glowing waveforms, futuristic holographic UI floating in zero gravity. Cosmic rock blended with cyberpunk. Dramatic lighting, anamorphic lens flares, slow camera moves, particle effects. Story arc: lone creator rises from darkness, builds a musical universe, the universe expands into a glowing platform of light. Ends on triumphant astronaut hero shot. Reference: Blade Runner 2049 meets Daft Punk Interstella 5555.

## Colors

- `#04020a` — Deep space canvas
- `#7c3aed` — Neon purple (primary)
- `#ff2bd6` — Electric magenta-pink (primary)
- `#ff7aa6` — Soft pink accent
- `#8af6ff` — Cyber cyan (UI / waveform highlight)
- `#e0c0ff` — Lavender highlight
- `#ffe070` — Hot gold (proof beats)
- `#ffffff` — Display text
- `#a0a0b0` — Muted body

## Typography

- Display: `"Space Grotesk", system-ui` — headlines, wordmark
- Mono: `"JetBrains Mono", monospace` — text cards, timestamps, labels

## Motion

- Slow cinematic camera moves on backdrops (60s+ wash)
- Fast text-card entrances (0.4–0.7s, `expo.out` / `power3.out`)
- Crossfade between scenes (0.5s)
- Glow pulses on waveforms and platform UI
- No bounce / elastic — confident, decisive

## What NOT to Do

- No `#3b82f6`, `#333`, default sans — palette is fixed
- No full-frame linear gradients (banding) — use radial nebulas + localized glow
- No scene-internal exit animations — the crossfade IS the exit
- No emoji-heavy UI — let typography and color carry mood
