# JW Empire — 18-Month Strategy

One path, five stages. The four goals (cash flow, salary replacement, audience/brand,
sellable asset) are not separate — they are stages in order. Each funds the next.

**Rule #1: Do not start the next stage until the current stage's GATE is cleared.**
The superpower is building fast. The kryptonite is starting the next thing before
finishing this one. The gates exist to fight the kryptonite.

**The one flagship:** STARLIGHTMIX Studio — it already takes payment (Gumroad license +
license Worker). Everything points at it. Everything else waits.

---

## Stage 0 — Finish the Build  (now → ~1 week)
**Goal:** the full 2-month podcast catalog, in hand, funnel-ready.

- Generate ep16–ep27 audio (Waves 3 + 4) → completes 20 shows × ep2–ep27.
- Add a standard call-to-action outro (→ STARLIGHTMIX Studio) from ep16 forward.
- Stand up Buzzsprout publishing (config + token) and email capture.

**GATE:** all episodes generated, CTA outro in place, publishing pipeline tested (dry-run green).

---

## Stage 1 — Launch & Cash Flow  (Months 1–3)
**Goal:** first real money in the account.

- All 20 shows live, Mon/Wed/Fri, auto-scheduled.
- Every episode funnels to the flagship.
- Email capture live everywhere — start the owned list.
- Win = consistent sales, however small. Proof the machine works.

**GATE:** sales are happening (not zero) AND the email list is growing.

---

## Stage 2 — Prune & Replace Salary  (Months 3–9)
**Goal:** steady, durable income.

- Read the download data. Sort shows: Winners (2–4) / Maybes / Dead.
- Keep winners at 2x/week, maybes at 1x/week, **pause the dead** (stop their hosting cost).
- Concentrate all promotion on winners.
- Convert one-time sales into recurring revenue.

**GATE:** monthly recurring revenue covers your bills.

---

## Stage 3 — Audience & Brand  (Months 6–15)
**Goal:** the JW / RHYTHMIX name means something.

- Winning shows + flagship compound into a real following.
- The email list (seeded in Stage 1) is now thousands who buy when you ship.
- Now — and only now — revive the best of the "untapped" 10, launching to an audience.

**GATE:** you launch something and people show up on day one.

---

## Stage 4 — Sellable Asset  (Months 12–18)
**Goal:** something worth buying.

- A buyer purchases three things: audience + revenue + brand. Stages 1–3 built exactly those.
- A focused brand with predictable revenue and an engaged list is a sellable asset.
  A pile of 30 prototypes is not.

**GATE:** you *could* sell — whether or not you choose to.

---

## The only metric that matters
Not launches. Not episode counts. **Paying customers / MRR.**
If a project doesn't move that number, it's a hobby, not a business.

## The biggest lever (don't forget)
Podcast ad CPM is small. The real money is pointing listeners to the apps.
One lifetime license beats thousands of ad downloads.

## Parked (not deleted) until Stage 3
The "untapped" 10 and duplicate app prototypes. Proof you can build — also focus-splitters.
Revive them when there's an audience to launch to.
