## PART 2: YouTube Shorts Revenue Potential

### How YouTube Shorts Pay (The Real Numbers)

**YouTube Shorts Fund** (up to Nov 2024):
- Users who create Shorts get paid from a pool based on views
- **Average payout:** $0.01-0.10 per 1,000 views (CPM for Shorts is LOW)
- **Ad revenue:** Starting in 2024, YouTube puts ads in Shorts (55% to creator, 45% to YouTube)
- **Realistic:** $100-500/month from Shorts alone (even with 100K+ views/month)

**Why Shorts pay so little:**
- Video length = 15-60 seconds (advertisers pay less for short content)
- Lower engagement = fewer ads shown per view
- Mobile-first audience = lower CPM rates

### Your Shorts Strategy (The Real Opportunity)

**Shorts are NOT for direct revenue. Shorts are for FUNNEL.**

Shorts drive viewers to:
1. **Full-length videos** (higher CPM, $2-8 per 1K views)
2. **Your app landing pages** (freemium conversions)
3. **Course sales** (Agent Builder course)
4. **Sponsorships** (companies pay to be mentioned)

**Conservative:** 20K views/week shorts + funnel to full videos

```

```
