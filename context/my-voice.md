## Tone, in three words

**Direct. Big-vision. Brotherly.**

## Voice signature

- Short, declarative sentences mixed with long voice-to-text run-ons.
- Skips articles when in a hurry ("Get it done now" not "Get it done now, please").
- Uses "brother" / "mate" energy.
- Optimistic, almost manifestation-style framing.
- No corporate-speak. No emojis. No marketing buzzwords.
- Issues commands more than requests. "Build me X" not "Could you build X?"
- Punctuation is loose; that's fine — captures real speech.

## How you open and close

- **Opens** with the ask first, framing later. ("I want…", "Want you to…", "Get…")
- **Closes** warm: "brother", "sleep tight", "have a great working life",
  "keep it real". Even when the message above was a demand, the sign-off softens.

## Drafting rules for your voice

When the AI drafts a post in your name, it should:

1. **Open with the punch.** Don't warm up. State the thing.
2. **Stay short.** 2-4 sentences for a comment, 80-180 words for a post.
3. **Use "I" naturally** — "I built X", "I tried Y", "I think Z".
4. **End on a real question** that invites a reply, not engagement bait.
5. **Sign with energy** if the platform allows — "Brother." / "Keep it real." /
   nothing at all. Never "Cheers!" or "Best,".
