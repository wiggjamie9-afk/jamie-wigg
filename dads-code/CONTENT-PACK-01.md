# DAD'S CODE — Content Pack 01
## 10 ready-to-film scripts · 30s + 60s versions · TikTok / Reels / Shorts

**How to use this pack:**
- Film on your iPhone, front camera, face in frame. No editing tricks needed. Natural light, talk like you talk.
- One video per day, same time each day, for 10 days. Consistency beats quality.
- 30s version = the script as written. 60s version = add the "EXTEND" lines.
- Post the SAME video to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. One film, three platforms.
- Captions and hashtags included under each script. Copy-paste.
- Do NOT link to anything yet. First 30 days = build trust, not sell.

**The voice:** You're not a guru. You're the bloke who got burned and is telling the truth about it. Rough edges are the brand. Don't polish the accent, don't hide the tiredness.

### 2. ONE THING
**HOOK:** "If your brain has forty tabs open, this is the only productivity tip that's ever worked for me."
**SCRIPT (30s):**
Don't write a to-do list. A to-do list is a guilt list — twenty things you'll stare at and do none of.
Every morning, one question: what's the ONE thing today?
Not the most fun one. The one that matters. Write it somewhere you'll see it. Do it badly if you have to — badly done beats perfectly planned.
The other nineteen things? They'll survive without you.
**EXTEND (60s):** Add: "This isn't laziness, it's physics for scattered brains. Momentum comes from finishing, not from planning. Finish one thing, you get the dopamine, tomorrow gets easier. Plan twenty, finish none, and tomorrow you're heavier. One thing. That's the whole code."
**CAPTION:** The to-do list is lying to you. #adhd #adhdtips #dadlife #productivity #onething

### 3. THE 10-MINUTE LIE
**HOOK:** "Your brain says the job takes three hours. It's lying. Here's the trick."
**SCRIPT (30s):**
The reason you can't start isn't laziness. It's that your brain prices the WHOLE job — three hours, so it refuses to open the invoice, the email, the shed door.
So don't buy the whole job. Buy ten minutes.
Set a timer. Ten minutes, then you're allowed to stop. That's the deal.
Nine times out of ten you keep going. But even if you stop — you started. And starting was the wall.
**EXTEND (60s):** Add: "Dads especially — that thing you've been avoiding for a month? The form, the phone call, the sorry you owe someone? It's a ten-minute job wearing a three-hour costume. Timer on. Go."
**CAPTION:** Starting is the wall. Here's the ladder. #adhd #procrastination #dadlife #mensmentalhealth

### 4. THE APOLOGY
**HOOK:** "The hardest sentence a dad ever says is seven words long."
**SCRIPT (30s):**
"I was wrong, and I'm sorry, mate."
Said to your kid. After you snapped, after you were short with them because your head was somewhere else.
Your old man maybe never said it to you. That's not a reason — that's the reason. It ends with you.
Kids don't need a perfect dad. They need a dad who repairs. That's the difference between a bad moment and a bad childhood.
**EXTEND (60s):** Add: "And here's what nobody tells you: the apology teaches them more than the calm would have. They learn that big people can be wrong out loud. They'll carry that into every relationship they ever have. Worst day, best lesson — if you repair it."
**CAPTION:** It ends with you. #dadlife #fatherhood #mensmentalhealth #parenting

### 5. THE SHINY TOOL TEST
**HOOK:** "Before you download that new app, ask this one question."
**SCRIPT (30s):**
"Will this help me finish the thing I already started?"
Not COULD it. WILL it. This week.
If the answer's no — it's not a tool, it's an escape hatch. New tools feel like progress. They're usually just procrastination with a receipt.
I know because I downloaded fifty of them. The pile didn't finish anything. Deleting half of them did.
**EXTEND (60s):** Add: "Same test for courses, same for gurus, same for that 50-repos-you-need list. The people posting those lists profit whether you finish anything or not. You've already got enough tools. What you're missing is Tuesday, doing the boring thing, again."
**CAPTION:** New tool or escape hatch? One question tells you. #productivity #adhd #sidehustle #aihype

### 6. THE RESET
**HOOK:** "For every dad who's lost it at his kids and hated himself after."
**SCRIPT (30s):**
The damage isn't the shout. It's what you do in the sixty seconds after.
Most of us spiral — guilt, shame, then we're snappy again because now we feel like garbage. The spiral does more harm than the shout.
Instead: walk to another room. One minute. Breathe out longer than you breathe in — that's the switch that calms your nervous system.
Then go back in softer than you came out. That's it. That's the whole skill.
**EXTEND (60s):** Add: "You're not a bad dad because you have a temper. You're carrying pressure your kids can't see and shouldn't have to. But the repair is on you. Sixty seconds, breathe, back in, 'sorry mate, Dad got loud.' Do that a hundred times and your kids grow up safe around anger — theirs and everyone else's."
**CAPTION:** Losing it doesn't make you a bad dad. What happens next is the code. #dadlife #angermanagement #mensmentalhealth #fatherhood

### 8. THE ALGORITHM ISN'T YOUR MATE
**HOOK:** "Why you feel behind every single time you open your phone."
**SCRIPT (30s):**
Your feed shows you twenty-two-year-olds with Lamborghinis and passive income. You feel behind. So you scroll more, looking for the answer. The feed shows you more of them. Rounder and rounder.
Here's the thing: feeling behind is the product. That feeling is what they sell to advertisers and course-buyers.
You're not behind. You're a grown man with real responsibilities being farmed by an app.
Unfollow every account that makes you feel broke. Watch what happens to your head in a week.
**EXTEND (60s):** Add: "I kept screenshots of all the 'money hack' posts I saved over three months. Went back and checked — not one of them made me a dollar. Every one of them made the poster money though. That's the whole business model, and you're the crop."

### 9. WHAT YOUR KIDS ACTUALLY WANT
**HOOK:** "Asked around: not one kid said 'I wish Dad earned more.'"
**SCRIPT (30s):**
Kids don't clock your income. They clock your face when they walk in the room.
Does he look up? Does he put the phone down? Is Dad here when he's here?
The hustle says: grind now, be present later. But kids don't bank presence for later. It's now or it's missed.
Ten minutes, phone in the other room, doing whatever dumb thing they're into. That's the wealth transfer that actually lands.
**EXTEND (60s):** Add: "I'm not saying money doesn't matter — pressure is real, bills are real. I'm saying don't let the chase for the second thing torch the first thing. You can be broke and a great dad. You cannot be absent and a great dad. That maths doesn't move."
**CAPTION:** They don't remember the income. They remember the face. #dadlife #fatherhood #presence #family

### 10. THE CODE
**HOOK:** "Every family runs on a code. Most dads never write theirs down."
**SCRIPT (30s):**
Your kids are learning rules from you either way — from your worst moments if you don't choose, from your best if you do.
So write it down. Four or five lines. Not goals — rules you refuse to break.
Mine starts: "I show up, even when I'm scattered. I say sorry when I get it wrong. I do the one thing before the ten things."
Stick it somewhere you'll see it on the bad days. Bad days are what it's for.
That's Dad's Code. Write yours.
**EXTEND (60s):** Add: "And tell your kids what's in it. Out loud. Because a code your family knows about is a code they'll hold you to — and one day, one they'll write their own version of. That's the inheritance that doesn't cost a cent."
**CAPTION:** Write it down. Bad days are what it's for. #dadscode #dadlife #fatherhood #values
