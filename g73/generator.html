The algorithm is real even though the drive isn't. This is a textbook discrete-event simulation: a seeded PRNG (mulberry32) feeds a loop that advances mission time in fixed steps, accumulates pillar drift as a random walk, and triggers events when state crosses the §3.2 thresholds. Deterministic seeding is the same technique procedural games (Minecraft, roguelikes) use for shareable worlds.
