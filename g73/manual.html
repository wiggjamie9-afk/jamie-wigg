02 The 7.3 Hz Resonance Hypothesis

G-73 canon holds that the vacuum expectation value can be "grounded" — made to do mechanical work — when driven at a universal resonance frequency:

fG73 = 7.3 Hz
the anchor frequency (fictional constant)

The Negative Sink is the fraction of bubble-formation energy a vessel must supply from its own reactors; the remainder is drawn from the grounded vacuum. Certified engines achieve a Negative Sink of η = 0.01 — the ship pays 1%, the vacuum pays 99%.

Zero-point energy is real; extracting it is not. The vacuum expectation value exists in quantum field theory, but no known mechanism "grounds" it for thrust, and a drive that accelerates without expelling reaction mass would violate conservation of momentum unless it pushes on some yet-unobserved field. 7.3 Hz corresponds to no known universal constant — it is close to the Schumann resonance of Earth's ionospheric cavity (~7.83 Hz), which is a property of our planet, not of spacetime.

04 G73-015 Chronos Stabilizer

Without stabilization, a vessel at relativistic vconv accrues a time debt against the reference frame. The Chronos Stabilizer phase-locks the ship's local clock lattice to the 7.3 Hz anchor, holding t_local = t_ref for the duration of the lock, so crews return to the calendar they left.

tlocal = tref · (1 − v²/c²)½  →  tlocal = tref (stabilized)
left: unstabilized dilation (real physics) · right: under G73-015 lock (fiction)
Time dilation is proven physics — confirmed from muon decay to GPS clock corrections. The left-hand relation is the real Lorentz factor at work. What is fictional is the right-hand side: no frequency, 7.3 Hz or otherwise, can "anchor" proper time. Dilation is geometry, not a signal you can phase-lock away.
