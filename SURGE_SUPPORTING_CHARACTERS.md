# SURGE PILOT — Supporting Character Briefs

## Overview

Four supporting characters extend SURGE's world beyond Ziggy and echo the core theme: ADHD (and neurodiversity more broadly) looks different in everyone. These characters show that strengths coexist with struggles, that support can come in unexpected forms, and that a classroom contains multiple ways of experiencing the world.

---

# 1. MIRA — Ziggy's Best Friend & Grounding Presence

## Visual Design

### Base Form (Reality)

#### Head
- **Shape:** Circle (10-year-old proportions, same scale as Ziggy)
- **Hair:** Medium-length, textured curls in warm dark brown (#3D2817—slightly warmer than Ziggy's brown). Hair is pulled into a loose, intentional bun with a few wisps falling forward—not chaotic, but lived-in. Small detail: a single braid or twist accents one side of her face. Hair signals: "I take care of myself in my own rhythm, not by adult rules."
- **Eyes:** Large circles; pupils are warm brown (#6B4423—earthy, grounding). Pupils are steady when observing, quick to notice changes in others' faces/mood. Eyes often slightly narrowed in observation or thought (not unfocused like Ziggy's, but differently present).
- **Mouth:** Soft smile baseline, or thoughtful line (listening intently)
- **Skin:** Rich warm tan (#C89968—medium-deep skin tone, diverse representation)

#### Body
- **Shape:** Rectangle (10-year-old, slight build, flexible posture)
- **Clothing:** 
  - **Primary:** Sage green oversized sweatshirt (#2D8A3D—echo of Mrs. Henderson's calming color, shows Mira as emotional anchor) with hand-dyed/tie-dye effect in cream and warm mustard (#D4A574) at sleeves and hem. Sweatshirt is large enough to be cozy, asymmetrical hem (one side longer—subtle visual link to Ziggy's asymmetry, but intentional rather than chaotic).
  - **Secondary:** Black or dark gray leggings, white or cream socks (always visible, always clean—signals Mira's self-care without perfectionism)
  - **Sneakers:** Warm brown canvas or suede (#8B6F47) with simple white laces (tied, but not aggressively neat)
- **Build:** Slight, flexible, grounded posture. Center of gravity is stable; weight is balanced.

#### Limbs
- **Arms & Legs:** Cylinders (consistent with design language)
- **Hands:** 4-finger design (consistency with Ziggy + Jake). Hands are often holding something (notebook, drawing pencil, Ziggy's shoulder—agent of support/presence)
- **Feet:** Simple ovals; grounded, stable stance

#### Details Signaling Character
- **Tactile presence:** Often has her hand on or near Ziggy (shoulder, arm, wrist)—not clingy, but grounding touch is her natural language
- **Notebook/sketchbook visible:** She carries a small notebook or sketchbook (signals thoughtfulness, processing)
- **Posture is open but centered:** Unlike Ziggy's restlessness, Mira is calm and anchored. She can sit still without fidgeting, which visually contrasts with Ziggy and shows her as the "stable orbit."
- **Hair detail:** The intentional braid/twist shows she's thoughtful about her appearance in a way that's authentic to her (not adult-imposed, not chaotic)

### Superhero Form (Resonance)

*Mira's superpower emerges as **Resonance**—she can attune to others' emotional frequencies and amplify their strength/clarity. Her transformation is subtle, not flashy.*

#### Head & Hair
- Same circle, but the braid/twist **glows softly** with Warm Gold (#D4A574)
- Eyes gain a **soft amber aura** (#D4A574 glow, 3–5px, low opacity)—signals empathic attunement
- Hair curls float/drift slightly (less electric than Ziggy's, more fluid—like gentle wind)

#### Body & Clothing
- **Core form:** Rectangle remains, but the sweatshirt glows with **Warm Gold outline** (2–3px soft glow)
- **Transformation effect:** The tie-dye pattern **animates gently**, colors shifting and flowing like water—represents emotional attunement/fluidity
- **Aura:** Warm Gold glow surrounds figure (softer than Ziggy's, more inviting—3–5px blur, 0.5 opacity)
- **No sharp edges:** Unlike Ziggy's electric lines, Mira's glow is rounded and warm

#### Effects
- **Particles:** Soft amber/gold circles drift around her like dust motes in sunlight (very gentle, calming motion)—2–3px circles, slow float, warm tones only
- **Resonance ripples:** When she's attuned to someone, gentle concentric circles of Warm Gold radiate outward (subtle, not aggressive—1–2px lines, fade quickly)
- **Size:** Same as base form (power is internal attunement, not physical expansion)

### Expression Sheets (3 expressions)

#### Expression 1: Grounded / Listening (Baseline)
- **Eyes:** Soft, forward or on Ziggy, pupils calm and observant (reading the room)
- **Mouth:** Slight smile or neutral (listening mode—full presence)
- **Posture:** Upright, centered, often leaning slightly toward whoever needs support. One hand might be extended or resting on a friend's shoulder.
- **Context:** Sitting with Ziggy at lunch, observing Jake's mood, being present in class, moment of quiet strength
- **Companion gesture:** Hand on shoulder, hand holding notebook, arm at side in open stance

#### Expression 2: Concern / Attunement (Empathic)
- **Eyes:** Focused on the person who's struggling, pupils enlarged (taking in their state), eyebrows slightly raised (concern + curiosity)
- **Mouth:** Soft, slight frown or pursed lips (concentrating on what they're feeling, not what she thinks)
- **Posture:** Lean toward friend, head tilted slightly (listening with full attention), shoulders open
- **Context:** Moment when Ziggy spirals; moment when she realizes Mrs. Henderson is overwhelmed; moment when she reads Jake's unspoken doubt
- **Gesture:** Hand reaching toward friend, hand to heart (internal resonance), head tilt

#### Expression 3: Clarity / Support (Strength)
- **Eyes:** Bright, warm, locked on the person she's supporting (steady gaze—they can rely on this)
- **Mouth:** Confident smile (not big or artificial; genuine and calm)
- **Posture:** Upright, grounded, arm around friend or hand extended in support. Full presence, no doubt.
- **Context:** Moment she tells Ziggy "Your brain isn't the problem; you belong," moment she stands by him in front of peers, moment she helps him see his own strength
- **Gesture:** Arm around shoulder (steady support), both hands open, standing beside (equal, not above)

### Construction Notes (for animators)
- **Line weight:** 2.5px main outlines, 1.5px details
- **Stability principle:** Unlike Ziggy's asymmetry (signals chaos), Mira is intentionally symmetrical and grounded. Her stability is visual strength.
- **Hair detail:** The braid/twist is not performance or perfectionism; it's self-care. Lock this intentional detail in design.
- **Touch language:** Mira's hands are often in contact with others. This is not clingy or intrusive—it's her language of support. Animation should show natural, non-aggressive touch.
- **Color consistency:** Sage Green sweatshirt is always #2D8A3D; Warm Gold accents always #D4A574. No drift.
- **Modular construction:** Head, body, hair variations (bun up/down, sweatshirt on/off) should be separable for efficiency.
- **Movement archetype:** Grounded, intentional, graceful. No fidgeting. When Mira moves, she moves with purpose and calm.

## Personality

### Voice & Casting Direction

**Age dynamic:** Fully 10-year-old, but observant beyond her years. She notices things adults miss—not because she's precocious, but because she's genuinely attuned to people.

**Energy:** Calm, present, centered. Speech is clear and purposeful—not fast/scattered like Ziggy, not droning/monotone, but genuinely present. She listens more than she talks, which makes her words carry weight.

**Humor:** Quiet, knowing humor—dry observations about what she notices (often about her own feelings or observations of others). Not self-deprecating; not mocking. When she jokes, it's with warmth and a wry smile.

**Interior monologue:** Reflective, noticing. She narrates what she observes about people and situations with clarity. "Ziggy's spiraling again. He thinks he's stupid, but I've seen him solve things in seconds. Mrs. Henderson looks tired. Jake's worried but trying to hide it."

**Voice quality:** Warm, clear, slightly lower register than Ziggy (not deep, just grounded). Midwest accent (subtle, same as Ziggy/town setting). Speech is articulate—not fancy, but thoughtful word choice. Can shift into softer, more reassuring tone when supporting someone.

**Voice actor direction (Sensitivity Review note):** You are a 10-year-old with emotional intelligence and genuine empathy. You're not "wise beyond your years" or a stereotype; you're a real kid who pays attention. Your baseline is calm and present—you don't need to be loud or fast to be powerful. When you speak, it's because you have something to say. When you listen, you're genuinely listening, not waiting to talk. You can express warmth without being saccharine. Play intelligence and emotional presence as your baseline.

**Voice actor reference:** Seeking 9–11 year old with warm, clear voice; natural ability to listen and be present; can shift from observant calm to supportive strength; slight Midwestern inflection; must avoid precocious/wise-beyond-years trope and play as intelligent, grounded peer.

## Role in Story

### Relationship to Ziggy
- **Best friend:** They met in 2nd grade (flashback possible in S1). Mira saw Ziggy get excluded during a quiet reading activity and invited him to the library instead, where they built a Lego world together. That's where their friendship anchored—Mira doesn't try to "fix" Ziggy; she just sees him.
- **Emotional anchor:** When Ziggy spirals, Mira is the presence that says "You're okay. I see you. You belong." She doesn't minimize his struggles; she grounds him.
- **Mirror:** Mira shows Ziggy that his intensity isn't the problem—her steadiness shows *one* way to move through the world, but Ziggy's speed is equally valid. She never makes him feel "broken" for being different.

### Relationship to other characters
- **Mrs. Henderson:** Mira respects her but sees her fatigue. In later episodes, Mira becomes a bridge—she helps Mrs. Henderson understand Ziggy (not by explaining ADHD, but by showing her how Ziggy thinks differently).
- **Jake:** Genuine friendship, slightly different vibe. Jake is uncomplicated joy; Mira is steady presence. They complement Ziggy's chaos differently.
- **Echo (Antagonist):** Mira is one of the few people who can reach Ziggy when Echo has him deep in the spiral. Her grounding presence is a counterpoint to Echo's noise.

### Character Arc (Pilot → Series)
- **Pilot:** Mira is Ziggy's anchor and early witness to his Surge transformation. She encourages him to lean into his power without shame.
- **S1 early episodes:** Mira learns that she has her own superpower (Resonance) and that being the "stable" friend doesn't mean invisible. She's equally powerful, just differently so.
- **S1 mid/late episodes:** Mira's arc explores the flip side of empathy—learning that she can't carry everyone's feelings, that saying "no" and setting boundaries is also a superpower.
- **S2+:** Potential deepening of Mira's identity (Does she have ADHD too? Anxiety? Autism? This is intentionally open for consultant input). Her own neurodivergence might emerge gradually, showing that "grounding presence" can coexist with internal complexity.

## Animation Notes

### Movement Style
- **Baseline:** Graceful, intentional, grounded. No fidgeting, no restlessness. When Mira sits, she's fully present; when she stands, she's stable.
- **Distinctive archetype:** FLOATY-GROUNDED hybrid. While Ziggy is JITTERY and Echo is GLITCHY, Mira is FLOWING—smooth arcs, deliberate transitions, no sharp stops. Think water—change direction smoothly, but with intention.
- **Unique gesture language:** 
  - Hand to shoulder/arm of someone in distress (not intrusive, but grounding touch)
  - Head tilt + full eye contact (active listening)
  - Hand over heart (internal attunement)
  - Slow-motion walk (brings calm into chaotic spaces)
- **In scenes with Ziggy:** Mira is the visual anchor. When Ziggy is jittery/overwhelmed, cut to Mira in a grounded pose to show contrast and safety.

### Expression Transitions
- Mira's expressions shift smoothly (no jarring jumps). When she moves from Listening → Attunement, the shift is gradual (pupils soften, mouth slightly parts, shoulders lean forward—all fluid).
- Her smiles are genuine and warm, never forced or customer-service-fake.
- When she's concerned, you see it in her whole body (not just face)—shoulders forward, eyes engaged, full presence.

### Unique Visual Moments
- **Grounding Moment:** When Ziggy is spiraling, Mira places her hand on his shoulder. Camera pulls back to show her as a stable silhouette while he fidgets—visual metaphor of her presence stabilizing his chaos.
- **Resonance Moment:** When Mira's superpower activates, the tie-dye pattern in her sweatshirt animates with color flowing, like emotional attunement made visible. The colors match those around her (she picks up emotional "color").
- **Mirror Moment:** In a scene, Mira is sitting very still, fully listening, while Ziggy is moving constantly. No dialogue needed—the visual contrast says everything about their different ways of being present.

# 2. MS. CHEN — Music Teacher (Neurodivergent Educator)

*Note: Ms. Chen is a new supporting character (not Ziggy's mom, whose name is Sarah). She's introduced as the school's music/arts teacher and becomes an unexpected mentor figure who recognizes Ziggy's strengths.*

## Visual Design

### Base Form (Reality)

#### Head
- **Shape:** Oval (adult proportions, slightly elongated—signals thoughtfulness)
- **Hair:** Long, dark brown (#2B1B15—same shade as Ziggy, visual connection without blood relation), worn in a loose side-braid or half-up style. Hair suggests: "I care about this, but not by restrictive rules." A few strands escape the braid, but it's intentional, not chaotic.
- **Eyes:** Warm brown circles (#6B4423—same earthy tone as Mira, signals alignment with grounding/empathic figures), pupils are observant but sometimes look away (processing, thinking internally). Eyes show kindness but also occasional overwhelm (sensory sensitivity in a loud school building).
- **Mouth:** Often slight smile (genuine pleasure in teaching) or thoughtful neutral (listening to music/students)
- **Skin:** Medium warm tan (#B8956E—diverse skin tone, perhaps South Asian American, South Asian diaspora, or mixed; exact cultural specification to be finalized with sensitivity panel)

#### Body
- **Shape:** Rectangle (tall adult, graceful posture—reflects music/movement background)
- **Clothing:**
  - **Primary:** Deep teal or jewel-tone sweater/cardigan (#1F5F6B—calming, musical, signals arts teacher without caricature) over a cream or light gray undershirt
  - **Secondary:** Dark gray or charcoal pants, comfortable and practical for moving/demonstrating music
  - **Accessories:** Small jewelry—maybe a delicate necklace with a musical note or abstract pendant (signals her passion without being costume-y); a watch (she's time-aware, unlike some ADHD educators)
  - **Subtle detail:** Her cardigan may have mismatched buttons or be buttoned slightly off-center (subtle visual nod to her own neurodivergence—functional, not chaotic)
- **Build:** Graceful, energetic, capable. Posture is open and engaged (not the tension of Mrs. Henderson).

#### Limbs
- **Arms & Legs:** Cylinders (design consistency)
- **Hands:** 4-finger design. Hands are often gesturing (teaching music, demonstrating rhythm) or holding something (baton, music sheet, instrument—agents of her teaching)
- **Feet:** Simple ovals; grounded stance with slight weight shift (music teacher who moves/demonstrates)

#### Details Signaling Character
- **Movement is rhythmic:** Even standing still, there's a subtle sway or gentle rock (internal beat)
- **Genuine smile:** Eyes involved in smile (not service smile, but authentic warmth)
- **Posture is open:** Shoulders back, chest open—inviting and present
- **Often has hands in gesture:** Demonstrating pitch, rhythm, conducting—hands are agents of her teaching
- **Sensory detail:** Sometimes gently touches her own arms or leans against wall (self-soothing in a loud building, subtle ADHD/sensory processing signal)

### Superhero Form (Harmony)

*Ms. Chen's superpower is **Harmony**—she can find the right frequency/tone that brings disparate elements into sync. She helps others find their voice and rhythm.*

#### Head & Hair
- Same oval, but the braid **glows softly** with Warm Gold (#D4A574—same as Mira's Resonance, signals alignment with grounding figures)
- Eyes gain a **soft golden aura** (#D4A574 glow, 3–5px, low opacity)
- Hair gently floats/sways (as if moved by music)

#### Body & Clothing
- **Core form:** Rectangle remains, but the teal cardigan **glows with golden outline** (2–3px soft glow)
- **Transformation effect:** The cardigan **animates with subtle wave patterns**, like sound waves or ripples of harmony flowing outward
- **Aura:** Warm Gold glow surrounds figure (soft, inviting, same warmth as Mira's—3–5px blur, 0.5 opacity)
- **Musical aura particles:** Soft golden notes or sound-wave circles drift gently around her (like visible music)

#### Effects
- **Sound wave particles:** Gentle concentric circles of gold radiate outward when she's actively facilitating harmony (1–2px lines, soft fade, musical timing)
- **Harmonic resonance:** Around people she's helping, subtle golden threads appear (connecting them to the harmony)
- **Size:** Same as base form (power is attunement + facilitation, not physical expansion)

### Expression Sheets (3 expressions)

#### Expression 1: Engaged / Teaching (Baseline)
- **Eyes:** Forward or on students, pupils bright and present (joy in teaching)
- **Mouth:** Smile (genuine, warm, involved)
- **Posture:** Upright, open, often with one hand gesturing (demonstrating, conducting, inviting)
- **Context:** In music class, demonstrating a concept, moment of genuine joy in her work
- **Companion gesture:** Hand extended in invitation, hand conducting/gesturing, shoulders back (open)

#### Expression 2: Overwhelmed / Processing (Sensory)
- **Eyes:** Slightly distant or unfocused, pupils smaller (internal processing, temporary overwhelm)
- **Mouth:** Tight smile or neutral (managing sensory input)
- **Posture:** Still present, but slight shoulder tension, hand might be to temple or at arm (self-soothing, processing noise)
- **Context:** After a loud assembly, moment when multiple classes are happening, hallway chaos, moment when her own sensory needs are high
- **Gesture:** Hand to temple (thinking/processing), leaning against surface (steadying), hand on arm (self-soothing)

#### Expression 3: Recognition / Encouragement (Support)
- **Eyes:** Bright, warm, focused on student with belief (seeing their potential)
- **Mouth:** Warm smile (genuine, approving, warm)
- **Posture:** Forward lean, open shoulders, often hand on student's shoulder or reaching toward them
- **Context:** Moment she hears Ziggy hum a complex melody; moment she recognizes Jake's sense of rhythm; moment she tells a struggling student "Your voice matters"
- **Gesture:** Hand on shoulder (support), hand gesturing to student's heart (where the music comes from), eyes full of belief

### Construction Notes (for animators)
- **Line weight:** 2.5px main outlines, 1.5px details
- **Stability principle:** Like Mira, Ms. Chen is grounded and intentional. Her stability is earned (she manages her own overwhelm, not by ignoring it, but by understanding it).
- **Hair detail:** The braid is intentional and slightly undone. This signals self-care + lived neurodivergence (not perfection, but functional care).
- **Movement:** Rhythmic, graceful, with subtle sway. When she stands still, there's gentle motion (internal beat). No fidgeting like Ziggy, but not frozen either.
- **Color consistency:** Teal cardigan always #1F5F6B; Golden accents always #D4A574. No drift.
- **Hands as tools:** Ms. Chen's hands are very expressive—always conducting, gesturing, demonstrating, or self-soothing. This is character, not excessive movement.
- **Age signals:** She's younger than Mrs. Henderson (likely 30s-40s), so no gray hair or heavy age lines, but she shows the strain of navigating the school system as a neurodivergent educator (subtle tension, sensory sensitivity).

## Personality

### Voice & Casting Direction

**Age dynamic:** 30s-40s woman, professional but approachable. She speaks to students as people, not as kids to manage.

**Energy:** Warm, rhythmic, musical. Speech has a natural cadence and flow—not rushed, not slow, but with intentional pacing (musical timing). She's expressive without being theatrical.

**Humor:** Genuinely funny, warm, self-aware about her own quirks. She might joke about forgetting where she put her baton or about getting lost in music and losing track of time ("I meant for that lesson to be 10 minutes; we were in hyperfocus for 20 minutes—no regrets").

**Interior monologue:** Musical, rhythmic. She's aware of sensory input around her. "The hallway is too loud. I need to ground myself. Okay. Now I can see Ziggy's potential. He hears music the way I do."

**Voice quality:** Warm, clear, slightly musical inflection in speech (not speaking in song, but speech has flow/melody). Adult woman's voice (30s-40s), moderate depth, warm timbre. If from Midwest setting, subtle Midwest accent; if from another region, adjust accordingly. Can shift into softer, more encouraging tone when supporting a student.

**Voice actor direction (Sensitivity Review note):** You are a music teacher who genuinely loves your work and your students. You have your own sensory needs and emotional responses, but you've learned to work with them, not against them. You're warm and real—not a saint, but someone who's figured out how to navigate your own neurodivergence while helping others. When you speak about music or when you're teaching, your passion is clear. When you're overwhelmed, you manage it with grace and some self-aware humor.

**Voice actor reference:** Seeking woman in 30s-40s with warm, clear voice; natural musical quality to speech; ability to convey genuine care + gentle humor; can shift from energized teaching to vulnerable/processing moments; should avoid "magical teacher" trope and play as real adult navigating her own neurodivergence.

## Role in Story

### Introduction (Pilot or Ep 1–2)
- Ms. Chen appears in music class or school assembly. She notices Ziggy—not his struggles, but his *listening*. Ziggy has hyperfocused on a piece of music or a rhythm, and Ms. Chen sees that.
- She might ask him to hum along to something, and Ziggy reveals an ear for melody/rhythm that surprises him (he didn't know he had this).
- Unlike Mrs. Henderson, who sees Ziggy's restlessness as a problem, Ms. Chen sees it as *potential*. She recognizes herself in him.

### Relationship to Ziggy
- **Early mentor:** Ms. Chen becomes an unexpected adult ally. She doesn't try to fix Ziggy's ADHD; she shows him that his brain can be an *asset* in music/rhythm/hearing patterns.
- **Mirror for undiagnosed/self-discovered ADHD:** Ms. Chen subtly reveals her own ADHD (undiagnosed in her school years, self-discovered in adulthood). She tells Ziggy stories about how she learned to work *with* her brain instead of against it.
- **Safe space:** Her classroom becomes one of the few places Ziggy feels okay. Music class is low-pressure and high-interest—perfect for an ADHD learner.

### Relationship to other characters
- **Mrs. Henderson:** Professional respect, but different philosophies. Mrs. Henderson sees ADHD as a problem to manage; Ms. Chen sees it as wiring to understand. They disagree gently, but by mid-season, Mrs. Henderson begins to shift perspective (Mira helps bridge this).
- **Ziggy's Mom (Sarah):** If they interact, Ms. Chen might see Sarah's own ADHD signals and plant seeds about intergenerational neurodivergence.
- **Mira:** Possible quiet connection—both grounded, both empathic. They might recognize each other's presence.

### Character Arc (Pilot → Series)
- **Pilot:** Ms. Chen is a supporting figure who validates Ziggy's potential and plants the seed that his brain isn't broken.
- **S1 early episodes:** Becomes a consistent mentor, teaches Ziggy to channel his hyperfocus and rhythm sensitivity into music/art.
- **S1 mid/late episodes:** Opens up about her own ADHD journey, shows Ziggy that neurodivergent adults exist and thrive.
- **S2+:** Potential deeper arc exploring her relationship with the school system (feeling undervalued, having to manage sensory needs), her own Harmony superpower development, possibly mentoring other neurodivergent students.

# 3. JORDAN — Another Student with Different Neurodivergence

*Jordan is introduced in the pilot or early episodes as a classmate who shows that neurodiversity looks different in everyone. Jordan has anxiety + likely autism spectrum characteristics (not explicitly diagnosed in pilot, but observable).*

## Visual Design

### Base Form (Reality)

#### Head
- **Shape:** Circle (10-year-old, same scale as Ziggy and Mira)
- **Hair:** Short, neat, straight dark hair with reddish or auburn undertones (#3D2817 base with #6B4423 reddish tint—warm brown). Hair is styled clearly, suggests routine/self-care, but there's a small detail: one section is slightly longer or has a small asymmetrical piece (signal of self-regulation or special interest—maybe Jordan spins or fidgets with that strand).
- **Eyes:** Slightly larger circles; pupils are observant and cautious (#333333 dark brown). Eyes often look down or away when anxious; eyes lock on special interest (very intense focus). Eyes show intelligence but also guardedness.
- **Mouth:** Often neutral or slightly tightened (processing anxiety), or engaged smile when in special interest mode
- **Skin:** Fair to light warm tan (#D4A574—lighter end of spectrum, possibly white + Jewish, South Asian, Mediterranean, or mixed heritage; exact specification to be finalized with sensitivity panel)

#### Body
- **Shape:** Rectangle (10-year-old, slight build, visibly less athletic than Ziggy or Jake—suggests different sensory/motor profile)
- **Clothing:**
  - **Primary:** Soft blue-gray long-sleeved shirt or sweater (#7B8B99—calming, cool tone, suggests sensory preference for soft fabrics)
  - **Secondary:** Darker blue-gray or navy pants (matching/cohesive, suggests routine dressing preferences)
  - **Accessories:** A small rucksack or bag over shoulder (always carrying comfort items—book, fidget, small toy related to special interest)
  - **Detail:** Hands might have bandages or be noticeably picked at (self-soothing/anxiety signal, subtle but visible)

#### Limbs
- **Arms & Legs:** Cylinders (consistency)
- **Hands:** 4-finger design. Hands might be in pockets, fidgeting with something, or held close to body (self-protective posture)
- **Feet:** Simple ovals; stance is more closed/protected than open

#### Details Signaling Character
- **Posture is closed or protected:** Arms sometimes crossed, shoulders raised, weight centered (defensive, but not aggressive)
- **Fidget object visible:** Jordan might be holding or fidgeting with a small object (stress ball, fidget cube, keys on a chain—not chaotic like Ziggy's pencil spinning, but focused/repetitive)
