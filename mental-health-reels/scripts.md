## **Reel 1: Understanding Anxiety**
**Duration:** 45-50 seconds  
**Tone:** Relatable, compassionate, hopeful

---

**[OPEN WITH DIRECT EYE CONTACT]**

"Hey. I want to talk about something a lot of us experience but don't always talk about—anxiety.

You know that feeling? Your heart's racing, your thoughts won't stop, you're overwhelmed by everything at once. You feel like something's wrong, but you can't quite name it. That's anxiety.

And here's what I learned: it's not weakness. It's not something you're doing wrong. It's your nervous system working overtime, and there are actually things that help.

**[LEAN IN SLIGHTLY]**

Simple breathing—in for four, hold for four, out for four. Grounding exercises—name five things you see, four you can touch, three you hear. And honestly? Talking about it. Telling someone you trust what you're experiencing.

You don't have to white-knuckle your way through this alone.

**[WARM SMILE]**

You're stronger than you think."

---

## **Reel 2: Depression is Real**
**Duration:** 50-55 seconds  
**Tone:** Raw, honest, grounded in hope

---

**[PAUSE, LOOK DIRECTLY AT CAMERA]**

"If you're struggling with depression, I want to say this clearly: it's real. It's not laziness. It's not weakness. It's not something you're doing wrong.

Depression is your brain chemistry, your energy, your ability to feel hope—all of it gets affected. And it's heavy. It's lonely. It's confusing.

**[SOFTEN YOUR EXPRESSION]**

But I also want to tell you what I've learned: recovery is possible. It doesn't look the way you think it will. It's not linear. Some days are better than others.

What helped me: talking to someone who actually understood. Moving my body—even just five minutes of walking. Letting myself rest without guilt. And reaching out, even when it felt impossible.

**[LOOK DOWN BRIEFLY, THEN BACK UP]**

You deserve to feel okay again. And you don't have to do it alone.

If you're struggling right now, please talk to someone. There's no shame in that. There's only courage."

---

## **Reel 3: Dyslexia Isn't a Limitation**
**Duration:** 40-45 seconds  
**Tone:** Empowering, confident, positive

---

**[START WITH CONFIDENCE]**

"Let me be clear about something: dyslexia is not a limitation. It's a different way your brain processes information.

If you're dyslexic, yeah, reading and writing might be harder. That's real. But here's what else is real:

**[COUNT ON FINGERS OR USE HAND GESTURES]**

You probably think creatively in ways other people don't. You solve problems differently. You see patterns others miss. You're resilient—you've had to be.

Your brain isn't broken. It's just wired differently. And different? Different is powerful.

**[DIRECT, WARM EYE CONTACT]**

I'm here to tell you: own your neurodiversity. Don't apologize for how your brain works. Use it. Celebrate it.

You're amazing exactly as you are."

---

## **Reel 4: You're Not Broken**
**Duration:** 50-55 seconds  
**Tone:** Hopeful, grounded, authentic

---

**[WARM, GENUINE OPENING]**

"Whatever you're facing right now—anxiety, depression, dyslexia, or something I haven't mentioned—I want to say this:

You're not broken.

You're human. You're dealing with real things. Your struggles are valid. Your pain is real. And so is your strength.

**[PAUSE, LET IT LAND]**

Healing doesn't happen overnight. Recovery isn't linear. Some days you'll feel like you're moving forward, and some days you'll feel like you're back where you started. That's normal. That's part of it.

But it does happen. You do get better. I promise.

**[LEAN IN SLIGHTLY, MORE INTIMATE]**

The things that helped me: reaching out even when I didn't want to. Talking to people I trust. Being honest about what I was struggling with. Letting myself feel everything without judgment.

**[LOOK DIRECTLY AT CAMERA]**

And remembering that one moment of pain doesn't define my whole story.

If you're struggling, please reach out. Tell someone. Call a helpline. Go to therapy. You deserve support, and asking for it is brave.

You've got this. And you're not alone."

## **Script Notes for Video Production**

### **Visual Direction:**
- **Lighting:** Warm, natural light (golden hour if possible)
- **Background:** Clean, calm, simple (plant, wall, soft blur)
- **Clothing:** Comfortable, approachable (colors that match mental health brand)
- **Camera:** Slightly off-center, eye contact or natural gaze shifts

### **Pacing:**
- Speak naturally, conversationally (not read-like)
- Use pauses for emphasis
- Vary your energy—serious moments vs. lighter moments
- Let emotions show authentically

### **Text Overlay Suggestions:**
Each reel should have 1-2 key phrases as text overlays:

**Reel 1 (Anxiety):**
- "Anxiety is manageable"
- "You're not alone"

**Reel 2 (Depression):**
- "Recovery is possible"
- "You don't have to do it alone"

**Reel 3 (Dyslexia):**
- "Different is powerful"
- "Own your neurodiversity"

**Reel 4 (Hope):**
- "You're not broken"
- "You've got this"

---

## **Audio/Music Notes:**
- Quiet, warm background music (no lyrics, minimal instrumentation)
- Keep audio level low so your voice is front and center
- Consider adding a subtle ambient sound (birds, breeze) for authenticity
