## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**1. Inconsistency**
- ✗ Publish 3 videos Week 1, then nothing for 2 weeks
- ✓ Mon/Wed/Fri schedule every single week (even if boring)

**2. No email list building**
- ✗ Just post on YouTube, hope people find you
- ✓ Get emails with every single video (description link + landing page)

**3. Ignoring comments**
- ✗ Post video and ghost
- ✓ Reply to first 10-20 comments within 24 hours (builds algorithm boost)

**4. Unclear CTA**
- ✗ "If you like this, subscribe"
- ✓ "Enroll in the course" + clear link (specific ask)

**5. Underpricing**
- ✗ Offer course for $19 because you're nervous
- ✓ Start at $99, price increase every 30 days (scarcity)

**6. No social proof**
- ✗ Launch with zero testimonials
- ✓ Gather 5-10 testimonials from beta students before launch

**7. Perfectionism**
- ✗ Spend 10 hours editing to perfection
- ✓ 80/20 rule: spend 2 hours editing, release (good enough > perfect)

**8. Audience building second**
- ✗ Spend 4 weeks on landing page UX
- ✓ Launch rough landing page, focus on content (audience first)
