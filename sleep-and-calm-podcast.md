# SLEEP & CALM — Podcast Specification

**Status:** READY FOR LAUNCH (Day 1)
**Launch Date:** TONIGHT
**Frequency:** 3-4 new episodes per week + a weekly long-form (45 min) "All-Night" release
**Episode Length:** 20-45 minutes (sleep content runs long by design — listeners drift off before the end and let it play)
**Target Audience:** Anyone who struggles to sleep — stressed adults, light sleepers, the over-worked and over-wired, ages 20-60 (a massive, perennial passive-listening niche)
**Revenue Target:** $30k/mo by Month 4 (slower ramp than reactive niches; library compounds with repeat plays)

---

## Show Positioning

**Sleep & Calm** is a soft place to land at the end of the day. Each episode is a single, slow, gently-told sleep story or guided wind-down, narrated in a calm, unhurried voice over a bed of quiet ambience. No news. No cliffhangers. Nothing to keep you awake. Just a long, warm exhale you can fall asleep inside of.

**Why it works:**
- Sleep is a NIGHTLY need — listeners return every single night (habit baked into bedtime routine)
- The catalogue is EVERGREEN — a story about a snowy cabin is as good in three years as it is tonight
- Repeat plays are enormous — listeners replay the same comforting episodes hundreds of times because familiarity itself is soothing
- Low churn — once an episode becomes "the one that puts me to sleep," it sticks
- TTS-friendly — a calm, consistent narrated voice is exactly what the format wants, so production scales cheaply
- Premium, brand-safe sponsors PAY for this audience (mattresses, sleep aids, meditation apps, teas) — calm content sits beside calm products

**Why the niche is lucrative for passive listening:**
- One episode can be played thousands of times by the same person — lifetime value per listener is unusually high
- Listeners leave it running all night, inflating total listen-hours far beyond the audience size
- The mood is the product — sponsors want association with calm, trust, and self-care, and pay a premium for it
- Almost no content goes stale, so the back catalogue keeps earning indefinitely

## Show Format (Every Episode)

**Structure (20-45 minutes):**
- **Soft intro / settling (1-2 min)** — gentle welcome, permission to let the day go, two or three slow guided breaths, one-line preview of tonight's scene. Calm, never energetic.
- **The story / wind-down (main body, 15-40 min)** — slow descriptive narration or guided relaxation. Deliberately repetitive and low-stakes. Generous silences. The middle is where most listeners fall asleep.
- **The fade (final 3-8 min)** — sentences thin out and spread further apart, the narrator gives explicit permission to stop following the words, then the voice drops away entirely and only the soft ambience remains before fading to silence. No "next episode" tease, no call to action that could re-alert the listener.

**Tone:**
- Soft, slow, soothing — always
- Warm and safe (enclosure, warmth, weight, gentle motion are the recurring sensory anchors)
- Repetitive on purpose — predictability is calming
- No tension, no surprises, no rising stakes, no loud sounds
- Second person, present tense, "nothing you need to do"
- Voice volume even and consistent; consistent loudness matters because spikes wake people

**Production notes (TTS):**
- Use one warm, low, slow narrator voice across the whole catalogue for familiarity
- Reduce speaking rate; insert real silence at every `[ … ]`
- Keep the ambience bed very low and continuous; never let music swell
- Master to a low, even loudness with no sudden peaks (no sponsor reads or stings inside the body of the episode)

## Metrics to Track

> Sleep podcasts have unusual metrics. **Completion rate is low BY DESIGN** — success looks like listeners falling asleep partway through, not finishing. Judge the show on returns and replays, not finishes.

**Daily:**
- New plays / starts per episode
- Total listen-hours (the headline number here — sleep content over-indexes on hours vs. unique listeners)
- New vs. returning listeners

**Weekly / ongoing:**
- **Repeat-play rate** — how often the SAME listener replays the SAME episode (the key health metric for this niche; high is great)
- **Return frequency** — how many nights per week a listener comes back (target: a nightly habit)
- **"Most replayed" episodes** — identify which scenes become listeners' go-to comfort episodes; make more like them
- **Drop-off point** — where listeners typically fall asleep (a HIGH/early drop-off is a SUCCESS signal, not a failure; it means the episode worked)
- Membership conversion and retention (ad-free / bonus tier)
