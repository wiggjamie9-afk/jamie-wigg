About

HerdCheck is a decision-support tool for smallholders. It assists observation — it is not a replacement for a veterinarian. Risk tiers combine your observations with validated scoring systems (Sprecher 5-point locomotion, CMT-style symptom checklists, Williamson/Mee calving signs).
