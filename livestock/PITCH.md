# HerdCheck — pitch for dairy co-operatives

**One line:** Put structured mastitis, lameness, and calving screening in every
member's pocket — and roll the results up to the co-op.

---

## The problem you already pay for

Two conditions quietly drain a smallholder dairy co-op:

- **Mastitis** → contaminated and rejected milk, discarded withhold-period milk,
  and quality penalties on your whole intake when it slips through.
- **Lameness** → lower yield, missed services, and preventable culls that hit
  your members' incomes and your supply volume.

Today most members have **no screening tool at all**. The first you hear of a
problem is at the collection point — after the milk, and the loss, are already
in the tank.

## What HerdCheck is

A phone app (no app-store install required — it opens in the browser and works
**offline**) that walks a member through a structured check and returns a clear
**red / amber / green** tier plus plain next steps:

- **Lameness** — Sprecher 5-point locomotion scoring.
- **Mastitis** — visible-sign checklist + milk appearance, with a phone-photo
  heuristic as corroboration (never the deciding factor).
- **Calving** — gestation-day timing + pre-calving signs, with an "active
  labour" flag.

Grounded in published references; honest about its limits (decision support,
not diagnosis).

## Why it's a co-op product, not just an app

The member gets the check. **You get the herd.** A co-op deployment adds an
aggregate dashboard: which members are screening, how many red flags this week,
which herds are trending worse — plus exportable records. That visibility is the
part worth paying for, and it's what turns scattered phone checks into a
herd-health program you can manage.

## What we ask for to start

1. A **pilot group** — one route or collection centre, ~50–100 members.
2. A **validation partner** you trust — a vet school or extension service — to
   sign off the scoring before we make any clinical claim to your members.
3. One contact on your side who works with the members day to day.
