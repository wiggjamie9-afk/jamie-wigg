HerdCheck — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

HerdCheck is a livestock-screening tool for farmers. It is built to work entirely on your own device. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. HerdCheck has no account, no login, and no analytics or tracking. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or store any personal information on any server.

Where your data lives

All information you enter — your animals, checks, photos, and videos — is stored only on your device (in your browser/app storage). It never leaves your phone unless you choose to export or share it.

Camera and photos

Some checks let you take a photo or short video of an animal. These are used on your device to help you record and score the check. They are stored locally and are never uploaded to us.

Exporting and sharing

You can choose to export your records (as CSV or JSON) or share an alert summary. When you do this, you control where that data goes (for example, to your co-op, an email, or an SMS). HerdCheck does not receive a copy.

Children

HerdCheck is a tool for farmers and is not directed at children.
