  • Age: Middle-school (11–13 years old)
  • Skin Tone: Warm peachy complexion with realistic proportions
  • Hair: Short dark spiky hair with shine lines and movement indicators
  • Eyes: Large, bright, expressive with animated sparkle/shine effects
  • Body Type: Athletic, realistic proportions (not chibi or stylized)
  • Posture: Dynamic stance showing barely-contained motion

Visual Style & Technical Specs

Line Work & Rendering

  • Outline Weight: Consistent 1–2px bold strokes
  • Style: Clean, crisp, cel-animation-friendly
  • Curves: Smooth, intentional (no wobbly lines)
  • Shading: Cel-shading technique with flat color blocks
  • Highlights: White or light blue for form definition
  • Shadows: Subtle dark accents, clean edges

Color & Composition

  • Saturation: Vibrant, high-contrast palette
  • Avoid: Default blue, pastels, muddy tones
  • Hair Effects: Shine lines for movement and dimension
  • Background: Clean white studio with soft shadows only
  • Quality: High-res, crisp edges, no soft focus
  • Style: Zero generic AI aesthetic

Animation Readiness

  • Symmetry: Head/body proportions allow for 360° rotation
  • Joints: Clear articulation points (shoulders, elbows, hips, knees, neck)
  • Silhouette: Clean, unambiguous outline for rigging
  • Expression Layers: Eyes, brows, and mouth separable for animatic use
  • Reference Quality: Matches production-level character sheets
