THE 9 PATHS

Stop fighting Spotify. Start stacking.

The fix isn't higher per-stream rates. It's owned monetisation in three tiers — active, passive, and fan-funded. Pick three. Run for 90 days. Double down on the winner.

PATH 01 · ACTIVE
Sync Licensing
$1K–$10K per placement

TV, film, games, ads. The single highest dollar-per-hour path. AI brief-matching has crushed the 50%-cut sync agents.

Start: Musicbed, Marmoset, Songtradr, RHYTHMIX sync matching.
Tools: clean instrumental + 30s edit + cue-sheet metadata.

PATH 02 · FAN-FUNDED
Fan Investment
$5K–$50K per release

Superfans buy a fractional share of your master and earn royalty splits with you. The fans become your distribution.

Start: SongVest, Royal, RHYTHMIX fan investment.
Tools: 1-page pitch deck + transparent royalty terms.

PATH 03 · FAN-FUNDED
Direct-to-Fan Drops
$500–$5K per drop · 5K-fan list

Drop tracks straight to your fans via Bandcamp, Substack, or your own checkout. AI mastering = radio quality, no studio.

Start: Bandcamp, Substack, Beehiiv, Stripe checkout.
Tools: AI mastering (LANDR, RHYTHMIX) + email capture.

PATH 04 · PASSIVE
Merch (Print-on-Demand)
$5–$15 margin / unit

Zero inventory. The merch you wear in your video sells the merch in your store. Print-on-demand handles fulfillment.

Start: Printful, Bonfire, Spring (Teespring).
Tools: one iconic design tied to a release.

PATH 05 · PASSIVE
Sample / Preset Libraries
$200–$2K / month passive

Build once, sell forever. Producers pay for the exact sounds you used. AI stem-splitting = 30 minutes per release.

Start: Splice, Loopcloud, Beatport Sounds.
Tools: your DAW + an AI stem splitter.

PATH 06 · FAN-FUNDED
NFTs / On-Chain Releases
$1K–$25K per drop

Limited-edition track releases with on-chain ownership. Up-front payment plus secondary-sale royalties forever.

Start: Sound.xyz, Catalog, Royal.
Tools: 100+ fans willing to pay $10–$100 per release.

PATH 07 · ACTIVE
Live · In-Person + VR
$200–$5K per show

Live is still the most reliable per-event payout. VR concerts (Wave, RHYTHMIX) scale globally without flying.

Start: local venues (in-person), VR platforms (global).
Tools: 30-min set + visuals + livestream cam.

PATH 08 · ACTIVE
Teaching / Coaching
$50–$300 / hour · $500–$5K cohort

Other musicians will pay you to learn what you already know. Production tutorials, mixing breakdowns, songwriting workshops.

Start: 1:1 on Calendly → cohort once you have proof.
Tools: webcam + DAW + curriculum doc.

PATH 09 · PASSIVE
YouTube Monetization
$2–$10 per 1K views

Compounds over years if you target search intent — "how to mix vocals like [reference artist]" beats algorithmic luck.

Start: YouTube Studio, basic camera setup.
Tools: capture device + basic editor.
