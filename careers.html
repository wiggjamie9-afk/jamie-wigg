  • No marketing-led engineering. The roadmap is what creators ask for after they've used the tool.

  • Not a SaaS unicorn play. Not VC-tracked. Not interested in the next round.
  • Not a place to learn audio DSP from scratch — you'd need to be fluent already.
  • Not remote-global today (Australian + adjacent timezones until the team grows).
