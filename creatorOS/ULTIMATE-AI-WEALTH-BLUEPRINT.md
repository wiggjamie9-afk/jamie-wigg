# THE CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

## 1. Content Consistency (Months 1-6)
You MUST post TikTok daily. This is non-negotiable.
- 30 mins/day = 10.5 hours/week
- Result: 50k followers by month 6
- Without this: No audience, no leverage for sponsorships/partnerships

**Hack:** Batch-create weekly (spend Sunday afternoon making 7 videos, then schedule them)

## 2. Community Building (Months 1-12)
Discord is your moat. Build a real community.
- Daily engagement (respond to members)
- Weekly discussions (ask questions, facilitate)
- Monthly events (AMAs, workshops, spotlights)
- Result: 5,000+ engaged members = word-of-mouth growth

**Without this:** Reliant on paid ads, no defensible advantage

## 3. Early B2B Traction (Months 3-4)
Your first 3-5 B2B customers prove the model.
- Each is worth $2-5k/mo recurring
- They become case studies for the next 5
- They give feedback for white-label improvements

**Without this:** Stuck at $10-20k/mo SaaS ceiling

## 4. Multiple Revenue Streams (By Month 6)
Reduce dependency on any single revenue source.
- 1 stream going down doesn't kill the business
- Multiple streams = easier exit (acquirers love this)
- Diversification = profitability even in downturns

**Without this:** Fragile business (one bad month = crisis)

## 5. Automation Discipline (Ongoing)
Every task that repeats should be automated.
- Email sequences (not manual)
- Invoice generation (not manual)
- Referral payouts (not manual)
- Reporting (not manual)

**Without this:** Stuck working 40+ hrs/week forever
