## VA #1: GROWTH LEAD (TikTok + Discord)

### Job Description

**Title:** Growth & Community Manager  
**Hours:** 15-20 hrs/week (flexible, can be async)  
**Salary:** $600-900/month (can negotiate based on timezone + experience)  
**Timeline:** 2-week onboarding, then fully autonomous

**Responsibilities:**

1. **TikTok/Reels Content (30 mins/day)**
   - Post 1-2 videos daily from pre-made content library
   - Write engaging captions with hook + CTA
   - Respond to comments (auto-replies first, then personalized)
   - Track which videos perform best
   - Report weekly: views, engagement, click-through rate

2. **Discord Community (1-1.5 hours/day)**
   - Daily: Reply to new members, welcome questions, facilitate discussions
   - 3x/week: Post curated resources/tutorials in #resources channel
   - Weekly: Host 1 "office hours" or Q&A session (30 mins)
   - Monthly: Identify top contributors, give them special roles
   - Track: member growth, activity level, sentiment

**Required Skills:**
- Social media experience (TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts)
- Basic video editing (CapCut, Adobe Premiere, or similar)
- Community management (Slack, Discord, or similar)
- Writing engaging captions/hooks
- Time zone: US-friendly (EST/CST/MST/PST preferred, but flexible)

**Tools they'll use:**
- TikTok creator studio
- Discord desktop/mobile app
- CapCut or similar (for light edits)
- Slack (for daily standup)
- Airtable (content calendar)

**Nice-to-have:**
- Experience with AI creator tools
- Video trending knowledge
- Community building experience

### Hiring Sources
- Upwork: Search "TikTok content manager" or "Discord community manager"
- Fiverr: Look for creators with 4.8+ stars
- Belay/Time Etc: Vetted VA companies
- Reddit: r/forhire, r/slavelabor
- Your own email list: "Hiring part-time VA, reply here"

### Interview Questions
1. "Show me a Discord community you've managed. How did you grow it?"
2. "Show me TikToks you've posted or worked on. What was the strategy?"
3. "What's your process for writing engaging captions?"
4. "How do you handle 20+ TikTok comments in a day?"
5. "Timezone? Can you work 2-3 hours daily?"

### Onboarding Checklist
- [ ] Invite to Discord server (practice managing)
- [ ] Share 5 TikTok videos to analyze (why they work)
- [ ] Give access to content calendar (Airtable)
- [ ] Share social media templates (captions, hooks)
- [ ] Daily standup for 5 minutes (first 2 weeks)
- [ ] Week 1 goal: Understand the brand voice
- [ ] Week 2 goal: Post 5 videos independently, zero revisions needed
- [ ] Week 3+: Fully autonomous, weekly check-ins

### Payment Structure
- $600-900/month (negotiable by experience)
- Payment on 1st of month via Stripe, PayPal, or Wise
- Increase to $1,000/mo after 3 months if KPIs met

## VA #2: PERFORMANCE LEAD (Google Ads + Influencer Outreach)

### Job Description

**Title:** Paid Growth & Partnerships Manager  
**Hours:** 15-20 hrs/week (flexible)  
**Salary:** $600-900/month  
**Timeline:** 2-week onboarding, then autonomous

**Responsibilities:**

1. **Google Ads Optimization (1 hour/day)**
   - Daily: Check ad performance in Google Ads dashboard
   - Pause underperforming ads, scale winning ones
   - A/B test landing pages (swap copy, images, CTAs)
   - Test new keywords/audiences weekly
   - Track: CTR, conversion rate, cost per conversion, ROAS
   - Weekly report: Ad spend, conversions, revenue, ROI

2. **Influencer Outreach (2-3 hours/day)**
   - Send 50 personalized cold emails/week to creators
   - Track open rates, reply rates, conversions
   - Follow up with non-responders (3 email sequence)
   - Negotiate affiliate terms with interested creators
   - Set up tracking links in database
   - Weekly report: emails sent, reply rate, partnerships closed

**Required Skills:**
- Google Ads experience (must have run ads before)
- Email marketing / sales outreach experience
- Spreadsheet skills (Google Sheets, Airtable)
- Basic analytics (understanding CTR, CPC, conversion rates)
- Writing persuasive outreach emails
- Time zone: US-friendly preferred

**Tools they'll use:**
- Google Ads dashboard
- Google Sheets (performance tracking)
- Gmail or Outlook (outreach)
- Airtable (influencer database)
- HubSpot or Pipedrive (CRM, optional but helpful)
- Slack (daily standup)

**Nice-to-have:**
- SaaS marketing experience
- Affiliate marketing background
- Creator outreach experience
- Analytics experience

### Hiring Sources
- Upwork: "Google Ads manager" + "email outreach"
- Fiverr: Search "Google Ads specialist"

- Belay/Time Etc

### Interview Questions

3. "Show me an outreach email you've written. What's your reply rate typically?"

### Onboarding Checklist

- [ ] Share influencer list (50 initial targets + how to find more)
- [ ] Share Airtable database (tracking structure)
- [ ] Week 1: Analyze current ads, identify 3 optimization opportunities
- [ ] Week 2: Run 3 A/B tests, send 50 cold emails
- [ ] Week 3+: Autonomous, weekly check-ins

### Payment Structure

- Payment on 1st of month

## DAILY TASK TEMPLATES

### VA #1: Growth Lead (TikTok + Discord)

#### Daily TikTok Task (30 mins)
```
MORNING (9 AM):
- [ ] Check TikTok notifications (comments, DMs)
- [ ] Reply to top 5 comments (auto-reply template, then personalize top 3)
- [ ] Post today's video from content calendar
  - [ ] Write caption (hook + value + CTA to landing page link)
  - [ ] Check video length < 60 seconds
  - [ ] Use trending audio (check TikTok Discover tab)
  - [ ] Post at 6 AM - 8 PM (peak hours)
- [ ] Check yesterday's video performance:
  - Views? ____
  - Engagement rate? ____% (likes + comments / views)
  - Clicks? ____ (from link in bio tracking)

WEEKLY (Friday):
- [ ] Report: TikTok analytics (views, followers, top 3 videos)
- [ ] Analysis: What worked? What flopped?
- [ ] Next week: Plan 5 videos based on trends + what worked last week
```

#### Daily Discord Task (1.5 hours)
```
MORNING (10 AM):
- [ ] Check #introductions — welcome 2-3 new members (personalized message)
- [ ] Check #general — answer any questions (max 3 responses)
- [ ] Check #help — flag any VIP questions for owner review

MIDDAY (2 PM):
- [ ] Post 1 resource in #resources (curated tutorial, template, or tool)
- [ ] Respond to DMs (support questions)

WEEKLY (Tuesday + Thursday):
- [ ] Post discussion prompt in #general (e.g., "What's your biggest challenge with AI video?")
- [ ] Facilitate conversations (reply to all comments, encourage participation)

MONTHLY (First Friday):
- [ ] Report: Discord stats (new members, active members, sentiment)
- [ ] Identify top contributors → give them "MVP" or "Expert" role
- [ ] Plan next month's discussion topics

QUARTERLY (Every 3 months):
- [ ] Suggest new Discord channels based on member requests
- [ ] Plan 1 special event (AMA, workshop, giveaway)
```

## AUTOMATION THAT REDUCES VA WORK

### Email Sequences (Fully Automated)
- Day 0: Welcome email (sent automatically 1 hour after signup)
- Day 3: Social proof email (sent automatically)
- Day 7: Upgrade pitch (sent automatically)
- Day 14: Final follow-up (sent automatically)
- **VA only monitors open rates + troubleshoots bounces**

### Referral Tracking (Fully Automated)
- Every Friday 5 PM: Referral payouts processed automatically
- Every month 1st: Invoices sent automatically
- **VA only tracks which creators are top performers**

### Analytics Reporting (Fully Automated)
- Daily 8 AM: Slack report with signups + revenue
- Weekly Friday: Summary report to your email
- **VA only adds context/analysis**

### Lead Scoring (Partially Automated)
- Supabase automatically tags users as "warm" or "cold" based on email opens
- VA focuses on warm leads first

## RED FLAGS (When to replace a VA)

🚨 **Replace if:**
- No TikTok posts for 2+ days (without notice)
- Reply rate on outreach drops below 5% (not doing good work)
- Google Ads ROAS drops below 1.0x and doesn't improve (not optimizing)
- Communication: Missed daily standups 3+ times in a week
- Quality: Captions are generic or off-brand

## WHERE TO HIRE

### Best Platforms
1. **Upwork** (most reliable)
   - Search filters: "Social media manager" + "TikTok"
   - Look for: 4.8+ rating, 100+ completed projects
   - Budget: Set max $25/hr for your budget range

2. **Fiverr** (cheaper but less vetted)
   - Search: "TikTok content creator" or "Google Ads specialist"
   - Look for: 4.8+ rating, proven portfolio

3. **Belay / Time Etc / Fancy Hands** (most vetted)
   - Higher cost ($15-20/hr)
   - But pre-screened + support if VA underperforms

4. **Your own email list** (best)
   - Post in your email/Discord: "Hiring part-time VA for [specific role]"
   - Your customers = best VA candidates (they know your product)

## ONBOARDING TIMELINE

### Week 1: Hiring
- [ ] Post job on Upwork, Fiverr, Reddit
- [ ] Screen 10-20 candidates
- [ ] Do 5-7 interviews
- [ ] Hire VA #1 + VA #2

### Week 2: Onboarding
- [ ] VA #1: TikTok + Discord training
- [ ] VA #2: Google Ads + Outreach training
- [ ] Daily 15-min standups with each VA
- [ ] You review their work (before they post)

### Week 3: Autonomous
- [ ] VA #1: Posting independently (you review after)
- [ ] VA #2: Running ads + outreach independently
- [ ] Move to weekly check-ins instead of daily

### Week 4+: Optimize
- [ ] Weekly 30-min sync with each VA
- [ ] You focus on: product, customer support, strategy
- [ ] VAs focus on: growth, partnerships, community

---

## TOTAL SETUP TIMELINE

- **Week 1:** Hiring (post jobs, interview)
- **Week 2:** Onboarding (training VAs)
- **Week 3:** Ramp (first content, first ads, first outreach)
- **Week 4:** Autonomous (you step back, VAs execute)
- **Month 2:** First revenue arrives, optimize based on metrics
- **Month 3:** Scaling (increase ad spend, more outreach, expand Discord)

**By Month 4:** You should be at $5-10k/mo, working 5-10 hours/week
