## Animation Philosophy

**Movement should communicate:**
- Presence (she's fully there)
- Warmth (genuine care in every gesture)
- Stability (grounded, reliable)
- Compassion (understanding and acceptance)
- Authenticity (real emotions, not performed)

---

## What NOT to Do

✗ Change the color palette  
✗ Add complex shading or gradients  
✗ Make expressions cold or distant  
✗ Use jerky, mechanical movement  
✗ Distort character proportions  
✗ Break from the warm aesthetic

## Design Checklist

Before publishing MIRA art:

- [ ] Colors match hex codes exactly
- [ ] Proportions consistent across all poses
- [ ] Expressions convey intended emotion
- [ ] Posture remains grounded and balanced
- [ ] Overall feel is warm and approachable
- [ ] Lines are clean and definable
- [ ] Design is scalable (vector-friendly)

---

## Animation Checklist

Before publishing MIRA animation:

- [ ] Movement feels smooth and natural
- [ ] Breathing visible in idle poses
- [ ] Expression changes are timed correctly
- [ ] Gestures are purposeful, not jerky
- [ ] Eyes maintain character warmth
- [ ] Overall feel matches calm, grounding essence
- [ ] No sudden movements or jerks
