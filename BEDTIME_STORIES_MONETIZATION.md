## The Production Setup (Dead Simple)

**Equipment:**
- Microphone: $30-100 (USB mic or phone mic)
- Script: Google Docs
- Voice: Just you reading
- Video: Still image/animation (not required)
- Editing: Simple (iMovie or DaVinci Resolve - FREE)

**Time per story:**
- Write/find story: 10-20 min
- Record (one take, no editing needed): 15-20 min
- Upload/optimize: 10-15 min
- **Total: 40-55 minutes per story**

**Per week: 2 stories × 50 min = 1.5-2 hours production**
**But editing them in batches: 1 hour per week**
