# MIRA Character Design Specification

## Overview
MIRA is the best friend character in Ziggy's story — a calm, grounding, reliable presence. This design specification captures her visual identity for professional 2D cartoon illustration.

## Character Role & Personality
- **Role**: Best friend, grounding presence, emotional anchor
- **Age**: Middle-school (12-14 years old)
- **Personality Traits**: Calm, reliable, accepting, kind, thoughtful listener
- **Visual Tone**: Warm, approachable, stable, trustworthy

---

## Physical Design Details

### Skin Tone & Base
- **Skin Tone**: Warm medium brown (earth-tone undertones, not overly saturated)
- **Base approach**: Warm, inviting, inclusive representation
- **Luminosity**: Medium value (not too dark, not too light) — allows for good contrast with clothing

### Eyes & Facial Features
- **Eyes**: Kind, expressive, warm brown or dark amber
- **Eye shape**: Slightly larger than life (cartoon proportional), friendly and open
- **Gaze**: Typically warm and understanding — often in listening/supportive pose
- **Eyebrows**: Soft, expressive; conveys empathy and attentiveness
- **Mouth**: Gentle smile or warm neutral expression (not overly exaggerated)
- **Face shape**: Rounded, approachable (not angular or harsh)

### Hair
- **Style**: Shoulder-length, natural texture
- **Color**: Rich medium to dark brown (matches skin tone warmth family)
- **Texture**: Curly, wavy, or natural texture (conveying ease and authenticity)
- **Styling**: Casual, not overly styled — conveys groundedness
- **Details**: No excessive shine or artificial look; natural, lived-in appearance

### Body & Posture
- **Build**: Healthy, age-appropriate middle-school physique
- **Posture**: Balanced, stable, grounded
- **Common poses**: 
  - Sitting/listening with attention
  - Standing with arms open or offering hand
  - Walking alongside Ziggy (supportive, side-by-side)
  - Warm greeting or celebration
- **Proportions**: Age-appropriate cartoon proportions (slightly large head/eyes relative to body)

## Design Sheet Layout (16:9)

### Overall Structure
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1920 × 1080 pixels (16:9 landscape)
- **Background**: Clean white or warm off-white
- **Arrangement**: Professional model sheet / design sheet style

### Sections

#### 1. CENTER FULL-BODY (Large)
- **Size**: ~40-50% of sheet
- **Position**: Center-left or center
- **Content**: Full-body character in relaxed standing pose, three-quarter view
- **Details**: Show complete outfit, posture, proportions, hair, and color palette
- **Pose**: Warm, open, slightly turned toward viewer

#### 2. HEAD VARIATIONS (Top Row)
- **Count**: 3 variations
- **Size**: ~15-20% of sheet total
- **Content**: 
  - Head 1: Straight-on / front view
  - Head 2: Three-quarter right view
  - Head 3: Profile or three-quarter left view
- **Purpose**: Show design consistency and facial features from multiple angles

#### 3. EXPRESSION VARIATIONS (Middle Section)
- **Count**: 4 expressions
- **Arrangement**: 2×2 grid or horizontal row
- **Size**: ~20-25% of sheet
- **Expressions**:
  1. **Warm/Welcoming**: Open smile, kind eyes, inviting
  2. **Listening/Understanding**: Attentive expression, slight head tilt, thoughtful
  3. **Playful/Laughing**: Big warm smile, eyes crinkled, joyful
  4. **Concerned/Supportive**: Slight frown or serious expression, caring eyes, focused on other person
- **Format**: Head/shoulder shots showing facial variation only

#### 4. ACTION POSES (Bottom Section)
- **Count**: 4 poses
- **Arrangement**: Horizontal row or 2×2 grid
- **Size**: ~20-25% of sheet
- **Poses**:
  1. **Walking Together**: Walking alongside Ziggy-like figure (or just walking forward, relaxed gait)
  2. **Sitting/Listening**: Seated position, attentive posture, leaning in to listen
  3. **Offering Hand**: Standing with arm extended, offering hand to help (warm, supportive gesture)
  4. **Celebration**: Celebratory pose (arms raised or open, happy expression, celebrating together)
- **Format**: Full-body action shots

## Illustration Style

### Overall Aesthetic
- **Style**: Modern 2D cartoon illustration
- **Reference**: Contemporary children's animation / illustrated media
- **Quality**: Professional, world-class illustration quality
- **Approach**: Clean, intentional line work with warm, welcoming color palette

### Line Work
- **Line Weight**: Consistent, clean bold lines
- **No sketchy lines** — polished, finished artwork
- **Line Style**: Clear outlines around forms; not super thin or overly thick
- **Consistency**: All character elements should feel cohesive

### Color Application
- **Saturation**: Medium (warm, inviting, not oversaturated or pastel)
- **Shading**: Subtle, soft shading to show form without harsh shadows
- **Highlights**: Minimal, soft highlights (particularly in hair, eyes)
- **Blending**: Smooth transitions between color areas
- **No harsh outlines in shadow** — soft shading approach

### Design Principles
- **Warm Over Cool**: All color choices have warm undertones
- **Friendly Not Cutesy**: Appealing without being overly stylized or "chibi"
- **Age-Appropriate**: Looks like a real middle-schooler, not infantilized
- **Grounding Elements**: 
  - Stable, balanced posture
  - Natural (not exaggerated) proportions
  - Calm, kind facial features
  - Earth-tone color palette

### What to Avoid
- **No anime style** — eyes shouldn't be huge or overly sparkly
- **No overly realistic/photorealistic** — maintain cartoon sensibility
- **No 3D rendering** — pure 2D illustration
- **No neon or oversaturated colors** — stay warm and grounded
- **No generic AI art aesthetic** — world-class illustration quality
- **No grotesque or unfriendly elements** — keep kind and approachable

## Usage Notes for Animation/Design Work

### Animation Consistency
- Keep hair at consistent length and texture across all poses and expressions
- Maintain eye color and shape consistency
- Preserve outfit colors and silhouette
- Clothing folds should be minimal and natural (not exaggerated)

### Pose Direction
- In side-by-side poses with Ziggy: MIRA often slightly behind or at equal level (supportive, not dominating)
- In listening pose: Head slightly tilted toward speaker, eyes engaged
- In support pose: Body language open and warm, hand extended naturally
- In celebration: Genuine joy, not overly exaggerated

### Expression Guidelines
- **Welcoming**: Genuine smile, relaxed eyes, open body language
- **Listening**: Slight head tilt, focused eyes, mouth slightly open or closed gently
- **Playful**: Full smile reaching eyes (crinkle), energetic but not manic
- **Concerned**: Softer eyes, slight frown or pressed lips, forward lean (showing care)

### Interaction with Ziggy
- MIRA is slightly older or more grounded in bearing
- She often stands/sits slightly back, giving Ziggy space to be the focal point
- Her body language conveys support and presence (not pushing or leading)
- Eyes often look toward Ziggy in scenes with both characters
