## Generation Quick Guide

### Step 1: Choose a Tool
- **Recommended:** Replicate FLUX 1.1 Pro (~$0.15, professional quality)
- **Alternative:** Midjourney (excellent quality, subscription)
- **Budget:** DALL-E 3 ($0.08 per generation)
- **Free:** Pollinations (rate-limited but free)

### Step 2: Get the Prompt
Copy from **GENERATION-INSTRUCTIONS.md** or use this excerpt:

```
Professional 2D cartoon character design sheet in the style of Vectteezy and
modern animation studios. Ziggy, energetic middle-school ADHD superhero
protagonist. [... complete prompt in GENERATION-INSTRUCTIONS.md]
```

### Step 3: Set Parameters
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Size: 1920×1080
- Format: PNG
- Quality: High (95+)

### Step 4: Generate & Save
Execute generation and save output as: `ziggy-illustrated.png`

## Character Design Summary

**Physical Design:**
- Warm peachy skin (#F4A570)
- Short dark spiky hair with shine/movement
- Big bright expressive eyes with highlights
- Warm, friendly smile

**Costume:**
- Vibrant Orange hoodie (#FF8C00) — main focus
- Electric Blue trim (#00D8FF) on cuffs/zipper/pockets
- Comfortable, movement-friendly fit

**Character Sheet Layout:**
1. **Center** — Large full-body standing shot, arms raised, confident smile
2. **Top Row** — 3 head variations (front, 3/4 left, 3/4 right)
3. **Right Column** — 4 expressions (happy, focused, nervous, powerful)
4. **Bottom Row** — 4 action poses (running, jumping, thinking, celebrating)

**Character Essence:**
- Energy: Constant motion, barely contained
- Hope: Bright, positive, forward-looking
- Brilliance: Smart, capable, confident
- Relatability: ADHD as superpower

- [ ] Colors are vibrant with no gradients or shadows
