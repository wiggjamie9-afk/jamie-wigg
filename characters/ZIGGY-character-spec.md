# ZIGGY Character Design Specification

**Character Name:** Ziggy  
**Role:** Energetic middle-school ADHD superhero protagonist  
**Art Style:** Professional 2D cartoon (Vectteezy / modern animation studio)  
**Target Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (1920×1080)

---

## Visual Identity

### Physical Characteristics
- **Skin Tone:** Warm peachy (#F4A570 or similar)
- **Hair:** Short, dark spiky hair with shine/movement lines
  - Color: Deep charcoal or dark navy
  - Style: Tousled, perpetually windswept (suggests constant energy)
  - Texture: Bold lines showing movement
- **Eyes:** Big, bright, expressive with animated highlights
  - Color: Warm brown or hazel with bright white shine
  - Expression: Always conveying hope, curiosity, determination
- **Facial Expression:** Warm, friendly smile; eyebrows expressive

### Primary Costume
- **Hoodie (VIBRANT ORANGE #FF8C00):** Central focus
  - Fit: Comfortable, slightly oversized (movement-friendly)
  - Condition: Clean but lived-in
- **Trim Details (ELECTRIC BLUE #00D8FF):**
  - Cuff edges
  - Zipper accent
  - Pocket outlines
  - Hood trim
- **Overall Posture:** Conveys barely-contained energy
  - Shoulders slightly raised
  - Ready-to-move stance
  - Body language: optimistic, brilliant, action-oriented

## Character Sheet Composition

### Center Panel (Largest)
**Full-Body Standing Shot**
- Confidence and energy radiating from posture
- Arms raised with palms open (welcoming, powerful)
- Friendly smile, direct eye contact
- Orange hoodie with blue accents clearly visible
- White background, no shadows

### Top Row - Head Variations (3 angles)
1. **Front Facing:** Direct, confident gaze
   - Center of frame
   - Eyes looking straight ahead
2. **3/4 Left Turn:** Dynamic angle
   - Head slightly tilted
   - Spiky hair shows movement
3. **3/4 Right Turn:** Mirror dynamic
   - Balanced composition
   - Shows hair texture from different angle

### Right Column - Emotional Expressions (4 variations)
1. **Happy/Excited:** Wide smile, bright eyes, eyebrows raised
2. **Focused/Determined:** Slight frown of concentration, narrowed eyes, set jaw
3. **Nervous/Anxious:** Concerned eyes, slight grimace, eyebrows worried
4. **Powerful/Confident:** Strong smile, chest puffed slightly, intensity in gaze

### Bottom Row - Action Poses (4 variations)
1. **Running Forward:** Dynamic stride, arms pumping, hair flying backward
2. **Jumping:** Mid-air, knees bent, arms spread, joyful
3. **Thinking Pose:** Hand on chin, relaxed but thoughtful, looking upward
4. **Celebrating:** Fist pump raised, big smile, victory pose

## Art Style Guidelines

### Visual Treatment
- **Line Style:** Clean, bold outlines (cel-shading friendly)
- **Color:** Vibrant, saturated (not desaturated or muted)
- **Shading:** Flat colors, NO gradients or shadows
- **Details:** Minimalist but expressive
- **Background:** Pure white
- **Quality:** High-resolution studio illustration suitable for production

### Reference Aesthetics
- Vectteezy illustration style
- Modern animation studio standards (Disney/Cartoon Network influenced)
- Clean, professional, ready for animation
- Approachable and relatable for middle-school audience

### What to Avoid
- Photorealism
- Heavy shadows or complex lighting
- Subdued colors
- Generic AI aesthetic
- Cluttered background
- Overly detailed textures
