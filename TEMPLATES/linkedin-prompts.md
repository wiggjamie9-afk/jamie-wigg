# LinkedIn prompts

> Source: TikTok @yourgptguide. Five prompts for building out a LinkedIn
> profile with Claude. Paste, edit the bracketed parts, send.

## 1. Headline

> Write a LinkedIn headline that effectively communicates my qualifications,
> work experience, and the unique value I bring to the table. Include
> keywords relevant to my industry as mentioned in my resume below.
>
> [Paste your resume]

## 2. About section

> I want to enhance my About section on LinkedIn. Write me a LinkedIn About
> section using the below format:
>
> - Para 1: Highlight your education, interests, academic achievements
> - Para 2: Mention your previous job experiences, projects, and industry
>   you worked in
> - Para 3: Share your future career goals and plans
>
> Limit the summary to 200–300 words.
>
> Here's my old LinkedIn About section: [paste it here]

## 3. Skills

> I want to enhance my LinkedIn skill section. Suggest 20 skills that are
> relevant to my job role [paste your job role] and a list of software/tools
> relevant to [your job role].

## 4. Experience

> I want to improve the experience section on LinkedIn. I'm a [Job Title]
> at [Company Name]. Write a concise, impact-focused description for the
> role. I'm sharing some of my responsibilities below for your reference.
> [Mention the responsibilities]
>
> Present the results in bullet points.

## 5. Recommendations

> I want to get recommendations on my LinkedIn profile. Write me a
> persuasive message that I can send to my [colleagues / managers / clients].
> Keep it short and sweet.

---

## Notes for using these in this repo

When you're ready to actually build out your LinkedIn, fill in
`context/about-me.md` first — then point Claude at these templates and your
context, and it'll produce drafts in your voice instead of generic
LinkedIn-speak.

Better workflow than copy-pasting into ChatGPT: feed it your
`context/about-me.md` + `context/my-voice.md` + the relevant prompt above.
The output will sound like you, not like everyone else's LinkedIn.
