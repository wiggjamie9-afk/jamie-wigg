⚡ SURGE CHARACTER DESIGNS ⚡

Meet the heroes and challenges of Season 1

ZIGGY

Protagonist • Middle School • ADHD Superhero
Restless, brilliant, constantly moving. Sees his ADHD as a superpower once he accepts himself.
⚡ Superpower: Hyperfocus & Speed

ECHO

Antagonist • Manifestation • Sensory Overload
Embodiment of sensory chaos, self-doubt, and overwhelm. Multiplies stimuli and noise. Not a villain—a challenge to overcome.
🌀 Power: Amplifies Overwhelm

MIRA

Best Friend • Grounding • Emotional Anchor
Calm, centered, grounded presence. Sees Ziggy's wholeness. Bridge between chaos and acceptance.
🎵 Superpower: Resonance (Emotional Attunement)

MS. CHEN

Music Teacher • Neurodivergent Mentor • Adult Model
Adult mentor who validates Ziggy's strengths through music. Shows neurodivergence is lifelong and navigable.
🎼 Superpower: Harmony (Finding Balance)

JORDAN

Student • Anxiety/Autism Spectrum • Protective
Mirror character showing neurodiversity looks different. Anxiety contrasts with Ziggy's externalized ADHD. Mutual support.
🛡️ Superpower: Shield (Protective Boundaries)

KAI

Younger Sibling • Mentee • Joy & Reflection
Chaotic energetic joyful presence. Shows where Ziggy is heading emotionally. Unconditional belief in Ziggy.
✨ Superpower: Reflection (Amplifies Strengths)
