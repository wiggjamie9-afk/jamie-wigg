2,500 Years of Practice

The science isn't catching up to the woo.

It is catching up to traditions that already worked. Twenty-five centuries of humming, lined up against the modern research that vindicated them.

c. 500 BCE · India

The Mandukya Upanishad

Twelve verses on AUM. The closing M — held as a nasal, humming resonance — is treated as the symbol of the four states of consciousness. The original humming-as-cosmology.

c. 7th–8th c. CE · Arabia & Persia

Sufi dhikr

Rhythmic remembrance of divine names. "Loud dhikr" (jahri) involves chesty resonant chanting; "silent dhikr" (khafi) internalises the same vibration. A modern Istanbul study measured cardiac synchrony across participants.

12th c. · Rhineland

Hildegard of Bingen

Benedictine abbess. Composed 77 melismatic chants with long sustained tones. Treated singing as a unifying spiritual-physical act. Wrote her own books of medicine alongside her music.

15th c. · India

Hatha Yoga Pradīpikā

Earliest unambiguous codification of Bhramari Pranayama — the bee breath. Breathe in fast like a male bee, exhale slow like a female bee, "and the mind is absorbed in bliss." The named humming technique enters the textual record.

1475 · Tibet

Gyütö Tantric College

Tibetan monks chant in the lowest possible vocal register and selectively amplify upper harmonics, producing one-voice chords with three simultaneous pitches. Low-frequency drone meets laryngeal vibration — humming's harmonic cousin.

2002 · Karolinska Institute

Weitzberg & Lundberg

The paper. Ten healthy subjects, chemiluminescence measurement of nasal nitric oxide during quiet exhalation versus humming. 15-fold increase. Replicated by Maniscalco in 2003. The mechanism that the 1998 Nobel Prize in Physiology recognised — vasodilatory, antimicrobial NO — turns out to be released by something humans have been doing for four thousand years.

2011 · NIMHANS, Bangalore

Kalyani fMRI on OM

Twelve subjects, scanned during OM chanting versus a control "ssss." OM produced significant deactivation of the right amygdala — the same pattern seen during clinical vagus nerve stimulation for depression.

2023 · Cureus

The Trivedi Holter Study

Twenty-three participants on clinical-grade Holter monitors across humming, physical activity, emotional stress, and sleep. Humming produced the lowest stress index of any state measured — lower than sleep itself.

July 2025 · FDA

SetPoint Medical · RESET-RA

First-ever FDA approval for neuroimmune modulation. Implantable vagus stimulator for rheumatoid arthritis. The exact anti-inflammatory pathway humming touches via laryngeal vibration is now a recognised therapeutic target.

FAQ · The Short Answers
Does humming actually do anything, or is this woo?

The literature is genuinely strong. Weitzberg & Lundberg at Karolinska (2002) measured a 15-fold increase in nasal nitric oxide during humming versus quiet breathing. Otto Loewi won the 1921 Nobel for proving vagus-nerve modulation of heart rate. Coherence breathing at ~0.1 Hz is the rhythm HeartMath uses for clinical HRV biofeedback.

HUM doesn't claim to treat anything — it's a practice instrument. But the underlying physiology is some of the best-documented in nervous-system science.

How long is the daily practice?

Ninety seconds. Open it on your phone, inhale on the rising orb, hum on the fall. There are four optional slots through the day (Rise / Noon / 4pm / Sleep) but a single 90-second session is the whole prescription.

How is this different from Calm or Headspace?

Calm and Headspace are meditation libraries — guided audio, no biofeedback, no humming. None of them combine the four things HUM does: on-device pitch listening (the orb pulls to your throat in real time), 0.1 Hz coherence-breathing pacer, three research-linked frequencies (130 / 174 / 220 Hz), and lineage notes from Bhramari through Karolinska 2002.

It's the first humming-specific practice tool that ships all of that at sub-$50, browser-native.

Why lifetime instead of subscription?

The subscription wellness model is broken. You buy in, the bill arrives forever, and the cost of cancelling is losing your streak. We'd rather charge once at a fair price than rent your nervous system back to you monthly.

The maths works because HUM runs in the browser — no app-store cut, no server-side audio processing. After founding-member cohort closes, the price goes up for new buyers; founding members stay at AU$30 forever.

Do I need any hardware?

No. A phone with a browser and a microphone is the whole kit. Pitch detection runs on-device through the browser's microphone — no headset, no wearable, no chest strap. Audio analysis happens locally; nothing leaves your phone.

Is it safe to do every day?

For most adults, yes — gentle humming is one of the safest nervous-system practices on Earth. Yogis have been doing Bhramari daily for five thousand years.

The exceptions: if you have severe acute sinusitis, vocal cord nodules, or have had recent ear surgery, consult your doctor first. Gentle is always better than loud — HUM is not a volume contest.
