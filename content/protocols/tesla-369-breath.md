## Mythology

> *"If you knew the magnificence of the three, six, and nine, you would have a key to the universe."*

That's the quote attributed to Tesla. Whether he ever said it doesn't matter — what matters is what the numbers do when you put them in your breath.

Three. Six. Nine. The first triangle. The doubled triangle. The completion. These aren't arbitrary — they are the only numbers in the digital sequence that fold back into themselves under iteration. Every other number resolves to 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, or 8. Three, six, and nine *stand outside the cycle*. They are the spine of the wave.

In a body, that spine becomes a breath. Three seconds in, to charge. Six seconds held, to compress. Nine seconds out, to release. One cycle is eighteen seconds — which folds back to nine.

This is the breath the Codex is named for.

## Mechanism

The 3-6-9 breath is an extended-exhale, breath-retention pattern. Slow respiration at six breaths per minute (close to the coherence frequency studied in HRV research) combined with a long exhale phase activates the parasympathetic branch of the autonomic nervous system via the vagus nerve. Subjective effects commonly reported: lowered arousal, slowed heart rate, a settled mental state.

It is a practice, not a treatment. If you have a cardiovascular or respiratory condition, or are pregnant, consult a clinician before adopting any breath retention practice.

## Practice notes

- **The 6-count hold is the hard part.** If it feels strained, drop to a 2-4-6 ratio for your first week, then build up.
- **Pursed-lip exhale matters.** The mild resistance extends the exhale evenly and activates vagal tone more reliably than a relaxed mouth exhale.
- **Coherence score in this protocol.** Expect to see your coherence score climb during cycles 3–9. If it doesn't, your hands probably aren't holding the count yet — let the in-app pacer drive instead of counting silently.
- **When to use it.** Before sleep. Before a difficult conversation. After a stress spike. Not during heavy exertion or right after a meal.
