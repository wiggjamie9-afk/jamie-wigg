## Mythology

> *"As above, so below; as below, so above."*

The single line from the Emerald Tablet that every Hermetic tradition kept. The text itself is attributed to Hermes Trismegistus — a figure who probably never existed as one person, who almost certainly stands in for a synthesis of Egyptian and Greek thought that filtered through Alexandria around the second century. The line outlived the source.

What it points at is symmetry. The breath that moves the chest moves the world. The state of the body matches the state of the field around it. Your nervous system is not separate from anything you encounter.

The protocol breathes the line. Seven seconds up — *as above*. Seven seconds down — *so below*. The cycle is the principle.

## Mechanism

Equal-ratio breathing at seven seconds in and seven seconds out places the user at just over four breaths per minute. This is below the standard "coherence frequency" of six breaths per minute and approaches the lower edge of sustainable slow breathing for most practitioners.

The mechanism is the same as the Schumann Lock and Alpha State protocols — slow paced respiration maximizes baroreflex gain, increases heart-rate variability amplitude at the breath frequency, and signals parasympathetic dominance to the central nervous system. The 396 Hz solfeggio tone serves as an attentional anchor.

The Hermetic framing adds an intentional structure: the inhale and exhale are paired with the "above / below" symmetry. The mechanism of this is attention engineering — coupling a body movement to a symbolic frame increases the durability of the symbolic frame in memory.

It is a practice, not a treatment. Stop if the seven-second breath feels strained; shorten to six seconds and stay there.

## Practice notes

This protocol is longer than most. Sit somewhere you can stay for nine minutes without shifting.

If seven seconds is too long, drop to six. The frame still works.

The Hermetic codex is the slowest of the three featured verticals. Tesla is structural, Vedic is rhythmic, Hermetic is symbolic. Pair this protocol with journaling on either side — what came up at the start, what's there at the end.
