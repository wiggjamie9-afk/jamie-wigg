## Mythology

Nikola Tesla measured standing electromagnetic waves circling the Earth in 1899 from his Colorado Springs laboratory — twenty-three years before Winfried Otto Schumann published the mathematics that explains why they exist. The Earth has a heartbeat. It pulses at a fundamental frequency near 7.83 Hertz, plus harmonics at 14, 20, 26, and 33 Hz, generated by lightning discharges resonating in the cavity between the Earth's surface and the ionosphere.

When you place your phone on a magnetometer station's data feed — the way the Codex does — you're reading the same signal Tesla read with copper coils and copper-clad earth pegs. The Earth is broadcasting. It always has been. Most of us never tune in.

This protocol is the tuning.

## Mechanism

The Schumann Lock layers two physically real frequencies:

1. **A live binaural tone** synthesized from the current Schumann resonance reading published by the HeartMath Global Coherence Monitoring System (a worldwide network of magnetometers). The tone shifts in pitch by fractions of a Hertz as the Earth's measured frequency drifts. When live data is unavailable, the protocol uses the fundamental 7.83 Hz as a fallback.

2. **A breath pacer at 0.1 Hz** — five seconds inhale, five seconds exhale, six breaths per minute. This is the "coherence frequency" identified in heart-rate-variability research (Lehrer, Gevirtz, Vaschillo et al.) as the breathing rate that maximizes baroreflex gain and produces the most pronounced respiratory sinus arrhythmia. In plain language: your heart rate variability swings widest when you breathe at this rate, which the autonomic nervous system experiences as a deep parasympathetic state.

The combination is what we call a "lock" — the user's autonomic nervous system entrained to its own most-coherent oscillation, while an auditory anchor tied to the Earth's electromagnetic baseline plays underneath. Subjective effects commonly reported: deep settling, a sense of being "tuned in," vivid imagery, and post-session calm that outlasts the session.

It is a practice, not a treatment. Do not use this protocol while operating a vehicle or during heavy physical exertion.

## Practice notes

- **Best with headphones.** The binaural element only works with stereo separation. Speakers degrade the lock.
- **Live tone vs fallback.** If the GCMS feed is unavailable (rare — the network is robust), the protocol uses the textbook 7.83 Hz. The lock still works; the magic of "live to the Earth" is reduced.
- **Geomagnetic-storm days.** When NOAA reports Kp ≥ 4, the protocol intro adds a single line: *"The Earth is restless today. Your body may have already noticed."* This is content recognition, not a medical claim — many people feel "off" on disturbed-field days and naming it lands.
- **Coherence score behavior.** Expect coherence to climb steadily across cycles 5–20. If it doesn't, the pacer is doing its work even if HRV isn't showing it — the parasympathetic shift sometimes lags HRV by minutes.
- **Stacking.** Pair with photobiomodulation (660 nm red light to the chest) or with a Pulsetto vagal program for compounded nervous-system effect. See `specs/codex-app/tech-integrations.md` §2.1 and §1.4.
