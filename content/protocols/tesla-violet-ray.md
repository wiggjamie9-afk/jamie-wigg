## Mythology

In the 1920s, "violet ray" devices — high-voltage glass wands driven by Tesla coils — were sold across America as cures for nearly everything. The FDA shut down the medical claims in the 1950s. The devices themselves were never the medicine.

But the visual was: a deep violet glow surrounding a focal point, paired with a quiet hum. Two sensory anchors, one for sight, one for sound. People stopped, and watched, and breathed. The state was real even if the device wasn't.

The Codex re-creates the visual and the tone, without the high voltage. Five minutes of pure sensory anchor with a coherence-frequency breath.

## Mechanism

Focused-attention meditation with strong unimodal sensory anchors — one auditory (432 Hz pure tone), one visual (full-screen deep-violet field with a centered focal point). Combined with 0.1 Hz coherent breathing. The mechanism is the same as any anchor-based meditation: the brain's default-mode network suppresses under sustained sensory attention, and the autonomic system shifts toward parasympathetic dominance through the breath.

It is a practice, not a treatment. Do not stare at the screen at close range or in a dark room without breaks.

## Script

```
[00:00] — opening
Hold the screen at arm's length. Look at the center.

[00:15] — anchor
The tone is 432 Hertz. The field is violet. There's nothing else
right now — only the tone, the field, and the breath.

[00:45] — breath
Five in. Five out. The pacer is invisible — let your eyes stay on
the center. The breath happens on its own.

[02:00] — settle
The mind will wander. That's fine. Each time it returns, the anchor
gets stronger.

[03:30] — depth
The tone hasn't changed. The field hasn't changed. The breath
hasn't changed. Notice what has.

[04:30] — closing
Stay on the center. The Codex will fade the field.

[05:00] — fade
```

## Practice notes

- Use at least medium screen brightness — the violet field needs saturation to work as an anchor.
- This is a low-arousal protocol. Best for evening, post-stress recovery, or post-protocol cooldown.
- If you have a history of seizures or migraines triggered by flickering, skip this protocol.
