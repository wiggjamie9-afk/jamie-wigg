## Mythology

Between waking and sleeping, between figuring it out and forgetting the question, there is a state the old contemplatives called *the threshold*. Modern researchers measured it with electrodes and called it alpha — an eight-to-twelve Hertz rhythm that dominates the brain when the eyes close and the analytical mind softens.

Every contemplative tradition has a path into it. The Tibetans approached through silence. The Sufis approached through repetition. Tesla — by his own account — entered it nightly before sleep and emerged with his solutions.

The Codex builds the door three ways: a slow paced breath, a 432 Hz tone, and a 10 Hz binaural beat. Walk in.

## Mechanism

Alpha State stacks three converging mechanisms:

1. **Six-second equal breath** — five-to-six breaths per minute approaches the coherence frequency, maximizing heart-rate variability and signalling parasympathetic dominance to the central nervous system.

2. **432 Hz tone** — a sustained pitch in the low-mid range. The claim that 432 Hz is "the natural tuning of the universe" is mythology, not physics; the *mechanism* is unbroken auditory anchoring of attention, which is well-supported regardless of frequency choice.

3. **10 Hz binaural beat** — a 10 Hz difference between left and right ear pitches. Studies (Padmanabhan et al., 2005; Wahbeh et al., 2007) suggest binaural beats can shift dominant EEG frequencies toward the beat frequency in some individuals. Effects vary. Headphones required.

Subjective effects commonly reported: a soft drifting sensation, vivid mental imagery, a quieter inner monologue, and time distortion. Practitioners use this state for visualization, intention-setting, and creative ideation.

It is a practice, not a treatment. Do not use this protocol while operating a vehicle.

## Script

```
[00:00] — opening (headphones reminder)

This protocol uses a binaural beat. Headphones make it work. Without
them, you'll get the breath and the tone, but the alpha-frequency
entrainment won't land.

If you have headphones available, put them on now.

[00:25] — settle

Six counts in. Six counts out. Even and unhurried. Five breaths a
minute. Let the pacer guide you.

[00:45] — cycle 1, guided

In. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six.
Out. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six.

Again. In.

[01:30] — alpha frame

Underneath the breath, there is a tone. Underneath the tone, there is
a beat — ten cycles per second — the same rhythm the brain produces
on its own at the edge of sleep.

You don't have to make anything happen. The breath is the work.

[02:00] — silent stretch with pacer

(narration silent, frequency layer + binaural beat only, 4 minutes)

[06:00] — visualization prompt

If you came in with something to visualize, this is the moment.
See it specifically. Hold it without strain. Let the breath carry it.

If you came in empty, stay empty. That counts.

[06:45] — close

Two more breaths at your own pace.

When you open your eyes, write down one sentence. Whatever's there.
```

## Practice notes

Some users don't experience binaural beat entrainment. That's normal — it's an individual variation, not a failure of the protocol. The paced breath and the tone alone still produce the alpha-favourable state for most practitioners.

If the visualization prompt at 06:00 feels forced, skip it. The protocol is complete with breath alone.

This is the protocol for the morning practice that doesn't have a goal yet.
