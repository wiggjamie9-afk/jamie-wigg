## Mythology

Three reps in the morning, six in the afternoon, nine at night. Write the same sentence — the one thing you're calling into your life — eighteen times across the day. Tesla's numbers structure the repetition; the repetition structures your attention; your attention structures what your nervous system filters in and out of reality.

This is the viral 369 manifestation method, codified into the Codex as a daily ritual.

## Mechanism

Spaced repetition + intention-setting + reflective writing. The mechanism is well-supported: writing has been shown to consolidate memory and shift attentional filters (the reticular activating system selectively elevates relevant stimuli). Repetition across the day creates spaced rehearsal, which durably encodes the priority. Pairing the writing with a brief breath protocol grounds it in body state.

This is not magic. It is attention engineering with a strong frame.

## Script

```
[00:00] — opening
Take out your journal, or open the app's ritual screen.

[00:10] — the sentence
Today's sentence is one short, specific, present-tense statement.
Not "I want X." Not "I'll get X." Just X — as if it's already arriving.

  Example: "Codex of Reality has its first 100 founding members."

[00:30] — first three (morning) — or first six (afternoon) — or first nine (evening)
Write the sentence the required number of times. No multitasking. No
phone. Six minutes maximum.

[duration depends on number of reps — pacer runs 3-6-9 breath in
background between sets of three reps]

[02:30] — closing
The Codex tracks your three drops a day. After 21 days, the loop is
durable enough that you don't need the journal — but keep it anyway.

[03:00] — release
```

## Practice notes

- Morning is the strongest slot — set the day's filter while the nervous system is still neutral.
- Evening writes consolidate during sleep.
- Same sentence for at least 21 days. Changing it every day defeats the encoding.
- The breath pacer is optional in this ritual — the writing is the protocol.
