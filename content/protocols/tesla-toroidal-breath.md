## Mythology

Tesla's coils were toroidal — a donut-shaped magnetic field surrounding the coil at every moment of discharge. He believed the torus was the fundamental shape of energy itself: every cell, every planet, every galaxy looped its field through itself in the same recursive geometry.

The Toroidal Breath turns the body into a coil. Energy enters the crown, descends the spine, exits at the base, loops back up the outside, and re-enters at the crown. Six minutes. Thirty cycles. The field becomes self-sustaining.

## Mechanism

A 4-4-4 equal-ratio breath (box breathing) paired with directed-attention visualization of a recursive energy loop through the body. Mechanism is focused-attention meditation with a strong sensory anchor (the breath count) and a strong cognitive anchor (the toroidal map). Sustained attention on a coherent visualization is reliably associated with default-mode-network suppression and subjective state shifts.

## Script

```
[00:00] — opening
Sit upright. Crown lifted. Spine long.

[00:15] — visualization
Picture a donut of light surrounding your body. The light flows down the
inside of you — through the crown, down the spine, out the base — then
loops up the outside and re-enters at the crown.

[00:45] — breath joins it
On the inhale, the light enters the crown and descends.
Hold — the light is at the base.
On the exhale, it loops up the outside.
Hold — it re-enters the crown.

[01:30] — settle into the loop
Four counts each. The breath is the pump. The torus is the circuit.

[03:00] — silence with the loop
[reduce voice — let the user run the loop alone for 90 seconds]

[04:30] — closing
The field is self-sustaining now. Even when the breath ends, the loop
continues. That's the point.

[05:30] — release
Let the visualization fade. Notice the body. The torus remains, even
when you stop drawing it.
```

## Practice notes

- The visualization is the practice. If it slips, return to the breath count.
- Pair with the 432 Hz tone (Frequency Player → Tesla → Universal A).
- This is a "settle-in" protocol, not a high-arousal one. Best mid-day or pre-sleep.
