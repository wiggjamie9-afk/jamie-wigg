## 📋 Setup Instructions (1 Hour Total)

### Platform 1: Draft2Digital

```
1. Go to: draft2digital.com
2. Sign up (free account)
3. Click "Publish"
4. Upload files:
   - Cover image (2560×1600px)
   - Your ebook PDF
   - Title, author, description
5. Set pricing:
   - Ebook: $3.99
   - Coloring book: $2.99
   - Phonics: $3.99
6. Select distribution (all stores auto-enabled)
7. Publish!

Distribution includes:
├── Apple Books
├── Google Play
├── Kobo
├── Barnes & Noble
├── Amazon KDP (optional)
├── Scribd
├── Smashwords
└── 40+ others
```

**Timeline: 20 minutes**
**Revenue added: +$150-250/month**

### Platform 2: Smashwords

```
1. Go to: smashwords.com
2. Sign up (free)
3. Click "Publish"
4. Upload PDF
5. Fill metadata
6. Set price (Smashwords has specific pricing)
7. Submit for "Premium Catalog"
8. Distribute to partners

Distribution includes:
├── Apple Books
├── Google Play
├── Kobo
├── Barnes & Noble
└── 30+ others
```

**Timeline: 20 minutes**
**Revenue added: +$100-150/month**

### Platform 3: Wattpad

```
1. Go to: wattpad.com
2. Sign up
3. Create new story
4. Paste chapter content (divide episodes)
5. Add cover image
6. Tag: #bedtime #children #quokka
7. Share on social media

Distribution:
├── Wattpad readers (20M+ users)
├── Wattpad Studios (TV/film)
├── Ad revenue sharing
└── Potential book deals

Bonus: Some stories get optioned for TV!
```

**Timeline: 30 minutes setup + ongoing updates**
**Revenue: Ad revenue + potential deals**

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Set Prices by Platform
```
Gumroad:    $3.99 (highest margin)
Etsy:       $4.99 (competitive)
Apple:      $4.99 (premium placement)
Google:     $3.99 (volume play)
Draft2D:    $3.99 (reach)
Wattpad:    FREE (ad revenue)
TpT:        $5.99 (educators)
```

### Monitor Performance
- Track which platforms drive most sales
- Double down on winners
- Optimize pricing per platform
- Test different cover images

### Time Your Releases
- Launch on main platforms first (Gumroad, Etsy, KDP)
- 1-2 weeks later, push to aggregators
- This creates initial buzz before wider distribution
