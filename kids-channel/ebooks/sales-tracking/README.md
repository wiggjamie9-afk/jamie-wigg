# E-book Sales Tracking

Track revenue from all e-book sales platforms.

## Files in this directory

- `2026-revenue.csv` — Monthly revenue log
- `gumroad-analytics.txt` — Gumroad-specific notes
- `amazon-kdp-analytics.txt` — Amazon KDP sales tracking
- `etsy-analytics.txt` — Etsy sales tracking

## How to track sales

### Weekly routine (take 5 minutes):

1. **Gumroad dashboard:**
   - Go to gumroad.com → Your Products → View Stats
   - Note: Number of sales this week
   - Note: Total revenue this week

2. **Amazon KDP dashboard:**
   - Go to kdp.amazon.com → Reports → Sales & Royalties
   - Filter by date range (this week)
   - Note: Kindle units sold
   - Note: Print copies sold

3. **Etsy shop:**
   - Go to your Etsy shop → Shop Manager → Sales
   - Filter by date range
   - Note: Number of orders this week

4. **Update spreadsheet:**
   - Add weekly totals to `2026-revenue.csv`

### Monthly summary:

Update with:
- Total sales per platform
- Total revenue
- Best-selling episodes
- Conversion rate (YouTube views → e-book sales)

## Dashboards to check

| Platform | Dashboard URL | Update frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Gumroad | gumroad.com/dashboard | Weekly |
| Amazon KDP | kdp.amazon.com/reporting | Weekly |
| Etsy | etsy.com/seller/sales | Weekly |

## Revenue alerts

- Sales milestone: 100 total e-books sold = $400+ revenue (month 1)
- Bestseller: Any episode with 50+ sales in a week
- Print milestone: First print-on-demand order
- Conversion: Calculate YouTube views ÷ e-book sales = conversion %
