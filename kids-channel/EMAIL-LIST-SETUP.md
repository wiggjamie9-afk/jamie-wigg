## 🚀 Setup (30 minutes)

### Step 1: Choose Email Platform

**ConvertKit (Recommended)**
- Free tier: Up to 1,000 subscribers
- Beautiful landing pages
- Automation workflows
- Integrates with Gumroad/Etsy
- Sign up: convertkit.com

**Alternative: Mailchimp**
- Free tier: Unlimited subscribers
- Basic automation
- Sign up: mailchimp.com

### Step 2: Create Free Lead Magnet

**Offer: "Episode #1 Complete Bundle"**
```
In exchange for email, user gets:
├── Colored Ebook (full PDF)
├── Coloring Book (full PDF)
├── Phonics Book - Letter S (full PDF)
└── Bonus: Sunny sticker printable
```

**File: Create a PDF bundle**
```
kids-channel/leadmagnet/
└── Sunny-Episode1-Complete-Bundle.pdf
    ├── Colored ebook
    ├── Coloring book
    ├── Phonics book
    └── Bonus materials
```

### Step 3: Create Landing Page

**ConvertKit Landing Page:**
```
Headline: "Get Your Free Sunny Story Bundle"

Copy:
"Download 3 Sunny books (ebook, coloring book, 
and phonics lesson) — absolutely free!

Perfect for bedtime or quiet time. Join 500+ 
parents who love Sunny's cozy adventures."

Form Fields:
- First Name (optional)
- Email (required)

Button: "Send Me My Free Bundle"

After signup: Redirect to download link
```

✅ Beautiful presentation (professional PDF)
✅ Clear benefit (what they get)
✅ Low friction (email only, no phone)
