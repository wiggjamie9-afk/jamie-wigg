# 🌟 Plum — A Cuddly Bedtime Friend

**One line:** Plum is a small, impossibly cuddly bear cub who, every night, snuggles
into bed, hugs her little star pillow, closes her eyes, and keeps one warm happy
thought from her day — then drifts off to sleep.

## Why this concept (trend-backed)
Current cozy-bedtime trends point here:
- **Soft, round, plush-looking characters** (huggable "squishy" pastel look)
- **Sleepy comfort animals** — bear cub, bunny, lamb
- **Gentle-parenting rituals** — gratitude / "happy thought", slow breathing,
  winding down *with* the child
- **A comfort object** — Plum's little star pillow / nightlight

It beats the Moon Trucks idea for bedtime because it's **warm and cuddly**, it's a
**single recognizable hero** (plush-toy + thumbnail friendly), and the
"close her eyes and keep a happy thought" ritual *is* the bedtime mechanic.

## The character
**Plum** — a small round bear cub with soft fur, big gentle sleepy eyes, wearing
cosy pastel pyjamas. Warm, tender, huggable. (Swappable to bunny / lamb / pelican.)

## The nightly ritual (repeatable episode formula)
1. Plum has a gentle little day (one small, sweet adventure).
2. Night comes; Plum gets cosy in bed with her star pillow.
3. She closes her eyes and thinks of **one happy thing** from the day.
4. She asks the child: *"What made you smile today?"*
5. Soft goodnight; Plum drifts to sleep in a warm nightlight glow.

## Look & palette
Hand-painted watercolour picture-book texture; warm cream, dusty pink, soft
lavender; nightlight glow; rounded plush shapes; nothing sharp or harsh.
