# 🌙🚚 Moon Trucks — Channel Concept

**One line:** Big, friendly trucks live and work on the Moon — and every night, one
by one, they finish their gentle work and settle down to sleep under the glowing
blue Earth.

## Why it works
- **Two giant toddler obsessions in one:** vehicles/trucks + space. Both are
  top-tier evergreen kids-YouTube niches; together they're distinctive.
- **The bedtime twist:** the trucks are huge and powerful but **slow, quiet, and
  sleepy** — no crashing or racing. Calm by design, perfect for wind-down.
- **Searchable, bingeable titles:** "Goodnight Digger", "The Sleepy Moon Crane",
  "Dumper Truck Says Goodnight" — endless episodes, strong thumbnails, natural
  playlists.
- **Recurring cast = brand recall:** kids latch onto named characters and ask for
  them by name.
- **Not region-locked:** travels worldwide, easy to dub later.
- **Fits the existing pipeline:** same watercolour/soft-render style, calm
  narration, 6-scene structure, lullaby music.

## The cast (gentle, named, recurring)
| Truck | Personality | Goodnight ritual |
|---|---|---|
| **Digger Dot** | Careful little excavator | Smooths one last patch of soft moon dust |
| **Dumper Dan** | Big, slow dump truck | Tips out a pile of soft glowing moon-pebbles |
| **Crane Belle** | Tall, gentle crane | Lifts the flag down for the night |
| **Mixer Max** | Round, hummy cement mixer | Slows his drum until it stops: round… round… still |
| **Loader Lou** | Sleepy front loader | Scoops the last star-dust into the bin |

## Setting
The Moon: soft grey dust, craters like cosy bowls, a sky full of stars, and the
big blue-green Earth glowing in the dark like a nightlight. Engines wind down with
a gentle "rrrmmm… rrmmm… shhhhh," never a roar.

## Title pattern
`Goodnight, Big Moon Trucks 🌙🚚 | Calm Bedtime Story for Sleepy Little Ones`

## Episode formula (repeatable)
1. Night falls on the Moon; Earth glows in the sky.
2. Each truck does ONE last slow, gentle job.
3. Each truck parks and powers down (engine sound winding to quiet).
4. They all rest together in the cosy crater garage.
5. Soft "goodnight" to each truck by name — child is the last one tucked in.
