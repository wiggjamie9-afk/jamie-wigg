# How to Put Your Books on Amazon (KDP) — Step by Step

You publish through **Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP)** — it's free, and you can do it all from your phone's browser.

## One-time setup (about 15 minutes)

1. Go to **kdp.amazon.com** and sign in with your normal Amazon account (or create one).
2. It will ask you to complete three things:
   - **Author/Publisher info** — your name and address.
   - **Payment** — your bank account (BSB + account number for Australia). Amazon pays royalties monthly.
   - **Tax interview** — a short form. As an Australian, choose "Individual", say you're not a US person, and enter your TFN where it asks for a foreign tax ID. This drops US withholding from 30% to 5%.

## Publishing each book (about 10 minutes per book)

1. On the KDP **Bookshelf**, tap **+ Create** → **Kindle eBook**.
2. **Details page:**
   - Language: English
   - Book title: e.g. `Sunny and the Little Numbat`
   - Series: tick "This book is part of a series" → Series name: `Sonny's Cozy Quokka Bedtime Tales`, number: `15`
   - Author: `Jamie Wigg`
   - Description: copy the book's description from BOOK-SEO-PACK.md (the file I sent you)
   - Publishing rights: "I own the copyright"
   - Categories: pick **Children's eBooks → Animals → Marsupials** (or Bedtime & Dreaming) — choose up to 3
   - Keywords: bedtime story, read aloud, australian animals, quokka, toddler book, goodnight story, kids picture book
   - Age range: **2–6**
3. **Content page:**
   - Manuscript: upload the book's **.epub** file (the ones I sent — they're already in Amazon's page-flip children's format)
   - Cover: choose "Upload a cover you already have" → upload the matching **kdp-cover.jpg** (from the zip I'm sending — these are the portrait covers Amazon requires)
   - Use the **Previewer** to flick through and check it looks right
   - AI content question: answer honestly — select **Yes**, images and text were created with AI-based tools with your creative direction.
4. **Pricing page:**
   - KDP Select (optional): enrolling makes the book part of Kindle Unlimited — good for kids' books
   - Royalty: choose **70%** and price between **$2.99–$4.99 USD** (a sweet spot for kids' ebooks). Amazon auto-converts other markets.
5. Tap **Publish Your Kindle eBook**. It goes live within about 72 hours (usually much faster).

## Tips

- Publish 2–3 books first, make sure you're happy, then batch the rest.
- Once several are live, KDP shows them as a **series page** so parents can buy the whole set.
- You can update anything later (price, description, even the file) without unpublishing.
- Want paperbacks too? The same Bookshelf has "Create paperback" — but that needs print-layout PDFs, ask me and I'll prepare them.
