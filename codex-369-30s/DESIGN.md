# 369 Codex — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Cosmic, intimate, ceremonial. A deep-indigo canvas with gold filigree light — like the inside cover of a vintage grimoire reimagined for a phone screen. Type alternates between an editorial serif (the wisdom voice) and a tight mono (the numerology stamps). Motion is slow, deliberate, breathy. Numbers 3, 6, 9 are the heroes — they punch in like seals on a page. Everything else is candlelight.

## Colors

- `#0a0a1f` — Canvas (deep cosmic indigo, near-black with violet bias)
- `#1a1340` — Card surface (slightly lifted indigo)
- `#e8c97a` — Hot gold (3·6·9 numerology, CTA, "Codex" wordmark)
- `#f5e1a4` — Soft cream gold (highlights, hover)
- `#9b6dff` — Astral violet (accent, mystic glyphs)
- `#ff8ec7` — Soft rose (heart, intuition, audience callouts)
- `#ffffff` — Display text
- `#b8b2d6` — Muted body (muted violet-grey)

## Typography

- Display: `"Playfair Display", "Times New Roman", serif` — headlines, "Codex" wordmark, the question hook
- Mono: `"JetBrains Mono", monospace` — the 3·6·9 stamps, eyebrows, labels
- Body: `"Inter", system-ui, sans-serif` — short body lines

## Motion

- Slow in (0.7–1.1s), `power2.out` / `power3.out` for entrances
- Numerology stamps (3, 6, 9) use `back.out(1.4)` — a gentle settle, like a seal pressed in
- Crossfade between scenes (0.6s) — slower than RHYTHMIX, ceremonial pace
- No bounce, no elastic — manifestation content reads cheap with overplayed motion

## What NOT to Do

- No `#3b82f6`, `#333`, or default Roboto/Arial — palette is fixed.
- No full-frame linear gradients (banding); use radial glows + solid indigo.
- No scene-internal exit animations — the crossfade IS the exit (final scene excepted).
- No magic sparkles, no rotating mandalas, no purple-pink "spiritual TikTok" clichés. Hold the line on ceremonial.
- No animating the 3·6·9 numbers individually after they land — they're seals, not glyphs that vibrate.
