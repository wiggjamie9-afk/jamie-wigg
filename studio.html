FAQ

The answers.

Do I need to know prompting?

No. You give Studio a one-line theme — "neon-soaked midnight city, lone musician on rooftop" — and it writes the per-scene prompts for you, varying them by section (intro / verse / chorus / bridge / outro).

How much does each video cost to render?

About $5–25 per video depending on length and which models the planner picks. A 60-second video using the cheapest model (MiniMax Hailuo) runs ~$8. The dry-run command shows the exact estimate before you spend anything.

What audio formats are supported?

Anything ffmpeg can read — MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, OGG. Studio probes the file for duration and you supply the tempo (auto-BPM detection coming next).

Can I edit the plan before rendering?

Yes — that's the whole point. plan writes a plan.json you can hand-edit (swap prompts, change models, adjust durations) before render-from-plan spends real money. You only pay for generation, never re-planning.

What does the lifetime deal include?

Unlimited use of the engine, all current and future models, every quality level, and every aspect ratio. You bring your own Replicate API key for generation costs (transparent pass-through, no markup). $149 once.
