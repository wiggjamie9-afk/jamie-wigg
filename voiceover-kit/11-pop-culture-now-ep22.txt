An artist nobody outside their local scene had heard of twelve months ago just became the most-streamed new act of the year so far. And the path they took was not the traditional one. What I think is interesting about this particular artist is not just the music, which is genuinely excellent, but the way they responded to the attention. Most artists in the early phase of sudden viral fame make at least one decision that feels reactive, something slightly off-brand that you can see was a response to the spotlight rather than an expression of who they actually are. This one did not do that. They kept putting out music at the same pace. They gave interviews that sounded like a real person having a real conversation. The consistency is what sold me.

The danger zone for them now is the sophomore trap, and I say this as someone who has watched a hundred artists navigate this exact moment. The first project did something the second project has to live up to, and every decision between now and release gets made under scrutiny. The ones who get through it are the ones who treat the pressure as noise and keep doing the thing they were already doing. Easier said. Genuinely hard to do. I'm rooting for them.

A very high-profile celebrity gave an interview this week and said something about a topic that is currently extremely sensitive in the entertainment industry. The ten-second clip did not have the context of the surrounding four minutes. It is a starting gun. And everyone who heard the starting gun ran in whatever direction they were already facing. The full interview is worth watching. Form your own view.
