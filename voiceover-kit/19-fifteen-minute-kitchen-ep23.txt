Welcome to fifteen minute kitchen. Today we are doing low-carb — and I want to start by saying this is not a sacrifice episode. We are not eating sad lettuce cups pretending to be a burger. We are making a proper, satisfying dinner that happens to be naturally low in carbohydrates because it is built around great protein and great vegetables, not around cutting things out. The meal is a pan-seared salmon with a warm lemon caper butter sauce and a side of garlicky wilted greens. It is restaurant-level food in under fifteen minutes.

Here is your grab list. Two salmon fillets, skin on if possible. Two big handfuls of any dark leafy greens — spinach, kale, silverbeet, whatever you have. Four cloves of garlic. A tablespoon of capers — those little briny green things in a jar, you will find them near the olives at the supermarket. Butter — two tablespoons. Olive oil. A lemon. Salt and black pepper.

Start by getting your greens out of the way so they are ready to go when the salmon is done. Into a wide pan over medium heat, a splash of olive oil and three of your four garlic cloves, thinly sliced. Let them sizzle for thirty seconds until fragrant, then add your greens in handfuls. Toss them around, add a tiny pinch of salt, and cook for two to three minutes until wilted and silky. Tip them into a bowl and keep them warm.

Now the salmon. Pat the fillets dry with paper towel — this is not optional, moisture is the enemy of a good sear. Season generously on both sides with salt and pepper. Into the same pan, still hot, add a splash of olive oil. When it shimmers, place the salmon skin-side down. Press it gently with a spatula for the first ten seconds to stop it curling. Then leave it completely alone for three to four minutes. You will see the colour change from translucent pink to opaque as it cooks up the sides of the fillet. When it is about two thirds of the way cooked through, flip it once and cook for just another sixty seconds on the flesh side. Take it out and rest it on a plate.

Now the sauce, and it takes ninety seconds. Same pan, medium heat. Add your two tablespoons of butter and let it melt and start to foam. Add the last garlic clove, minced finely, and the capers. Swirl everything around for thirty seconds. Squeeze in half the lemon juice and watch it bubble up. That is your sauce — briny, buttery, sharp, done.

Plate the greens, put the salmon on top, pour the sauce over everything, and slice a wedge of lemon alongside.

The tip for this one is the dry pat before searing. Wet salmon steams instead of sears and you will never get that golden, crispy crust that makes this dish. Spend five seconds patting it dry and you get a completely different result.

This meal has almost no carbohydrates because there are simply no grains or starchy vegetables in it, and you will not miss them because the salmon is filling, the greens are satisfying, and the butter sauce makes everything taste indulgent. The full recipe with timing notes is in the show notes.
