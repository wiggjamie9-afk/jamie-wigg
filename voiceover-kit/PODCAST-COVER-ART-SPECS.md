# jamie's Podcast — Episode Cover Art Specifications

## Design System

**Format:** 3000×3000px (podcast standard, square)
**Aspect Ratio:** 1:1
**File Format:** PNG with transparency or JPG
**Style:** Modern, warm, authentic, professional

**Brand Colors:**
- Primary: Deep Charcoal (#2C3E50) — trust, grounded
- Accent: Warm Orange (#E67E22) — energy, optimism
- Tertiary: Soft Cream (#F5F1E8) — warmth, humanity
- Text: White (#FFFFFF) for primary text

**Typography:**
- Episode Title: Bold sans-serif (Montserrat Bold or similar)
- Episode Number: Medium weight, slightly smaller
- "jamie's Podcast" branding: Consistent, top or bottom placement

---

## Episode 1: Season Premiere — Welcome to Dad's Code

**Visual Concept:** Gateway, Welcome, Invitation

**Description:**
- Warm, inviting foreground with a figure (silhouette of a father) reaching forward in a welcoming gesture
- Soft warm lighting suggesting dawn or early morning
- Background: soft blur of family/home imagery (suggestion of presence without clarity)
- Color palette: Warm creams, deep charcoal with orange accent highlights
- Typography: Bold, welcoming; "WELCOME TO DAD'S CODE" as the primary text
- Emotional tone: Honest, open, inviting—like someone opening a door

**Visual Elements:**
- Open door or threshold imagery (metaphorical)
- Warm light emanating from the center
- Subtle family silhouettes in the soft background
- Episode number: 01 (small, elegant)
- Series branding: "jamie's Podcast" at bottom

**Suggested Imagery:**
- Father figure in warm light, arms open in welcome
- Soft focus family moments in background
- Gradient from warm cream to deep charcoal
- Accent of orange suggesting warmth and authenticity

## Episode 2: Becoming Dad — The Moment Everything Changed

**Visual Concept:** Transformation, Awe, Love

**Description:**
- Central focus: A moment of profound connection (suggest without being literal)
- Imagery: Hands, light, connection—the moment when everything shifts
- Color palette: Warm lighting with hints of vulnerability (softer, more intimate than Ep 1)
- Emotional tone: Tender, powerful, life-changing
- Typography: Emphasize "BECOMING" as the transformation moment

**Visual Elements:**
- Two hands in connection (adult and child, metaphorical)
- Warm, golden light surrounding the moment
- Subtle aura or glow effect suggesting profundity
- Deep background fading to dark (the before and after)
- Episode number: 02
- Series branding: "jamie's Podcast"

**Suggested Imagery:**
- Intimate moment of connection (hands, light, vulnerability)
- Golden hour lighting
- Soft depth of field with focus on the central moment
- Warm color grading with subtle cool tones for contrast
- Emotional intensity balanced with tenderness

## Episode 3: Twins and Chaos — Doubling Down

**Visual Concept:** Energy, Chaos, Joy, Doubled

**Description:**
- Dynamic, energetic visual suggesting movement and multiplicity
- Imagery: Suggestion of two (doubled imagery, symmetry that breaks)
- Color palette: More vibrant—warm orange and charcoal with cream
- Emotional tone: Chaotic but joyful, energetic but grounded
- Typography: "TWINS AND CHAOS" with visual balance suggesting duality

**Visual Elements:**
- Doubled or mirrored imagery (two of something important)
- Dynamic lines suggesting movement and energy
- Playful composition that feels both organized and chaotic
- Warm but more saturated colors than previous episodes
- Episode number: 03
- Series branding: "jamie's Podcast"

**Suggested Imagery:**
- Symmetrical composition that subtly breaks (representing chaos within order)
- Doubled figures or objects suggesting "two"
- Dynamic lines suggesting motion and energy
- Vibrant warm lighting
- Playful composition—balanced but energetic
- Sense of joy within the chaos

## Technical Specifications for Generation

**Recommended Tools:**
- Higgsfield Soul (text-to-image AI)
- Replicate (FLUX, Sana, or similar)
- Midjourney or similar
- Professional design tool (Canva, Adobe)

**Prompt Structure for Each:**
Include: Style (modern podcast art), composition (described above), color palette, emotional tone, technical specs (3000×3000px, square format)

**Output Requirements:**
- Format: PNG or JPG
- Size: 3000×3000px minimum
- Color space: RGB
- No watermarks or logos (except "jamie's Podcast" branding)
- Ready for Buzzsprout upload

---

## Branding Consistency

All three episodes should:
- Share consistent typography and spacing
- Use the same color palette (primary, accent, tertiary)
- Include "jamie's Podcast" branding consistently placed
- Display episode number clearly (01, 02, 03)
- Feel like they belong to the same series while being visually distinct

**Series Title Placement:** Bottom center or top right, consistent across all episodes
**Episode Number:** Top left or bottom left, elegant and minimal
**Episode Title:** Center or slightly above center, prominent and readable at thumbnail size
