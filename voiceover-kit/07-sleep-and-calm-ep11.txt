# PODCAST #07: SLEEP & CALM — Episode 11
**Voice to use:** Sarah or Charlotte (Soft, slow, soothing)
**Episode 1 voice used:** [Check your EP1 recording]

---

Hello, I'm Mark Coggins, and this is Sleep and Calm. Let the day settle behind you now. You've done enough. There is nothing left to carry. Just be warm, and still, and quiet.

Take a slow breath in, and let it fall away. And again. Let this next breath be a little slower than the last.

Tonight we are going to drift on a calm lake, in the soft hush of evening. You don't have to picture every detail. Just let yourself float, gently, as the words go by.

You are lying in a small wooden boat, on a wide and quiet lake. The boat is soft with blankets beneath you. It rocks, very slowly, on water so still it barely moves at all.

The lake is smooth as glass. The sky above is deep and warm, fading from gold to soft rose to a gentle, sleepy blue. The last light of the day rests on the water in long, still ribbons.

There is no sound but the faint lap of water against the side of the boat. A soft, slow sound. The smallest movement. The lake breathing, slow and even, all around you.

You trail one hand over the side. The water is cool and silken. It moves between your fingers, then settles again. The boat turns, so slowly you can hardly feel it, drifting where the still water carries it.

The shore is far away and soft with trees, dark and quiet against the fading sky. There is nowhere you need to row to. Nothing waiting on the bank. The lake holds you, and that is exactly enough.

The boat rocks, gentle and slow. Your shoulders grow heavy. They sink into the blankets. Your hands rest, open and still. Your breathing slows to match the slow lap of the water.

One star appears, high in the soft blue. Then another. They lie reflected on the still lake, so the whole world feels lit by small, quiet lights. Above you and below you, gentle and far away.

There is nothing to do here but float. The water knows how to hold you. The boat knows how to rock you. You can let go, all the way.

You don't need to watch the stars. They will keep rising, soft and quiet, all night long, while the lake rocks you gently to sleep.

There is nothing to do. Nowhere to be. Just rest now, all the way down, into the warm and the still and the slow drift of the water.

Rest now. Sleep well. I'll see you again, when you're ready.
