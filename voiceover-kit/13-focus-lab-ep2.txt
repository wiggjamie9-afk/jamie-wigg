When was the last time you did one single task — no tab-switching, no glancing at your phone — for a solid forty-five minutes? For many, the honest answer is I genuinely can't remember. Yet we believe that's just who we are now. It isn't. It's a skill we stopped practicing.

Deep work is cognitively demanding work performed in a state of distraction-free concentration. The opposite is shallow work: email, Slack, busywork, logistical noise.

Attention residue means when you switch from one task to another, a chunk of your attention stays stuck on the first task. A two-second email check mid-project costs two seconds plus several minutes of mental reload time. Do that twenty times a day and your entire day is reload, never arrival.

Multitasking is a myth — it's really fast task-switching, and every switch pays the residue tax.

Step One: Time-block your calendar. A to-do list is a pile of wishes with no home. Time-blocking gives each task an actual appointment. Nine to ten-thirty, draft the report. When a task lives on the calendar, it stops being optional anxiety and becomes a real event. Start with just one block tomorrow.

Step Two: Make it shorter than you think you need. The Pomodoro Technique: twenty-five minutes of true focus plus five-minute break. Twenty-five minutes is short enough that your brain agrees to start. Starting is ninety percent of the battle.

Step Three: Define done before you start. Not work on presentation, but build slides three through six. Concrete, finishable, obvious. Vague tasks are focus-killers because the brain keeps re-deciding what it's doing.

Step Four: Protect the container. Phone in another room, notifications off, one tab. Tell people around you: I'm heads-down until ten-thirty. A protected block isn't selfish; it's the only way the important stuff gets done.

Step Five: Expect to get distracted inside the block. Your mind will wander. That's not failure — that's literally what brains do. The skill isn't never wandering; it's noticing you wandered and gently coming back. Every return is a rep.

Stop trying to focus all day. Start protecting one real block with one finishable target and one closed door. Twenty-five honest minutes.
