Welcome to fifteen minute kitchen. Today we are making dessert, and I want to say right upfront: we are making real dessert. Not a piece of fruit with a drizzle of honey over it. We are making a proper, warm, impressive chocolate lava cake — the kind with a molten centre — in under fifteen minutes, in a mug, in the microwave. And then we are making a salted caramel banana pan sauce that takes five minutes and tastes like something from a restaurant kitchen.

Let's start with the lava mug cake because it needs about two minutes in the microwave and then a minute to rest, and the whole thing from mixing bowl to eating is about six minutes.

Here is your grab list for the lava cake. Four tablespoons of plain flour. Four tablespoons of sugar. Two tablespoons of cocoa powder. One egg. Three tablespoons of milk. Three tablespoons of vegetable oil. A pinch of salt. Two squares of dark chocolate, or a tablespoon of chocolate chips. A large microwave-safe mug.

Into the mug, mix together the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, and salt with a fork. Add the egg, milk, and oil and stir until the batter is smooth — about thirty seconds of mixing. Then push the chocolate squares or chips into the centre of the batter, right into the middle, and push batter back over the top so they are completely buried. That is your lava.

Microwave on high for sixty to ninety seconds. Start checking at sixty seconds. You want the cake to look set around the edges and on top but still look very slightly underdone in the absolute centre. If it is still completely liquid in the middle, give it another ten seconds. Pull it out and let it sit for one minute — the centre will finish cooking from the residual heat, and the chocolate buried inside will stay molten.

Eat it straight from the mug, or tip it out onto a plate if you want to be fancy.

Now the salted caramel banana — which is honestly its own dessert but also works perfectly alongside the lava cake if you want to do both.

Extra grab list: two bananas, one tablespoon of butter, two tablespoons of brown sugar, a pinch of sea salt, and a splash of cream or coconut cream.

Into a wide pan over medium heat, melt the butter. Add the brown sugar and stir — it will dissolve into the butter and start to caramelise in about thirty seconds. Slice your bananas in half lengthways and place them cut side down into the caramel. Cook for about two minutes without touching them. Flip them gently. Add the splash of cream — it will bubble and spit, that is normal, stir it in. Add your pinch of sea salt. Cook for another minute until the sauce is glossy and coating the bananas. That is it.

Serve with vanilla ice cream, or pile them into a bowl next to the lava cake.

The technique tip for the lava cake: timing varies by microwave wattage, so the first time you make this you should check at fifty seconds and go from there. Overcooked by even thirty seconds and the centre sets completely — it becomes a perfectly good chocolate mug cake but you lose the lava. The margin is small, so stay close.

Both recipes are in the show notes with timing notes for different microwave wattages and a dairy-free swap for the cream in the caramel banana.
