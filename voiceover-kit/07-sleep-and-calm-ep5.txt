# PODCAST #07: SLEEP & CALM — Episode 5
**Voice to use:** Sarah or Charlotte (Soft, slow, soothing)
**Episode 1 voice used:** [Check your EP1 recording]

---

Hello, I'm Mark Coggins, and this is Sleep and Calm. Let the day settle behind you now. You've done enough. Now it's time to be warm, and still, and quiet.

Breathe in slowly, and let it go. And again. Let each breath be a little slower than the last.

Tonight, we are going for a slow walk beside the sea, in the last soft light of evening. You don't have to picture every detail. Just let yourself drift into the calm of it.

You are standing on a wide, empty beach. The sand is cool and smooth beneath your bare feet, firm and damp where the water has been. The light is low and golden, fading slowly toward soft grey and blue.

And there is the sea. Wide and calm, stretching out as far as you can see. The waves come in slowly, gently, one after another. They rise, and curl, and spill onto the sand in a long, soft hush. And then they slide away again. In. And out. Slow, and endless, and calm.

You begin to walk, slowly, along the edge of the water. There is no hurry. Each step sinks a little into the cool sand. The water reaches up to meet your feet, warm and gentle, and then slips away again, back into the sea.

Listen to the waves. That long, soft sound. The rush of water rolling in. The hush as it spreads thin across the sand. And the quiet pull as it slides back out. Over and over. Slow as breathing. In. And out.

The air is cool and soft against your skin, and it smells of salt and clean water. A gentle breeze moves past you, and is gone. Far out, the sea and the sky meet in a soft, blurred line, glowing faintly in the last of the light.

You walk on, slow and easy, and your breathing falls into the rhythm of the waves. In with the water rolling up the sand. Out with the water sliding away. Slower now. Softer now. Matching the sea.

There is no one else here. Just you, and the wide calm sea, and the long empty beach, and the soft fading light. There is nowhere you need to be. Nothing waiting. Only this slow walk, and the gentle hush of the waves.

The light is going now, soft and slow. The first faint stars are coming out, high above the water. The sea turns silver, then grey, then dark, and still the waves come in, gentle and endless, washing the day away.

You feel calm here. So calm. With every wave that rolls in and slides away, you let a little more go. The waves take it out to sea. There is less and less to hold.

You don't have to keep walking. You can stop here, at the edge of the water, and just stand, and breathe, and listen. The waves will keep coming in, soft and slow, whether you listen or not, all through the night.

In. And out. The hush of the water. The cool of the sand. The wide dark sea, rolling gently, on and on.

You are growing heavy now. Your eyes are heavy. Your breathing is slow and deep, all on its own, in time with the sea.

There is nothing to do. Nowhere to be. The sea will keep its slow rhythm all night long.

Rest now. Let the waves carry the day away. Sleep well. I'll see you again, when you're ready.
