# jamie's Podcast — 26 Episodes from Dad's Code
## Personal Memoir Podcast: Fatherhood, ADHD, Resilience & Presence

Your personal podcast drawing from "Dad's Code" — your memoir about fatherhood, ADHD, anxiety, resilience, discipline, family, coaching, dancing, chaos, and learning that being present matters more than being perfect.

---

## Episode 1: Season Premiere — Welcome to Dad's Code
**Title:** Season Premiere — Welcome to Dad's Code
**Description:** Welcome to jamie's Podcast, where fatherhood meets real life. In this premiere, I introduce "Dad's Code"—my personal memoir about becoming a father, navigating ADHD, managing anxiety, and learning that presence matters infinitely more than perfection. Over the next 26 episodes, you'll hear stories from my journey: the joy and chaos of raising kids, the challenges of managing ADHD as a parent, the vulnerability of admitting struggle, and the resilience that comes from showing up, even when you're not sure how. This podcast is for fathers, for parents, for anyone navigating the beautiful mess of family. Welcome.

---

## Episode 2: Becoming Dad — The Moment Everything Changed
**Title:** Becoming Dad — The Moment Everything Changed
**Description:** The first time I held my daughter, my world shifted permanently. In this episode, I share the moment I became a dad—the excitement, the fear, the overwhelming responsibility, and the love that changed everything. Becoming a father isn't something you prepare for; it's something you experience and learn from. I explore what that first moment taught me about what matters, what doesn't, and why the pressure to be the "perfect dad" was actually the biggest obstacle to becoming a good one.

---

## Episode 3: Twins and Chaos — Doubling Down
**Title:** Twins and Chaos — Doubling Down
**Description:** Finding out we were having twins completely flipped my world upside down. In this episode, I talk about the moment we discovered twins were coming, the panic that followed, and the beautiful chaos that ensued. Parenting became nappies, bottles, sleepless nights, and unforgettable memories—multiplied by two. I share what I learned about resilience, support, and the fact that perfect parenting isn't possible, but present parenting absolutely is. Twins taught me more about my own limits than anything else ever has.

---

## Episode 4: ADHD and Fatherhood — Understanding Myself
**Title:** ADHD and Fatherhood — Understanding Myself
**Description:** ADHD doesn't disappear when you become a parent. In this episode, I explore how ADHD shaped my parenting journey—the challenges with organization, the struggles with routine, the constant feeling of being scattered even when trying to be present. But I also share how understanding my own ADHD helped me understand my kids better, how it made me more compassionate, and how it taught me that "different" doesn't mean broken. Managing ADHD as a parent isn't about being fixed; it's about being aware.

---

## Episode 5: Anxiety and Parenting — Learning to Sit With Fear
**Title:** Anxiety and Parenting — Learning to Sit With Fear
**Description:** Becoming a parent amplified my anxiety in ways I didn't expect. In this episode, I talk about the constant "what ifs," the catastrophizing, the pressure I put on myself to prevent anything bad from happening. I share the turning point: realizing that my anxiety was actually getting in the way of being the present father I wanted to be. I explore how I learned to acknowledge fear without letting it drive my parenting, and how that shift changed everything.

---

## Episode 6: Discipline Without Damage — Finding the Balance
**Title:** Discipline Without Damage — Finding the Balance
**Description:** How do you discipline kids without crushing their spirit? In this episode, I explore the difference between punishment and discipline, between controlling kids and teaching them. I share mistakes I made, lessons I learned the hard way, and the realization that discipline is actually an act of love when done with intention and compassion. Discipline isn't about being strict; it's about being consistent and clear while protecting the relationship.

---

## Episode 7: The Coaching Mindset — Teaching Through Connection
**Title:** The Coaching Mindset — Teaching Through Connection
**Description:** My background in coaching fundamentally changed how I approach parenting. In this episode, I explore what coaching taught me about asking questions instead of giving orders, about listening more than speaking, about helping people (including kids) discover their own answers. When you coach instead of control, you're teaching kids to think, problem-solve, and become independent. It's slower than commanding compliance, but it builds real capability.

---

## Episode 8: Dancing Through Chaos — Finding Joy in the Mess
**Title:** Dancing Through Chaos — Finding Joy in the Mess
**Description:** In the middle of chaos—screaming kids, spilled milk, missed deadlines—I learned to dance. In this episode, I talk about finding moments of lightness and joy in the beautiful mess of family life. Dancing, literally and figuratively, became my way of shifting perspective when everything felt overwhelming. I share how humor, play, and intentional moments of joy became survival tools and bonding experiences.

---

## Episode 9: The Real Cost of Perfectionism — Letting Go
**Title:** The Real Cost of Perfectionism — Letting Go
**Description:** Perfectionism steals presence. In this episode, I explore how my pursuit of being the "perfect dad" was actually making me a worse one. I was so focused on doing everything right that I missed the moments unfolding in front of me. I share the journey of letting perfectionism go—the relief, the guilt that came with it, and the realization that good enough is genuinely good enough when it's real and present.

## Episode 10: Resilience as a Skill — Not Just Survival
**Title:** Resilience as a Skill — Not Just Survival
**Description:** Resilience isn't something you're born with; it's something you practice. In this episode, I talk about what resilience actually means, how it's different from just "toughing it out," and how I've watched resilience develop in my own kids. I share moments when I thought everything was breaking, and how those moments became opportunities to model resilience, to show my kids that struggle doesn't mean failure.

## Episode 11: Presence Over Performance — The Real Win
**Title:** Presence Over Performance — The Real Win
**Description:** Being present isn't about performing perfectly. In this episode, I explore what true presence actually looks like—being there physically, emotionally, and mentally, even when you're tired, even when you mess up, even when you're not sure what you're doing. I share moments when I got it right, and more importantly, moments when I got it wrong and what I learned. Presence is the foundation of everything that matters in parenting.

## Episode 12: The Vulnerability of Admitting Struggle — Strength Through Honesty
**Title:** The Vulnerability of Admitting Struggle — Strength Through Honesty
**Description:** Admitting you're struggling as a parent takes courage. In this episode, I talk about the shame I felt initially—admitting that parenting was harder than I expected, that I sometimes wanted to escape, that I wasn't naturally good at this. ## Episode 13: Sleep Deprivation and Sanity — Surviving the Impossible
**Title:** Sleep Deprivation and Sanity — Surviving the Impossible
**Description:** When you're a parent of twins with ADHD and anxiety, sleep becomes a luxury. I share strategies that worked, the ones that didn't, and the reality that sometimes you just survive, and that's enough.

## Episode 14: Support Systems — You Can't Do This Alone
**Title:** Support Systems — You Can't Do This Alone
