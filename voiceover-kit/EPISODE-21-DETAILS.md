## Episode 21: The Counterargument

**Description:**
Certainty is fragile. Episode 21 deliberately examines the strongest counterarguments to popular positions. By understanding what smart people on the other side believe, we develop more robust thinking. Intellectual honesty means grappling with opposing views seriously. In this episode, we explore the most compelling objections to widely-held beliefs, not to be contrarian, but to strengthen our own thinking through opposition. The best ideas survive scrutiny.
