Welcome to Calm Mind. Find a comfortable place to rest. Let your eyes close if that feels safe and natural.

Today we are going to do a body scan meditation together. A slow, gentle journey through the body from head to toe. There is nothing you need to fix or change. You are simply being invited to notice.

Begin by taking three full breaths to settle in.

In through the nose. Long and slow. And out through the mouth, completely.

Again. In. And all the way out.

One more. In. And out.

Now let your breathing return to whatever is natural. Simply observe it without directing it.

We will begin at the top of the head. Bring your attention there. The crown of your skull, the scalp. Notice any sensation — warmth, tingling, tightness, or simply nothing at all. All of it is welcome. You are not looking for relaxation. You are looking for awareness.

Now move your attention to the forehead. Notice the space between your eyebrows. Is there tension there? If so, you do not need to push it away. Simply observe it, as if you were looking at a cloud.

The eyes, lightly closed. The space around them. Let them soften.

Move down to the jaw. This is a place where so many of us hold without realising. Let the teeth part just slightly. Let the tongue rest softly in the mouth. No gripping.

Now the throat. Notice if there is anything there. Tightness. A held quality. Breathe into it gently and let the exhale carry it outward.

The shoulders. Without pushing them down, simply notice where they are. Let them be heavy.

Move your attention down the arms. The upper arms, the elbows, the forearms. The wrists. The hands. Notice the palms, the fingers, the very tips. Warmth, perhaps. Or a faint pulse.

Now bring your awareness to the chest. Place your attention here and feel the rise and fall of your breathing. Notice the ribs expanding with each inhale. Feel the gentle settling of each exhale.

The heart is here. Whatever you have been feeling lately, it lives in this general neighbourhood. You do not need to do anything with that. Just acknowledge it.

Move to the belly. With each exhale, feel it soften. You do not need to hold it in. Just let it be.

The lower back. If there is tension here, breathe into it. Not to fix it. Just to meet it.

Down through the hips and pelvis. The tops of the legs. The thighs. Feel the weight of them where they rest.

The knees. Behind the knees. Down through the calves to the ankles.

And finally the feet. The heels resting on the floor or the bed. The arches. The balls of the feet. The toes — all five on each foot.

You are fully here. From the crown of your head to the soles of your feet, you are present in your body. This body that carries you through every day, that does so much without being asked, that holds everything you feel.

Let it rest for a moment. Just as it is.

Take one deep breath in. From the top of your head all the way down through your body. And release it slowly, completely.

When you are ready, gently bring your awareness back to the room. Open your eyes softly.

Carry this sense of your own presence with you.
