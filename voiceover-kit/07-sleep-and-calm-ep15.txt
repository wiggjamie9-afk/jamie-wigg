# PODCAST #07: SLEEP & CALM — Episode 15
**Voice to use:** Sarah or Charlotte (Soft, slow, soothing)
**Episode 1 voice used:** [Check your EP1 recording]

---

Hello, I'm Mark Coggins, and this is Sleep and Calm. Let the day settle behind you now. You've done enough. There is nothing left to carry. Just be warm, and still, and quiet.

Take a slow breath in, and let it fall away. And again. Let this next breath be a little slower than the last.

Tonight we are going to count down, slowly, all the way to sleep. A soft and gentle counting, each number a little step further down into the quiet. You don't have to try. Just let each number carry you lower.

You are lying somewhere warm and safe. A soft bed beneath you. A blanket over you, heavy and kind. The room is dim and still. There is nothing left to do now but drift, one slow step at a time.

Picture a long, soft staircase, carpeted and warm, leading gently down into a deep and quiet calm. With each number, you will sink one step lower, a little heavier, a little softer, a little closer to sleep.

Ten. Let your shoulders drop. The first slow step down. Feel yourself begin to settle into the warm.

Nine. A little heavier now. Your breathing slows. Your hands grow soft and still.

Eight. Down another gentle step. The room grows dimmer and warmer around you.

Seven. Your legs grow heavy. They sink down into the bed. Lower, and softer, and slower.

Six. Let your jaw soften. Let your face grow smooth and still. Halfway down now, and so much calmer.

Five. Heavier still. Each breath slower than the last. There is nothing to hold. Nothing to do.

Four. Down another quiet step. The world grows faint and far away. Only the warmth remains.

Three. So heavy now. So soft. Sinking gently into the deep, still calm.

Two. Almost all the way down. Your whole body warm, and held, and at rest.

One. The last soft step. Down into the warm and the quiet and the dark. Let go now, all the way.

You don't need to count any more. You don't need to hold on to anything. The quiet will carry you the rest of the way, soft and gentle, all night long.

There is nothing to do. Nowhere to be. Just rest now, all the way down, into the warm and the still and the deep, soft dark.

Rest now. Sleep well. I'll see you again, when you're ready.
