# PODCAST #07: SLEEP & CALM — Episode 6
**Voice to use:** Sarah or Charlotte (Soft, slow, soothing)
**Episode 1 voice used:** [Check your EP1 recording]

---

Hello, I'm Mark Coggins, and this is Sleep and Calm. Let the day settle behind you now. You've done enough. Now it's time to be warm, and still, and quiet.

Tonight, we are simply going to breathe, slowly and gently, and let the breathing carry you down into sleep. You don't have to do anything else. You don't have to try. Just follow my voice, and let each breath grow softer than the last.

So settle in. Feel the weight of your body against the bed. Feel yourself sinking down, soft and heavy, held by the dark.

Let's begin. Breathe in, slowly, through your nose. Gently. Filling up, soft and easy. And hold, just for a moment. And now let it go, slowly, out through your mouth, a long, soft breath, like a quiet sigh.

And again. Breathe in, slow, and full, and soft. Hold it, gently. And let it go, slowly, all the way out. Feel your shoulders drop a little as you breathe out. Feel yourself sink a little deeper.

There's no need to force it. Just let the breath be slow, and soft, and easy. In, through the nose. A gentle pause. And out, through the mouth, long and slow.

Breathe in. Two. Three. Four. And hold. And out. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six. Let the out-breath be longer than the in. That long, slow breath out is what carries you down into rest.

Again. In, slowly. Two. Three. Four. Soft pause. And out, longer now. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six. Feel your body grow heavier with each breath out. Sinking. Softening.

You don't have to count if you don't want to. You can just let the breathing happen, slow and gentle, on its own. In, and out. Soft, and slow. Each breath a little quieter than the one before.

With every breath in, a little calm comes in. With every breath out, a little of the day goes out. In comes the quiet. Out goes whatever you've been holding. Slowly. Gently. Breath after breath.

Your face is soft now. Your jaw is loose. Your shoulders have dropped away from your ears. Your hands are warm and heavy. And still you breathe, slow and easy, in, and out.

If your mind wanders off, that's alright. That's what minds do. Just notice it, gently, and come back to the breath. Back to the slow rise and fall. In. And out. Nothing to do but breathe.

Slower now. The breath is finding its own gentle rhythm. You don't even have to guide it anymore. Just let it be. In, soft and slow. Out, long and soft. Carrying you down.

You are warm. You are safe. You are held. There is nowhere to be and nothing to do but rest here, breathing softly, sinking down.

Each breath out, a little deeper into sleep. Each breath out, a little farther into the warm and the dark.

Let the breath carry you the rest of the way now. In. And out. Slow. And soft. And far.

Rest now. Sleep well. I'll see you again, when you're ready.
