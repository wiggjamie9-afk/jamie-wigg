# STARLIGHTMIX Audio Network — Show Descriptions

Five-sentence show description for each of the 20 podcasts. Paste each into the
**Podcast Description** field in Buzzsprout (Account → Podcast → Details). Cover
art for each show lives in `covers/NN-<slug>.jpg` (3000×3000, Apple/Spotify spec).

Every episode closes with the STARLIGHTMIX Studio call-to-action
(`studio.starlightmix.com`).

## 01 — True Crime Brief
*Category: True Crime · Cover: `covers/01-true-crime-brief.jpg`*

True Crime Brief takes one real case and tells the whole gripping story in about twelve minutes. No padding, no rambling co-hosts — just the facts, the timeline, and the human drama, delivered with the pace of a great thriller. Every episode walks you from the first clue to the final verdict so you finish knowing exactly what happened and why it matters. It's built for the commute, the gym, or the dog walk, when you want a complete story rather than a cliffhanger. New cases land every week for listeners who love the genre but hate the filler.
