# PODCAST #07: SLEEP & CALM — Episode 2
**Voice to use:** Sarah or Charlotte (Soft, slow, soothing)
**Episode 1 voice used:** [Check your EP1 recording]

---

Welcome back. Let the day settle behind you. You've done enough. Now it's time to be warm, and still, and quiet.

Breathe in slowly and let it go. And again. Let each breath be a little slower than the last.

Tonight we are going to a small cabin in the snow. Warm inside, white and soft outside. You don't have to picture every detail. Just let yourself sink into the warmth of it.

You are inside a small wooden cabin. The walls are honey-coloured pine, glowing in the light of a low fire. The fire is gentle — not roaring, just a soft, steady flicker. Orange light moves slowly across the ceiling.

You are wrapped in a heavy blanket, deep in a soft chair by the window. Your feet are warm. Your hands are warm. The blanket is heavy in the best way — it holds you, gently.

Outside, it is snowing. Slow, fat flakes, drifting down without a sound. They turn and tumble in the still air, catching the firelight in the window. Down they come. Soft. Endless. Silent.

The world out there is white and hushed. The trees wear thick coats of snow. The ground is smooth and untouched. No footprints. No sound but the soft hush of falling snow, and the gentle tick of the fire.

The fire crackles softly. A small, warm sound. A log settles. A few orange sparks rise and fade. The warmth reaches you in slow waves.

You sink a little deeper into the chair. Your shoulders loosen. Your jaw softens. Your breathing slows to match the slow fall of the snow.

Watch the flakes drift down past the window. One. And another. Turning slowly in the dark. Lit gold for a moment, then gone into the white.

There is nowhere to go tonight. Nothing waiting. The snow has closed the world down to this one warm room. And that is exactly enough.

A kettle somewhere ticks as it cools. The blanket is heavy and soft. The fire glows low and steady. The snow keeps falling, slow and silent, covering everything in soft white.

You are so warm. So safe. So held.

Your eyes are heavy now. The firelight blurs gently. The window is a soft grey square, full of slow-drifting snow.

You don't need to stay awake for the snow. It will keep falling, soft and quiet, all night long.

The fire will keep you warm. The blanket will hold you. There is nothing left to do but drift.

Rest now. Let the snow tuck the whole world in.
