# PODCAST #07: SLEEP & CALM — Episode 9
**Voice to use:** Sarah or Charlotte (Soft, slow, soothing)
**Episode 1 voice used:** [Check your EP1 recording]

---

Hello, I'm Mark Coggins, and this is Sleep and Calm. Let the day settle behind you now. You've done enough. Now it's time to be warm, and still, and quiet.

Breathe in slowly, and let it go. And again. Let each breath be a little slower than the last.

Tonight, perhaps your mind is busy. Perhaps it keeps turning, going over things, racing on ahead. That's alright. That happens to all of us. Tonight, we are going to let the racing mind grow slow, and quiet, and still, gently, with no force at all.

First, let's not fight it. If your mind is busy, that's alright. You don't have to make the thoughts stop. Fighting them only wakes you more. Instead, we'll just let them slow down on their own, the way a spinning wheel slows when you stop pushing it. Round, and round, but slower. And slower.

Imagine your busy thoughts are like water in a glass that's been stirred, swirling round and round, cloudy and quick. You don't have to do anything to it. You only have to set it down, and be still, and wait. And slowly, all on its own, the swirling settles. The water grows calm. And clear. And still.

So let's be still together. Feel the weight of your body on the bed. Feel where you touch the mattress, heavy and warm. That's something real, and quiet, and slow. Just feel it. The weight. The warmth. The stillness of your body, here, in the dark.

Now listen, for a moment, to the quiet around you. The soft sounds of the night. The hush of the room. Maybe a far, gentle sound, somewhere outside. You don't have to name the sounds. Just let them be there, soft and far, holding the quiet open.

And when a thought comes, and it will, you don't have to chase it, and you don't have to push it away. Just notice it, kindly, the way you'd notice a cloud drifting across the sky. There it is. And there it goes. Drifting past, slow and quiet, and gone. And the calm sky stays behind it.

Another thought comes. That's fine. Let it drift by too. You are not your thoughts. You are the calm, wide sky, and the thoughts are only clouds, passing through. They come, and they go, and you stay, still and quiet beneath them.

Each time you notice you've drifted off into thinking, that's good. That noticing is the calm itself. Gently, with no frustration, come back. Back to the weight of your body. Back to your slow, soft breathing. Back to the quiet of the night.

The thoughts are slower now. There is more space between them. The swirling water is settling, growing clear. The wheel is slowing, round, and round, and slower still.

There is nothing you need to think about tonight. Nothing to solve. Nothing to plan. The night will hold all of it for you, safe, until morning. For now, you can set the thinking down, and simply rest.

Slower now. Quieter now. The mind growing soft, and calm, and still, like water gone clear, like a calm wide sky.

You are warm. You are safe. You are held. There is nothing to do but rest, here, in the quiet.

Let your thoughts grow soft and far now, and let yourself sink down, into the warm and the still and the dark.

Rest now. Let the quiet settle over you. Sleep well. I'll see you again, when you're ready.
