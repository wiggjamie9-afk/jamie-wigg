# PODCAST #07: SLEEP & CALM — Episode 10
**Voice to use:** Sarah or Charlotte (Soft, slow, soothing)
**Episode 1 voice used:** [Check your EP1 recording]

---

Hello, I'm Mark Coggins, and this is Sleep and Calm. Let the day settle behind you now. You've done enough. There is nothing left to carry. Just be warm, and still, and quiet.

Take a slow breath in, and let it fall away. And again. Let this next breath be a little slower than the last.

Tonight we are going to a small cabin high on a mountain, while soft rain falls on the roof. You don't have to picture every detail. Just let the warmth of it gather around you.

You are inside a small cabin near the top of a quiet mountain. The walls are warm timber, dark and smooth. A single low lamp glows in the corner, soft and amber. Everything in here is gentle and dim.

Outside, it has begun to rain. Not a storm. Just a slow, steady rain, falling soft and even on the roof above you. A quiet, rolling hush, like the mountain breathing. You listen to it move across the timber. Soft. Steady. Endless.

You are wrapped in a thick blanket on a wide, low bed. The blanket is heavy in the kindest way. It holds you. Your feet are warm. Your hands are warm. The pillow is deep and soft beneath your head.

The rain finds its rhythm. It runs in slow threads down the dark window, catching the lamplight, sliding gently away. You watch one thread, then another, drifting down the glass without a sound.

Far below, the valley is lost in soft grey cloud. There is nothing to see and nowhere to go. The rain has closed the whole world down to this one warm room. And that is exactly enough.

The roof keeps you dry. The blanket keeps you held. The lamp glows low and steady in the corner. The rain keeps falling, soft and even, all across the mountain.

Your breathing slows to match the rain. In, and out. Slow and even. Your shoulders loosen. Your jaw softens. You sink a little deeper into the warm.

You don't need to listen for every drop. The rain will keep falling, soft and quiet, all night long, whether you follow it or not.

There is nothing to do. Nowhere to be. Just rest now, all the way down, into the warm and the dark and the soft sound of the rain.

Rest now. Sleep well. I'll see you again, when you're ready.
