Welcome back to Side Hustle School. Yesterday we built a store. Today we go after the highest-margin product any human being can sell: knowledge packaged as a course or a paid workshop. No cost of goods. No shipping. No supplier. You know something useful, and people pay you to learn it from you directly.

Let me address the voice in your head right now that says I am not an expert. Here is the truth: you do not need to be the world's leading authority. You only need to be a few steps ahead of the person you are teaching. A first-year nurse can teach a nursing student. Someone who paid off forty thousand dollars of debt can teach the person who just started the process. Someone who went from zero to five thousand monthly listeners on their podcast can teach a beginner with zero. The gap between you and your audience is your product.

There are two main formats for beginners. The live workshop and the recorded self-paced course. Start with the live workshop. Here is why: a live workshop requires almost no production. You show up on Zoom for sixty to ninety minutes, you teach, people ask questions, and you charge for the seat. No video editing. No fancy platform. A free Zoom account and a PayPal or Stripe link is all you need to run your first one.

Step one. Choose one specific outcome your workshop delivers. Not a general topic like productivity. A specific before-and-after: by the end of this session you will have your first Etsy product listed and live. Or: you will leave with a complete ninety-day content calendar for your business. The more specific the outcome, the easier it is to sell.

Step two. Price it at a point where you only need a small audience to earn real money. A forty-nine dollar workshop needs just twenty people to make nearly one thousand dollars from a single hour of your time. A ninety-seven dollar workshop needs eleven people. You are not selling to thousands. You are selling to a small room of people who care enough to pay.

Step three. Fill the room through your existing network, not through advertising. Post in three communities where your target audience already gathers. Write a post that describes the pain point your workshop solves, and invite people to join the waitlist. Direct messages to people you already know in that world will fill the first workshop faster than any paid ad.

Step four. Run the live workshop, deliver more value than people expected, and record it. That recording becomes your self-paced product. You now have a video course you can sell forever at a lower price point, twenty-seven to forty-seven dollars, without running another live session unless you want to.

Step five. Collect testimonials from every attendee. Before the session ends, ask people to share one thing they learned and one thing they will do differently. Screenshot those. Those words become your marketing for the next workshop. Social proof from real people is the most powerful sales tool you have.

Let me make the math clear. If you run one forty-nine dollar workshop per month with twenty attendees, that is just under one thousand dollars per month for roughly two hours of actual delivery time, plus a few hours of prep and promotion. Run it quarterly with sixty people and you are making three thousand dollars in a weekend. Add a self-paced recording at thirty-seven dollars and it earns in the background all month. This is how solo educators build six-figure income from a laptop.

Your assignment: identify one thing you know that would save someone else time, money, or stress. Write a one-paragraph description of the outcome. Set a date four weeks from today, pick a price between thirty-seven and ninety-seven dollars, and tell ten people this week. You do not need a platform, a logo, or a course page. You need a Zoom link and a payment link.

These are projections, not promises. Results depend on your audience, your topic, and your follow-through. What I know for certain is that the workshop you run beats the course you plan forever.
