# PODCAST #07: SLEEP & CALM — Episode 8
**Voice to use:** Sarah or Charlotte (Soft, slow, soothing)
**Episode 1 voice used:** [Check your EP1 recording]

---

Hello, I'm Mark Coggins, and this is Sleep and Calm. Let the day settle behind you now. You've done enough. Now it's time to be warm, and still, and quiet.

Breathe in slowly, and let it go. And again. Let each breath be a little slower than the last.

Tonight, we are going to walk into a deep, quiet forest, in the soft hour just before dark. You don't have to picture every detail. Just let yourself drift into the green hush of it.

You are standing at the edge of the trees, where a soft path leads in. The ground is covered in moss and fallen leaves, deep and springy beneath your feet. Each step is gentle and silent, soft as walking on a thick green blanket.

You step in, under the tall trees, and the forest folds quietly around you. The light here is soft and green, falling in slow shafts through the leaves high above. It is cool, and calm, and very still. The air smells of moss, and earth, and clean green growing things.

You walk slowly along the path, in no hurry at all. The great trees rise all around you, tall and old and patient. They have stood here for a hundred years, calm and unhurried, and they make you feel calm too. There is no rush in a forest. Only slow, quiet growing.

High above, the leaves move gently in a breeze you can barely feel. They make a soft, far sound, a slow green whisper, rising and falling, like the forest breathing. You don't have to listen closely. Just let it be there, all around you, soft and endless.

Somewhere, far off, a bird calls once, soft and sweet, and then the quiet returns. And somewhere nearby, there is the gentle trickle of a small stream, running slow over smooth stones, a quiet, silvery sound, somewhere just out of sight.

You come to a soft, mossy clearing, and there is a place to rest. A bed of deep moss at the foot of an old tree, soft and cool and inviting. You lie down, and the moss holds you, gentle and giving, like the softest cushion. The great tree is at your back, old and steady and sure.

You look up, through the branches, at the last of the soft light fading in the leaves. Green, and gold, and slowly turning to grey. The forest grows dim and quiet around you, settling down for the night, the same as you.

It is so peaceful here. So calm. The slow whisper of the leaves. The far trickle of the stream. The cool green air, and the soft moss holding you, and the old tree at your back. There is nowhere to be. Nothing waiting. Only this deep, quiet rest.

Your breathing slows to match the breathing of the forest. Slow in. Slow out. Soft as the wind in the high leaves. There is nothing to do but lie here, and rest, and let the forest keep its quiet watch.

You are growing heavy now. The moss holds you. The tree shelters you. The leaves whisper you down, soft and slow, into rest.

You don't have to stay awake. The forest will keep its slow, green hush all night long, whether you hear it or not.

Rest now. Let the quiet forest hold you. Sleep well. I'll see you again, when you're ready.
