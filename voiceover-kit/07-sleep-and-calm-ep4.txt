# PODCAST #07: SLEEP & CALM — Episode 4
**Voice to use:** Sarah or Charlotte (Soft, slow, soothing)
**Episode 1 voice used:** [Check your EP1 recording]

---

Hello, I'm Mark Coggins, and this is Sleep and Calm. Let the day settle behind you now. You've done enough. There is nothing left to carry. Just be warm, and still, and quiet.

Breathe in slowly, and let it go. And again. Let each breath be a little softer than the last.

Tonight, we are going to rest the body, piece by piece, the way you might lower yourself into warm water. You don't have to try. You only have to listen, and let go.

Start with your feet. Just bring your gentle attention down to your feet, resting heavy at the end of the bed. Let them grow warm. Let them grow soft. Let them sink down, all on their own.

Now your ankles, and your lower legs. Feel them growing heavy. Feel the weight of them settling into the bed beneath you. There is nothing for them to do now. Nothing to hold. So let them rest.

Your knees soften. The long muscles of your legs let go, one at a time, slow and warm. Your legs are heavy now. So heavy. Sinking down into the soft.

Let the warmth move up, slowly, into your hips, and the small of your back. Feel that part of you settle. Feel it spread out, and grow soft, and let go of the day. There is nowhere to be. Nothing to hold up.

Your stomach softens, rising and falling with each slow breath. Your chest grows quiet and easy. Each breath comes a little slower than the last, gentle as the tide going out.

Now your hands. Let your fingers uncurl. Let your hands grow warm and heavy, resting just where they are. Let your arms grow heavy too, sinking down, all the weight of them drifting into the bed.

Your shoulders. Let them drop, away from your ears, down and back, soft and loose. So much is carried in the shoulders. Let all of it go now. Let them sink.

Your neck softens. Your jaw loosens, your teeth a little apart, your tongue resting soft in your mouth. The space between your eyebrows smooths and opens. Even your face is resting now.

And all of you is warm, and heavy, and still. Held by the bed. Held by the dark. There is nothing left to do but breathe, slow and soft.

If a thought drifts by, you don't have to chase it. Let it float past, like a leaf on slow water, and come back to the warmth of your body, sinking down.

Heavier now. Softer now. Each breath a little slower. Each part of you a little more at rest.

You are so warm. So safe. So held.

There is nothing to do. Nowhere to be. Just rest now, all the way down, into the warm and the quiet and the dark.

Rest now. Sleep well. I'll see you again, when you're ready.
