# PODCAST #04: SIDE HUSTLE SCHOOL — Episode 15
**Voice to use:** Adam or Brian (Upbeat male coach)
**Episode 1 voice used:** Wade

Welcome back to Side Hustle School. This is Adam. Yesterday we put machines to work earning while we slept. Today we end on one of my favorite long-game hustles, one that needs no inventory, no clients knocking on your door, and almost no money to start. It is just you, an email, and a small group of people who care about the same thing you do. We're talking niche newsletters. You write a simple email, you build an audience one reader at a time, and eventually that list becomes a quiet, growing paycheck.

Let me set the expectation honestly right away, because this is the slowest hustle in the series and also one of the most durable. You will not make real money in week one. What you are building is an asset: a list of people who trust you and open your emails. That asset compounds. A list of a few thousand engaged readers in the right niche can earn for years, and unlike a job, it grows even when you sleep, because the back catalog keeps pulling in new subscribers.

Here is the key that beginners miss. The word niche is doing all the work. A newsletter about everything is a newsletter for nobody. A newsletter for left-handed guitar players, or for parents of toddlers in your city, or for people restoring old campervans, or for indie coffee roasters, that is something. The narrower and more specific your audience, the easier it is to grow, and the more an advertiser will pay to reach them. Small and specific beats big and vague every time.

Step one. Pick a niche where you have either genuine interest or real knowledge, and where the audience already spends money. Hobbies with gear, professionals who buy tools, parents, pet owners, local communities. You want a group that is passionate and that companies already want to advertise to. Your own curiosity is what keeps you writing every week, so do not pick a topic you secretly find boring.

Step two. Use a free tool built exactly for this. Platforms like beehiiv or Substack let you start a newsletter for free, collect subscribers, and send emails without any technical setup. Pick one, name your newsletter clearly so people instantly know who it is for, and you are publishing the same day.

Step three. Commit to one consistent format, and ship it on schedule. Maybe it is the five best links in your niche every week. Maybe one short useful tip plus a deal you found. Consistency is the entire product. A reliable, useful weekly email is worth far more than a brilliant one that shows up once and disappears. Pick a day, and never miss it.

Step four. Grow the list patiently, one trustworthy reader at a time. Share each issue in the online communities where your niche already gathers, the subreddits, the Facebook groups, the forums, but share by being genuinely useful, not by spamming. Add a line at the bottom of every issue asking happy readers to forward it to one friend. Slow and real beats fast and fake.

Now, how it actually pays, once the list is real. The main path is sponsorships: a company pays to put a short ad in your email. A common rough rule is around twenty to forty dollars for every thousand subscribers who open an issue. So a newsletter with two thousand engaged readers can charge roughly forty to eighty dollars per sponsored slot, and a five thousand reader list, a few hundred dollars an issue. Other paths: an affiliate link to gear you recommend, or a small paid premium tier. Stack a weekly sponsor onto a five thousand person list and you are looking at four figures a month from one email.

The honest reminder, and it carries extra weight here. These are projections, not promises. This is a marathon. Your first month might earn zero dollars while you build trust, and month twelve might earn a thousand. The people who win this are the ones who keep shipping the email every single week while it is still small. You find out by publishing, not by planning.

Your assignment this week, and it is the last one in this series. Pick your niche, name your newsletter, and create it on beehiiv or Substack today. Write and send issue number one to even five people you know. The list of zero is the only one you cannot grow.

That is where we will leave the school for now. Fifteen hustles, fifteen real paths, and every one of them starts the same way: you stop planning and you ship something this week. Pick the one that made you lean in, and go begin. This is Side Hustle School. Go send your first email.
