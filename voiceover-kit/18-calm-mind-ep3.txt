Now, I want to be careful and honest here, because there's an important difference I don't want you to miss. Self-compassion is not the same as making excuses, and softening the critic does not mean you stop growing. There's a real difference between helpful self-correction and cruel self-attack.

Helpful self-correction sounds like, that didn't go the way I wanted, I care about doing better, here's what I'll try next time. It's specific. It's about the action, not your whole worth. And underneath it, there's warmth. It's the voice of someone on your side.

Cruel self-attack sounds like, you always ruin everything, you're hopeless, what is wrong with you. It's global. It's about you as a person, not the thing you did. And underneath it, there's contempt. Here's the test you can use. Helpful correction leaves you feeling motivated and steady. Cruel attack leaves you feeling small and frozen. One opens a door. The other locks it. You're allowed to hold yourself to a standard. You're just not required to be cruel to get there. In fact, the kindness works better.
