# PODCAST #07: SLEEP & CALM — Episode 7
**Voice to use:** Sarah or Charlotte (Soft, slow, soothing)
**Episode 1 voice used:** [Check your EP1 recording]

---

Hello, I'm Mark Coggins, and this is Sleep and Calm. Let the day settle behind you now. You've done enough. Now it's time to be warm, and still, and quiet.

Breathe in slowly, and let it go. And again. Let each breath be a little slower than the last.

Tonight, we are going to set the whole day down. All of it. Everything you carried today, you can lay it down now. You don't need it tonight. It will keep, if it needs to keep, until morning.

Imagine, for a moment, that the day is a heavy bag you've been carrying on your shoulder. All day long, you've held it. The things you did. The things you didn't get to. The words you said, and the ones you didn't. All of it, gathered up in that bag, growing heavier as the day went on.

And now, gently, you set it down. Right there, beside the bed, where you can't quite reach it. You don't have to throw it away. You don't have to sort through it. You just set it down, and let your shoulder rest, light and free.

Let's let the day go, one piece at a time. The thing that worried you today. You can feel how tightly you were holding it. Now, gently, loosen your grip. Just a little. Let it grow soft in your hands, and then let it float up, and away, like a balloon let go, rising slowly into the dark, getting smaller and smaller, until it's gone.

And the thing left unfinished. The task still waiting. It's alright. It will still be there tomorrow, and tomorrow you'll have fresh strength for it. For now, let it go too. Watch it drift up and away, small and quiet, into the night.

And anything you wish you'd done differently. Be gentle with yourself now. You did the best you could with the day you had. Let that be enough. Let it be enough. And let it go, softly, up into the dark.

There is nothing you need to solve tonight. Nothing you need to decide. The night is not for fixing things. The night is for resting. The morning will come, and you'll be stronger then, and clearer, and rested. For now, there is only this. Only rest.

Feel how much lighter you are now. With the day set down, your whole body can soften. Your shoulders drop. Your chest opens. Your breathing slows. There is so much less to hold.

If a worry drifts back, and one might, that's alright. You don't have to grab it. Just let it pass by, like a cloud across the moon, slow and quiet, and gone again. Back to the warmth. Back to the rest.

The day is behind you now. It is finished. It asks nothing more of you. You can let it close, gently, like a door easing shut, soft and quiet, leaving you here, warm and still in the dark.

You are warm. You are safe. You are held. There is nothing left to carry. Nothing left to do. Only rest.

Let yourself sink down now, light and free, into the warm and the quiet and the dark.

Rest now. The day is done. Sleep well. I'll see you again, when you're ready.
