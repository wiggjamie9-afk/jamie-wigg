### Episode 06: MONEY MINDSET

**Podcast Show Description:**
```
The calm, no-jargon personal finance show for people who were never taught how money works.
12 minutes. No spreadsheets you'll never open. No shame about where you're starting.
Just one clear, doable idea at a time — explained the way a patient friend would explain it.
```

**Episode 1 Title:**
```
Why We Overspend (It's Not Because You're Bad With Money)
```

**Episode Description:**
```
You want to stop impulse spending. But you keep failing. So here's the thing: it's not a willpower problem. It's a wiring problem.

Overspending is not a character flaw. It's your brain doing exactly what evolution designed it to do.

In this episode: why the dopamine hits BEFORE you buy (not after), why tapping a card feels different from handing over cash, and the three modern traps that make stopping harder than ever.

Then: the one step that actually works. It's called the 24-hour pause, and it cuts impulse spending by 30-40% without ever telling yourself "no."

Real number: $18/day of casual lunch spending = $4,680/year. Almost none of it was a decision you actually made.
```

**Show Notes / Podcast Description:**
```
Episode 1: Why We Overspend (It's Not Because You're Bad With Money)

Key Takeaways:
• Overspending is brain wiring, not weakness
• Dopamine peaks BEFORE the purchase, not after
• Cashless payments hide the "ouch" — so your brain thinks it's free
• Small automatic spending ($18/day) adds up to $4,680/year
• The 24-hour pause: put it in the cart, close the app, set a reminder. Tomorrow, the dopamine fades.

The Psychology:
Three modern traps make spending feel effortless:
1. Spending stopped feeling like spending (tap a card = no "ouch")
2. You're often not shopping — you're soothing (tired, stressed, bored)
3. Everything is designed to remove the pause (1-click checkout, saved cards, buy-now-pay-later)

The One Step:
24-hour pause on non-essentials over $20. You don't say no (that triggers rebellion). You say "not yet."

Result: most people cut impulse spending 30-40% and genuinely enjoy what they do buy.

Disclaimer: This is general financial education, not personal financial advice. For big decisions, talk to a licensed professional.
```
