## Recording tips

1. **One voice per show** — keep it consistent across all future episodes of that podcast.
2. **Stability ~50%, Similarity ~75%** is a good default in ElevenLabs settings.
3. Each script is ~5–13 min of audio. Your 168k+ credits cover all of these comfortably.
4. **Filename when you save** — keep the number prefix (e.g. `07`) so I can match the MP3 to the right show instantly.
