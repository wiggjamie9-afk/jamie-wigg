# PODCAST #07: SLEEP & CALM — Episode 14
**Voice to use:** Sarah or Charlotte (Soft, slow, soothing)
**Episode 1 voice used:** [Check your EP1 recording]

Hello, I'm Mark Coggins, and this is Sleep and Calm. Let the day settle behind you now. You've done enough. There is nothing left to carry. Just be warm, and still, and quiet.

Take a slow breath in, and let it fall away. And again. Let this next breath be a little slower than the last.

Tonight we are going to let the body grow heavy, one slow part at a time. A gentle release, from your feet all the way up. You don't have to try. Just let each part soften as we go.

You are lying somewhere warm and safe. A soft bed beneath you. A blanket over you, heavy in the kindest way. The room is dim and quiet. There is nothing for you to do now but let go.

Begin with your feet. Let them grow heavy. Feel them sink down into the warmth. Let your toes uncurl, soft and loose. Let your feet rest, with nothing left to hold.

Now your legs. Let the heaviness rise slowly up through your ankles, your calves, your knees. Let your legs grow soft and warm and still. Let them sink down into the bed, heavier with every breath.

Now your hips, and your lower back. Let them settle. Let them spread and soften into the warmth beneath you. Feel the whole lower half of your body grow heavy, held, at rest.

Now your stomach and your chest. Let your breathing slow. Let each breath grow softer than the last. Feel your chest rise gently, and fall, and rise, slow and even, all on its own.

Now your hands. Let your fingers uncurl. Let your hands grow heavy and open, resting where they lie. Let the heaviness move up through your wrists, your arms, your elbows, until your arms are warm and soft and still.

Now your shoulders. Let them drop, away from your ears. Let them fall, heavy and loose. Feel all the holding of the day slide off them, and away, and gone.

Now your neck, and your jaw. Let your jaw soften. Let your tongue rest soft in your mouth. Let the small muscles around your eyes go smooth and still. Let your whole face soften into the quiet.

Your whole body is heavy now. Warm. Held. Sinking slowly into the bed with every slow breath. There is nothing left to hold. Nothing left to do.

You don't need to keep track of any of it. The heaviness will stay, soft and kind, all night long, whether you follow it or not.

There is nothing to do. Nowhere to be. Just rest now, all the way down, into the warm and the heavy and the deep, soft quiet.

Rest now. Sleep well. I'll see you again, when you're ready.
