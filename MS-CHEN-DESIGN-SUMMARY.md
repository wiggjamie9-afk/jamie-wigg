## Key Design Principles

### 1. Wisdom & Mentorship
Every element communicates that MS. CHEN is an experienced, caring guide. Her eyes show understanding. Her posture shows presence. Her clothing suggests confidence without pretension.

### 2. Grace & Musical Sensitivity
The character design constantly references music and movement. Postures suggest rhythm. Hair and clothing suggest graceful motion. This reflects her role as a music teacher.

### 3. Accessibility & Warmth
Nothing about the design is cold, intimidating, or disconnected. The warm brown skin, teal-and-gold palette, open postures, and kind eyes all communicate "you belong here."

### 4. Healthy Adult Presence
MS. CHEN shows what healthy, grounded adulthood looks like. Not perfect. Not robotic. Genuinely present. Genuinely caring. Genuinely wise without being preachy.

### 5. Neurodivergent Friendliness
The design should feel particularly welcoming to neurodivergent mentees:
- Clear, legible features (not overly detailed)
- Predictable, readable expressions
- Grounded, present body language
- No overwhelming patterns or colors
- Warm, accepting energy

### 6. Inclusive Representation
East Asian mentor figure shown with professional confidence. Not exoticized. Not secondary. Central, wise, primary.
