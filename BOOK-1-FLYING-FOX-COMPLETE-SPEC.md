## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Character Consistency (CRITICAL)
- **Sunny appears in EVERY page** where she should be visible (pages 1-2, 4-17)
- **Same appearance always:** Golden-brown soft fur, warm brown eyes, gentle permanent smile
- **Consistent size/scale** relative to environment
- **Consistent emotional expression** throughout (peaceful, wonder, calm, sleepy)

### Color Palette (THE BIBLE)
- **Primary:** Soft pastels - pale creams, gentle golds, soft purples, deep navy
- **Secondary:** Warm tones, earth colors, gentle greens
- **Avoid:** Bright digital colors, harsh contrasts, modern aesthetics
- **Style:** Watercolor painted effect, soft edges, breathing room

### Text Placement
- **Bottom of page, centered or split into two lines**
- **Readable white or soft cream text** against backgrounds
- **2 lines per story page** (pages 2-17)
- **Font:** Soft, readable, child-friendly serif or rounded sans-serif

### Dimensions
- **All illustrations:** 1920×1080 pixels (16:9 landscape)
- **High quality:** 300+ DPI for print production
- **Color space:** RGB for digital, prepare for CMYK for print
