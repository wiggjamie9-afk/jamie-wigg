# 📋 The Plan — Jamie's First Product

A simple summary of what we're doing and why, in plain English.

---

## 🎯 The goal
Make money online as a beginner digital marketer — without a big audience or
any budget — by building a real, free asset I can promote on my socials and my
hoo.be link page.

## 💡 The strategy (how this actually makes money)
A product only earns if people see it. So instead of writing an ebook nobody
finds, we start with a **free, genuinely useful tool** that does the work for me:

```
Post short videos  →  People visit the free tool  →  They join my email list  →  I sell to that list later
```

- The **free tool** gives people a reason to click and share.
- The **email list** is the real asset I'm building (I own it).
- The **paid product** comes later, sold to the people the tool collected.

The honest truth: the tool is the easy 20%. **Posting consistently is the 80%**
that makes the money.
