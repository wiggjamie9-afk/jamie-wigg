## BATCH PRODUCTION SYSTEM

To sustain 6 videos/week without burnout, operate in 4-week cycles:

**Week 1: Ideation + Scripting**
- Brainstorm 12-15 topics (30 min)
- Write scripts (2-3 hours)
- Voiceover generation via ElevenLabs (30 min)

**Week 2: Composition Building**
- Update/create HTML + GSAP compositions (4-6 hours)
- Parallel: asset collection (images, SFX)

**Week 3: Rendering + QA**
- Batch render all 12-15 videos (2 hours)
- Color grade + audio mix (2-3 hours)
- Final QA (1 hour)

**Week 4: Publishing + Analytics**
- Schedule videos to YouTube
- Write descriptions, tags, metadata
- Track KPIs, iterate based on data

**Result:** 12-15 videos per month = 50-60/quarter = 200+/year (far exceeds 100-160 goal, giving buffer)

## TOP RISKS & MITIGATION

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Algorithm demotion** (platform changes Shorts algorithm) | 50% sub growth loss | Build email list early; diversify to TikTok/Instagram Reels |
| **Sponsor pivot** (financial sponsors lose budget) | Revenue drops 30% | Diversify sponsors across industries |
| **Creator burnout** (6 videos/week unsustainable) | Content quality drops | Automate voiceover + rendering; plan 2-week vacations quarterly |
| **Topic saturation** (Business Economics becomes trendy, competition rises) | CPM drops | Pivot to emerging angles (AI economics, creator economy) early |
| **YouTube account strike** (3 strikes = termination) | Total loss | Follow community guidelines religiously; monitor copyright |
