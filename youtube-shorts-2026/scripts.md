# YouTube Shorts Scripts - 4 Launch Videos

## VIDEO 1: "Why Netflix Killed Blockbuster"
**Duration:** 60s | **Type:** Business Economics | **Voiceover:** Narrator

### Script (VoiceOver)
```
[0-5s] HOOK
"Blockbuster had 9,000 stores and $6 billion in revenue.
Netflix was a tiny startup.
So how did Netflix destroy them?"

[5-25s] THE SETUP
"Blockbuster's entire business model depended on ONE thing:
Late fees.

If you rented a movie and kept it, Blockbuster made money.
That was profit."

[25-50s] THE INSIGHT
"Netflix flipped the entire model.

No late fees.
No stores.
One price, unlimited rentals.

Blockbuster couldn't compete because they WERE the stores.
They had 9,000 reasons to NOT change."

[50-60s] CTA
"Subscribe for why companies fail."
```

## VIDEO 2: "Why Starbucks Doesn't Sell Coffee"
**Duration:** 60s | **Type:** Business Economics | **Voiceover:** Narrator

```

"Starbucks makes 60% of its revenue from things that aren't coffee.

Two: Brand. Premium status. Instagram photos.

Three: Experience. The ritual. The routine.

Four: Ecosystem. Rewards. The app. Loyalty."

Subscribe for why."
```

## VIDEO 3: "Fart Man: The Office Meeting"
**Duration:** 60s | **Type:** Comedy | **Sound:** SFX Only + Reactions

### Script (Physical Comedy - No Dialogue)
```
[0-10s] SETUP
Fart Man enters a professional boardroom. 
Executives around a table. Dead serious.
He sits down. Adjusts his chair.

[10-25s] FIRST FART
PPPPFFFFFTTTTT (loud, unmistakable)
Everyone freezes.
Fart Man's face: completely blank/dead inside.
CEO's eye twitches.

[25-40s] ESCALATION
He shifts. Another one.
Slightly quieter but more... wet sounding.
Woman across table puts hand over mouth.
Man to his left slowly stands up.

[40-55s] FINALE
One more for good measure - the grand finale.
Everyone just... leaves. 
One guy stays, points at him, shakes head.
Fart Man: 🤷

[55-60s] TEXT OVERLAY
"Don't go to the office meeting"
Subscribe.
```

## VIDEO 4: "Why Adults See Obstacles But Kids Don't"
**Duration:** 60s | **Type:** AHAD/Life Insight | **Voiceover:** Calm, Wise Narrator

### Script (VoiceOver)
```
[0-5s] HOOK
"You walk past a pillar.
You see an obstacle.

A 4-year-old walks past the same pillar.
They see something completely different."

[5-25s] THE DIFFERENCE
"Kids see: A column to hide behind.
Adults see: Something to walk around.

Kids see: Adventure.
Adults see: Efficiency.

Kids see: Play.
Adults see: Inconvenience."

[25-50s] THE INSIGHT
"Somewhere between childhood and adulthood,
we stopped seeing possibility.

We started seeing problems.

We optimized for efficiency
and lost imagination."

[50-60s] THE ASK
"When was the last time you saw like a kid?

Subscribe for insights that matter."
```
