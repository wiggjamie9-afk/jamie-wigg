# RHYTHMIX — Brand Style

## Product

RHYTHMIX is an end-to-end AI music platform: type a description, get a finished song, distribute to Spotify, master it, split it into stems, license it to film/TV, sell merch, host VR concerts, and let fans invest in your royalties. One subscription replaces Suno + Udio + LANDR + DistroKid + Splice + a DAW.

## Audience

- **Bedroom creators with a DAY JOB** who tried Suno, hit the "now what?" wall, and want one tool that takes them from prompt → released track → first dollar.
- **TikTok-native musicians** with an existing audience (1k–500k followers) who need volume — sync placements, merch, fan-invest revenue — without a label.
- **Producers/engineers** who already have skills and want the speed boost: stem-split a demo, master to radio level in 30s, regenerate one section without re-tracking.

## Voice

- We are: **specific, evidence-loaded, bold-with-numbers**.
- We are NOT: **corporate, hype-soaked, "excited-to-announce" energy**.
- Sample sentence: *"Sam made $7,200 from one Apple TV+ sync. He clicked apply, the algorithm matched him. That's the whole story."*

The voice across testimonials and copy is real-name + real-number + plain-words. Always anchor a claim to a person and an amount. Never say "creators are loving it" — say "Maya raised $14,800 from 148 fans."

## Visual identity

- **Palette** (locked — never deviate, see `rhythmix-teaser-60s/DESIGN.md` for full):
  - Canvas: `#08050d` (near-black, violet bias)
  - Primary CTA: `#ff1f5a` magenta
  - Secondary: `#7c3aed` purple
  - Accent cyan: `#00d8ff`
  - Signal green: `#00e887`
  - Hot gold (money): `#f5c000`
  - Soft pink (counts): `#ff6fc8`
  - Display text: `#ffffff`
  - Muted body: `#a0a0b0`
  - Card surface: `#1a1325`
- **Display font**: `"Space Grotesk", system-ui` — headlines, big numbers, wordmark.
- **Mono font**: `"JetBrains Mono", monospace` — taglines, labels, microcopy, numerals.
- **Motion**: 0.4–0.7s entrances on `power3.out` / `expo.out`. 60–120ms stagger across grids. 0.5s crossfade between scenes. No bounce, no elastic.

## Anti-patterns

- No `#3b82f6`, `#333`, default Roboto, or Arial. Palette is fixed.
- No "we're excited to announce." No "thrilled." No "delighted."
- No emoji-heavy decoration. One feature-card icon glyph max per visual.
- No full-frame linear gradients (banding) — use radial or solid + localized glow.
- No bouncy/elastic eases — wrong tonal register.
- No vague claims without a number, name, or specific outcome.
- No reposting hype-account content uncritically — every external claim gets a specific RHYTHMIX-feature angle.
