Model Family

Three models. One voice stack.

VibeVoice covers the full speech pipeline — recognition, synthesis, and streaming — each optimised for long-form, real-world audio without chunking or context loss.

Core Architecture

What makes it different

Conventional pipelines chunk audio into 30-second windows, losing speaker identity and context at every boundary. VibeVoice processes entire recordings as a unified sequence.

⚡
7.5 Hz Continuous Tokenizers
Acoustic and semantic tokenizers operating at an ultra-low frame rate — preserving audio fidelity while compressing sequence length dramatically, making 64K-token audio windows tractable.
🧠
Next-Token Diffusion
A Large Language Model handles textual context and dialogue flow; a diffusion head generates high-fidelity acoustic details. The LLM "understands" conversation structure; the diffusion head handles naturalness.
🎙
Single-Pass Diarization
ASR, speaker diarization, and timestamping are solved jointly in one forward pass — no pipeline stitching, no post-processing speaker clustering, no segment-boundary errors.
🔤
Customized Hotwords
Domain-specific terms, product names, and proper nouns can be injected as context at inference time — boosting accuracy on technical or brand-specific content without fine-tuning.

Changelog

Release history

2026-03-06
VibeVoice-ASR in Transformers 🚀
VibeVoice-ASR is now integrated into the Hugging Face Transformers library — use the model directly with a standard pipeline("automatic-speech-recognition") call.
2026-01-21
VibeVoice-ASR open-sourced
7B unified speech-to-text model. Natively multilingual (50+ languages). Finetuning code and vLLM inference support also available. Technique report published.
2025-12-16
VibeVoice-Realtime experimental speakers
Multilingual voices in 9 languages (DE, FR, IT, JP, KR, NL, PL, PT, ES) and 11 distinct English style voices added to the 0.5B streaming model.
2025-12-03
VibeVoice-Realtime-0.5B open-sourced
Real-time TTS with ~300 ms first-audible latency. Supports streaming text input and long-form generation up to ~10 minutes. Colab notebook available.
2025-09-05
VibeVoice-TTS code removed
After discovering misuse inconsistent with responsible AI principles, the TTS training code was removed from the repository. Model weights remain available for research.
2025-08-25
VibeVoice-TTS open-sourced · ICLR 2026 Oral
Long-form multi-speaker TTS model: up to 90 minutes, 4 speakers, expressive speech generation. Accepted as an Oral presentation at ICLR 2026.
Responsible Use

Risks & limitations

VibeVoice is designed for research and development. High-fidelity synthetic speech carries real-world misuse potential — review these limitations before deploying in production.

⚠️
Not for production use without further testing
VibeVoice may produce unexpected, biased, or inaccurate outputs. The TTS model in particular can generate convincing synthetic speech, creating risks for deepfakes, impersonation, and disinformation. Users are responsible for ensuring transcripts are accurate, disclosing AI-generated content, and complying with applicable laws. The base model (Qwen2.5 1.5B) may inherit its biases and errors. Use responsibly.
