# SURGE PILOT — Antagonist Character Brief

## Character: The Stimulus Sentinel ("Echo")

### Visual Design
- **Form**: Tall, angular humanoid made of swirling sound waves and visual noise
- **Color Palette**: Harsh greys, jarring neons (clashing magentas, acid greens, blinding whites)
- **Eyes**: Multiple overlapping circles constantly shifting - represents competing attention demands
- **Body**: Fractured, glitchy appearance like corrupted video compression artifacts
- **Movement**: Jerky, discordant, breaks reality around it
- **Aura**: Chaotic noise patterns, opposite of Ziggy's warm healing glow

### Character Profile
- **Role**: Main antagonistic force (not true villain)
- **Motivation**: Wants to keep Ziggy overwhelmed, small, invisible
- **Voice**: Overlapping, distorted, speaking in multiple tones simultaneously
- **Manifestation**: Internal representation of sensory overload and ADHD challenge

### Powers
1. **Multiplies Stimuli** - Amplifies sensory input exponentially
2. **Amplifies Self-Doubt** - Magnifies anxious internal voice
3. **Creates Noise Walls** - Blocks focus, drowns out clarity
4. **Feeds on Avoidance** - Grows stronger when Ziggy hides

### Character Arc
- **Act 1**: Appears as external threat in classroom
- **Act 2**: Reaches peak power, nearly overwhelms Ziggy
- **Act 3**: Revealed as manifestation, not external enemy
- **Epilogue**: Transforms - becomes source of Ziggy's strengths
  - The same intensity that felt overwhelming becomes hyperfocus
  - Sensory awareness becomes empathic superpower
  - Speed/reaction becomes agility superpower

### Design Notes for Animation
- **Animation Style**: Match Ziggy's character but inverted
  - Ziggy: Warm, round, flowing lines → Echo: Sharp, angular, fractured
  - Ziggy: Golden/cyan glow → Echo: Grey/neon harshness
  - Ziggy: Unified form → Echo: Fragmenting/multiplying
  
- **Transformation in Act 3**: 
  - Echo doesn't disappear, it TRANSFORMS
  - Sharp edges become dynamic energy
  - Noise becomes power
  - Jarring colors align into purposeful aura

### Story Function
- Physical manifestation of ADHD experience for young viewers
- Teaches that the challenge isn't the enemy - it's learning to channel it
- Shows disability as difference, not deficit
- Demonstrates that struggles become superpowers
