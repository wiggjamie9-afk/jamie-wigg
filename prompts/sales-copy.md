# RHYTHMIX Sales Copy Prompts

Adapted from the "Offer Positioning & Sales Copy" set, parameterised for RHYTHMIX. Run any of these by pasting the prompt into a Claude Code session — the brand voice and audience are already loaded via `CLAUDE.md` and `DESIGN.md`.

**Standing context (assume in every prompt):**
- Product: RHYTHMIX, AI music platform — generate, master, distribute, monetise.
- Audience: independent musicians, producers, content creators (4,800+ existing users).
- Anchors: 14 AI features, 200+ genres, 40+ platforms, 3.2M samples, sync placements averaging $2.4K, lifetime tier at $149.
- Voice: confident, outcome-led, creator-first. Lead with metric or result. No "amazing", "revolutionary", "great question".

---

## 1. Sales-page headlines

> Write 10 RHYTHMIX hero/sales-page headlines. Each under 15 words. Lead with a clear outcome, timeframe, or audience. No vague adjectives. No questions. After listing, pick the top 3 and explain why each beats the others.

## 2. Value proposition (3 outcomes)

> Write a RHYTHMIX value proposition built on 3 key outcomes. For each: state a specific result with a metric or timeframe, explain its impact in 2–3 sentences, and include one concrete creator example (use plausible numbers consistent with our existing testimonials — $94 first month, $7,200 sync placement, $96K VR concert ticket sales).

## 3. Problem section (under 200 words)

> Write a problem section under 200 words for the RHYTHMIX landing page. Describe the independent musician's current situation and frustrations — fragmented tools, low streaming payouts, gatekept sync licensing, no way to monetise fans directly — in their own words. Make them feel understood, not lectured. Match the brand voice: confident, not preachy.

## 4. "This is for you if" (5 statements)

> Write a 'This is for you if' section with 5 specific, sentence-length statements for RHYTHMIX. Describe the ideal user's situation, beliefs, and past attempts (DAWs they've abandoned, distributors that took too much, sync libraries that ignored them). Make each precise enough that the right person instantly recognises themselves.

## 5. Positioning statement (70–90 words)

> Write a 70–90 word positioning statement for RHYTHMIX. Cover: who we help (specific situation), outcome we deliver (with metric or timeframe), how we do it (the 14-feature unified platform method), and why it works (proof: 4,800+ creators, sync placements, real revenue numbers). Clear, specific, instantly recognisable.

## 6. "This is not for you if" (4 statements)

> Write a 'This is not for you if' section with 4 specific, sentence-length statements. Explain who won't benefit from RHYTHMIX and why — focus on mindsets and situations (people who want a hobby tool not a business platform, people unwilling to learn the workflow, people expecting overnight viral hits). Direct but respectful.
