# SURGE PILOT — Episode 1: "The Classroom Before the Storm"
## Full Script (10 minutes / ~1,400 words dialogue + narration)

---

## EPISODE OVERVIEW
**Airtime:** 10 minutes  
**Target Audience:** 8–13 years old  
**Themes:** Anxiety, sensory overwhelm, self-doubt, the moment before discovery  
**Emotional Arc:** Safe → Pressured → Overwhelmed → Desperate → Glimpse of possibility

---

## ACT 1: THE SETUP (2.5 minutes)
*Classroom calm before the storm. Introduce Ziggy's internal experience—their sensitivity, awareness, and growing anxiety.*

---

### SCENE 1A: MORNING BELL
**Interior: Meadowbrook Elementary, Classroom 4B. 8:45 AM**

*Soft morning sunlight streams through tall windows. A dozen kids settle into their seats. Most are chatting, opening backpacks, focused on their own morning routines.*

*ZIGGY (11, warm brown eyes, curly dark hair, wearing a soft blue hoodie) sits in the second row, backpack tucked neatly under their desk. They're scanning the room—unconsciously aware of every detail: the hum of the fluorescent lights, a kid dragging their chair loudly, the squeak of dry-erase markers on the whiteboard.*

**ZIGGY (V.O., warm, observant):**
"Some mornings, my brain feels like it's got a thousand radio stations playing at once. And I'm the only one who can hear them all."

*ZIGGY's POV shifts. We see the classroom through their eyes—sounds get slightly AMPLIFIED: fluorescent hum WHOOOOSH, chair drag SCREEEECH, marker squeak SQUEEEEAK.*

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"By the time the bell rings, I've already heard everything three times."

*The MORNING BELL CHIMES—a bright, clear sound. Most kids barely flinch. ZIGGY winces slightly, but recovers quickly, offering a small smile to MAYA (11, organized, energetic), who slides into the seat next to them.*

**MAYA:**
"You ready for the presentation?"

**ZIGGY:**
"Yeah. I mean... I think so?"

*Their voice is small—not quite a whisper, but definitely uncertain. MAYA doesn't notice. She's already pulling out a notebook, perfectly outlined in color-coded pens.*

**MAYA:**
"Don't be nervous. You've been working on yours all week. It's gonna be awesome."

*ZIGGY gives a thumbs up, but their stomach tightens. A BEAT of quiet internal awareness.*

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"Maya's right. I've practiced. I know my stuff. But something in my chest is already starting to feel... crowded."

---

### SCENE 1B: MR. HARRISON'S WELCOME
**Interior: Classroom 4B. 8:47 AM**

*MR. HARRISON (50s, well-meaning, wearing a tucked-in polo shirt and a clip-on bowtie) walks to the front of the class, clapping his hands together.*

**MR. HARRISON:**
"Good morning, Meadowbrook! Today is a big day. Who's excited for presentations?"

*Hands shoot up. ZIGGY raises their hand about halfway, hesitant.*

**MR. HARRISON (CONT'D):**
"Excellent! We have ten presentations today. That means you each have exactly seven minutes to share your natural wonder project. Seven minutes. The timer will go off when your time is up."

*ZIGGY's heart rate picks up slightly. We hear a subtle THUMP-THUMP in the audio design—not dramatic, just... there. The first sign.*

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"Seven minutes. Okay. Seven minutes. I can do seven minutes."

*MAYA leans over, whispering.*

**MAYA:**
"Who's going first?"

**MR. HARRISON:**
"Let's go alphabetically. Starting with... AIDEN?"

*AIDEN (12, confident, wearing a science club shirt) jumps up. He's clearly done presentations before. He sets up his poster at the front of the class—a colorful, carefully researched board about ecosystems.*

**MR. HARRISON (CONT'D):**
"The timer starts... now!"

*The digital timer on the whiteboard BEEPS and starts counting down: 7:00, 6:59, 6:58...*

*As Aiden speaks, we shift to ZIGGY's perspective. Sounds layer: Aiden's voice, the timer ticking, kids rustling papers in the background, the fluorescent hum, a kid clicking a pen repeatedly in the third row.*

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"And that's when I notice it. All the little things. The clicking. The humming. Aiden breathing between sentences. How bright the lights are. How many faces are looking up at him."

*ZIGGY's breathing is still normal, but their hands grip their desk edge. They're beginning to *feel* the crowd of stimuli.*

---

### SCENE 1C: WAITING ZONE
**Interior: Classroom 4B. 9:15 AM**

*Three presentations have finished. ZIGGY is next.*

*The room feels smaller now. ZIGGY can feel their heartbeat in their throat. They're arranged their presentation materials three times. Their natural wonder project is on photosynthesis in plants—a poster with drawings, a small potted plant, and note cards with talking points.*

*MAYA is still beside them, but ZIGGY has retreated inward, barely hearing her supportive comments.*

**MAYA:**
"Your poster looks really cool, Ziggy. Did you draw the leaf diagram?"

**ZIGGY:**
(quiet) "Yeah."

*A BEAT. MAYA tries again.*

**MAYA:**
"The colors are so neat. Very you."

**ZIGGY:**
(still quiet) "Thanks."

*The third presentation ends. AIDEN sits down. MR. HARRISON checks his list.*

**MR. HARRISON:**
"Excellent work, BRIANA. And next up... ZIGGY!"

*Time slows slightly. ZIGGY's hands feel clammy. They stand up, gathering their materials. The walk to the front of the class feels impossibly long. They can feel everyone's eyes on them.*

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"This is the moment. The moment before."

*ZIGGY reaches the front, sets up their poster on the easel. Turns to face the class.*

*A BEAT of silence.*

**MR. HARRISON:**
"Whenever you're ready. The timer starts... now!"

---

## ACT 2A: RISING PRESSURE (2.5 minutes)
*The sensory details begin to multiply. Small distractions grow. ZIGGY's awareness of being watched amplifies. The room starts to feel less like a safe place.*

---

### SCENE 2A: THE FIRST MINUTE
**Interior: Classroom 4B. 9:18 AM**

*ZIGGY opens their mouth. Their note cards are in their hand. They've practiced this.*

**ZIGGY:**
"Hi, um. My project is about photosynthesis. It's the process where plants turn sunlight into... into energy."

*Their voice is smaller than they want it to be. Some kids aren't looking up from their desks. One kid in the back is drawing on their hand.*

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"Start strong. You practiced this. Just keep going."

*ZIGGY points to their poster, showing the diagram of a leaf, explaining the role of chlorophyll.*

**ZIGGY (CONT'D):**
"So the green part—the chlorophyll—it absorbs the light, and then the plant uses that to make glucose, which is like... plant food."

*Good. This part is okay. ZIGGY feels a tiny bit of confidence.*

*Then—SCREEEECH. A chair drags. Someone getting up to go to the bathroom. ZIGGY loses their train of thought for half a second.*

**ZIGGY (CONT'D):**
(recovering) "And then the plant uses that energy to grow."

*But something has shifted. ZIGGY now has a separate awareness running in the background: *I just messed up. Did anyone notice? That chair thing was loud. Do people think I'm boring?*

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"The noise threw me off. And now there's another voice in my head. Not my voice. A different one."

*As ZIGGY continues speaking, we subtly introduce a second VOICE in the audio design. It's not coming from anywhere in the room. It's inside ZIGGY's head.*

**SECOND VOICE (V.O., lower, overlapping with ZIGGY's presentation voice):**
"They're not listening. Look at that kid—he's literally not even looking at you."

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"I know that voice. I don't like that voice."

---

### SCENE 2B: THE WEIGHT SETS IN
**Interior: Classroom 4B. 9:22 AM**

*ZIGGY is three minutes into their presentation. The timer reads 4:00 remaining.*

*The room feels full now. Lights feel brighter. The hum is louder. A kid next to MAYA is tapping their pencil—not obviously, but ZIGGY hears it. Another kid is yawning. BRIANA is texting someone (which is not allowed, but ZIGGY notices).*

*ZIGGY's presentation is factually solid, but their delivery is getting quieter. They're speaking faster, trying to get through it.*

**ZIGGY:**
"And water comes in through the roots, and CO2 comes in through the stomata, which are little pores on the bottom of the leaf, and then—"

*Their hands are shaking slightly. They adjust their grip on the note cards.*

**SECOND VOICE (V.O., growing clearer):**
"You're talking too fast. Slow down. No, wait, don't slow down, because then it's boring. You can't win. Look how uncomfortable you are."

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"I can feel it now. That thing that happens sometimes. Like I'm being split in two. Part of me is doing the presentation. The other part is watching me do it and being really mean about it."

*ZIGGY glances at MR. HARRISON. He's nodding supportively—this is a good presentation. But ZIGGY doesn't register it.*

*They're too busy noticing: the scratch on their shoe, how weird their voice sounds, the fact that MAYA looks like she's about to sneeze, the smell of someone's lunch from the back of the classroom, the way the light from the window is making their eyes feel tired.*

**SECOND VOICE (V.O.):**
"Everyone can tell you're nervous. That's what they're all thinking about. Not your project. You. How bad you are at this."

*ZIGGY swallows hard. Their throat feels tight.*

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"The voice is wrong. I know it's wrong. But it's so *loud*."

---

### SCENE 2C: THE DISTRACTION SPIRAL
**Interior: Classroom 4B. 9:24 AM**

*Timer: 3:00 remaining.*

*ZIGGY is explaining the benefits of photosynthesis—how plants produce oxygen that we breathe. It's the important part. The part they care about.*

*But now there are TOO MANY SOUNDS. The fluorescent lights WHOOOOSH, a student coughs, someone's stomach GURGLES (embarrassingly), a pencil sharpener in the corner whirrs loudly, MAYA shifts in her chair, the timer TICK-TOCK-TICKs, and underneath it all, that SECOND VOICE.*

**SECOND VOICE (V.O., now echoing slightly):**
"You sound like a robot. Are you even breathing? You're probably not breathing right. Oh my God, think about your breathing and now you'll mess it up because you're thinking about it."

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"The sounds are getting louder. Or maybe my ears are getting too open. Like someone turned up the volume on everything."

*ZIGGY's eyes start to feel tight. They're close to tears, though they don't cry. They just go very still.*

**ZIGGY (CONT'D):**
(mechanically, losing energy) "So... plants are really important because they give us oxygen and they're part of the ecosystem and, um..."

*They glance down at their note cards. The words aren't making sense anymore. They're just symbols. Too many symbols.*

*MR. HARRISON shifts in his seat. MAYA looks concerned.*

**ZIGGY (CONT'D):**
"Sorry, I... Can I see how much time is left?"

**MR. HARRISON:**
"You've got about two and a half minutes. Keep going!"

*ZIGGY's jaw clenches. Two and a half more minutes. That's 150 seconds. 150 thousand seconds when everything is too loud and too bright and too much.*

---

## ACT 2B: PEAK CHAOS (2.5 minutes)
*Everything breaks open. The sensory input becomes overwhelming. ZIGGY's anxiety peaks. The second voice becomes more than just a voice—it starts to manifest as something visual, something real in the room.*

---

### SCENE 2D: THE ARRIVAL
**Interior: Classroom 4B. 9:25 AM (Ziggy's subjective time)**

*The world starts to glitch.*

*ZIGGY is still standing at the front of the class, but their perception is fracturing. The sounds begin to distort. The fluorescent lights flicker and buzz BZZZZZZZZZZ. The digital timer's numbers are too bright—they're making a faint ringing sound just by existing.*

*And then—*

*A FIGURE materializes in the corner of the room. ECHO.*

*It's not quite there. It's more like the space around it is wrong. It's tall, angular, made of fractured grey and neon—static and jarring colors that don't belong together. Its form flickers like corrupted video. Its eyes are multiple overlapping circles, all of them looking at ZIGGY.*

*NO ONE ELSE SEES ECHO. This is Ziggy's internal experience made external.*

*ECHO moves—jerks, really—closer to the front of the class. Its movement distorts the sound around it. The ambient noise of the room gets worse.*

**ECHO (V.O., multiple voices overlapping, distorted):**
"Hello, hello, hello, Ziggy, Ziggy, ZIGGY. You're doing so bad, so bad, SO BAD."

**ZIGGY (V.O., panicking):**
"No no no no—what is that—"

*ZIGGY's hands are shaking now. Visibly shaking.*

**ECHO:**
(jerking movements, glitchy sound) "They're all looking at you. Look at their faces. They're bored and uncomfortable and tired and—"

*ECHO's form expands suddenly. The classroom gets darker around it. Shadows spread from where it stands.*

**MAYA (concerned, off to the side):**
"Ziggy? You okay?"

*ZIGGY hears Maya, but it's like her voice is coming through water. The only clear voice is ECHO, which is now standing directly in front of the class, right next to ZIGGY.*

**ECHO (overlapping voices, growing louder):**
"Nobody cares about your plants, nobody cares about your leaves, nobody cares about you, you're small and quiet and boring and—"

**ZIGGY:**
(whispered, to themselves) "Stop."

*ECHO tilts its head. All its overlapping eyes focus on ZIGGY.*

**ECHO:**
"Stop? You can't stop me. I'm not outside. I'm inside. I'm you."

---

### SCENE 2E: BREAKDOWN
**Interior: Classroom 4B. 9:26 AM (Real time)**

*MR. HARRISON notices something is wrong. ZIGGY's voice has gotten very quiet. They've stopped talking. They're staring at a spot in the room where nothing visible exists.*

**MR. HARRISON:**
"Ziggy? Do you want to finish?"

*ZIGGY blinks. The classroom snaps back into focus. ECHO is still there—still visible only to ZIGGY, but vivid and terrible.*

*ZIGGY's note cards have dropped to the floor. They don't remember dropping them.*

*The timer reads 1:47.*

**ZIGGY:**
(barely audible) "I... I can't."

**MAYA:**
"What?"

**ZIGGY:**
(small voice, louder) "I can't. I'm sorry. I can't finish."

*A long, uncomfortable silence in the classroom.*

*ZIGGY looks around—eyes wide, breathing shallow. They see:
- Confused faces. AIDEN looks embarrassed for them. BRIANA is genuinely paying attention now. MR. HARRISON looks disappointed.
- And overlaid on all of it, ECHO, pulsing with satisfaction.*

**ECHO:**
"Yes. Yes yes yes. This is perfect. This is where you belong. Small. Scared. Alone."

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"I can't breathe right. Everything is too much. The lights are too bright. The room is too full. That voice is too loud. And I'm disappearing."

*ZIGGY is still standing. Still present. But they feel like they're shrinking.*

**MR. HARRISON:**
"It's okay, Ziggy. You can sit down. We'll talk after class, okay?"

*ZIGGY nods—a small, defeated motion—and walks back to their seat. MAYA watches, concerned.*

**MAYA (whispering):**
"Hey... you good?"

*ZIGGY doesn't answer. They can't.*

*ECHO follows them back to their desk, settling into the air above them like a storm cloud.*

**ECHO:**
"You failed. And now everyone knows you're not like them. You're broken. You're too sensitive. You're too much and not enough at the same time."

*The fluorescent lights buzz again. The timer counts down: 1:30, 1:29, 1:28...*

*ZIGGY sits very still, staring at their desk. Inside, everything is screaming.*

---

## ACT 3: THE MOMENT OF TRANSFORMATION (2.5 minutes)
*In the depths of the overwhelm, Ziggy encounters Echo not as a threat to overcome, but as something to understand. The first glimpse of their potential power emerges.*

---

### SCENE 3A: THE BREAKING POINT
**Interior: Classroom 4B. 9:28 AM**

*MAYA has gone back to paying attention to the next presenter (CAMERON, giving a report on volcanoes). But ZIGGY is not here. Not really.*

*ZIGGY is floating in a space that's half the classroom, half ECHO's chaotic realm. It's a space made of overlapping sounds and fractured visuals. It should be unbearable.*

*But ZIGGY stops running from it.*

**ZIGGY (V.O., quiet and steady):**
"You know what? I can hear all of this. I always could. Every sound. Every voice. Every light. Every feeling. Everyone else just... doesn't."

*ECHO flinches—a jerky, glitchy motion.*

**ECHO:**
(confused) "That's not... you're supposed to be breaking apart. You're supposed to hide."

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"What if I didn't?"

*The moment ZIGGY stops struggling, the noise doesn't go away. But it changes. It stops being *chaotic* and becomes *information*. ZIGGY can suddenly distinguish each sound: the timer, the pencil tapping, Cameron's presentation, the hum of the lights, their own heartbeat.*

**ZIGGY (V.O., growing stronger):**
"I can hear all of it. And... I can work with it. I can understand it. I can feel everything everyone else is trying to avoid feeling."

*ECHO is still there, but it's shifting. Its jerky movements become less angry and more... uncertain. The overlapping eyes start to focus.*

**ECHO:**
(softer, almost confused) "What are you doing?"

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"I'm not running anymore."

---

### SCENE 3B: THE GLOW
**Interior: Classroom 4B. 9:30 AM**

*ZIGGY sits at their desk, but something has changed about them. They're still anxious. They're still feeling every sound, every face, every light. But they're no longer *hiding from it*.*

*A soft GOLDEN GLOW begins to emanate from ZIGGY—not bright, not blinding, but warm and steady. It's their own light.*

*The glow doesn't erase ECHO, but it pushes back against it. ECHO's edges become less defined. The harshness of its neon colors begins to look less like a threat and more like... energy. Raw, untamed energy.*

**ECHO:**
(voice less distorted, almost curious) "You're not small anymore."

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"No. I'm not. I'm just... different. The way I take in the world is different. But different doesn't mean broken."

*ZIGGY looks down at their hands. They're still trembling slightly, but the trembling has a purpose now. It's not fear. It's *aliveness*.*

**MAYA (tapping Ziggy's shoulder, back to the present moment):**
"Hey... you're okay now, right?"

*ZIGGY looks up at MAYA and smiles—a real smile, tired but genuine.*

**ZIGGY:**
"Yeah. I'm... I think I am. I'm okay."

*The classroom comes back into full focus. The timer is beeping—CAMERON's presentation is over. MR. HARRISON is calling out the next name.*

*ECHO is still there—still visible to ZIGGY—but changed. Its fractured form is now shot through with ZIGGY's golden glow. They're no longer enemies. They're not partners yet, either. But something has shifted.*

**ECHO (quieter, less like a voice attacking and more like a voice of awareness):**
"You're strong. Stronger than I thought."

**ZIGGY (to themselves, barely audible):**
"So are you."

---

### SCENE 3C: THE ECHO AND THE PROMISE
**Interior: Classroom 4B. 10:15 AM (End of presentations)**

*The presentations are over. MR. HARRISON is collecting the posters. Kids are chatting, getting back to normal.*

*ZIGGY is still at their desk. MAYA has moved on to something else. ZIGGY is alone with their feelings and with ECHO.*

*ECHO hovers above their desk, no longer menacing. Its form is still fractured, still made of grey and neon, but now those colors are intertwined with ZIGGY's golden glow. They're creating something new together.*

**ECHO:**
"I won't go away. You know that, right? I'm part of you."

**ZIGGY (V.O., calm, accepting):**
"I know."

**ECHO:**
"All the sensitivity. All the loudness. All the feelings. That doesn't disappear. But maybe... maybe you could use it."

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"Use it for what?"

**ECHO:**
"I don't know yet. But you will. You're going to find out something about yourself. Something unexpected."

*ECHO's form begins to fade, but it's not disappearing. It's merging. Becoming part of ZIGGY's golden glow.*

*And in that moment, we see a flash—a glimpse of what ZIGGY will become. A figure surrounded by light and energy and awareness and strength.*

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"I don't have a name for what just happened. I don't understand it yet. But something is different. Something inside me is different. And maybe... maybe that's the beginning of something."

*ZIGGY takes a deep breath. The classroom is full of normal, everyday sounds—chairs scraping, kids talking, papers rustling.*

*But ZIGGY hears them differently now. They hear the information in the sound. They hear the story in the noise.*

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"Tomorrow, everything feels like too much again. But today... today I learned something. I learned that being different isn't something to be ashamed of. Being sensitive, being aware, being a person who feels everything..."

*ZIGGY stands up, gathering their materials, ready to move on to the next class.*

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"Maybe that's not a problem. Maybe it's a superpower I haven't figured out how to use yet."

*MR. HARRISON notices ZIGGY is moving around. He calls them over.*

**MR. HARRISON:**
"Ziggy, I wanted to say—you did a good job. I know it was hard. But you showed courage. That matters."

*ZIGGY smiles—the same smile from before, tired but genuine.*

**ZIGGY:**
"Thank you, Mr. Harrison."

*And they mean it.*

---

## EPILOGUE: THE QUESTIONS BEGIN
**Interior: School Hallway. 10:20 AM**

*ZIGGY is walking to their next class. MAYA catches up, linking arms.*

**MAYA:**
"Hey! That was intense back there. You sure you're okay?"

**ZIGGY:**
"Yeah. Actually... I think something just happened. I'm not sure what yet."

**MAYA:**
"Like, something bad?"

**ZIGGY:**
"No. Something... different. Something big."

*They walk down the hallway together, surrounded by the familiar chaos of the school day.*

*Above ZIGGY, we see the faintest shimmer—ECHO, no longer separate but fully merged now, but still present. The golden glow and the fractured neon are now one thing.*

**ZIGGY (V.O.):**
"And that's when I didn't know yet—couldn't have known—that my life was about to get a lot stranger, a lot louder, and a lot more powerful. My name is Ziggy. And I'm about to discover that everything I thought was wrong about me might actually be exactly what I need."

*FADE TO BLACK.*

**THE END — EPISODE 1**

## TECHNICAL NOTES FOR PRODUCTION

### Sound Design

**Act 1:**
- Ambient school hallway hum (subtle, 50-60Hz fluorescent tone)
- Morning bell: bright, clear, single note (F sharp)
- Chair drag: distorted, exaggerated (400-800Hz range)
- Marker squeak: high-frequency (1500-2000Hz)
- Natural classroom chatter underneath

**Act 2A:**
- All Act 1 sounds LAYERED: hum + chair + markers + chatter + pencil click
- Introduce SECOND VOICE at low volume, gradually INCREASING
- Timer: steady mechanical ticking (starting subtle, becoming more prominent)
- Sounds become SLIGHTLY DISTORTED as Ziggy's anxiety rises

**Act 2B:**
- Full sensory overload: hum BUZZES (200Hz + harmonics), timer RINGS (high frequency), heartbeat POUNDS (in the background)
- SECOND VOICE now using echo/reverb, overlapping with itself
- Add sound glitches (digital artifacts, clicking, stuttering) as Echo manifests
- All sounds are 1-2x their normal volume

**Act 2D-E (Peak chaos):**
- Full sensory chaos, then SUDDEN DROP to only Ziggy's breathing + Echo's voice
- Echo's voice is DISTINCTLY SYNTHETIC: multiple voices layered, some reversed, some sped up, some slowed down
- As Ziggy accepts the overwhelm: sounds SHIFT from chaotic to CLEAR—each one distinguishable
- Golden glow SFX: soft, warm, almost bell-like tones (C major chord)

**Act 3:**
- As Ziggy transforms: chaos → clarity → harmony
- Echo and Ziggy's sounds begin to INTERWEAVE rather than conflict
- Final moment: layered but BALANCED—all the sounds Ziggy hears, but they're integrated, not overwhelming
- School hallway returns to normal, but Ziggy's awareness is DEEPER (subtle reverb on all sounds, indicating internal perspective)

### Visual Direction

**Color Palette:**
- **Ziggy's default:** warm skin tones, soft blue/teal clothing, warm lighting wherever they are
- **Classroom:** natural daylight (cool, slightly clinical), fluorescent overhead lights (cool-green tint)
- **As Ziggy's anxiety rises:** desaturation around them, increase in blue/grey tones in the environment
- **Echo's appearance:** jagged, angular shapes; colors that clash (magenta + acid green + blinding white); glitchy edges; form never quite stable
- **Golden glow:** warm, soft-edged aura (golden-yellow, honey-toned light)

**Animation Style:**
- **Ziggy's baseline:** smooth lines, natural movements, expressive hand gestures
- **As anxiety rises:** movements become more hesitant, hands shake slightly, breathing is visible
- **Echo's movement:** jerky, fractured, discontinuous (like frame-skipping in animation)
- **The Merge (Act 3):** Ziggy remains smooth-lined, but now surrounded by/containing Echo's fractured energy—the contrast creates dynamism rather than conflict

**Camera Work:**
- **Act 1:** Wide angles on the classroom, establishing shots; camera is steady, comforting
- **Act 2A-B:** Tighter on Ziggy's face; occasional quick cuts to sound sources (the chair, the timer, kids' faces)—disorienting but not nauseating
- **Act 2D (Echo's arrival):** Single glitchy JUMP to show Echo; camera slightly tilted/unstable
- **Act 2E (Breakdown):** Medium shot on Ziggy; world is slightly slow-motion
- **Act 3:** Camera begins to ROTATE slowly around Ziggy as they stand/accept; by the merge, we're seeing them from multiple angles simultaneously—creating a sense of transformation
- **Epilogue:** Return to normal, steady framing; slight golden bloom around Ziggy as they walk

### Character Notes for Voice Acting

**ZIGGY:**
- Speak with genuine uncertainty; not whiny, but genuinely unsure
- V.O. should be slightly older/wiser than their spoken voice (like an internal observer)
- As anxiety rises: faster speech, higher pitch, shorter breaths
- By Act 3: voice becomes calm, grounded, slightly deeper (more confidence)

**MAYA:**
- Bright, organized energy; speaks quickly but kindly
- Unaware of Ziggy's internal experience (she can't hear the sounds the way Ziggy does)
- Genuine care without condescension

**MR. HARRISON:**
- Well-meaning, enthusiastic about teaching
- Not oblivious, but busy managing 30 kids; doesn't catch the nuances of Ziggy's distress until it's obvious
- Supportive adult voice; never judgmental

**ECHO:**
- Multiple voices at once: a young voice, an adult voice, a distorted voice, a whispered voice—all overlapping
- Never quite in unison; always slightly off-sync (unsettling but not demonic)
- Tone: accusatory, amplifying, but not purely malicious—it believes what it's saying
- By the end: less distorted, more curious, almost vulnerable

**ZIGGY'S V.O. (Internal Awareness):**
- Warm, observant, non-judgmental
- Like an older version of Ziggy narrating their younger self's experience
- Becomes stronger as the episode progresses

## WRITING NOTES

**Authenticity for the target audience (8-13):**
- No diagnosis talk, no clinical language—just the *experience* of ADHD
- Anxiety is portrayed as real and difficult, but not pathologized
- Sensory overload is rendered through concrete details (sounds, lights, physical sensations) rather than abstract descriptions
- Ziggy's shame and self-doubt are treated with respect; recovery isn't instant, just beginning
- ECHO is neither demonized nor minimized—it's a real part of Ziggy's internal experience

**Emotional truth:**
- The moment of breakdown is realistic: Ziggy doesn't cry, doesn't scream, just goes very still and quiet
- Recovery begins not with external help but with Ziggy's own reframing (stopping the struggle, accepting the overwhelm)
