# Streaming Royalty Math — LinkedIn

## Hook
The median musician made $1,450 from streaming in 2024.

## Body

The median musician made $1,450 from streaming in 2024.

The average was $11,523.

The gap is the whole story.

Spotify's worldwide payout averages $0.00238 per stream. 87% of tracks never earn anything — they don't clear the 1,000-stream threshold to qualify. Your distributor took $20 to put your track on a platform that decided it doesn't exist.

When 1 in 20 full-time musicians lists streaming as their top income source, the math isn't broken. The strategy is.

The other 19 stopped fighting Spotify for $0.003 and started stacking:

→ Sync placements ($1K–$10K per Apple TV brief)
→ Fan investment / royalty splits direct from superfans
→ Direct-to-fan drops with AI mastering
→ Merch and merch-on-demand (zero inventory)
→ Sample / preset / stem libraries
→ NFT and on-chain releases
→ VR and livestream concerts

Streams became the marketing layer. Income came from owned distribution.

If you're an indie artist building a catalogue right now, the question isn't "how do I get more streams." It's: what fraction of my revenue should depend on streams at all?

The artists clearing $50K+ a year answer 10%, max. The ones earning the median answer 100%.

Pick one to copy.
