# Platform Rules

> **DRAFT** based on each platform's published guidelines + general best practice as of mid-2026. Edit if you have research that contradicts.

## LinkedIn

- **Body limit:** 3,000 characters. **Sweet spot:** 1,200–1,800 characters.
- **Format:**
  - Short paragraphs (1–3 lines max).
  - Blank line between every paragraph.
  - Hook in line 1. Payoff or pivot by line 3.
  - Lists are fine. Bullets render. Numbered lists pull more eyes.
  - No links in the post body — drop them in the first comment. LinkedIn suppresses outbound posts.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 max, end of post. Niche > broad.
- **Hooks that work:** specific number, contrarian flat statement, named role + transformation ("After 7 years signing artists, here's what labels don't want you to know").
- **What loses:** anything that reads like a press release; "I'm excited to announce"; corporate hedging.
- **Cadence:** posting more than once per day is diminishing returns.

## Instagram

- **Caption limit:** 2,200 characters. **Sweet spot:** 800–1,500 for carousels, 400–800 for single posts.
- **Format:**
  - First line = hook. Truncates after ~125 chars on mobile, so land in 8 words.
  - Line breaks render. Use them.
  - End every post with a clear CTA. For lead capture: "Comment SYSTEM and I'll DM you the guide."
- **Hashtags:** 8–15. Mix of broad / niche / tool-specific. Place at end of caption (not first comment — engagement parity is fine, end is cleaner for AI-paired captions).
- **Carousels:** 7 slides is the sweet spot for completion rate. 10 max. Cover slide carries the swipe; final slide carries the CTA.
- **Hooks that work:** stat in slide 1, transformation in slide 2, payoff slide last. "This is the post you'll wish you saw 6 months ago."
- **What loses:** vague aesthetic posts with no payoff; carousels with text smaller than the headline; any caption longer than the carousel can carry.

## Threads

- **Body limit:** 500 characters per post. Chains up to 25 posts (default to 1–3).
- **Format:**
  - Short, sharp, conversational.
  - Lots of white space. Single-line "posts" are fine.
  - Plain English. No jargon, no hashtags inside the post body.
- **Hashtags:** Threads de-prioritises them. Skip unless tracking a specific tag.
- **Hooks that work:** must land in the first 6 words. "Spotify is a $0.003 lottery."
- **What loses:** copy/paste from Twitter/X without a Threads-native edit; long monologues; corporate tone.
- **Chains vs single:** if it's a single insight, post one. Only chain if there's a real argument arc.

## TikTok

- **Caption:** up to 2,200 characters but 80–150 is the sweet spot. The video carries the message; the caption supports it.
- **Format:**
  - Hook in the first 3 seconds spoken. Hook in the first 8 chars of caption.
  - Captions are part of the video — bake them in (auto-captions help).
  - End with a verbal CTA: "Comment KEYWORD on my Instagram post and I'll DM you the guide." (Off-platform, because TikTok DM/ManyChat is unreliable.)
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 in caption. Mix one trending + niche tags. Don't stuff.
- **Hooks that work:** number, contradiction, payoff promise. Under 12 words. Examples: "I made $7,200 from one sync placement on Apple TV." or "Suno is the wrong tool if you want to get paid."
- **What loses:** slow openers; tutorials longer than 60 seconds without a payoff in the first 15; talking-head with no on-screen demo or text overlay.

## Universal rules (apply everywhere)

- Lead with the insight or the number. Never with setup.
- One CTA per post. Multiple CTAs split the action.
- For lead capture, route everything through Instagram comments → ManyChat DM. Same keyword across the campaign.
- Post times are platform-specific and audience-specific — defer to performance review for adjustments.
