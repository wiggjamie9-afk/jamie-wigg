# Brand Voice — RHYTHMIX / The Ai Impact

> **DRAFT.** Inferred from existing brand materials in this repo (RHYTHMIX landing pages, DESIGN.md, video scripts). Edit before relying on it. Spots marked `[CONFIRM]` need your sign-off.

## Tone & energy

- Confident, sharp, stat-driven. We lead with numbers and outcomes, not setup.
- High-energy without being hyped — we sound certain, not desperate.
- Contrarian by default. We name competitors (Suno, Udio, LANDR) and explain what they get wrong. [CONFIRM: still positioning this hard against them?]
- Optimistic about creators making real money with AI; impatient with gatekeepers, labels, and "AI will replace musicians" doomers.

## Words & phrases I always use

- "Real creators. Real results."
- Outcome verbs: ship, distribute, monetize, mint, sell, mix, master, split.
- Hard numbers in copy. "$7,200 from one sync." "31,000 streams." "127 countries." If a stat exists, it leads.
- "Practical AI workflows for people who don't have time for the hype." (The Ai Impact tagline.)

## Words & phrases I refuse to use

- "Game-changer", "revolutionize", "unlock your potential", "AI-powered" as filler.
- "In today's fast-paced world..." or any soft-open setup.
- "Just" (as in "just sign up"), "simply", "easy" — they undersell the work.
- Emojis as decoration in copy. One icon per feature card max. Never in body paragraphs.
- "We're excited to announce" — start with the announcement, not the feeling about it.

## Hooks I trust

- A specific number in the first 6 words. ("$7,200 from one sync placement.")
- A contrarian flat statement. ("Suno is the wrong tool if you want to get paid.")
- A named transformation. ("From 0 streams to 31K in 30 days.")
- A direct callout. ("Stop generating tracks. Start shipping records.")

## Hooks I ban

- Questions as openers ("Have you ever wondered...").
- "Let me tell you about..." or any meta-introduction.
- Generic wisdom ("Success isn't about talent, it's about consistency").
- "Here's a thread on..." — show, don't announce.

## Sentence structure preferences

- Short. Then shorter. Then a longer one when the rhythm needs it.
- One idea per paragraph. Two lines max for body paragraphs.
- White space is a tool. Use it.
- Drop pronouns when the subject is obvious. ("Spent 3 hours on this. Worth it.")
- Active voice always. If you catch yourself writing "is being done", rewrite.

## Voice test (apply to every draft)

Before publishing, the post must pass both:

1. **The Stat Test.** Does the first 8 words contain a number, a named outcome, or a contrarian claim? If no, rewrite the hook.
2. **The Setup Test.** Could you delete the first sentence and the post would be sharper? If yes, delete it.
