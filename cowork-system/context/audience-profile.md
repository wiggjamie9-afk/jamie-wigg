# Audience Profile

> **DRAFT.** Inferred from RHYTHMIX testimonials and feature positioning. Edit before relying on it.

## Who they are

- Independent musicians, beatmakers, producers, content creators with a music angle.
- Age 22–40, mix of full-time creators and side-hustlers building toward full-time.
- Income: most are making $0–$2K/month from music today. The aspirational ceiling they see is $5K–$20K/month.
- Tech-comfortable but not engineers. They use TikTok, ManyChat, Notion, ChatGPT, and a DAW (Logic, FL Studio, Ableton). They are not afraid of new tools but they are tired of tools that don't pay.
- Mindset: scrappy, builder, distrustful of labels, allergic to gatekeepers.

## What they're trying to achieve

- Make money from music without a label, manager, or industry connections.
- Replace day-job income inside 12–24 months.
- Build a fan base that pays them directly (sync, NFTs, fan investment, merch, sub) instead of $0.003-per-stream platforms.
- Look professional fast. Master quality, distribution, merch — without hiring 6 specialists.

## What pains them

- Spending months on a track that earns $14 in streaming royalties.
- Watching peers go viral on TikTok and not knowing how to engineer that.
- Suno / Udio output sounds AI when you A/B it next to a real master.
- Distribution platforms charge them, then take 30 days to pay.
- "Build an audience first" advice with no concrete next step.

## What they believe right now (some of it wrong)

- "If I just keep posting, I'll break through." (Half right — posting without a system burns them out.)
- "AI music tools are toys, not tools." (Wrong, but defensible — most are.)
- "Streaming is the only path." (Wrong — sync, fan investment, NFTs, merch all pay better per hour.)
- "I need a label to scale." (Wrong — every testimonial in our materials is independent.)

## The exact questions they ask out loud

- "How do I actually monetize this?"
- "What's the difference between Suno, Udio, RHYTHMIX, LANDR?"
- "Can AI music get on Spotify? Do they even pay?"
- "How do I get sync placements without a publisher?"
- "Is it too late to start?"
- "How long until this replaces my job?"

## What earns their attention

- A specific dollar amount tied to a specific creator and a specific play. ($7,200 from one Apple TV+ sync.)
- Side-by-side comparisons (RHYTHMIX vs Suno) that show the gap.
- Real workflow demos. Not slideshows of features.
- Behind-the-scenes of someone going from $0 to $5K/month.

## What loses them instantly

- Vague AI optimism ("AI is changing everything!").
- Influencer-style "what nobody's telling you" hooks.
- Tutorials that don't end in a deliverable they can post or sell.
- Anything that smells like a course funnel pretending to be free advice.
- Posts that name no platform, no number, no creator.
