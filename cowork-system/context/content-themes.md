# Content Themes / Pillars

> **DRAFT.** Inferred from RHYTHMIX product positioning + the testimonials in the landing page drafts. Edit before relying on it.

## The transformation we sell / stand for

Independent musicians going from $0–$2K/month from streaming royalties to $5K–$20K/month from owned monetization (sync, fan investment, merch, NFTs, VR concerts, AI mastering, sample licensing) — using AI tools that don't compromise on quality.

## Pillar 1 — Real Money, Real Creators

The cornerstone pillar. Every post here ties a specific dollar amount to a specific creator and a specific tool.

- "Sam Wu made $7,200 from one Apple TV+ sync placement. Here's the brief he submitted."
- "Maya Rivera raised $14,800 from 148 fans. Now they earn royalties with her."
- "31,000 streams in 30 days. Zero promo budget. Here's the full RHYTHMIX workflow."

## Pillar 2 — Tool Wars (Contrarian Comparisons)

Direct head-to-head with Suno, Udio, LANDR, DistroKid, etc. Show the gap, name the winner, explain why.

- "Suno vs RHYTHMIX: I generated the same brief in both. One sounds like a demo. One sounds like a record."
- "LANDR mastering vs AI Master in RHYTHMIX. Same track. A/B test."
- "Why I left DistroKid for [alternative]: $0.003 vs $0.017 per stream."

## Pillar 3 — The Workflow (Behind the Scenes)

Demos. Not feature lists. Not slideshows. Actual screen-recordings of someone going from idea to deliverable.

- "From a 3-line description to a Spotify-ready master in 11 minutes."
- "How I split stems from a 2008 demo and rebuilt the track with AI."
- "The exact 4-step workflow I use to ship a track per week."

## Pillar 4 — The Industry Lie

Contrarian education. What labels, distributors, and gatekeepers don't want indie creators to know.

- "The streaming royalty math nobody shows you. (It's worse than you think.)"
- "Why labels still sign artists who already have a fan base."
- "Sync agents charge 50%. AI matching does it for free. Here's what actually happens."

## Pillar 5 — Build in Public

Personal POV from the founder / brand. Wins, losses, decisions, numbers. Not curated.

- "I just turned off [feature]. Here's why."
- "First 100 paying users in 30 days. The 3 things that worked, the 1 that didn't."
- "What it costs to run an AI music platform per user per month."

## Pillar 6 — Practical AI for Creators (broader Ai Impact umbrella)

For when the audience overlap with The Ai Impact's wider creator audience matters.

- "5 AI tools every musician should be using in 2026 (RHYTHMIX is one)."
- "How creators are stacking ChatGPT + RHYTHMIX + ManyChat + Blotato into a 1-person studio."
- "The full content system I use to ship 4 platforms a week from one transcript."
