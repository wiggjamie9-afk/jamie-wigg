The Origins

Three geniuses.
One truth.

Working 500 years apart, Pythagoras, da Vinci, and Tesla each arrived at the same conclusion: reality is vibrational, the body has natural resonant frequencies, and specific Hz values produce specific, predictable effects on human biology.

~500 BCE · Ancient Greece
Pythagoras
Mathematician · Philosopher · Healer
"Music is the harmonisation of opposites, the reconciliation of warring elements, and union of those that are contrary."
Key frequencies: 256 Hz (C) · 384 Hz (G) · Ratios 3:2, 2:1, 4:3
Pythagoras discovered that musical intervals are mathematical ratios — the octave (2:1), the fifth (3:2), the fourth (4:3). He prescribed specific harmonic ratios as medicine for diseases of the body, emotions, and soul. Pythagorean tuning forks are still used in sound therapy today. He was the first to document that beauty is mathematical and sound follows cosmic law.
~1500 CE · Florence, Italy
da Vinci
Polymath · Artist · Acoustic Pioneer
"The smallest feline is a masterpiece." — and sound, he believed, was no different: perfect in its mathematical proportions.
Key discovery: Sound travels as waves · Bell resonance mapping · Golden Ratio in proportion
Da Vinci was the first person in recorded history to prove that sound moves in waves (~1500 CE) — 150 years before Western science recognized it. He observed water ripples to demonstrate that energy travels through a medium without moving the medium itself. He documented bell acoustics in 40+ manuscript entries, designed new instruments, and devoted his life to demonstrating that the Golden Ratio (1.618) governs both the human body and musical harmony — the same mathematical constants Bach later embedded in his most resonant compositions.
1856–1943 CE · Serbia · New York
Tesla
Electrical Engineer · Inventor · Visionary
"If you want to find the secrets of the universe, think in terms of energy, frequency, and vibration."
Key framework: 3-6-9 mathematics · 432 Hz digital root 9 · Resonant frequency matching
Tesla's entire body of work was built on resonance: he showed that matching a system's natural frequency amplifies energy transfer exponentially. His 3-6-9 theory — "if you only knew the magnificence of 3, 6 and 9, you would have a key to the universe" — describes mathematical control points in natural energy systems. The digital root of 432 is 9. His electromagnetic frequency research became the direct predecessor of FDA-approved PEMF therapy and TMS (transcranial magnetic stimulation) now used to treat depression in hospitals worldwide.
1685–1750 CE · Germany
Bach
Composer · Mathematical Architect
"The aim and final end of all music should be none other than the glory of God and the refreshment of the soul."
Key tuning: A = 415 Hz (Baroque standard) · Well-Tempered Clavier · Fibonacci structures
Bach composed in Baroque tuning — A at approximately 415–420 Hz, not the modern 440 Hz adopted in 1939. His Well-Tempered Clavier was built on Pythagorean mathematical ratios. Musicologists have documented Fibonacci sequence proportions throughout his fugues and counterpoint. When you hear Bach's music, you are experiencing a mathematical template that mirrors the body's own proportional architecture — which is why it consistently produces the strongest evidence for focus enhancement in ADHD research: 60 BPM Baroque music produces alpha brainwaves in EEG across multiple replicated studies.
