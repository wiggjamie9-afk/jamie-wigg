## 🎥 Production Tips

### Recording
- **Screen recording**: OBS Studio (free) or ScreenFlow (Mac)
- **Facecam**: Use webcam for intro/outro (builds connection)
- **Audio**: Blue Yeti or built-in is fine (clarity > quality)
- **Screen resolution**: 1920x1080 @ 60fps

### Editing
- **Free**: DaVinci Resolve (professional-grade)
- **Paid**: Adobe Premiere ($55/mo) or Final Cut Pro ($300 one-time)
- **Cut ruthlessly**: Remove pauses, dead time, "ums"
- **Pacing**: 2-3 second cuts keep viewers engaged
- **Text overlays**: Highlight key concepts
- **B-roll**: Show code, architecture diagrams, product screenshots

### Thumbnails
- **Template**: High-contrast background + face + text
- **Tools**: Figma (free), Canva, Adobe Express
- **Best practices**:
  - Large, readable text (80pt+)
  - 3 colors max
  - Your face (clickable)
  - High contrast (red, yellow, blue work)

### Analytics (What to Watch)
- **Click-through rate (CTR)**: Thumbnails + titles working?
- **Watch time**: Are people staying engaged?
- **Audience retention**: Where do people drop off?
- **Traffic source**: Suggested videos, search, subscriptions?

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Uploading sporadically** → Stick to schedule (Mon/Wed/Fri)
2. **Boring intros** → Hook viewers in first 5 seconds
3. **Long videos without energy** → Cut ruthlessly
4. **No clear CTA** → Tell viewers what to do next
5. **Ignoring comments** → Reply to first 10 comments = boost algorithm
6. **No thumbnails** → Bad thumbnail = 50% fewer clicks
7. **Hiding product link** → Link in description + pinned comment
8. **No community tabs** → Post between videos (growth without uploading)
