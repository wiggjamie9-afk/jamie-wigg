# ECHO Character Design Specification

## Character Overview
**ECHO** is the dark antagonist representing sensory chaos and the negative manifestation of ADHD traits including distraction, overwhelm, and chaotic energy.

---

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette
- **Primary Dark Colors**: Deep purples (#2d1b4e), blacks (#0a0a0a), charcoal grays (#2a2a2a)
- **Electric Neon Accents**: 
  - Cyan (#00d8ff) - representing fractured attention/distraction rays
  - Violet (#b800e6) - representing chaotic energy pulses
  - Electric yellow (#ffff00) - representing sensory overload spikes

### Line Work & Style
- Clean, bold cartoon lines with consistent professional weight
- Dynamic motion lines throughout the design suggesting chaos, distraction, energy
- Sharp, jagged edges on clothing and details conveying discord
- Professional 2D cartoon illustration quality suitable for animation/game assets
- Studio-quality rendering with clean edges and high contrast

---

## Character Design Elements

### Body & Silhouette
- **Posture**: Angular, dynamic stance conveying constant restless motion and tension
- **Build**: Form-fitting, otherworldly physiology with sharp angles
- **Clothing**: Dark form-fitting suit/bodysuit with:
  - Jagged, irregular edges
  - Electric pulse/lightning patterns woven throughout
  - Asymmetrical design suggesting instability
  - Layered look with fractured segments

### Head & Face
- **Eyes**: Sharp, intense, fractured appearance suggesting distraction and sensory overwhelm
  - Sometimes appearing as spirals (distraction motif)
  - Sometimes narrowed with intense focus (overstimulation)
  - May have multiple focal points or flickering quality
- **Hair**: Spiky, sharp, wind-swept with electric aesthetic
  - Defies gravity suggesting constant motion
  - Dark base with neon highlights/traces

### Expression Range
The character sheet includes **4 primary expressions**:

1. **Chaotic/Wild** - Peak distraction state
   - Spiraling or multiple-directional eyes
   - Manic, unhinged grin
   - Energy radiating outward with motion lines
   - Body in aggressive forward lean

2. **Mocking/Taunting** - Condescending antagonism
   - Cruel, knowing smirk
   - Narrowed, contemptuous eyes
   - Posture suggests superiority and threat
   - Hand gestures conveying dismissal or challenge

3. **Overwhelming/Intense** - Sensory overload
   - Laser-focused but fractured gaze
   - Mouth potentially distorted or oversized
   - Intense visual density with motion lines
   - Body rigid with tension and overstimulation

4. **Defeated/Retreating** - Loss of power
   - Fading, translucent form
   - Weakened expression, closed eyes or downturned gaze
   - Dissipating energy wisps
   - Posture collapsed or retreating inward

### Action Poses
The character sheet includes **4 dynamic action poses**:

1. **Creating Chaos** - Antagonistic offensive
   - Arms spread wide or reaching outward
   - Scattering motion lines radiating from body
   - Body position suggests mid-attack or summoning
   - Surrounding chaos visually emphasized

2. **Swooping Attack** - Aerial assault
   - Dynamic diagonal or angled flight pose
   - Forward momentum clearly indicated
   - Trailing energy or motion blur
   - Menacing approach vector

3. **Energy Scattering/Explosion** - Peak chaos release
   - Form breaking apart or fragmenting
   - Explosion of energy, fragments, and motion blur
   - Neon colors dominant and dispersing
   - Surrounding space filled with chaos

4. **Imploding/Collapsing** - Defeated form
   - Body folding or drawing inward
   - Form appearing to break apart or disintegrate
   - Energy retreating and inverting
   - Visual suggests defeat and withdrawal

## Character Narrative Context

### Role in Story
ECHO embodies the physical manifestation of:
- **Sensory overwhelm** - The paralyzing effect of too much stimulation
- **Distraction embodied** - Chaotic energy that pulls focus in multiple directions
- **Negative manifestation** - The shadow side of ADHD traits (vs. positive neurodivergence)
- **Defeatable antagonist** - Represents a challenge that can be overcome through focus and grounding

### Visual Themes
- **Chaos vs. Order** - Jagged vs. clean lines
- **Distraction** - Multiple focal points, motion lines, fractured details
- **Energy** - Constant motion suggested through posture and trailing effects
- **Sensory Overload** - Visual density, neon saturation in moments of peak chaos
- **Darkness with Light** - Dark palette with electric accents suggesting "noise" in the signal

---

## Design Notes for Animation/Implementation

- Maintain consistent character proportions across all expressions and poses
- High contrast between dark base and neon accents for visual clarity
- Motion lines should feel dynamic but not cluttered - suggest movement without obscuring form
- Electric effects should pulse/flicker to suggest constant chaotic energy
- Ensure expressions read clearly even at small sizes
- Hair and clothing details can flex with emotion while maintaining core silhouette
- Body language should clearly communicate emotional state in each pose/expression
